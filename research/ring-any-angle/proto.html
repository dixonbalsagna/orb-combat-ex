Watch: the same scripted fight on every world, with the choreographer asking for a new angle every few seconds. Play, on the keyboard: A D move the pair, Shift fly flat out, Space a send that lands in a crater, X dig a crater here, Q E ask for 30 degrees either way, G aim the line at the last crater, R start again. On a pad: left stick moves, RT flat out, LB RB ask, X a send, A dig, Y aim. Try: dig, fly out, press G and come back over the crater from a new angle, on C and then on D.
