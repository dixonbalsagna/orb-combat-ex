The turntable arena (Research, spike v2; paper: docs/world/ring-any-angle/research.md). A true sphere, 153,600 units round, with the world swinging round two bars. Everything you see is one planet map already on the graphics card and one rotation: nothing is rebuilt when it swings. No character art: fighters are bars. Move: A D or the left stick. Flat out: hold Shift or RT. Send (a launch): Space or pad X. Swing: Q E or LB RB. Aim the line at a landmark: G or pad Y. Dig a crater: C or pad A. Start again: R. It plays itself until you touch something.
