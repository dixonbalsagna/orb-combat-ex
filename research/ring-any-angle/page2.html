The turntable arena (Research, spike v2; paper: docs/world/ring-any-angle/research.md). A true sphere, 153,600 units round, with the world swinging round two bars. The ground is tiles keyed to the planet, drawn from one map already on the graphics card, and turned by one matrix: nothing is rebuilt when it swings. No character art: fighters are bars. Move: A D or the left stick. Flat out: hold Shift or RT. Launch: Space or pad X. Swing by hand: Q E or LB RB. Aim the line at a landmark: G or pad Y. Form's pause: F or LT. Beam (hold; the world comes to rest): B or pad B. A heavy blow (the world jolts): H or the right stick's click. Dig a crater: C or pad A. Start again: R. It plays itself until you touch something.
