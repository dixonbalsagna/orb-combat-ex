The turntable arena (Research, spike v2; paper: docs/world/ring-any-angle/research.md). A true sphere, 153,600 units round, with the world swinging round two bars, on real gen 2 ground. Press 1 to 5 for the five levels: each changes only what's drawn; the sim's ground stays the same. Every setting a level uses is below it, to change one at a time. Stand-ins only: no character art, and towers, trees and debris are placeholders. No character art: fighters are bars. Move: A D or the left stick. Flat out: hold Shift or RT. Launch: Space or pad X. Swing by hand: Q E or LB RB. Aim the line at a landmark: G or pad Y. Form's pause: F or LT. Beam (hold; the world comes to rest): B or pad B. A heavy blow (the world jolts): H or the right stick's click. Dig a crater: C or pad A. Start again: R. It plays itself until you touch something.
