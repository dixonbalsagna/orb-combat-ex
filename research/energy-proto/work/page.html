Energy overhaul prototype (Research, 2026-10-23), built to Game Design's frame (docs/design/energy-overhaul.md, with its section 19 rulings). The clips are its section 13, C1 to C10, plus C7b, the four grabs: watch one step by step, or Play. The four fighters carry Combat's energy kits (names provisional) in Art's colour lanes, drawn to Legal's look rules. Bodies are flat stand-ins, two at a time. Knock-backs are drawn at a third of the body's scale to fit the frame.
