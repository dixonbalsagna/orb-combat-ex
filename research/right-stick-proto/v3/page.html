Right hand attacks, v3 (Research, 2026-10-28): the left hand survives, the right hand hurts (docs/ep/right-hand-attacks-pitch.md). The left stick moves; LT, LB or both plus a left flick slip, brace or vanish; LB alone guards. The face buttons are the quick moves; the right stick is the big move: a strike (flick it, or hold it through the wind-up to charge), and with RB held a beam. The pitch switch below changes how a stick strike is costed and where the signature lives. The right thumb keeps Controls' rule: a button after a stick move waits 6 ticks, a stick move after a button waits 8, so a claw grip is never faster. Numbers are the pitch's first guesses.

    The pitches. 4a: stick strikes are their own class, quick, they stun, with their own pace. 4b: the stick is the heavy's direction, with the heavy's wind-up and pace and no stun. 4c: quick strikes that stun, but costed like the heavy (6 ki, the heavy's pace), and the signature moves to RT and the stick. The clips each name their pitch on the panel; in Play the switch takes effect at once (keys 1 2 3).

    Play: a gamepad works as it is (the standard mapping: both sticks, LT, LB, RT, RB, A, B, X, Y and the D-pad). Keyboard: W A S D the left stick, ← ↑ → ↓ the right stick (tap to strike, hold to charge), Shift is LT, Q is LB, E holds RB, R holds RT, J K L I are X, Y, B and A, T F G H the D-pad (the right stick's mirror). A left-hand move: hold Shift, Q or both, then tap W A S D. The Rival closes in and throws a blow, a high one, a heavy, a low one, a flurry and a grab in turn, with a shot from range; he is a training dummy and his health refills.
