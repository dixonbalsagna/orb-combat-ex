Right hand attacks, v3 (Research, 2026-10-28), built to Controls' spec (docs/controls/right-hand-attacks.md) and Game Design's rulings (docs/design/right-hand-attacks-rulings.md): the left hand survives, the right hand hurts. The left stick moves. LT starts the dodge and a left flick in the next 6 ticks makes it a slip that way; LB guards, and a quick left flick from rest with LB down sets a brace; LB and LT within 6 ticks and a direction is the vanish. The face buttons are the quick moves. The right stick is a fifth button with a direction: back within 12 ticks it is the quick strike; held, a charge, thrown when you let go. With RB held it is the beam; with RT held in 4c, the signature. While the stick is held the face buttons are inert, so a claw grip is never faster. Numbers are the rulings' first values.

    The pitches. 4c (the default, Game Design's split by gesture): the quick strike is its own class (wind-up 12, a 20-tick stun, a medium's worth); held, it is the charged heavy aimed by the stick (B held's numbers: armoured against lights, levels at 12, 30 and 50); the signature is on RT and the stick, hold and release. 4a: own class all the way, with its own ladder (12, 30, 54; placeholder numbers). 4b: the stick is B with a direction. In 4a and 4b the signature stays on RT and B. The switch takes effect at once in Play (keys 1 4a, 2 4b, 3 4c).

    Play: a gamepad works as it is (the standard mapping: both sticks, LT, LB, RT, RB, A, B, X, Y; the D-pad when its mirror is on). Keyboard (Controls' solo layout): W A S D the left stick, the arrow keys the right stick (tap for the quick strike, hold to charge, let go to throw), Space LT, Shift LB, Q RB, E RT, J K L I X, Y, B and A, C the vanish chord in one key. A slip: Space, then tap a direction. A brace: Shift held, tap a direction (a held direction walks guarded). The vanish: Shift and Space together (or C), then tap a direction. The Rival is a training dummy: he closes in and throws a light, a medium at three heights, a heavy, a flurry and a grab in turn, guards for a while, and shoots from range; his health refills.
