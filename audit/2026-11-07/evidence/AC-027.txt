Terms: (1) air and light never dims or tints the sky, never darkens any part of the frame, and is never a screen-wide wash: it is a local effect around the front and along the ground under the beam's path; (2) the ripple is a local distortion band at the front, never a full-screen blur or warp (ADR 0010) and never over a fighter; (3) the ground colour sweep is the shooter's lane colour, thin, moving at the front's speed with no pulse or strobe, never gold, white or red (k03), and no brighter than the beam itself; (4) dust lifts as a light drift at the front, not a rubble ring, crack and wind at a fighter (stacking mark 4, k01); (5) the screen-edge light is a steady thin line that ramps in and out over at least 6 ticks, never blinks, flickers or pulses, never white or red, never wider than the thickness cap Game Design names, and sits at the screen's edge only, never over or between the fighters; (6) Reduce flashing turns the strong setting and any ramp faster than (5) off, and the family obeys the flash rules like any glow: flash clips run with it on at Standard and Full, in both modes, and in the night sky (RL-223); (7) colour-vision presets keep the line readable without gold, white or red; (8) the caption is plain text and needs no ruling; (9) it ships through the look gate with a side-by-side. ## Nothing here fades, pulses or moves: a mark is drawn or it is not, and nothing is drawn over a fighter (a mark that would meet a body is not drawn).
## What a player chose, one value per family, with Reduce flashing's limit applied: the strong air, the bright glint, the thick line and the loudest, hardest answers come down a step.
## The host (VFX, Audio, Controls) reads this through UiHud.alert_for(slot); UI reads it for the line and the caption.
static func effective(opts: Dictionary, slot: int) -> Dictionary:
	var sfx: String = suffix(slot)
	var out := {}
	for k in FAMILIES:
		out[k.trim_prefix("alert_")] = opts.get(k + sfx, null)
	if bool(opts.get("reduce_flashing", true)):
		if out["air"] == "strong":
			out["air"] = "subtle"
		if out["glint"] == "bright":
			out["glint"] = "normal"
		if out["edge_width"] == "thick":
			out["edge_width"] = "medium"
## The edge line's strength, 0 to 1: it ramps in over 6 ticks when the alert starts and out over the same when it ends (RL-227: never blinks, never faster than 6 ticks). Steady between.
static func line_alpha(alert: Dictionary) -> float:
	var ramp: float = 6.0 / 60.0
	var a_in: float = clampf(float(alert.get("age", 0.0)) / ramp, 0.0, 1.0)
	var a_out: float = 1.0
	if alert.has("end_t"):
		a_out = 1.0 - clampf(float(alert["end_t"]) / ramp, 0.0, 1.0)
