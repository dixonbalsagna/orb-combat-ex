## glasses-rules.md (HEAD) :5 :24-27 :46 :54
**Verdict: GO.** Glasses that flash opaque with glare are a stock anime trope, free to use. Several well-known characters own particular versions of it, so the rules below keep ours generic and ours.
1. **Shape:** a hard-edged glint cut in his blade language, a flat wedge or a sweeping slash across the lens, never a round white disc with a star twinkle. The lens goes opaque for a beat, then clears.
2. **Colour:** a pale tint of his own violet, not pure white, and never gold, orange or red. Normal lenses are a dark violet tint, not orange.
3. **Timing:** short. A quick attack and a hold of 0.4 s at most. At key moments only: reading a move, pride, a taunt, the seal break (cracked lens). Never every exchange.
4. **Never at a transformation break,** and never with a shouted form name. That is how a white flash becomes mark 7 of the stacking rule.
| 3 Chamfer plates | **CHANGE** or drop |
- **Chamfer plates (3):** wide, near-rectangular lenses with clipped corners are the closest to a plain tinted rectangle, which the rules keep out. Keep it only if the chamfers are deep enough to read as plates and not a rectangle at 24 px. Otherwise drop it. The other five are clear.
