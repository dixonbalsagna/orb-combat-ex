Orb Combat EX (working title)

The whole planet is the arena. No walls, no corners, and a brawl choreographed around you.

A one-person project: Orb directs it and builds it with AI tools.

A limited test build is up for now.

It sits behind a notice because our own automated check found some scenes that go over the recommended limit of three flashes a second, and they are not fixed yet. No independent photosensitivity analyzer has been run. It is a limited test build and may be taken down without notice. There is no date for a full release.

Combos that build as you fight

No combo lists to memorise. Press a button, land a blow. The fight director is designed to chain your light, medium and heavy strikes into combos on the fly, shaped by where you stand and how the fight is going. No two exchanges are meant to play out the same way twice. The fight director is ordinary game code, not an AI model.

A brawl in your hands

Planned: steer a flurry across the sky. Charge a heavy that shrugs off jabs. Aim a launcher with the stick. Fire point-blank energy. Zip past a rival's guard and out the other side.

No edges. No corners.

No walls, no side to be cornered on. Fly one way and you come back round the other: city, desert, ocean, mountains. Planned: a sky that turns from noon to night as you cross it.

Hit hard enough and it stays broken

Beams gouge trenches into the ground. Buildings crack and break apart in stages. The world keeps every scar until the match ends.

Features described here are in development and may change.

Orb Combat EX is a one-person project: Orb directs it and builds it with AI tools. AI tools wrote the game's code and drafted its art, animation data, dialogue, UI text and design documents. Its procedural systems (planets, fight choreography, the computer-controlled opponent) are ordinary code, not AI models.

This page collects and sends no personal data. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs.
