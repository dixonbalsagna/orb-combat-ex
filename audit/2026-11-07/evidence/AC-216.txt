== docs/legal/store-copy-screen.md:23
5. **"[Title] is a trademark of [holder]"** (A2): use "™" only once the title is settled and in use, and **never "®" or "registered"** until a registration exists (a false ® is a deceptive mark). Say "[Title] and its logo are trademarks of [holder]" only when the holder line is decided.
== docs/legal/never-use-words.md:19
- "open source" for the whole project; "official", "licensed" or "exclusive" for the music; "first" and "best" claims; "balanced" or "fair"; "registered" or the ® symbol before a registration; "Steam Deck Verified" before the badge; "approved by", "in partnership with" or "powered by" for any platform, engine or AI tool.
== docs/legal/pre-store-checklist.md:13
- **Registering:** you can apply at the US trademark office yourself for the title, and later the logo, in the classes for game software and entertainment. A lawyer makes it smoother. Until then, use the ™ mark. The trademark is what protects the name and logo, and it does not depend on who drew them.
