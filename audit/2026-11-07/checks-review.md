# Checks review

Independent Audit of 2026-11-07. Every automated gate and standing safety check, with what it claims to protect, what it really tests (from reading the code), whether it runs on the build players get or on a stand-in, whether it can pass while the danger is present (vacuous pass) or fail when it is absent (false alarm), its upkeep, and a verdict: KEEP, FIX (what), MERGE (with what), RETIRE or MISSING.

How this table was made: a helper inventoried the flash checks and the other checks from code (evidence/gates-flash.json, evidence/gates-other.json); a second helper merged them into families with the standing rules and the EP's account (audit/disclosure/3-gate-inventory.md, used as a pointer only) and re-checked the cited lines (command output in evidence/G-90.txt); the auditor reviewed every row against the findings and corrected the user-profile path count (52 files at HEAD, evidence/F-07.txt). Node-only self-tests were run read-only; no Godot or browser check was run.

**The headline:** of 50 rows, 12 KEEP, 22 FIX, 2 MERGE, 2 RETIRE and 12 MISSING. The checks that act on what players actually get are few: the runtime register (K-04), the Reduce flashing lock and the interim site check (K-14), the exported-build hook check (K-27) and the nightly (K-08), which has lapsed. Every one of the CI checks has been off since 2026-11-06 (F-03). The recount that CI calls a flash gate cannot fail on a granted flash (K-03).

## Practice changes

1. **Start a CI liveness guard outside ci.yml.** A tiny separate workflow runs check-workflows.mjs on every push, plus a daily check that ci.yml ran and passed on main. Make check-workflows a step in the EP's gate before every push. Rows K-20, K-32, M-01, M-02.
2. **Start a take-down path that waits on nothing.** An offline-only deploy needs only check-site-offline.mjs. Take the prototype suites, the ratchet and the phone-head check out of the deploy's needs. Rows K-29, K-26, K-15, K-19.
3. **Stop calling the register recount a flash-safety gate.** It is a register-regression and coverage test, and it cannot fail on registered flashes. Merge flash_reg_check and effects_check's flash section into it, and say plainly that the live mitigations are the register, the lock and the notice. Rows K-03, K-04.
4. **Start scheduled checks on what is live.** Run the interim expiry and the nightly's age daily, and a pixel run on every pin change. Until that exists, either run the nightly when it is due or take /play/ down. ADR 0010 names the nightly as a safeguard, and it has lapsed for 8 days. Rows K-08, K-14, K-05, M-04a, M-04b.
5. **Stop silent passes.** "Grandfathered", "owner: report only" and "CHECK" are printed as exceptions in the deploy summary Orb reads, never as "ok". Retire the stale d3f1ca0e record. A FAIL or CHECK clip is not handed on. Rows K-06, K-07, K-10.
6. **Start reading the build, not the plan.** Every hand-over (Tools' kept build, the LAN kit) reads its packed data back against the switch list, records the git SHA, runs the route scan, and carries either a flash reading or the private warning. Rows K-33, K-34, M-06.
7. **Merge Legal's prose clip conditions into one machine-read list** that the release pass and the pin move read, so an open condition blocks a pin. Rows K-09, K-05.
8. **Fix the analyser before trusting its margins.** Break the counter-phase tie and add the case to the self-test. Before any store release, calibrate against a recognised analyser on a fixed clip set. Rows K-01, K-02, K-11, M-05.
9. **Start a public-repo content screen on every push.** Turn on GitHub secret scanning and push protection, and lint for user-profile paths, franchise words beyond generator output, SDK paths and RL-014 on fighter data. Rows M-08, M-09, K-17.
10. **Make sign-offs name their evidence, and file gates where they belong.** Every Legal, QA or phase sign-off names the build SHA and what it read, and says "screen" where it is one. The look gate and the machine-discipline guards stay, but leave the safety records: they are Orb's creative direction and dev-machine hygiene. Rows K-35, K-36, K-37, K-39.
