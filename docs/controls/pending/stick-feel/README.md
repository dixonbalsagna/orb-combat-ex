**Gates** (clean export of HEAD + the frozen Controls files + `quick-read.patch` + these three): all 26 input tests pass on a clean export of the current HEAD with every park applied (`stick_feel_test` 89, `haptics_test` 80, `quick_read_test` 30), `validate` 284 files 0 errors 0 warnings, `lint-determinism` ok. The kinds keep the haptics rules: distinct, each over within 160 ms, the motor on at most half the time at the least gap, none the high motor alone but the brake's, and a phone pattern of its own.

**What each does.** Pitch 1: with `hapticLadder` on, a held stick strike pulses once as each rung of the ladder is reached, 12 / 30 / 50 live ticks from the request's start (tick, knock, thud), none for a quick flick, none after a cancel, again for a second charge. Pitch 3: with `hapticIncoming` on, the defender's pad gets one cue when a rival's stick strike starts, high for toward and up, low for away and down; the striker feels nothing. Both need `rightStick`. Neither reads or writes the sim, the replay or the hash; rumble is never the only cue and has the existing off switch (the `haptics` option).

For the preview: `tools/preview/switches.mjs` can add `hapticLadder` and `hapticIncoming` beside `quickRead`. Delete this folder when the three land.

**Row 40's rumble (`hapticIncomingBeam`).** Needs Encounter's `beam_window` cue, which is not in the tree yet; the fields it reads are UI's contract: `text` `start` or `end`, `actor` (shooter), `target` (receiver, else the other slot), `n` (ticks from the hand to arrival); `k` and `dodge_from` are not used. `main.gd` parses with the host patch on the current HEAD (a Godot `--check-only`), and `stick_feel_test` covers the pulses (inside the window, rising, 100 ms or more apart, the last the loudest, closed by `end` or a zero length, nothing with the switch off, the cue itself rumbles nothing).

## Round 4 (2026-11-01): the signature on RT + B only

`signature-rt-only.patch` (`sim/input/input_data.gd`, `layout.gd`, `test/stick_feel_test.gd`; apply after `first-press.patch`, independent of `input-buffer`) and `switches.signatureRtOnly` in `haptic-switches.patch` (off). With it on: B alone is the big blow, **RT + B is the signature**, and B with LB, RB or LT held is the heavy of that stance (LB and LT: the heavy blow, 21 at close range; RB: Y's blast, 25, 48 held), so a guard or a dodge never fires a beam by accident. The first-playable build should turn it on. The signature costs 45 ki (measured: ki 100 to 55.3 on the beam's tick), as the card says.
