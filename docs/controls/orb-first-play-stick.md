# What Orb's raw stick did on the path (first play, 1 November; counts only, the log stays private)

From the black box's `rawRightStick` (6,059 samples, an Xbox 360 pad) and the replay's stick words, read by `docs/controls/clips/read-stick-log.cjs`. A 6.1-minute match; the stick left the dead zone 32 times.

| Question | Answer |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Flicks lost to the reader?** | **None.** 30 of 30 pushes that reached the request line (raw 0.70) made a request. The other 2 never got there (peaks 0.45 to 0.65, 6 ticks or fewer): short leans, lost to the rules, not to the thumb. Orb's thumb is fast: median 2 ticks from leaving the dead zone to the line, and always full deflection (peak about 1.0) |
| **Do holds reach their levels?** | **Yes.** All 15 holds (out longer than 12 ticks) were released, at the level their length earns: out 39 to 48 ticks gave level 2, 60 or more gave level 3 (the ladder is 12, 30, 50; 12, 30, 54 for a beam). The rare level 0 on a hold of 13 ticks is the settle |
| **RB + hold: did it release?** | 4 of 4 RB holds released (levels 2, 3, 3, 3), and the 2 RB flicks released as quick. Six RB uses, all inside 11 seconds (188 s to 223 s). Whether a beam left the hand cannot be read here: the log carries the intent, not the director's cues |
| **Any phantom or spring-back?** | No request without a push; rest never reached the dead zone's edge in a way that mattered (mean 0.05, highest 0.24). One burst at 357 s made 5 requests in 21 ticks without the stick falling back inside the dead zone (a pump; the reader handled it as designed) |
| **"Holding starts the charging, letting go releases": true on the path?** | **Yes, with no hidden delay.** The request starts 3 ticks after the stick passes the line (2 to the settle, 1 to the build), and charging counts from there; the release edge comes as the stick falls under the release line (raw 0.51), a median of 2 ticks after it re-enters the dead zone and up to 9 ticks **before** it does, because the thumb is still returning. The wait Orb may feel is the director's, not the input's: a quick strike lands 7 ticks after it starts, a charged one on the next live tick |

**Why "couldn't figure out how to fire a beam"** (Orb's words): the log shows the order. At 32 s, 181 s and 185 s Orb held the stick out with **no RB** (out 60, 116 and 69 ticks, level 3 each): that is a charged strike, not a beam. At 188 s Orb held RB and the stick (out 124 ticks, level 3), then again at 196 s and 199 s. Orb's own sentence, "holding the right stick should start the charging, letting go should release the attack", is what the stick does **without** RB; the beam needs RB held first, and nothing on screen says so. The sheet now says it, and it is the first thing a hint should say (a Back-button line, or a cue on a level 2 hold with no RB).
