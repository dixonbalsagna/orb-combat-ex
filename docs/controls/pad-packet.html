The pad

This is a generic pad drawn for the team. Button names are by position, so X is the left face button, Y the top, B the right and A the bottom.

Five laws

Beams are the right stick's

Orb, 2026-11-01: "beams are exclusively fired with the right stick." Y is explosive lobs. B is kinetic and focused. No face button fires a beam, and no answer to a beam does either.

One press, one attack

Every press does something you can feel, or the pad tells you it couldn't. A press never vanishes silently.

No claw advantage

A thumb is on the stick or on the buttons, never both. Holding the pad in a claw grip gains nothing, and a gate of 262 checks holds this line.

A held stick wins

The player stays in control of their fighter. Nothing takes control without a reason they can feel.

No dead hints

If a press does nothing yet, it isn't listed on screen. It's off until it answers in a fight.

Every combination

Read a row across. A held shoulder changes what your right hand does. IN BUILD works in the first-play build. OFF is planned and shows no hint. COMING OUT breaks a law. UNDECIDED needs a ruling.

Also off for now: brace (LB + a left-stick flick), the vanish (LB + LT, a question for Orb), transformations (LT + RT), and steering a beam with the right stick. R3 is unbound. LT + RB (the kite) makes the right stick fire energy too. LB + RB (the lay) leaves it doing nothing.

The right stick

Four directions, same worth

The damage is the same in all four. What changes is where they end up, so the stick puts them where you aimed.

Flick or hold

Two dull knocks on the pad mean the press was refused, for example because your fighter can't act.

The answer window

A beam travels, so you have time to answer while it's on its way. The hit is decided when its front reaches you. This is a signature beam at close range, in the first-play build. Further away, or with a bigger beam, you get more time, up to 60 ticks.

Beam answers: the workshop

Orb asked for these on 2026-11-01. The first three are the required window. Everything marked as an idea is a starting point for Game Design, Controls and Combat to test and change. None of the ideas is built.

Each idea has to fit the laws above: left hand for moving and defending, right hand for attacks, no beam from a face button. It also has to work with two different thumbs on the same stick, and at either end of the planet.

On screen

A sketch of the first-play layout. In a real frame, the panel on the left only appears while the buttons mean something new, and it's gone during a beam struggle. There's no marker over the fighters.

Open questions

  1. Where does a signature beam go? It's leaving RT + B. RT + right stick is one candidate, since it does nothing today. Game Design recommends and Orb decides.
  2. What does RT + B become? B is meant to read as high damage, focused and kinetic.
  3. Which beam answers ship with the first fighters, and which belong to one fighter? Orb's C3 note asked for emergent play across a roster of twelve.
  4. The Driver on RB + B is Game Design's pick, and Orb hasn't confirmed it.
  5. The vanish on LB + LT: teleporting has been on hold since 2026-10-01.

Words used here

tick
One game step. There are 60 in a second, so 36 ticks is 0.6 seconds.
bh
Body height, about 75 units. Distances are measured in these.
layer
What a held shoulder does to your right hand: guard, dodge, energy or power.
flick
The stick out and back to rest within 12 ticks.
Charge, ki
The same energy bar. Players see Charge, and team pages say ki.
send
A blow that launches someone a long way.
struggle
Two beams locked, pressed on the beat, with a meeting point that moves.
the mirror
A setting that makes the D-pad act as the right stick, for pads without one.
