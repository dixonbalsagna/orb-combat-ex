# dodgeHeld: LT held, in the intent's second word (Controls, 2026-11-06; for Encounter, Netcode and Simulation)

Game Design ruled the flight strike's take-back (docs/design/playable-build.md section 1): **a face press within 4 ticks after LT, LT still held at the face press, never button-first, measured on the face edge the sim sees (after the strike law).** The intent had LT's press edge (`dodge`) and `sprint` only after a long hold, so "LT still held at the face press" could not be read. This adds the bit. The park is `docs/controls/pending/dodge-held/`; behind `switches.dodgeHeld` (off, needs `rightStick`, which owns the second word).

## What it is
`SimIntent.dodgeHeld`, bit 26 of the second word (`pack2`, now 27 bits, `BITS2` 27): **the dodge control is physically down this tick.**
- True on LT's press tick (the `dodge` edge tick) and on every tick LT stays down; false from the tick after the release.
- **A tap that came and went between two builds** (a stall, see stall-keep) is a `dodge` edge with `dodgeHeld` false: LT was not held at any face press that follows.
- It is the finger's state: a **toggled** dodge (the hold-or-toggle option) that is on with the finger up reads false; a keyboard dodge key and the swapped layout's dodge control read the same way.
- Off, nothing is written: the word is 0 for LT held exactly as it was, so a record is what it always was.

## For Encounter: reading the take-back
On the tick of a face edge (`light`, `heavy` or `sig`) the take-back is: `dodgeHeld` is true, and the last `dodge` edge was 0 to 4 ticks ago (`dodge` on the face's own tick counts: LT then face on the same tick converts). A face edge before the LT edge never converts (the blow started on its press tick). The face edge is the one after the strike law, so a face press that waited behind a strike stick has aged out of the 4 ticks, or was dropped, and is not a take-back: the claw verdict (docs/controls/lt-take-back-claw.md) holds. `sim/input/test/dodge_held_test.gd` has the rule as a function over the intent stream (the `_converted` helper) with the cases: held, let go before the press (not held), let go after (held at the press), face first (never), same tick, X, Y and B, a held strike stick, the swapped layout.

## NEEDS FROM EP (the files that are not Controls')
- **Netcode** (`net/session.gd`, `net/tools/word_check.gd`, `net/bot.gd`, `net/tools/rollback_check.gd`, `docs/net/rollback.md`): the word is 27 bits now, so `predictWord` must keep LT held (a held state, like the first word's held fields): without it every LT hold is a misprediction. Measured: with `--dodge-held` (new flag, the bots hold LT in runs of 17 ticks of every 51) the rollback check shows **240 and 209 rollbacks on p80j and p200 with the old prediction, 167 and 167 with the new** (correct either way). `word_check` now tests the wire width at `BITS2` (bit 26 is valid, bit 27 is refused) and the prediction of the bit (623 checks). **A CI step for `rollback_check --dodge-held`** (tools: `.github/workflows/ci.yml`) would keep the bit carried through rollback.
- **Simulation:** nothing to change in code. `SimHash` reads `pack2(f.input)` (hash.gd:171) and the net digest reads it, so **the bit enters the state hash and the digest on the ticks it is set**: with the switch off or no LT held the hash is what it always was (parity golden passes). A match recorded with the switch on carries bit 26 in its word-2 entries, so **an old reader (BITS2 26) refuses it** (`unpack2` rejects a bit above the width); old replays play in the new reader unchanged. Every reader of `inp[4]` I found calls `SimIntent.unpack2` (replay.gd, `docs/perf/tools/preplay/*`, `qa/godot/*`), so none has a width of its own. `sim/core/replay.gd`'s header comment says 26 bits: the patch makes it 27 (comment only).
- **Not in the switches-on patch:** `dodgeHeld` stays off there (the EP asked); turning it on is `"dodgeHeld": true` in `data/input/timing.json` once Encounter reads it. The patch's pin helper lists it so the old-behaviour tests stay pinned off.
- **Touch:** the touch layout (`SimTouch`) has no LT; it never sets the bit.
