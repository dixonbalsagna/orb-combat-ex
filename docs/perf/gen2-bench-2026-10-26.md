# Gen 2 (planet generation 2) benches in the web build (2026-10-26)

Owner: Performance and Platform, for the EP and World (docs/world/gen2-readiness.md, the Performance row). Scratch export of HEAD 7f336633 with a hook that switches gen 2 on **per call** (`WorldClimate.useGen`, `setup.planetGen`); nothing in data changes. Web export through the guarded launcher, **every figure from a valid run** (load rule), best of two or three repeats. "x1" is this PC unthrottled (RTX 5070 Ti, and the integrated Radeon: the CPU work is the same on both, so one column); **x4 is Chrome's 4 times slower CPU (the old-laptop tier, T2); x6 is 6 times slower (the phone tier, T3 estimate: a lower bound, it cannot show a phone's memory or small caches)**. The hook is `docs/perf/tools/scratch-hooks/hook-gen2.mjs`; the run is `frame-bench.mjs --slice --slice-modes make1,make2,match1,match2,turn1:100000,turn2:100000,turn1:32,turn2:32,flow,water`.

## Verdicts against the budgets (docs/perf/budgets.md: a strip read of 150 ms in one call at x1 and 400 ms at most, done within 5 s on the phone tier; no frame over 50 ms after the warm-up; the page at 450 MB target and 600 MB limit)

1. **The cold planet make cannot run inside a frame on any tier.** 1.7 s at x1 (gen 1 was 0.36 s, already over the 150 ms single-call target), 9.5 s on the old laptop and 16 s on a phone (budget: 5 s). A match start with a cold gen 2 planet is 1.8 s, 10.5 s and 17.8 s. **A menu, load screen or step-wise make (as `SimRun.beginGround` does) is needed before any planet match on gen 2**; the lobby default must not start one cold. Once the planet is made a match start is 6 ms (35 ms at x4, 58 at x6): fine.
2. **The flow field is a second one-off**: 155 ms at x1 (just over the 150 ms target), 0.85 s at x4, 1.4 s on a phone. It belongs in the planet job's steps, not on a frame.
3. **The calm turn's first warm tick is a hitch only when the whole ring is read in one tick** (World's 330 ms measure is the k = 100,000 stress). At the real rate (k 8 to 32 a tick) the gen 2 tick is **4 ms at x1** (gen 1 2.5), fine; **at x4 it is 26 ms and at x6 44 ms for k = 32, and the worst of 80 ticks is 72 and 110 ms**, which on top of a 17 to 20 ms frame (x4) or a 30 ms frame (x6) puts a frame over 50 ms. The gen 2 ring read costs about 130 us a column at x1 (gen 1: about 50), 0.8 ms at x4 and 1.4 ms at x6. Because k must be the same on every platform (it changes when a ring is warm, so it is in the sim's state), the lever is the read itself: **gen 2's per-column cost needs the profile World gave the gen 1 read (9 times cheaper)**; at 130 us a column, k = 16 on two rings costs 4 ms at x1 but 26 ms at x4.
4. **The swap tick is fine**: 1.3 to 1.9 ms at x1 (the prepared swap), 8 ms at x4, 13 ms at x6.
5. **The water step with fills open fits**: 0.3 ms at x1 (a quarter of the eight flow windows it replaces as today's worst), 1.8 ms at x4, 2.9 ms at x6; nothing open costs 0.3 to 2.3 us a tick.
6. **Memory is not resolvable here**: the engine's static-memory counter reads 0 in the web build, and the page's renderer process is 511 to 535 MB after a gen 1 planet, 527 and 529 MB after gen 2 and 518 and 530 MB after gen 2 with its flow field: the same within the 10 to 20 MB noise, so gen 2's grids and flow arrays add **under 20 MB** (World's arrays are tens of MB at most). The page already sits at about 520 MB at boot, against a 450 MB target and a 600 MB limit, so **the headroom is 70 MB**; a phone's real limit needs a phone.

## The frame of a match rendered on a gen 2 planet, against gen 1 (added 2026-10-26)

Scene `pb` (hook-pbiome.mjs): planet 7's Whole ring (touch 3), the pair idle in the middle of the longest stretch of the wanted ground (desert, city, forest; the coast is where the ring's ocean share in a 41-column window is 30 to 70%), gen 1 against gen 2 on the same planet, 600 ticks, reduced mode, two interleaved repeats, valid runs; p50 / p95 ms, frames over 25 ms of 600, median primitives and draw calls (the stretches differ between the generations, so the geometry does too).

| Ground | Radeon x1, gen 1 | Radeon x1, gen 2 | Radeon x4, gen 1 / gen 2 (p50) | RTX x1, gen 1 / gen 2 (p50 / p95) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Desert (run 117 / 182 columns) | 8.8 / 16.7 to 22.9; 23, 28 | **6.8 / 14.9 to 15.4; 9, 15** | 26.4, 26.6 / 29.5 (one valid run) | 4.8 / 8.3 to 9.1, 5.1 / 9.1 |
| Forest (145 / 154) | 8.5 / 25 to 26; 35, 30 | **6.2 / 14.1 to 14.3; 12, 9** | 26.5, 25.8 / 27.6, 24.6 | 5.0 / 8.9, 4.9 / 8.2 to 9.3 |
| City (392 / 371) | 8.9 / 26.1 to 26.8; 34, 35 | 9.2 / 26.7; 40, 42 | 29.6, 27.6 / 30.8, 28.3 | 5.0 / 9.1 both |
| Coast (17 / 33) | 6.8 / 14.5 to 15.2; 11, 15 | **8.2 / 24.0 to 25.9; 27, 30** | 27.3, 29.5 / 26.2, 29.7 | 5.1 / 8.8, 4.9 to 5.8 / 9.0 to 9.6 |

- **Gen 2 costs the same as gen 1 on the RTX and at x4 (a CPU-bound old laptop): p50 and p95 within the spread, draw calls +2 to +4 (216 to 219 against 213 to 215), primitives 131 to 157 k against 126 to 153 k.** Nothing in the frame of a gen 2 planet needs a budget of its own.
- **On the Radeon at x1 it is better in the desert and the forest (-2.0 and -2.3 ms, frames over 25 ms down by two thirds: the gen 2 ground draws fewer primitives there, 131 k against 153 k in the desert) and a little worse in the city (+0.3 ms, 40 frames over 25 against 34) and at the coast (+1.4 ms, p95 24 to 26 against 15, 27 to 30 frames over 25 against 11 to 15).** The coast and the city are where a gen 2 ground draws more (the coast 145 k against 136 k primitives, +4 draws); owner for the coast: Rendering (the shore's water chunks), with World.
- **x6 (the phone tier) is an estimate only**: the x4 medians are 25 to 30 ms on the Radeon for both generations, so a phone's CPU at x6 would sit at about 38 to 45 ms in these scenes (under the 30 fps floor) with gen 1 or gen 2 alike; gen 2 adds at most 1 ms at x4 (about 1.5 ms at x6). A real phone is not available.
- **Verdict: a gen 2 planet's frame fits the old-laptop budget wherever gen 1's did; the one place to watch is the coast on a weak GPU.**

## Phone estimate and what is not measured

The x6 column is the phone tier's estimate (a CPU 6 times slower than this one: a lower bound, a real phone's memory pressure and thermal limits are not in it). On it: a cold gen 2 match start is 17.8 s, the flow field 1.4 s, a k = 32 warm tick 44 ms. Not measured: a real phone, the far map's layers in memory (World's list), and the water step inside a match with real fights.
