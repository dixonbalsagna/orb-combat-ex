# The swing tick on Simulation's re-cut field-frame, in whole AI matches (Performance and Platform, 2026-10-31)

For the EP, on the owed re-bench of `swing-tick-2026-10-31.md`: the re-cut `docs/architecture/pending/field-frame` (the list `S.bList`: buildings within `poseReach` of the pivot, `poseBody` of each of the pair, `poseShot` of a shot and a sent body's aimed building, laid again every 8 swing ticks or when the pivot crosses 500 units; the incremental `_rebucket` is in it), on the stack Orb's page builds (field-frame, tile ground, swing director on, `d5a0924f`). **Two things came out of it that matter more than the tick: a 2.7 s freeze in one line of Simulation's, and 66 to 80 ms hitches at launches with the swing director on, localised to Combat's PROP SMASH flight with a 3-line exact fix.**

## Method

Scratch tree of the stack (a copy; nothing in the repo changes), `hook-7-recut.mjs` (own-time counters in `swing.gd`), `swing_bench2.gd` (**it finishes the ring's bake after `newMatch`, as the game's load does; my first runs did not, and a bare `newMatch` leaves the 512 bake to be built on the spot by the first reads, which inflated the first launches; every number below is from the baked bench**): whole AI-vs-AI matches of 3,600 ticks (planets 7, 12, 21, seed 77, `planetGen` 3), the swing director **on** as the game ships it, so swings are the director's own, not asked by the bench. Native, headless, below-normal, 3 Godot jobs at once, 2 reps; the match is deterministic (end state printed, identical across runs), so the per-tick minimum over reps is the cost and load only adds time. The machine was busy with other sessions' captures. Staleness is measured as before (a building's stored pose against its true one, depth counted) for the buildings **not** in the list, by distance from the pair's midpoint, now out to 29,000.

## Finding 1: a 2.7 s freeze at the first jolt (Simulation's `detmath.gd`)

A 900-tick run showed one tick of 2,694,488 us (and the baked 3,600-tick runs show it again: 2.69 s on planet 7 at tick 446, 2.75 s on planet 21 at tick 1423) with the swing's own time at 2 us. Instrumenting `atBlow` statement by statement put all of it on one line: `score = ... - recency * SimDetMath.exp(-since / recencySec)` with `since = 1.0e9` when no jolt has happened yet (`_timing` has the same pattern). `SimDetMath.exp` reduces to `k = floor(x / ln 2)` and then runs `while j > k: y = y / 2.0`, here about 36 million iterations. **2.7 s native; the browser is several times that, a mid-play hang at the first heavy blow with the swing director on.** Fix, scratch-tested: `if k < -1100.0: return 0.0` after `var k` in `SimDetMath.exp` (halving a value under 2 eleven hundred times is exactly 0.0; old loop against new at 1,128 points from -3,000 to 800: 0 differ; the call now takes 5 us). The JS oracle `detmath.js` needs the same line. It does not change a value anywhere the old code returned. `exp_check.gd` is the proof.

## Finding 2: a launch costs 66 to 80 ms in one tick with the swing director on; the cost is PROP SMASH's flight (Combat's `DirLaunch._propFlight`)

With the 2.7 s fixed and the ring baked, planet 7 has launch ticks of 24, 16, 73, 80, 70, 66, 28 and 45 ms (eight in 3,600 ticks), planet 12 one of 26 ms, planet 21 none; each is a launch tick (`SWING launch ...` then `LAUNCH: ...` in the feed). With the swing director off planet 7 has none over 20 ms and planet 12 two (22 and 31 ms, launches). **Localised with statement-level timers (not wrappers: those change the match):** `DirLaunch.chooseLaunch` is 60 to 75 ms of the hitch tick (the swing's own scoring `scored` 1 to 3.5 ms, the two `poseAll` passes 1 to 2 ms each, the rest of the swing about 5 ms); inside it `_biomeCands` is 50 to 60 ms; inside that `_cliff`, the DOWNHILL scan and the tree line are under 1 ms each; **`_propFlight` (PROP SMASH's aim: a flight simulation of the send, `WorldContact.moveAir/groundPhase/stepContact` for up to `TICK_CAP` ticks, per aim force) is 20 ms steady and 40 ms the first time on the swung line, against 0.13 ms on the match's first line with the same seven props.** On the swung line the call makes 7,010 `col()` reads and 1,816 `nativeDepth` reads (429 and 18 on the first line); steady they are about 10 ms of the 20, the loops around them the rest. The same flight on a line where it ends at once costs nothing, so it is a cost of the terrain under the swung line, not of the swing as such: it appears whenever props are ahead and the flight is long, with or without the director, and the swing moves the fight onto such terrain.

**The fix, small, scratch-benched** (Combat's file, `sim/director/launch.gd`; `fix-propflight.py` applies it): props are only offered within `reach` ahead of the launcher and sorted nearest first (`_propTargets`), and a body in the air moves one way (`moveAir`'s vx never changes sign), so once the body is in the air and past the farthest prop plus `PROP_STOP`, no later tick can hit one. Add in `_propFlight`, before the loop, `var bound: float = float(props[props.size() - 1].d) + WorldContact.PROP_STOP + 1.0 if not props.is_empty() else 0.0`, and as the loop's first statement `if rec == null and b.mode == WorldContact.AIR and SimWrap.sdx(D.x, b.x) * b.dir > bound: return -1`. **Checked: 3,042 flights (planet 7, props ahead, three forces, both directions, all `aimFm`), original against fixed: 3,042 the same, 0 differ, 1,573 of them hitting a prop; total time 5.10 s down to 0.48 s (10.7 times quicker).** The natural match with the fix ends in the same state (tick 3,600, same positions and hp, 7 swings), and its launch ticks fall to 28, 18, 36, 33, 31, 31, 26 and 46 ms. **What is left after it (26 to 46 ms)** is the planner's other reads on the turned line: the column memo is cold for each new frame (710 ground reads at 28 us the first time, then `predictFlight` and the brunt aims paying for the first-read fill), so the next cut is the number of columns the planner reads and what a cold column costs; not benched. **Encounter's lead, tested and not the cause:** `_planOn` and `_planOff` swap `S.frame` and the column memo is keyed by the frame, so each swap should empty it. Slow ticks are not exactly those with a plan frame in place (`_was != null` at the start of `chooseLaunch`): of the eight launch ticks planet 7 has, the worst four are 93 ms with no plan frame (tick 2696, the swing already running), 108, 117 and 89 ms with one, and 33 and 53 ms without. And a second memo (`field.gd`, the frame swapped away from keeps its columns and is found again when the frame comes back; same match, same end state) changed nothing: launch ticks 94 / 100 / 107 / 107 / 48 ms against 90 / 108 / 117 / 89 / 51 ms (noise). A plan's frame is a new frame each time, so its columns are cold by construction whatever happens to the old frame's memo.

## The launch tick on field-frame-2 plus Combat's propflight (main 9e69930c + gen3-field 08 to 10 + field-frame + field-frame-2 + propflight; same bench, two reps, three jobs at once)

Target: under 8 ms of sim work on the launch tick. **Not met: 20 to 40 ms, down from 76 to 190 ms.** Planet 7's eight launch ticks (the step's whole time, minimum of two reps): 26, 33, 23, 25, 23, 36, 26 and 40 ms; planet 12's one: 20 ms; planet 21 has none. Where it goes (planet 7, ms, `chooseLaunch` of the step): choose 19 / 27 / 20 / 20 / 17 / 31 / 23 / 38 = predictFlight 3 to 21 (four or five flights, each up to `PREDICT_STEPS` ticks of ground and sea reads), the building aims (`WorldBrunt.aim`, `_insideAim`) 0.3 to 17.5, the biome candidates 4 to 9 (PROP SMASH no longer in it), the rest the swing's own scoring and the two `poseAll` passes (1 to 2 ms each). The match ends one hundredth of a unit from the old end state (x 87294.950 against 87294.954: field-frame-2 changes the far reads, so decisions differ a hair), deterministic across runs. Next cuts, not benched: the forecast loop's per-tick ground and sea reads while the body is far above any ground (a bound on the terrain's height skips them exactly), and the building aims' reads on a new line. Both are inside `far()` already, so the cost is the GDScript loops, not field reads.

## Owners and what to do

1. **Simulation: the one-line `exp` fix** (and the JS oracle's). Today, bit-identical, removes a 2.7 s freeze.
2. **Combat: the `_propFlight` early exit** (3 lines, exact on 3,042 flights, 10.7 times quicker) takes launch ticks from 66 to 80 ms down to about 30 ms; **then Simulation: the swung line's cold column memo** for what remains. Reproduce with `swing_bench2.gd --mode=natural --planet=7 --ticks=3600` (it prints `HITCH` and `LAUNCH` lines with the feed and the marks).
3. **Simulation: the swing tick** is acceptable (0.07 to 0.24 ms mean, 0.63 ms worst) but planet 12 is over the 0.2 ms mean; the list's laying is not the cause. Nothing to cut yet.

## Limits

Native only, one seed per planet, three planets; unpaired (the swing's total excess over a no-swing tick is read from medians of different ticks); shots and sent bodies appear in few ticks, so the list's cost under many shots is unmeasured; the machine was loaded (the minimum over two reps stands in for CPU time); the browser is several times slower than native on the script side. The 2.7 s tick was seen on planets 7 and 21 (not 12). The props test used planet 7 only (planets 12 and 21 had no props ahead in their samples). The first-line comparison (0.13 ms) is at the same position with the match's first frame; why the flight ends at once there I did not trace.
