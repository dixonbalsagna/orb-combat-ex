# Accessibility audit, 2026-10-28

Owner: Accessibility and Localization. Tree: origin/main at 767ed0d. First audit; it sets the baseline for `docs/accessibility/checklist.md` (still to write).

**Method.** Every claim below was read in the code or data named beside it. Nothing was played: this is a cloud session with no screen, so anything that needs eyes or ears (contrast as drawn, how a caption reads at speed, whether a sound is audible over the mix) is marked *unverified*. The checklist items follow the common game-accessibility guidelines (input, holds, colour, text, motion, photosensitivity, captions, audio cues, difficulty), not one certification scheme.

**Priority.** P1: affects players in the build that is public now, or a Legal condition. P2: before the next public build. P3: before the P5 accessibility gate.

## Summary

The HUD was designed for accessibility from the start, and it shows: no cue is colour-only, three colour-blind presets, remapping, hold-to-toggle for energy, latched charges, reduced motion, shake and hit-pause sliders, a flashing notice that blocks the game until it is read, and a text floor. The gaps are elsewhere: **no volume controls at all**, **no difficulty choice** (the AI has three levels in data, none reachable), **no text-size option**, several actions that **must be held with no toggle** (Guard, Transform, three of the stances), **captions that have nothing to caption** in the live build, and Settings help (including the colour-blind help) **cut off with "..."** even on a desktop screen.

## 1. Input remapping

**Exists.**
- A Remap controls screen for every keyboard and pad layout, and a second one for player 2: `ui/data/settings.json` `sections[0].items` entries `remap` and `remap_two`, gated by `ui/data/features.json` `remap` (true). Words in `settings.json` `remap`.
- The rules: `ui/core/ui_remap_model.gd` (header). Remappable: move (four keys), light, heavy, signature, guard, dodge, power, mode, context, escape, transform. Conflicts offer a swap (`settings.json` `remap.conflict`). Saved to `user://input.json`; a reset per layout (`remap.reset`).
- Reserved and not remappable: keyboard N, T, Y, P and Escape; pad Start and Back (`ui_remap_model.gd` `KB_RESERVED`, `PAD_RESERVED`). Chords and the stick are fixed rows.
- Two keyboard layouts for hot-seat play: `ui/data/options.json` `hotseat_alt_layout`.
- Touch: a left-handed mirror (`options.json` `left_handed`) and Simple or Full layouts (`touch_preset`).

**Missing.** Touch buttons cannot be moved or resized. N, T, Y and P are fixed single keys for system actions; a player whose keyboard or switch setup cannot reach them has no other route except the pause menu (unverified that every one of them has a menu route).

**Priority** P3. **Owner** Controls (rules), UI (screen).

## 2. Holds and toggles

**Exists.**
- Energy: hold or toggle, per player: `options.json` `energy_style`, `energy_style_p2` ("Toggle is for players who cannot hold a button while pressing another").
- Charged blows: `latch_charge` (tap starts the wind-up, the next press releases) and `charges_off` (every press is a tap), per player.
- A Simple pad layout where "the game picks heavy, mode and specials for you": `options.json` `pad_preset` choice `simple-pad`; Simple touch: `touch_preset`.

**Missing.**
- **Guard is hold-only**: `settings.json` `remap.helps.guard` "Hold to guard."; the defensive stance is entered by holding it (`ui/data/stances.json` `stances.defensive.enter` "Hold the guard button").
- **The charging and manoeuvre stances are hold-only**: `stances.json` `stances.charging.enter`, `stances.manoeuvre.enter`. Only Energy has a toggle.
- **Transform needs a half-second hold**: `settings.json` `remap.helps.transform` "hold it for half a second". No toggle or tap option.
- Power is "hold to charge" (`remap.helps.power`); `latch_charge` covers Medium and Heavy only (`options.json` `latch_charge.help`).
- Orb asked for "simplified and accessibility controls [that] let a player hand stance choices to the choreographer at any granularity" (`docs/ep/vision.md`, 2026-10-04). Simple layouts are a start; no per-stance auto option exists.
- Mashing: Audio's beat cues name "the mashed Y's grind" (`audio/README.md`, `data/beat_cues.json` row); it is not yet known whether any outcome needs a fast mash. To check when `three_strengths` (off in `features.json`) is turned on.

**Priority** P2. **Owner** Controls (a toggle per hold, as `energy_style` does), UI (the Settings rows), Game Design (whether a toggled guard changes balance).

## 3. Colour-blind modes

**Exists.**
- Presets off, protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia: `options.json` `colour_vision`; the colours in `ui/data/colour_vision.json` (wound stages remapped, each fighter's lane colour from Art's `data/art/colour-vision.json`, chosen with a simulated-vision check, CIEDE2000 20 or more apart).
- The preset reaches the 3D: `render/vfx/aura.gd`, `render/vfx/beam_look.gd`, `render/anim/anim_fighter.gd` and `anim_body.gd` read it.
- The rule "No cue is colour-only. Every colour role is paired with a shape, pattern, icon or motion" (`ui/README.md`, Rules), and the preset "adds a second route, never the only one" (`colour_vision.json` `note`). Orb wants colour-blind options but no stylistic palette change (`docs/ep/vision.md`, 2026-10-06); the presets respect that.
- Telling your fighter apart: `options.json` `you_marker` (default always).

**Missing.**
- **The colour-blind help is cut off on a desktop**: at 1920x1080 the Settings foot shows two lines and ends `colour_vision`'s help (and `camera_launch_follow`'s and `hud_sides`') in "..." (`tools/l10n/overflow_check.gd` report; the cut is in `ui/widgets/ui_settings.gd`, the foot's `help_lines` and the "..." loop). At 1024x576 ten helps are cut. A player choosing a vision mode cannot read what it does.
- No achromatopsia (monochrome) preset; the shapes carry it, so P3.

**Priority** P2 for the cut help. **Owner** UI.

## 4. Text size and contrast

**Exists.**
- A floor: 14 px, or 12 dp on a dense screen (`ui/core/ui_look.gd` `MIN_TEXT_PX`; `ui/core/ui_layout.gd` `text_floor`). Widgets shrink toward the floor, never below it (`ui/widgets/ui_cards.gd`, `ui_center.gd` `toll_fs`, `ui_howto.gd` header).
- Text is outlined over the 3D, and plates sit on scrims (`ui/core/ui_text.gd` `draw`, `outline`).
- Damage ring thickness: `options.json` `thickness` (thin, normal, thick).

**Missing.**
- **No text-size option.** Players cannot make text larger than the design size.
- **Contrast is not measured.** The HUD spec lists "Set minimum contrast per role" as a to-do (`docs/ui/hud-spec.md`, the readability table, "Contrast" row).
- How to play already shrinks its type to x0.67 at 1280x720 and x0.45 on a small window in English (`overflow_check.gd` report), so long text sits at the floor on common laptop screens.
- One font, Godot's built-in Open Sans SemiBold, with no dyslexia-friendly or larger-x-height choice (`docs/ui/font-note.md`).

**Priority** P2 (size option), P2 (contrast per role). **Owner** UI; Art for the palette's contrast numbers.

## 5. Reduced motion

**Exists.**
- `options.json` `reduced_motion` (off by default): no flicker, shimmer or shrinking rings in the HUD (`ui/widgets/ui_crown.gd` `_still`), the split screen swaps with a quick fade instead of a swing (`render/camera/split_rig.gd` `reduced_motion`, `T_SWING_REDUCED`, `SLAM_TIME_REDUCED`), VFX trims (`render/vfx/vfx_hub.gd` and the views it drives), shake capped at a quarter.
- `camera_shake` 0 to 10 (default 2), `hitstop_scale` 0.5 to 1, `camera_panels` full, still or off, `camera_zoom`, `vfx_quality`.
- A start-menu box for screen shake (`ui/data/terms.json` `prompt.menu_shake`).

**Missing.** No option for the camera's fast pans on a launch beyond `camera_launch_follow` (Orb's whip-pan haze decision, `docs/ep/vision.md` 2026-10-15) — *unverified* how fast the remaining pans are. The fade used by reduced motion is a dissolve, which Orb rejects for the default look (CLAUDE.md, ADR 0010); as an opt-in accessibility path it should stay, and that exception is worth writing down.

**Priority** P3. **Owner** UI, Camera, VFX.

## 6. The flashing notice and the lock

**Exists.**
- The gate is the first thing in a session, opaque, no timeout, only its own buttons close it (`ui/README.md`, section 51 summary; `ui/widgets/ui_notice.gd`). Its words are Legal's cleared lines, word for word (`terms.json` `prompt.photo_title`, `photo_body_a`, `photo_body_b`, `menu_notice`, `menu_rf_locked`, `photo_start`, `menu_not_now`; source `docs/legal/page-fixed-lines.md`, RL-192). I compared them: identical.
- Reduce flashing defaults on (`options.json` `reduce_flashing`); the row is shown (`features.json` `reduce_flashing` true).
- The lock: `features.json` `lock_reduce_flashing` hides the toggle and ignores a saved Off (`docs/ui/reduce-flashing-lock.md`). It is **false in the tree** on purpose; `tools/check-site-interim.mjs` fails an interim export unless it is true. The notice's lock line is true only while the flag is on (Legal's condition 1).
- Head flashes can be turned off (`options.json` `info_flashes`).

**Missing.**
- Flash work is paused (Orb, 2026-10-18), so the notice says the scenes "are not fixed yet". No independent analyser has been run (`terms.json` `photo_body_b`).
- Tools' British-spelling scan reads `ui/data` only, so it misses code strings: `ui/widgets/ui_licences.gd` draws "Licence:" and "Licence texts" on the licences page. Its word list also lacks "manoeuvre" (`stances.json` `stances.manoeuvre.name` MANOEUVRE and five more) and "armour" (`terms.json` `card.hatch_open` "ARMOUR OPEN"). Not flash text, but the same check.
- `docs/ui/strings.md` (UI's generated table) still shows the old notice words ("We are fixing them"). The live data is right; the table is stale.

**Priority** P1 (the lock and the words are Legal conditions; both are met today). **Owner** UI, Tools (scan scope), Legal (the spellings).

## 7. Subtitles and captions for barks and voice

**Exists.**
- Bark lanes and a caption style in the HUD (`ui/widgets/ui_barks.gd`, `ui/core/ui_event_hub.gd` `_on_bark`), with face cut-ins (`ui/data/faces.json`).
- `options.json` `captions` (on by default): "Bracketed sound tags such as [wince] on spoken lines", the tags in `terms.json` `caption`.
- `options.json` `edge_captions` (off by default): a line at the bottom edge names a cue (a beam struggle, an opening), from `terms.json` `prompt.cap_struggle`, `cap_open`.
- Lines exist in data: `data/narrative/combat_barks.json` (shouts, taunts, last stand) and `audio/data/babble_captions.json`.

**Missing.**
- **No live line is ever sent to the HUD.** Only `ui/mock/ui_mock_feed.gd` emits `bark` events; the sim's `SimFx.cue` carries a bark trigger but nothing turns it into a line (`sim/director/exchange.gd` `_opCue`, `sim/core/fx.gd` `cue`). So the Captions setting has nothing to caption in a match.
- **The grunts are uncaptioned.** The live build plays effort and pain grunts and impacts (`audio/audio_cues.gd`, hosted by `render/core/main.gd` `AudioVoices`); none has a caption.
- No caption size, background or speaker-name option (they come with lines).

**Priority** P2 (before voices or babble ship, which `features.json` `about_voice` gates). **Owner** Narrative and Encounter (the emitter), Audio (a caption per grunt), UI (options).

## 8. Audio cues for visual-only information, and audio settings

**Exists.**
- Head flashes have a sound each (`audio/data/flash_cues.json`, `AudioCues.flash`, called from `render/core/main.gd`).
- The brawl's events have sounds: launcher ready ping, wind-up gather, charge full tick, guard, misses (`audio/data/brawl_cues.json`, `beat_cues.json`).
- Vibration on "you can transform", never the only signal (`options.json` `haptics`).

**Missing.**
- **No volume controls of any kind**: `options.json` has no master, music, effects or voice volume, and no mono option. A player who needs to hear the cues over the rest, or cannot stand a sound, has only the device's volume. This is the most common accessibility ask and the cheapest to add (Godot buses already exist: `audio/data/mix.json`).
- The beam struggle's sound is parked (`audio/data/tug_cues.json` `enabled: false`), so a struggle is visual only unless edge captions are on.
- The parry and chain windows have no sound of their own that I could find (*unverified*; the beat ring, `options.json` `beat_ring`, is a visual answer, off by default).

**Priority** P1 for volume (cheap, expected by every player). **Owner** Audio (buses and cues), UI (the Settings rows).

## 9. Difficulty and assists

**Exists.**
- The AI has three levels in data: `data/director/ai.json` `levels` easy, medium, hard; `level` medium.
- A guided first match with hint lines (`ui/data/reads.json` `hints`), `keep_hints`, `show_prompts`, `control_hints`, the beat ring (`beat_ring`), the Simple layouts.
- The design itself lowers the input load: stances, not combos (CLAUDE.md, pillar 2): "No combo inputs to learn" (`ui/data/howto.json` `pages.0.items.1.text`).

**Missing.**
- **No way to choose the difficulty.** Neither the start menu (`terms.json` `prompt.menu_*`) nor Settings (`options.json`) offers the level; `ai.json` says the host may set it "unless the host sets another", and nothing does.
- No game-speed or timing-window assist (wider parry and finisher windows). The finisher contest's AI chance is mapped to difficulty in `data/combat/finishers.json` (`aiHitChance` note); a human's window is fixed.

**Priority** P2. **Owner** Encounter (levels), Game Design (assist rules), UI (the choice).

## 10. Screen reader and menu narration

**Missing.** No text-to-speech anywhere (no `tts_speak` call in the tree). Godot 4 has `DisplayServer.tts_speak` on desktop and the web. Menus drawn as custom canvas text have no accessibility tree.

**Priority** P3. **Owner** UI.

## Other findings

- **English already overflows** at a 360x640 window: the start menu does not fit for one or two players (`overflow_check.gd`, "English already fails"). hud_check's own cases stop at 390x844 and 667x375 for this screen.
- **Hard-coded words reach the screen**: 39 string literals in the default build bypass `ui/data` (`node tools/l10n/strings.mjs`): 27 pieces of sim banners (`sim/core/` and `sim/director/` `SimFx.banner`, for example "GUARD BREAK", "PERFECT BLOCK", and "NEED " + n + " KI", which the HUD fixes up in code: `ui/core/ui_data.gd` `banner()` replaces " KI" with the hard-coded " CHARGE" when `terms.json` `banner_rename` has no whole-string match), the boot line (`render/core/look.gd` `BOOT_WORDS`), the licences page headings, the Start and Back glyph labels, the "s" unit beside a countdown (`ui/widgets/ui_incoming.gd`). They block translation and are listed in `docs/accessibility/l10n-plan.md`.
