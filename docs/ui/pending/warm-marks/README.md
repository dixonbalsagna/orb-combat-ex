# The press-acknowledgement mark and the legend's other marks, warmed at boot (parked, not committed)

`docs/ui/pending/warm-marks/warm-marks.patch` (EP's brief: Rendering's Radeon hitch at tick 6 of `signaturecity --igpu`, after the `press_ack` cue at tick 4).

**What the press-ack mark draws.** `UiHints.draw_ack` is vector shapes only: a dark disc (`draw_circle`) and, by kind, a cross (two lines), a dash, a dot or a ring (`draw_arc`), at about 12 px, for 0.5 s. **No font, no texture, no material.** Its only cost is that it is recorded inside the legend layer, so each of its six alpha steps (`ack_step`) re-records the whole legend; the legend's text and key letters are drawn at the HUD's sizes, which the text warm-up already covers. The data loaders were measured too (headless, first call of each of the 13 `UiData` JSON files: 0.24 to 0.76 ms), so a first JSON parse is not it either.

**The change.** `UiWarm` also draws, once at boot with the text, **every one of the legend's overlay marks** (the five ack marks, the wind-up ring and B's armoured ring, the launch window's ring with 4 and with 3 ticks, and the flurry's ring of dots) and the legend's **key-letter size at every scale** (`UiGlyphs._fs`, which the HUD's design sizes do not reach at 2.5x). Ten marks, 1% opaque for three frames. Rendering's warm-list: **no lines needed** (UI has no shader or material).

**Before and after, ticks 1 to 60 of `signaturecity --igpu`, four runs each, interleaved** (Performance's `frame-bench.mjs`, scratch exports of main and of main with the patch). **The tick-6 hitch does not reproduce on main**: in the four runs of main frames 4 to 8 are 5.2 to 7.7 ms and the worst frame of ticks 1 to 60 is 6.7, 13.0, 6.8 and 6.7 ms (one run valid by the load rule, three loaded). With the patch: worst 15.6 (valid, at tick 47), 6.9, 6.8 (valid) and 19.9 (loaded; ticks 43 and 46), frames 4 to 8 are 5.7 to 6.8 ms. So **no gain is shown (nothing to remove) and no cost at ticks 1 to 60**; the spikes at ticks 43 to 47 in two of the patched runs are beyond the press-ack and were not seen in main's four. A frame-time read of ticks 1 to 60 on the Radeon where Rendering saw it is what decides whether the patch earns its place; if it does not, the hitch is not in the HUD's first draws, and the next suspect is the legend's re-record (the overlay marks in a layer of their own, so an ack does not re-record the legend): I did not build that, because it is a layout split and the evidence does not ask for it yet.
