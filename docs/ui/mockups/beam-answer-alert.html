Beam answer alert: mockups

Orb's ask: tell the player on the receiving end, without getting in the way, that there is a moment to fire their own beam back or dodge. Stand-in shapes only; nothing is drawn over the fighters.

Everything on screen here is a steady shape and a word. It appears and it goes: no fade, no flash, no pulsing. The numbers (how long the chance lasts) are made up; Game Design is setting the real window. Button words follow your layout.

The four alerts

1. Edge mark on screen

A small tag on the screen's edge on the side the beam comes from, with an arrow pointing at the rival, the words FIRE BACK or DODGE, and the button. A bar under it runs down as the chance runs out.

Best for: players who watch the middle of the screen. It never reaches the fighters because it lives at the edge.

2. Your plate on screen

A tag under your own name plate (top corner): YOUR CHANCE, then the same buttons and bar. Nothing moves about the screen; it is always in the same place.

Best for: players who check the plate often, and for a split screen, where each player has their own plate.

3. Glint in the world in the scene

A bright four-point sparkle on the beam, a little way out from the rival's hands (never on a body), for as long as the chance lasts. It is part of the picture, so it reads without looking away.

Needs Rendering and VFX to draw it. Size and strength are yours to set.

4. Buzz and sound feel

Two short buzzes on a controller and two quick rising notes. Nothing on screen.

Best for: players who look at the fighters and nothing else. The browser only buzzes on some phones; a controller always can.
