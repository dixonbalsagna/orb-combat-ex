- **`water_tile_check.gd` (Animation's): a reason, not a CI line.** On this tree it fails by itself, with no flag: its `dry` row (4 of 161 places up to 31.9 units from the drawn ground), and its `lake` and `shore` rows are findings of World's open lake and shore faults (GW2). A CI line would be red from the first run. The reason says to run it by hand with `--strict` and to wire it once Animation and World have cleared the dry row. That failure is Animation's to look at (it is a finding of its own check on the GW1b stack, not something I touched): flagged to the EP.

When the hold lands, a line `bounce_count.gd -- --lodge` belongs in `godot-sim`.
- The other new checks the stack brings are already in CI by their own patches (`world_default_check`, `default_gate_check`, `floor_rise_check` show as CI in the table; `stray_beams_input_test` through the input tests' glob).
