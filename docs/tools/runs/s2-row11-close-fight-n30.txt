READING
- well-own against today: the pad wins 5 matches fewer (16 of 30 against 21 of 30), the computer's rate goes from 30% to 47%. A two-sided Fisher exact test on 16/30 against 21/30 gives p about 0.29, and the two 95% bands overlap (53% +/-17 and 70% +/-16): the gap is a lean, not a result, at n=30.
- Against shipped, well-own is 8 matches down (24 to 16); this gap is larger (24/30 against 16/30: p about 0.05 by the same test) but it mixes the close mode with the 3500/1.5 values: today alone is already 3 matches down from shipped (24 against 21, p about 0.55).
- Refusals per 100 presses: today 24.6, well-own 14.8. That is a much larger move than the win rate and it holds at the larger n (at n=10 it was 20.6 and 9.8). The pad is refused fewer times with the own-body mode.
- Time without control stays at about 54 s/min in all three. Hits landed and taken are within about 10% of each other in all three; well-own is the one where the pad takes more than it lands (22083 taken against 20579 landed).
- Beams per match are 0 in all cells (the pad arm fired none); the computer fired some (foeBeams above).

NOT PROVEN
- One arm (the scripted orbs pad) and one level (easy). Nothing here speaks for medium, hard or other pad schemes.
- The median match column is the run's own reading (medianSec) and I have not checked its unit against the match clock; it is not a feel reading, and I would not lean on it.
- 30 matches a cell is the size the EP asked for. The 95% bands are about +/-16 points, so a gap of 17 points (what was measured) is on the edge of what this size can tell apart. A larger n on the same two cells would settle it.
