**Not a starved machine.** A capture is tick-stepped: the hook runs exactly one sim tick, draws it, and the driver then takes the screenshot; the driver refuses any step that does not advance the tick by exactly one (tools/flash/capture-web.mjs), so the number of frames a second is wall speed and cannot change what a frame shows. The two captures at 3.8 and 13 frames a second agree to the last counted change. (A capture below 1 frame a second is void by RL-131.)

**What happened in the windows.** Each is a run of 7 to 10 counted whole-area changes inside two to three seconds, alternating sign, each 100 to 380% of the area threshold: changes the margins table attributes to the camera (3 to 6 of them in a window) and to the world (2 to 4), the pattern the EP traced to the camera's inset opening and whole-frame cuts after blasts (Legal's addendum 10); not one strobe. The worst, ai-1009 reduced, is nine changes in 56 ticks (2806 to 2862).

**Fixed-window capture.** The frames of each window (30 ticks before the worst second to 90 after, and every dip) are kept, one PNG a tick with analysis.json and clip.json, in the scratch folder `seeds-repro/clips/<clip>/` on this PC. A seed is not stable across sim changes (on ff68e994 ai-1009 reads 1.5 normal and 2.5 reduced), so the frames are the fixed record; to test the analyser or a fix against them later run `node tools/flash/analyse-frames.js <folder>`. The seeds stay in the required set.
