## 2. Call-outs for a rally changing hands

In Game Design's ruling (item 10, "the turn"), a teched link, a wall kick or a recoil flip gives the defender a counter-rush, and **if it lands on the chaser in the air, the rally is now his.** One line is said **once, at that landing**, by the new chaser, and one by the fighter who just lost it. Both follow Legal's ln06 style for grabs:
- **One line, once, at the moment the rally turns.** Nothing before it and no second line.
- **Never over a dazed or bound fighter.** The line is only said when the fighter it concerns is in flight, not in a daze or a bind; if either is dazed, nothing is said.
- **Never the move's name, never a multiplier or a countdown.** Each is 4 to 12 words and passes the lint, including the per-speaker bans.

- **The Protagonist.** takes over: "My turn now! Hold on!" (alt "Nice chase! Now it's mine!") / loses it: "Whoa! Nice flip! Your turn!" (alt "Ha! You turned it on me!")
- **The Anti-hero (the rival).** takes over: "Mine now. Try to keep up." (alt "You overreached. Fly, amateur.") / loses it: "Lucky flip. It won't repeat." (alt "You turned it. Once.")
- **The Empress.** takes over: "Gahaha! Our turn, rat! Fly!" (alt "Hahaha! The chase belongs to us!") / loses it: "Hahaha! You flipped us? Cheeky!" (alt "Gahaha! Turned on us! Delightful!")
- **The Cyborg.** takes over: "Got you! Now you fly!" (alt "My chase now! Hold on!") / loses it: "Whoa! Flipped! My plating's spinning!" (alt "Turned on me? Rude!")
