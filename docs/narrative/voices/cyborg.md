## Speech rhythm
Brisk and bright, like a call-centre script, with a smile that never quite reaches the eyes. Very polite phrases wrapped around appalling things. He stutters and repeats when he is hit on the chip (a glitch). Hunger leaks through: he starts a sentence politely and finishes it with a lick. He never raises his voice; he only speeds up.

## Vocabulary
- **Likes:** your order, please stand clear, thank you for choosing, on the menu, five stars, fresh, crunchy, extra, portion, under new management.
- **Address:** "sir", "valued customer", "guest". Never a name.
- **Verbs:** serve, fulfil, plate, order, dock, rate.
- **About the world:** civilians are ingredients and stock. A planet is a kitchen.

## Never says
- A direct threat. He offers a menu.
- Rudeness without a polite frame.
- A real apology (only "our sincere apologies", which means nothing).
- "Sweets", "candy" or "chocolate" for the food: it is sandwiches.
- Any catchphrase from any source.

## Reactions

| Situation | How he reacts |
|---|---|
| **Winning** | "Your order is complete." Asks the opponent to rate the experience. |
| **Losing** | Demands a refund, then a manager. Glitches with rage. |
| **Collateral** | Delighted. It is stock. The more the better. |
| **Being mocked** | "Feedback noted." A pause. "Feedback will be digested." |
| **Transforming** | A software update: "system update in progress, please stand clear". Molts are "restructuring". |
| **Chip hit** | Full glitch: stuttering repeats and a shrill "not the chip". |

## Grunt and laugh palette
`static` (a growl with interference), `munch`, `chirp` (a cheerful digital ping), `servo` (a whirr when he moves), `laugh.glitch` (a stuttering laugh that loops on one syllable), `growl`, `effort.light`, `effort.heavy`, `sigh` (a fan spinning down).
