**In one sentence.** A brooding rival whose pride outranks his justice: cold, clipped and formal, who ranks people and speaks about his opponent as if he were already finished, until the facade cracks.

## Speech rhythm
Short, flat and formal. **He uses no contractions**: "I did not ask." Never an exclamation mark. He speaks about his opponent in the **past tense** ("You were adequate"), and he ranks him (first, second, last). Silence is part of his voice: he lets a line sit. **When his facade cracks (Pride below half), the contractions come back, the sentences turn plain and first person, and he slips into the present tense.** That is an audible tell.

## Vocabulary
- **Likes:** rank, adequate, beneath, permit, kept, finished, "you were", second, last, regalia.
- **Address:** "you", or a rank that is always compared ("Fourth. Behind the goons."). Never a name, and never a pet name.
- **Verbs:** permit, endure, finish, rank, allow.
- **About the world:** he treats it as an audience that has no say.

**The ranking gag, made unmistakable.** A rank is always compared to something absurd: "Fifth. Behind the goons.", "Fourth. Behind the furniture.", "Third. Behind the civilians." Where the point is pure contempt he says one word, **"Filth."**, spelled out and never as a rank. That way "Fifth" cannot be read as a typo.

## Never says
- "Please", "thank you", "sorry" (until the facade cracks).
- "Help" in a plain request (until the crack).
- Contractions, while the facade holds.
- Exclamation marks, jokes at his own expense, and a warm laugh.
- Any catchphrase from any source.

## Reactions

| Situation | How he reacts |
|---|---|
| **Winning** | Brief contempt. A rank, then silence. |
| **Losing** | He refuses to acknowledge it: "This does not count." Then silence. |
| **Collateral** | Indifference, or disdain for the crowd: "Nobody asked them to stand there." |
| **Being mocked** | A cold pause and a precise retort. He only shows anger when his Pride has already dropped. |
| **Transforming** | He announces the added regalia as a rank. His self-importance rises with his power. |
| **Swallowing pride** | A grit and a swallow. Then the contractions return. |

## Grunt and laugh palette
`effort.light`, `effort.heavy` (low), `growl` (from the chest), `sneer` (an exhale through the nose), `scoff`, `sigh`, `laugh.dry` (a single breath, rare), `roar` (only when transforming), `ragged` (breath and voice after the facade cracks). No warm laugh.

## Lines (5 per situation)

**Winning**
1. "You were adequate. That is the highest thing I say."
2. "Fourth. Behind the furniture. You leave the ranks as you entered them."
3. "It ends when I say. It has ended."
4. "You fought well. For someone who was never going to win."
5. "Remember this. You will not be asked again."

**Losing**
1. "This does not count."
2. "You were lucky. I will not be lucky in your favour twice."
3. "I do not accept this."
4. "...Enough. Leave."
5. "Do not tell anyone. I will know if you do."

**Collateral**
1. "Nobody asked them to stand there."
2. "Nobody will remember that {thing}. Or you."
3. "The crowd was decoration. Decoration falls."
4. "Noted. Move on."
5. "It was a fine {place}. It was never mine."

**Being mocked**
1. "You were funny once. Try again in the dark."
2. "Say that again. I would like the witnesses to hear."
3. (silence) "Fifth. Behind the goons."
4. "I have heard better from things that cannot speak."
5. "Laugh. It is the last sound you make well."

**Transforming**
1. "Regalia. Now you will know who was watching."
2. "Second rank. I permit it."
3. "You were unaware of what I was. You will be aware."
4. "Sovereign. Bow, or do not. The result is the same."
5. "This is what I hold back. It was never kindness."

**After the facade cracks (present tense, plain, contractions back)**
1. "I can't feel my arms. Help me."
2. "Fine. Fine. I'll take it."
3. "I didn't need you. I never did. ...Thanks."
4. "Don't look at me like that. I know."
5. "I'm still here. I'm still here."
