# World changes and transformations: second pass

Owner: Narrative and Fighter Identity. Version 1, 2026-09-30. Orb liked the first pass (`world-abilities.md`) and wants more in the same direction. Orb's picks so far: **world changes** are the Protagonist's Heat Wave and Proving Ground, the Anti-hero's Storm Crown and Scorched Plain, the Empress's Blockade and Golden Hour, and the Cyborg's Wired World and Harvest. **Transformations** are the Empress's escalating revisions, the Protagonist pushing past his limits (heat), the Anti-hero's pride ascension (forms lost when Pride crashes), and the Cyborg's assimilation (feeding on wreckage, toward a final form that brings his technology and hidden weapons online).

This file is the **character side**. Game Design pitches the mechanics in parallel. **No scripted lines**: I hold dialogue until the voice lab rules exist, since Orb is editing batch 1. Each defining moment below is described, not scripted. Working labels, all placeholders. Original only.

## 1. Four more world-change ideas per fighter

Each says who they are: what they would do to a world, and why.

### The Protagonist (earnest, protective, "I'll fix it after")

| Idea | What he does, and why | How it looks |
|---|---|---|
| **Tide Wall** | He holds the sea back with a wall of water around the fight, so no flood reaches the shore towns. He would rather hold a whole ocean than let it spill on someone. | The water rises into a curved wall, glassy and still, with fish visible inside, and the fight takes place under its lee. |
| **Hot Spring** | He heats the broken ground into geysers and warm springs, turning wreckage into something people can use. It is his sorry, made in advance. | Steam columns rise in slow rhythms, pools glitter in the craters, and the rubble is warm to look at. |
| **Green Up** | He coaxes grass and young trees back over the scorched ground as the fight moves on. "Fix it after," made literal, and in the middle of the fight. | A green tide follows him across the land, first moss, then grass, then saplings, always a few seconds behind the damage. |
| **Beacon** | He raises a plain stone beacon so everyone can see where the fight is and stay well away from it. He cares about being seen so people can stay safe. | A tall, rough tower of stone pushes up, and a steady lantern-light burns at the top, visible from any shore. |

### The Anti-hero (proud, hierarchical, needs a witness)

| Idea | What he does, and why | How it looks |
|---|---|---|
| **Obelisk of Names** | He raises a black stone pillar carved with the name of everyone he has beaten, his rank list made literal. There is a line waiting for the opponent's name. | A tall, dark column pushes up out of the ground, rows of small letters spreading down it, and one blank line near the top. |
| **The Long Silence** | He stills the wind, and the sound of the world, over the whole arena. He is proud of control, and a quiet world is a controlled world. | The clouds stop, the grass stops, the water goes flat, and the music thins to almost nothing. |
| **The Circle** | He cuts a deep circular chasm around the fight, so nobody can interrupt it and nobody can leave. It is a duel, and it will be finished. | A clean, dark ring opens in the land, wall-sheer, with the fighters on an island in the middle. |
| **The Watch Fire** | He lights an enormous bonfire that can be seen from every shore, so the whole world has to watch. He needs a witness, and he will make one. | A great ring of fire burns on a high ridge, its glow lighting the clouds from beneath, and the horizon shows small dots looking. |

### The Empress (image, empire, annexes what she sees)

| Idea | What she does, and why | How it looks |
|---|---|---|
| **Grand Avenue** | She lays a triumphal avenue straight across a continent, lined with statues of herself. An empire needs a road, and the road needs a view. | Paving unrolls to the horizon in a perfect line, with statues rising on both sides, gilded at the edges. |
| **Gilded Coast** | She gilds the shoreline, so the beach and the low rocks turn to gold. A planet's edge should look expensive. | The sand and shallows shimmer and harden into a bright metal shore, catching the light and the fighters. |
| **The Throne Hall** | She raises a roofed hall of stone over the fight, a vast indoor arena with columns and a high ceiling. Even a brawl should be held in a proper room. | Pillars grow up in ordered rows, a vaulted ceiling closes over them, and the sky is gone. |
| **Portrait Clouds** | She arranges the clouds into an enormous portrait of herself. Her image is her power, and the sky is the biggest canvas. | The clouds drift, gather and settle into a serene face, slightly flattering, and the wind keeps it there. |

### The Cyborg (a polite machine with an appetite)

| Idea | What he does, and why | How it looks |
|---|---|---|
| **Drive-Thru** | He lays a ring road around the planet with service windows along it, so his restaurant serves the whole world without anyone leaving their car. | A smooth loop of road unrolls across the land, with windows, menu boards and small glowing lights at intervals. |
| **Kitchen Weather** | He turns the weather into cooking steam and a fine drizzle of oil. A planet with a kitchen deserves a kitchen's climate. | The clouds thicken into hot white steam, and a warm, glistening drizzle falls, fogging everything. |
| **Pantry Mountain** | He hollows a mountain into a warehouse of stock, with shelves running into the rock. Somewhere to keep the supplies. | A mountain's face splits into rows of dark doorways, and lights flicker on inside, revealing tall metal shelving. |

## 2. Transformations: what each form means, and one defining moment

Each form gets **what it means to the character** and **one moment that defines it**, described, not scripted.

### The Empress: escalating revisions (Revision One, the form on file)

She hates to change. Each revision is the smallest change she could get away with, so the comedy is how little changes, and the real forms are when she can no longer avoid it. Her paperwork stays diegetic (voice and body), never on screen.

| Revision | What it means to her | The defining moment |
|---|---|---|
| **1 (the form on file)** | Her true self, as she wants to be remembered. | She stands perfectly still while her guard salutes, and looks bored. |
| **2 (louder)** | The smallest amendment she can defend. | The same body, but the sound of her cape and her voice are twice as loud, and she winces at the noise she made. |
| **4 (the hat)** | A joke she takes seriously. | She adds a hat, and every guard member turns to check whether they should salute the hat. |
| **6 (smaller)** | An insult to her own image, endured. | She shrinks a size, and her train stays the same length, trailing behind like an apology. |
| **9 (Field Revision)** | She finally admits the fight is serious. | Armour plates close over her regalia one by one, and she smooths each one as it settles, reluctantly. |
| **10 (Executive Revision)** | The train becomes the weapon. | The train unfurls and every hem catches the light, and she lets out a long sigh, like a woman lifting a very heavy box. |
| **11 (Council Revision)** | Not written in this pass: see `moveset-rules.md` (the fleet's shadow passes over and her volleys become barrages). | |
| **12 (Final Approved)** | Total commitment: the only form she has not been dreading. | The guard fall silent and drop their salutes, and she smiles for the first time without checking who sees it. |

*Aligned 2026-10-23 to the one revision order in `docs/design/moveset-rules.md`: 9 Field, 10 Executive, 11 Council, 12 Final Approved (this table had Executive at 11).*

### The Protagonist: pushing past his limits (Hot Blood)

Each form is a temperature, and each costs him something. The forms are also how much he lets the fight matter.

| Form | What it means to him | The defining moment |
|---|---|---|
| **Heated** | The joy of a real fight warming up. | He notices the steam off his own shoulder, and grins at it. |
| **Simmering** | Commitment: he is willing to get hurt for this. | He takes a hit he could have dodged, to shelter a wall behind him. |
| **Boiling** | He has stopped counting the cost. | His hands shake, and he keeps fighting anyway, and he looks apologetic about it. |
| **Boiled over** | The moment he pushed too far, and it shows. | The steam stops, his knees go, and for a moment he looks around for who he might have hurt. |
| **Open Hand** (the reveal on the proving ground) | Nothing left to protect, so nothing left to hold back. | The orbiting fragments spin out into a wide ring around him, and for the first time in the match he does not look worried. |

### The Anti-hero: pride ascension (forms are lost when Pride crashes)

Each form is a rung of his pride, and each is a piece of his self-image that he can lose. A crash strips the forms in reverse.

| Form | What it means to him | The defining moment |
|---|---|---|
| **Poise** | Composure as a weapon. | He straightens his back, and the noise goes out of the fight around him. |
| **Regalia** | The first proof that he is what he says he is. | A piece of regalia forms on him, and he glances at the opponent to check they saw. |
| **Hierarchy** | The world sorted into ranks, with him at the top. | He stops speaking to the opponent and starts speaking about them, as if they had left the room. |
| **Sovereign** | He no longer needs to move to be dominant. | He stands above the fight and lets the others come to him. |
| **Apex** | Total, unearned certainty. | He smiles once, and it is terrible. |
| **The crash** (a form lost) | The self-image cracking. | The regalia flakes away one piece at a time, and he looks down at his own hands as if they were someone else's. |

### The Cyborg: assimilation (feeding on wreckage toward the final form)

Each form is the machine absorbing more of the world, and every form is a little more polite about it. The final form brings his technology fully online and generates his hidden weapons.

| Form | What it means to him | The defining moment |
|---|---|---|
| **Base** | A courteous machine with a service smile. | He bows slightly, to no one in particular, before the first blow. |
| **Scavenger** | He has started taking the world apart to fit himself. | He pulls a girder from a collapsed tower and bolts it onto his arm, and pats it as if it were a colleague. |
| **Kitchen** | Everything is now an appliance. | He sets a table in the middle of the fight, straightens the cutlery, and only then looks up. |
| **Assembled** | The wreckage is armour, and the city is a coat. | A block of a fallen building settles onto his shoulders like a shawl, and he adjusts it. |
| **Online** (the final form) | Everything he has eaten and taken is now working together. | Lights come on across his body one by one, like a restaurant at opening time, and weapons appear on trays. |
