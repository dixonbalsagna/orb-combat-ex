## Updated Voice & Linting Rules (Orb Direction)

1. **Natural Dub Breath (4 to 15 words, Complete Clauses Preferred):** Do not chop sentences into caveman telegrams just to stay under 7 words. Allow taunts, signatures, and counters to stretch naturally (8–15 words) so voice actors have real grammatical momentum and Rule-of-Cool swagger.
2. **Anatomical & Keyword Variety:** Never repeat `"ribs"`, `"shoulder"`, `"rhythm"`, or `"calories"` across back-to-back beats. Rotate physical nouns across the whole body (*jaw, collarbone, spine, solar plexus, shins, knuckles, lungs, temples, wrists*).
3. **In a 1v1 every line is aimed at the opponent**, never at an object or an off-screen helper.
4. **Banned LLM Thesaurus Words:** commence, futile, demise, symphony, dance, crescendo, reckon, mere, behold, destiny, abyss, limit, surpass, eternal, indeed, shall, hence, thus, whom, regalia, splendid, sovereign, endure, adequate, dignity, courtesy, permit, allowed.
