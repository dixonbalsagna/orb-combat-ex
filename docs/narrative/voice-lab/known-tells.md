# Known tells: patterns that read as machine-written

Owner: Narrative and Fighter Identity. Version 1, 2026-10-01. For Orb to confirm, cut or add to.

These are the patterns Orb has called out, plus ones we noticed ourselves in our own drafts. Every example is a real line from the files in this folder, so you can search for it. **Mark each as: tell (ban it), fine (we can keep it sparingly), or not a tell.** Add your own at the bottom. This sheet is the seed of the lint check.

Rows 19 to 22 were added on 2026-10-30 from Orb's rules and the audit of the mid-combat master (`voice-lab/mid-combat-lines.md`, review notes). Counts are in the current drafts (`protagonist.csv`, `anti_hero.csv`, `empress.csv`, `cyborg.csv`, `kai.csv`, `vorr.csv`).

## What we are asking you

1. Which of these are the ones you reacted to? Mark them **tell**.
2. Which are fine in small doses (say, once per fighter per match)?
3. What is missing? Add anything you can spot on sight that is not here. Examples of things other people name: "stacked adjectives", "over-explained jokes", "everyone speaks in complete, grammatical sentences", "no one ever mishears or trails off".
4. Is there a pattern that is **right** for a character and should be exempt? (For instance, the Anti-hero's past tense and the Empress's "we" are deliberate devices, but if they feel like tells too, say so.)

## What happens next

The patterns you confirm become the first draft of the lint. It will flag any new line that repeats a word three times, ends on a one-word button, leans on a banned gag, and so on, and it will run before any line reaches you.
