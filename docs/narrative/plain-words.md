# Plain words for the HUD: Narrative's proposals

Owner: Narrative and Fighter Identity. Version 1, 2026-10-16. For UI, to merge into `docs/ui/plain-words.md` (UI's list). UI left these for Narrative: the Cyborg's cards (FACADE CRACKS, HATCH OPEN, CHIP, REVISION) and the short labels SIGN, CONTEXT, FORM, PRIDE, BRINK, MELEE, FINISHER. Orb called "the bolt's land" and "ink on the dark scrim" nonsense. **Every item here is an option for Orb**, and where Orb has already chosen a word, I keep it.

## The rules I used

1. **A word a player would say out loud**, mid-game, to a friend.
2. **Say what happened, not the system's name for it.** "Armour open: chest", not "HATCH OPEN".
3. **One word for one thing**, everywhere: HUD, hints, how to play, settings.
4. **No team nouns:** chip, hatch, station, scrim, register, tick, cue.
5. **Where Orb picked the word, keep it and teach it once:** Respect, Pride, Wrath, Hunger, Drop the Act, Humbled, "finisher", "blood: heated, simmering, boiling" are Orb's.
6. **Character voice goes in the lines, not the cards.** A card is the game speaking; the Empress's revision number and the Cyborg's service script live in what they say.

| **FACADE CRACKS** | The Anti-hero's Pride falls below half; he drops his composure | **RATTLED** | HE'S LOSING IT | "Facade" is a literary word. "Rattled" is what a player would say. *Drop the Act* and *Humbled* stay: Orb picked both. |
| **HATCH OPEN: {station}** | The Cyborg's plating opens at head, chest, back or hip | **ARMOUR OPEN: {part}** | OPENED UP: {part} | A player sees armour open on a body, not a hatch at a station. HEAD, CHEST, BACK, HIP stay as they are. |
| **CHIP: {stage}** | His weak point cracking (SCRATCHED, CRACKED, SPLIT) | **WEAK SPOT: {stage}** | keep CHIP | "Chip" means nothing before the first time you see it. If Orb likes the chip as an idea, keep the word once the How to play has shown it. The stage words are already plain. |
| **REVISION** | The Empress changes form | **NEW FORM** | FORM {n} | Her number and her complaint about the paperwork are in her lines (her "revision" joke), so the card doesn't need the joke. |
| **SIGN** (touch button) | Fires the signature move | **SIGNATURE** | the move's own name (KEEPER'S LANCE) | "Sign" reads as a verb or a document. If SIGNATURE doesn't fit, the move's name is plain *and* has character. |
| **CONTEXT** (button and settings) | The A button: what it does depends on the stance (a grab and throw in martial arts) | **Label by what it does now** (GRAB in martial arts, etc.) | ACTION | "Context" is our word for it. The stance table already says what each button does; show that word. |
| **FORM** (touch button) | Takes a transformation that is ready | **TRANSFORM** | POWER UP | "Form" is a noun; the button is a verb. "Transform" is already the How to play's word. |
| **PRIDE**, **RESPECT**, **WRATH**, **HUNGER** | Each fighter's ego meter | **Keep** | | Orb's own names. Teach each once, in a line: "Pride: fills when he's winning, drops when he's humbled." |
| **BRINK**, **ON THE BRINK** | A fighter is nearly beaten | **NEARLY BEATEN** | ON THE ROPES | How to play already says "nearly beaten". One term for the plate, the card and the How to play. |
| **MELEE** (finisher kind) | A finisher made of blows, as opposed to a launch or a beam | **BLOWS** | HAND TO HAND | "Melee" is a game word. GUARD a launch, DODGE blows, PRESS a beam. |
| **FINISHER** | The move that ends the fight | **Keep** | FINISHING MOVE (in text) | Orb's word. In running text, "finishing move" reads easier for a first-time player. |
| **SPECIAL** | The moves called by holding Power with a face button | **Keep, and show the move's name** | | Plain. The name on the button does the explaining. |
| **stance** (How to play) | The way the four buttons are set right now | **Keep, and define once** | | "Your stance is how you're fighting right now. Hold a shoulder button to change it." One sentence. |

## What I'd change in how it is written

- **One sentence each, with the player as the subject.** "Press B when it lights up", not "the launcher is ready".
- **Verbs for buttons, nouns for things:** TRANSFORM, GRAB, LAUNCH; SIGNATURE, ARMOUR, WEAK SPOT.
- **Teach a character word once, in the place the player first sees it,** not in a glossary.
- **Orb marks the screens that change.** Nothing from this list goes in unseen.
