# The first-time launch hint: options

Owner: Narrative and Fighter Identity. Version 1, 2026-10-16. For UI (`docs/ui/launch-teaching.md`). Orb couldn't find how to launch: "I couldn't quite figure out how to launch my opponent." UI's placeholder: "Keep hitting, then press the lit button to launch him." Written to Orb's rules (`writing-quality.md` section 0): plain words, one breath, what the player does, no team nouns ("gauge", "run", "launcher").

**What the player sees:** four hits fill the target's pips, the heavy button lights, and the hint shows once.

## The options

| | The hint | Words | What it does |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **Keep hitting. When B lights up, press it.** | 8 | The action in order, with no result named. The player looks at the lit button. |
| **B** | **Four hits in a row, then B sends him flying.** | 10 | Names the number (the player has just done it) and the result in the player's words. |
| **C** | **He's open! Press B and send him flying!** | 8 | Matches the moment: the opponent is staggered and the button is lit. The most excited. |

**My pick: B.** It teaches the rule (four in a row) and the payoff (flying) in one breath, and says nothing the player can't see. A is right if UI wants the hint to name nothing but the button; C is right if Orb wants the hint to sound like the fight.

**Notes.**
- **"Launch" is our noun, not the player's.** "Send him flying" is what a player says. "Launch" can stay on the legend; the hint uses the plain words.
- **"B" is the pad's name for the button.** On the keyboard and touch the hint should name the lit button's own glyph, as UI planned, so the player looks at what's lit.
- **"Him"** is the opponent. If the opponent is the Empress, "her" would be exact; "them" avoids the problem ("send them flying"). UI's call; I'd use "them" in all three.

## The second beat (the rest)

UI's placeholder when a heavy lands during the launch's rest: "Resting. B only hits for now." Plain version:

| | The hint | Words |
|---|---|---|
| **Rest** | **Launch is recharging. B just hits for now.** | 8 |

"Recharging" matches what UI already uses on the plate.

## What I would not do

- A hint that explains the system ("fill the gauge to unlock the launcher").
- A hint that praises ("Nice combo!"). A hint is a nudge; praise is the game congratulating itself (`writing-quality.md`, line 2 of the ten).
- A second hint in the same second as the first.
