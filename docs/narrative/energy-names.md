# Energy techniques: names, flavour and call-outs

Owner: Narrative and Fighter Identity. Version 1, 2026-10-23. Orb's overhaul (`docs/ep/vision.md`, "Energy attacks overhaul"): each fighter gets their own energy set, along the light, medium, heavy and grab combinations: a **light volley** (rapid small shots), a **medium lob** (a more substantial explosive blast), a **heavy technique** (the most devastating), an **energy grab** with a unique mechanic ("maybe someone has an energy net, someone has an energy ring"), a **mine or trap** (a block while hitting energy attacks leaves one nearby), and a **chargeable beam**. The EP asks me for each technique's name and a one-line flavour for four fighters, true to their voice rules and the hero and villain split, and two or three call-outs for the big ones. **Provisional:** Combat's one-pagers (`docs/combat/pending/energy/`) and Game Design's system had not landed when I wrote this, so each name is tied to its slot and the fighter's identity, not yet to what the move does. When they land I check each name against the move and change the flavour first, then the name. The data is `pending/energy_names.json`. Everything is an option for Orb; every name is unscreened by Legal.

## 1. How the names were made

- **Place plus verb, or place plus tool,** as the move names are (`move-names-draft.md`): a name says where it comes from or where it lands, and what it does. Two words, at most sixteen letters. Never shouted: only a signature and a finisher are shouted, so a generated technique has a plain name. The beam keeps the biome variants (HORIZON CLEAVE, RIDGE BORE and the rest), so a fighter's beam name is the base the variants hang on.
- **Each set is one idea,** shaped by the fighter's identity and the hero and villain split (Pillar 5):
  - **The Protagonist** is warm, tactile and protective: sparks, a thrown lump of heat, an open hand, a **net** (catching, not crushing), and a trap that holds a patch of ground hot. His techniques never read as cruel, and his net is an offer to stay still.
  - **The Anti-hero** is precise and ranked: needles, a step down, a sorting blow, a **ring** (a tight ranked loop), a pressure plate, a clean edge. His sets deny rather than celebrate.
  - **The Empress** is royal and theatrical: silk, a thrown crown, a stamp that makes the ground bow, **banners** that snare, a trap in the carpet, a line from the crown. Her guards appear in no technique name (they are 3v1 only).
  - **The Cyborg** is a ravenous machine: rivets, slag, a wrecker's swing, a **crane hook**, a wired heap of scrap, a bore through the ground. **His whole set runs on wreckage and metal,** never on people (Legal ln02, ln04).
- **No franchise echoes, no orbs, no spheres, no flashes.** The words ball, disc, bomb, flash and spirit are in no name, and every name is distinct (24 names plus 24 alternates, all different).
- **Flavour lines are in the fighter's voice** (4 to 12 words) and pass the lint, including the per-speaker bans.

## 3. Call-outs for the big ones (grab, beam release)

A call-out is said when the move happens: the grab lands, the beam is released at half charge, the beam is released at full charge. Each is 4 to 12 words and passes the lint.

- **The Protagonist.** grab: "Gotcha! Don't wriggle on me!" / beam half: "Here it comes! Don't blink!" / beam full: "All of it! Catch this one!"
- **The Anti-hero (the rival).** grab: "Third-rate wriggling. Hold still." / beam half: "One clean cut. Count it." / beam full: "Straight through, clown. Don't blink."
- **The Empress.** grab: "Gahaha! Come to us, rat!" / beam half: "Hahaha! A line for the rat!" / beam full: "Gahaha! Kneel before the line!"
- **The Cyborg.** grab: "Got you! Reeling you into the yard!" / beam half: "Limiter's cracking! Brace for it!" / beam full: "Limiter's off! Eat this one!"
