# Energy techniques: names, flavour and call-outs

Owner: Narrative and Fighter Identity. Version 2, 2026-10-23 (re-fitted to Combat's energy map). Orb's overhaul (`docs/ep/vision.md`, "Energy attacks overhaul"): each fighter gets their own energy set, along the light, medium, heavy and grab combinations: a **light volley** (rapid small shots), a **medium lob** (a more substantial explosive blast), a **heavy technique** (the most devastating), an **energy grab** with a unique mechanic ("maybe someone has an energy net, someone has an energy ring"), a **mine or trap** (a block while hitting energy attacks leaves one nearby), and a **chargeable beam**. The EP asked for each technique's name and a one-line flavour for four fighters, true to their voice rules and the hero and villain split, and two or three call-outs for the big ones. The data is `pending/energy_names.json`. Everything is an option for Orb; every name is unscreened by Legal.

**Re-fitted to Combat's map.** Version 1 was written before the moves were known. The EP then forwarded Combat's energy map (one-pagers still to follow), and the names and flavour now fit it: the net **yanks** the Protagonist's foe into reach, his level-2 lob **bounces** once, his beam is a **wide driving wall**, his pillar trap **throws a tripper up and away**; the Anti-hero's ring **binds** the arms and never cuts, his level-2 lob **splits into three**, his beam is a **sheaf of thin straight lances**, his plate fires a **fan of needles**; the Empress's banners **cage and slam a foe to kneel**, her crown **clings and goes off 20 ticks later**, her beam is a **thin sweeping cut from the hem**, her trap is a **line across the air** that fires her level-1 beam; the Cyborg's hook **throws** a foe 6 bh, his slag **rolls**, his beam is **ragged and unstable**, his trap is a **scrap-pit field** that clamps shut and drags a passer down. Fourteen names stayed, among them the four grabs (Harbour Net, Rank Ring, Banner Snare, Crane Hook) and Pressure Plate; ten changed (Breakwater, Trip Pillar, Stair Split, Lance Sheaf, Crown Toss, Velvet Rope, Hem Sweep, Slag Roll, Scrap Pit, Furnace Vent), and where an old name still fits it is the alternate. After Combat's kits arrived, one more changed: Silk Sparks became Silk Slivers.

## 1. How the names were made

- **Place plus verb, or place plus tool,** as the move names are (`move-names-draft.md`): a name says where it comes from or where it lands, and what it does. Two words, at most sixteen letters. Never shouted: only a signature and a finisher are shouted, so a generated technique has a plain name. The beam keeps the biome variants (HORIZON CLEAVE, RIDGE BORE and the rest), so a fighter's beam name is the base they hang on.
- **Each set is one idea,** shaped by the fighter's identity and the hero and villain split (Pillar 5):
  - **The Protagonist** is warm, tactile and protective: sparks, a lump of heat that skips, an open hand, a **net** that catches and hauls in (never crushes), a **pillar** that throws a tripper clear, and a wide wall of a beam (Breakwater).
  - **The Anti-hero** is precise and ranked: needles, a blast that splits into three steps, a sorting blow, a **ring** that binds the arms, a pressure plate that fires a fan of needles, and a sheaf of thin straight lances (Combat says drilling; Legal e11 bans a drill beam, so they are straight). His sets deny rather than celebrate.
  - **The Empress** is royal and theatrical: silk, a thrown crown that clings, a stamp that makes the ground bow, **banners** that cage and slam a foe to kneel, a velvet rope strung across the air, and a thin sweeping cut from the hem. Her guards appear in no technique name (they are 3v1 only).
  - **The Cyborg** is a ravenous machine: rivets, slag that rolls, a wrecker's swing, a **crane hook** that throws, a scrap-pit field that clamps shut, and a ragged vent of a beam from the furnace. **His whole set runs on wreckage and metal,** never on people (Legal ln02, ln04).
- **No franchise echoes, no orbs, no spheres, no flashes.** The words ball, disc, bomb, flash and spirit are in no name, and every name is distinct (24 names plus 24 alternates).
- **Flavour lines are in the fighter's voice** (4 to 12 words) and pass the lint, including the per-speaker bans.

## 3. Call-outs for the big ones (grab, beam release)

A call-out is said when the move happens: the grab lands, the beam is released at half charge, the beam is released at full charge. Each is 4 to 12 words and passes the lint. **Legal's rules (`docs/legal/energy-overhaul-screen.md`), kept:** a call-out **never shouts the move's own name** (no line contains a word of its move's name or alternate), **never splits into a charge chant plus a release shout** (each is one taunt-length line, said once, at the release; the half and full lines are alternatives for different charges, never a pair), and **never shouts a multiplier or a countdown**. They sit with the taunts, not the finishers.

- **The Protagonist.** grab: "Gotcha! Come in close!" / beam half: "Here it comes! Brace yourself!" / beam full: "All of it! Catch this one!"
- **The Anti-hero (the rival).** grab: "Arms down. You're being ranked." / beam half: "Thin and fast. Don't flinch." / beam full: "All of them, clown. Try to dodge."
- **The Empress.** grab: "Gahaha! Down in our cage, rat!" / beam half: "Hahaha! A thin cut for the rat!" / beam full: "Gahaha! Mind the cut, rat!"
- **The Cyborg.** grab: "Got you! Throwing you clear across!" / beam half: "Limiter's cracking! Brace for it!" / beam full: "Limiter's off! Everything goes!"

## 5. Checked against Combat's kits

**The Protagonist (`docs/combat/pending/energy/protagonist.md`, checked 2026-10-23).** Combat uses Knuckle Flick, Pier Lob, Whole Palm, Breakwater, Harbour Net and Trip Pillar as I named them, so no name changes. What the kit showed me, and what I changed in the flavour lines:
- **Knuckle Flick:** the sparks leave the heel of the open palm, and the fingers flick open from the knuckles. The name fits; the flavour now says "flicked off my palm".
- **Pier Lob:** the bounce is level 2 only (a heavy lump, thrown underhand off a kneel, a slow 40-tick arc). The flavour now says to charge it for the hop.
- **Whole Palm:** the palm lands flat on the breastbone in reach and drives heel-first at range. The name and the flavour already fit.
- **Breakwater:** a broad driving beam whose front flattens like a palm against a door. The name and flavour fit exactly.
- **Harbour Net:** slow, wide, a long catch, and the yank. The name fits, and the grab call-out ("Gotcha! Come in close!") fits the yank.
- **Trip Pillar:** a square of cracked ground that arms, then a solid slab rises. The flavour now says "cracked ground waits"; it is solid stone, never a column of light (Legal e14).
- **The snare and the two mines** are techniques of their own in Combat's kit (the net laid as a hammock, a spark mine, a blast mine), which my six slots did not cover. I added three extra names for each fighter (the table below).
- **Call-outs:** the beam's half-charge line fits levels 1 and 2 and the full-charge line fits level 3 (lengths 20, 36 and 54); Game Design confirms the mapping.
