## 1. What was drawn

A piece is a blow and a shot at once, so each is a **strike key set** (chamber, contact, follow) played by the same contact solve as every blow: the hand is set on his chest or gut and the shot goes off at the contact (VFX's). Two parked waves, generated by `wave_gen` like every wave: `protag11` (prefix `pk.`) and `rival8` (prefix `rk.`). Parked: no fighter's `more` list names them; the sim names them when C2t lands (Combat's row `keys` then says `set: pk.bolt_thrust`, `wave: protag11`).

His manner and hers (Combat's): the Protagonist's palm is **set** on one point, never swung, compact, back in his guard at once (`follow_t` 0.7); the rival's blade hand is driven in flat with the plate behind it, in straight lines, the tell open and the limb drawn back (h07). Three of the rival's four bolts are the blade hand and one is the lit fist; both his blasts are the lit fist; he has no open-palm bolt.

**Legal's conditions, as drawn:** one hand (the other open in guard, hands never together), shoulder height or lower, never at the hip (the lint's `hip_chamber`, `cupped_at_hip` and `two_hand_chamber` flags are empty on all twelve), nothing at the face, no pointed finger (every hand is the `open` state, curl 0, or the `fist` of the lit fist), never cupped, never both palms. The blast's 12-tick gather is a held pose (h05): it passes the held lint with the other 66 chambers, and the 60 wind-ups on the real solve.

## 4. What is not done

- The existing bolt and charged-shot presses (`pg.bolt`, `rw.bolt`, `pn.hold.*`) are unchanged: the point-blank pieces are separate key sets, so the ranged shots play as they do.
- The shot's light, rim and spill are VFX's. The pieces only put the hand on him at the contact tick and (for the blast) hold the gather.
- The launcher needs no pose: the 15 launchers are the tier's key sets and the stand-ins, played on a 14-tick wind-up (the director's `windup` argument) with the tell cut to its last third. Checked: all 16 tier pieces on 14 ticks solve every contact, with no NaN, nothing past a joint limit on screen and no limb above 80 before the last 6 ticks (`_test_windups`). A key set reaches its chamber `tell_at` of the way in (0.5), so on 14 ticks the tell is a 7-tick ramp then a 7-tick hold.
- **The close's stagger as the signal for B** (the launcher's cue) is not drawn: it needs the sim's name for that stagger (the cue and its text). The reel looks that exist are `s3.stagger_blocked` and `s3.stagger_countered`; a plainer one is a one-line mapping once the cue is named.

## 5. Legal's nudges (RL-112, RL-113; e09) and the burst's set (2026-10-06)

- **The contact differs by path, on screen.** In the side-on view an outward or inward arc is across the screen, so the three paths are drawn by the body and the forearm at the contact: a **line** is a level forearm from a middling stance; a **flick** (outward arc) is thrown from a crouch, the shoulder below the hand, so the forearm rises; a **sweep** (inward arc) is thrown from a tall stance, the shoulder above the hand, so the forearm falls onto the chest or the gut. Measured at the contact (`_test_energy_reach`): the Protagonist's lines -10 and -15 degrees, flick +22, sweep -34; the rival's lines -6 and -13, flick +14, sweep -22. The check fails unless the flick rises 12 above the lines and the sweep falls 6 below them.
- **Below the collar, a hand's width between the heads.** Every bolt lands the hand 38 to 62 high (the chest or the gut; the collar line is about 66). The lean, the head pitch and the step of every piece were cut so the bodies close less; with the defender flinching as in the study, the heads in a run of twelve stay at least 21 (Protagonist) and 22 (rival) apart (a hand's width is about 10; the check asks 18).
- **`rk.bolt_sweep`** is redrawn: the forearm comes down onto the chest below the collar from a tall stance, and the heads keep a clear gap.
- **The runs are Combat's sample runs of twelve** (`recipes.brawl.json` `reach.runs`, the first of each fighter, e09's order), with the arm of each bolt: the free arm (a mirror of the key set) takes bolts 4 and 9 of the Protagonist's run and 4 and 7 of the rival's. `pk.run` and `rk.run` show them 6 ticks apart. Mapping of Combat's `reach.01` to `04`: the Protagonist `bolt_thrust`, `bolt_flat`, `bolt_flick`, `bolt_sweep`; the rival `bolt_blade`, `bolt_fist`, `bolt_flick`, `bolt_sweep`. Combat's `keys` for these rows can now name the key sets (`set: pk.bolt_thrust`, `wave: protag11`; `rk.bolt_blade`, `wave: rival8`) instead of the ranged bolt press.

### The burst's set (Game Design brawl-second-pass section 3, 13d5e7e0)

One light at once (2 ticks from the press), then he sets himself for about 10 ticks, then the stream at 18, 22, 26, 31, 37, 45 and 56. Posed: wave `burst1` (prefix `bu.`, two poses, `bu.set_p.set` and `bu.set_r.set`), laid over the body by the `burst` press style's new `set` row (`pose` by fighter, `from` 3, `to_before` 2, `in` 2, `out` 2, `w` 0.9, `min_gap` 14) between a burst blow and a next blow 14 ticks or more behind it, and nowhere else (the stream's own gaps of 4 to 11 show none). It shows 8 ticks at more than half weight (35 to 42 for a light at 30 and the stream at 46).
- The Protagonist's: compact and forward, a low coiled guard, the head down on him, both open palms up at the chest (25 apart, no higher than 59).
- The rival's: the weight back on the rear leg, the blade hands drawn back at the shoulders and open to view (h07), the chin up (35 apart, no higher than 62): a show that more is coming.
- Honest to h05 although short of 12 ticks: hands 16 or more apart, never above the shoulder, never together (`_test_burst_set`).
- The flurry study's `xhold` clip is re-cut to the new ticks (`art/animation/review/flurry-study/xhold.gif` and `stills-xhold.png`: the light at 30, the set from 35 to 42, the stream from 46), so the gap Orb may find too long can be seen. The pose layer has no effect until a blow of the burst style is followed by one 14 or more ticks behind it, which is what the sim schedules when C2a lands.
