The names of Combat's `keys` for these rows are the key set ids above (`keys.level: posed`, `set: ru.su_knee`, `wave: rival7`); the two pinned pairs keep the ids the study named them.

**How a heavy is drawn, and what is new in the data** (key set fields, all optional, written by `wave_gen` from the wave spec):
- `tell_at` (0.05 to 1): how far into the wind-up the chamber is reached. Before, every B blow ramped linearly to its chamber and reached it on the contact tick, so the tell was a crouch. With 0.5 the chamber is reached half way and held in plain sight for the other half (Combat's "a tell in plain sight for most of 28 ticks"); the old ramp is 1.
- `squash_w` (0 to 1): the share of the press style's squash this piece takes. 0 draws the tell in its chamber alone (the style's squash crouches and leans back, which no drive but the stepping one asks for); the stepping pieces take 0.3.
- `drive`: Combat's drive word.
The sixteen use `tell_at` 0.5 and `squash_w` 0 or 0.3. Nothing else reads these keys; a key set without them plays as before.

## 2. The held lint takes h05 and f01 (`AnimHeldLint`, `held_scan --strict`)

- **h05, the chambers.** Every medium and heavy strike's chamber of every wave (`windups()`: 62 chambers: 16 of the tier, 46 of mediums) is a held pose of class `windup`: the pair test, the arms-wide test, and h05's own rules: the loaded hand (a hand, elbow or shoulder blow) never at a hip for any hand state; a both-hand blow's hands at least 20 apart; no crossed forearms. A chamber above 80 is not judged by the pose alone (the raise may come in the last 6 ticks): it is listed (the two double hammers, which Legal allows with the raise late) and judged on the real wind-up, below.
- **h05, the wind-up.** `anim_check` `_test_windups` plays all 56 medium and heavy wind-ups of both fighters on the real solve (mediums on 12 ticks, the tier and the held-only mediums on 28) and fails a limb above 80 before the last 6 ticks, a contact not solved, NaN, or a joint past a limit on screen.
- **f01.** `press_styles.json` rows used in a flurry (speed, burst, medium, super) draw at most 2 ghosts of a limb; no row more than 3 (a wire echo that pops off in turn). The per-instant ceiling itself (2 alive of a limb, 4 in all) is VFX's drawing.
- **What the lint found, and what changed.** Five mediums of each fighter failed h05 as chambers: the uppercut's loaded fist at the hip (x 12, height 36), the hammer and the dropping elbow raised to 92 and 84 (Combat's w5 says the arm starts at shoulder height), the drop kick and the double hammer (both held-only stand-ins). Redrawn in `wave1.mjs`: the uppercut chamber `[18, 46, 12]` (in front of the belt), the hammer and the dropping elbow chambers at 78. Both fighters inherit the change (`wave1` and `protag1` regenerated). The drop kick's open guard hand at the hip zone no longer fails (a foot blow's loaded limb is the foot).
- **The tier's drives** are checked on the poses (`_test_tier_drives`): stepping carries the body a pace (25 or more); a turn winds 50 or more and unwinds 40 or more, never over 100 at the blow; an unwind crosses one limb over his centre in the tell and out at the blow, never both arms; a heave sinks to -16 or lower and stands 16 or more taller, its standing feet in place; a fall is risen (hips 2 or more) with the limb at 70 or lower, then sunk 8 or more; a full turn winds 60, unwinds 50 and ends 20 to 60 past.

## 3. The mediums on 12 ticks (`data/anim/medium_wind.json`, `render/anim/tools/medium_wind_gen.mjs`)

Combat's eight rules (`parts.json` `strike.windup`) as the body does them, per key set (read off the moveset rows by the generator: 22 mediums on a rule, 9 held-only). Used only when a blow plays on the `medium` press style with a wind-up of 14 ticks or fewer (a held Y keeps the full coil) and `RenderAnim.medium_wind` (default true; false is the before):
| Rule | Fits | What the body does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| w1, w2, w3 | a leap, a spin, two arms | **held-Y only**: they never play on 12 ticks; no change here |
| w4 | a dropping kick | the chamber is reached at most 0.55 (the leg falls from hip height) |
| w5 | a dropping hand or elbow | the chambers are drawn at shoulder height (above) |
| w6 | a line | the step is halved (a lean, not a travel) |
| w7 | an arc | the chamber is reached at most a third: a flatter, tighter arc |
| w8 | a rise | the chamber is reached at most a quarter: no dip first |
`_test_medium_wind` checks the data and the effect (a w7 arc turns its shoulders less, a w8 rise lifts less, a w6 line travels 8 units less at 4 ticks out, a w4 kick lifts no higher). The provisional `burst`, `medium` and `super` rows become real rows when the sim names them (C2a); until then no live match names them.
