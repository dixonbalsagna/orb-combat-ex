## 2. Can the mannequin play the fighters' common key sets? **Yes, now.**

Before: a body whose roster id is neither fighter (the training dummy `REFERENCE`, the Empress's guards) had **no pair key**, so a beat that named a piece found nothing (`_pair_pick` returns "" without a key) and it fell to the go-live pick lists of wave 1 alone. **Now:** a third row in `fighters.json`, `common` (`replaces` `REFERENCE`; `pair_live.json` `aliases` `guard` and `ring_guard`), whose waves are wave 1, wave 2's entries, `rival2`, `rival4` (the `rb.*` and `rm.*` key sets Combat's reference and guard movesets name), the guards' wave `rival10` and the pair's shared wave. So for the dummy and the guards `strike.cross` is `w1.cross`, `strike.rib_shot` is `rb.rib_shot`, `strike.plate_drop` is `rm.plate_drop` and `strike.su_sole_fall` is `gd.su_sole_fall`, by the same lookup the fighters use. **Asserted** (`_test_pieces`): both roster ids reach `common`, ten shared names resolve to key sets, and the dummy plays a piece by name through the same solve (`_tier_scene` now takes `common`).

**What it does not give them:** the fighters' own `pr.*`, `pm.*`, `ru.*`, `pu.*` key sets (the Protagonist's and the Anti-hero's alone: Combat's rule "a fighter's own is never lent" is the same line), their ease rows (`ease` is per fighter; the dummy plays the global rows) and their energy roles beyond the bolt (section 4). A guard's look (a type's shape row, tier 1 of `fighter-onboarding.md`) is not made by this: it is the same body and the same moves.

**What it changes in a live match:** the dummy's random picks now come from `common`'s lists (wave 1 + `rb.*` + `rm.*`) rather than wave 1's go-live list. A trained-on dummy that strikes back would throw a slightly wider set. Nothing else reads the row.

## 3. The cash-in row

`press_styles.json` `styles.cash_in`, its own row: **2 ticks to the contact, no snap, no sink and no hold of a wind-up** (there is nothing to read and nothing to perfect-block), the follow-through the super row's, a little shorter (hold 6 against 8, recover 10), a lean-back of 0.12 against 0.34, 2 ghosts (Legal f01), **no carry** (it ends the chain) and no march. A beat with `cash_in: true` picks the row; **the press still says `style: "super"` to VFX**, with `press.cash_in` set for its flash, so VFX's style table does not change. Without the row in the data the old path (the super row at 2 ticks) plays, so the code stands on its own. Asserted: the row loads in 2 ticks, no snap, squash 0.

## 4. The beam's cast poses (no ball in the hand)

| Pose (`bm.hold.*`) | The Protagonist | The Anti-hero |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `charge_*` | Side-on, the weight back; **one arm drawn back at shoulder height with the open palm at the shoulder facing forward, flat, the fingers together and up, nothing in it**; the other hand open in guard at the chest, a shoulder width away (never together, never cupped, never at the hip) | Side-on; **the closed fist drawn back along the shoulder line with the forearm plate up**, the rear hand open and forward in guard (never pointing) |
| `fire_*` | The arm thrown straight out at shoulder height, **the heel of the palm leading** (the beam leaves the heel of the palm: Orb's wording in `beam-look.md`), the body lunged behind it, the other hand open and low at the rear | The fist driven straight out at shoulder height, the plate behind it; the light sits on the plate and the knuckle edges (VFX; Legal e06) |

How they play: `pair_live.json` `roles.<fighter>.beam` names `charge`, `fire` and `bone` (`hand_r`); `AnimFighter._beam_layer` mixes them where it mixed the generic closed-fist pair (`beam.charge`, `beam.fire`, which a body with no `beam` role still plays), and `_aim_arms` turns the arms to the aim as before. **For VFX:** `AnimFighter.cast` is `{state: "charge" or "fire", pose, w (0 to 1), bone}` while a beam is being cast (empty otherwise), and `cast_origin()` is the firing hand's model-space position (the heel of the palm for the Protagonist, the fist for the Anti-hero): the "palm pose at the beam's origin" that `beam-look.md` asked for.

The poses are **held-pose class `charge` and `energy`** (`anim_held_lint.gd` `bm.hold.charge` and `bm.`); the lint passes with them (0 fail). **For Legal:** RL-038 words the *signature* beam's pose as "no open palm". The Protagonist's cast is an open palm, so it needs Legal's reading as an *energy-in-reach* hand (R1 allows one open hand at shoulder height) or a swap to a heel-of-fist; the Anti-hero's is within RL-038.

**The guards' measured bolt** (`energy.x.01`: an open palm, a thrust, planted): the sequence `bm.guard_bolt` (wind 4 ticks with the palm drawn back to the shoulder, fire 3 with the palm thrown flat to shoulder height, settle 5), played through `roles.common.bolt`. One hand, open and flat, never cupped or at the hip.

## 5. The eleventh round's opening

Orb: "the rival's head snaps back and his arms fly open". The staggered fighter's `launcher_open` sequence `lq.open` had a reel that was 14% of the stagger (under 2 ticks of a 12-tick one). Now it begins with **`snap`** (4 ticks: the weight thrown back, the head snapped well back, the arms flung apart with the palms open: in the side-on view one up and forward and one up and back, because arms thrown out to the sides (the model's z) would point at the camera and not be seen) and **`open`** (3 ticks: the arms still at shoulder height and coming in, the head still back, the chest bare), then the daze and the come-up share what is left. Seven ticks in all, so the open arms never last 12 ticks (Legal h07: no held arms-wide); asserted. A stagger shorter than seven ticks scales the whole thing together. The ring on the chest and the ping are VFX's and Audio's; `AnimFighter.launcher` still carries the cue for them.
