# The right stick's class: Animation's side (Animation, 2026-10-31)

Owner: Animation. The EP's order (Orb's night order: a playable with the new controller mapping), from Combat's list in `docs/combat/pending/stick/README.md` "For Animation later": eight pieces of the class's own a fighter (Protagonist, Anti-hero), a charge stance by direction with no marker, a beam-firing pose by aim, a snap-beam pose, quick pieces tuned to the 10-tick wind-up, an air spike. Combat took the shapes below with no swaps; Combat's and Legal's conditions on the charged rows and Legal's RL-218 are met (section 2). Render only: no sim state and no hash moves. **Parked:** nothing plays these until Combat's `stick.json` names them and Encounter's hook is on (the stack builds `encounter-stick` on `encounter-swing`, which does not build yet).

## 1. The eighteen pieces (waves `protag13`, prefix `pz`; `rival10`, prefix `rz`)

A quick and a charged blow for each of the four directions (rulings section 2: up a rising blow, down a low sweep, toward a body blow, away a wide turning blow that pushes him off) and an air spike. Every piece is `named`: findable by the beat's `piece` (`strike.<name>`), in no pick list, so no other blow's look changes. Quick pieces are lights with the 10-tick wind-up (load 10, follow 4, recover 4 on the manifest row); charged pieces are heavies whose release is the 4-tick blow out of the held chamber (load 4, follow 6, recover 10).

## 2. The charge stances: a charged piece's chamber is the stance

The held layer draws the first key of the held piece while the sim waits, so each charged chamber is the charge stance of its direction and has to say which way the blow goes with no marker: **up** sunk low with the weight under him and the near hand low and forward (never at the hip); **down** tall, the blow's arm cocked beside the head with the elbow bent and back; **toward** side-on with the weight back, one hand at the shoulder and the other out toward him; **away** wound right round with his back half to him. The check (`anim_check` `_test_stick_class`) holds each fighter's four stances apart (nearest two 16.5 units of pose apart at rest, both fighters), and puts up at the lowest hips, down at the highest hand and away at the widest wind of the spine.

**Found by the check, fixed: the held medium's coil would have flattened them.** The stick's blow is a medium (the hook builds it from `DirBrawl.MEDIUM`), and a held medium sinks and winds toward one shared body from tick 10 (`press_styles.json` `hold`: squash 1.1 toward `ch.y_p.load`, a lean back, 36 degrees of twist back). On the real hold code, 60 ticks held: the nearest two stances fall from 16.5 to **7.9** units apart (the Anti-hero 16.4 to 7.3). A key set may now carry `hold_w`, its share of the style's coil (data, default 1, written by `wave_gen`); the eight charged pieces carry 0.25, and the nearest two stay **14.1** apart (13.6) after 60 ticks. Pieces without the field are untouched. If Encounter's stick blow takes a style of its own, its `hold` row can say the same and the field becomes redundant.

**The conditions** (the EP's message of 2026-10-31, Legal's zip-screen.md section 4, a charge can be held for 68 ticks): `plunge_blade` and `hammer_drop` have the arm cocked beside the head (a hand at 74, never straight up, the rear arm not raised); `lifting_heave` and `knee_launch` have their hands low; `lifting_heave` and `drive_palm` are open hands with both feet down, no glow; the Anti-hero has no palm (his open hands are plate and blade, as in `rival4`). The test reads them: no hand above 76 in any charged chamber, the two palms open with the feet down, the dropping blows lowered at once (a hand at 76 or lower six ticks after the contact, 80 after; the highest seen is 63) and the palm rise's hands and feet in every key. **Legal looks at the chambers as held poses before they go live:** the sixteen poses are `pz.<charged>.chamber` and `rz.<charged>.chamber`; `held_scan --strict` (the lint's own h05 reads a heavy's chamber as a held pose) passes.

**Legal's RL-218** (clear for the private build; before live, rendered stills with hands of `rz.swing_elbow.chamber`, `rz.plate_ram.chamber`, `pz.drive_palm.chamber` and `bn.hold.fire_back`): the Anti-hero's open hands must be flat blades edge on, and a head-target blow lands on the torso, or on the ground on a downed foe, with no repeat or close-up; `fire_back`'s rear hand stays under 32 with a straight elbow. What changed: **the Anti-hero has no open hand in any pose of his wave or of his beam poses** (his hands are closed fists, so there is nothing to read as a palm; the rig has one finger curl a hand, so an open hand edge on cannot be shown at all); **no piece aims at the head** (`plunge_blade`, `hammer_drop`, `swing_elbow` and the two air spikes now land on the chest, and the manifest rows say so); and **`fire_back` was re-posed**: the first draft read as arms spread wide in a side view, so the free hand is brought in and bent, and now the rear hand is 27 units out of the torso and the free hand 30 (both under 32), the sides z 19 and -5; the rear arm is nearly straight (the elbow at 37 degrees, the arm's reach into the shoulder's blind spot stopping it short of straighter). The stills are in `art/animation/review/stick-class/legal-rl218/`: the poses as authored, drawn by the real renderer with the fighters' views scaled up three times (the camera untouched), the hands as the renderer draws them. The test (`_test_stick_class`) holds all of it.

## 3. The beam poses (wave `beam2`, prefix `bn`; the hook in `AnimFighter._beam_layer`)

A firing pose by aim for each fighter: **up** and **down** at 45 degrees and **behind him** (`fire_up`, `fire_down`, `fire_back`), and the **snap-beam** (`snap`). The sketches keep Legal's rule for a cast (one hand, nothing in it, wrists apart, never at a hip): the Protagonist's beam leaves the heel of the open palm and the Anti-hero's the fist with the forearm plate behind it. Up throws the arm up and forward with the chest opened under it; down folds the body over the arm; behind turns the body right round (the throwing shoulder swung to the back, the free hand forward) so the arm is thrown out along the line and not back through the shoulder's blind spot; the snap is a short flat flick from the shoulder with no lunge.

The hook (switch `RenderAnim.beam_aim_poses`, `--no-beam-aim-poses` is the before; data `data/anim/pair_live.json` `roles.<fighter>.beam` gains `fire_up`, `fire_down`, `fire_back`, `snap`): the beam's own line in the body frame picks the pose, the toward pose mixed with the up pose by how far the line is above the level (full at 45 degrees), the down pose the same below, the behind pose from 75 to 140 degrees; what is left of the angle is the arms' turn, as it was. **A snap-beam is a beam whose life is under a quarter of a second, or one the sim flags with a `snap` field**; its pose goes with the beam's own short life (the long beam's 0.95 seconds would hold it past the beam). A fighter with none of the ids casts exactly as before, switch on or off (`_test_beam_aim` compares every bone and the hips, four aims, to the last bit); the roles get the ids for both fighters.

**Found by the stills, fixed:** the poses of the beam wave were not loaded in the live game: the pair's `shared` list in `data/anim/pair_live.json` did not name `beam2`, so `fire_up`, `fire_down`, `fire_back` and `snap` named poses that did not exist there and the hook would have done nothing (it fell back to the old cast, silently). `shared` names it now and `_test_beam_aim` reads the data. **Found by the test, fixed:** the first version asked `bool(b.get("snap"))` of a beam with no such field, which is not a valid call and would have broken every beam the first time the hook ran.

## 4. Joint posing during the moves (Orb's note: "more work is needed all around")

Everything is held to the joint limits at three levels, and the third is the one that was missing for new pieces: the **authored** pose (`pose_lint`: 27 poses of `protag13` 0 issues; 27 of `rival10` 1, `rz.short_ram.contact` right elbow at 153 degrees, an elbow blow folds the arm; 8 of `beam2` 4 notes of 1.1 units on the behind pose's free hand), the **limited IK** (`joint_scan --strict`, poses and live: 0 past a limit on screen) and the **last pass** (`anim_check` `_test_pulled`: every key set at 60 units and at its own range, 161 of them with these 18, **0 frames pulled over 30 degrees**, 1 over 20). Getting the 18 to that took the free-hand rule below: a free hand has to be at least ten units forward of its shoulder, and the shoulder is a few units past the hips when the body lunges; a hand behind the shoulder is refused by the shoulder's blind spot and drawn where the limit puts it, not where it was authored (every first draft had five or six). The reach table of each piece is in the manifest rows' `range` (borrowed from the base blow until Combat's rows land; the keysets carry their own limits).
