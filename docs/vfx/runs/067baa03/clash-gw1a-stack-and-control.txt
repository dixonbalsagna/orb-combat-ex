ATTRIBUTION (a reading of the pictures, not yet a control): the orange crater floor is the largest, fastest change in the second and it is the tile ground's crater (Rendering's crater look on the globe ground); the speed streaks, chips and sparks are VFX's but are small in area. So the evidence points at the globe ground's crater floor, which is Rendering's, not at the clash look.
