## The nine rows, and what each was
1. **Eight biome rows: staging, not an effect fault.** `_biome_fx` stages its spots on the hand-made arena's biomes (desert at 6,000 columns, forest 5,000, mountains 7,000, city 3,000). On the planet those x are other ground: planet 7 has the sea at the desert spot, so the rows read "no plume" and a column of "-1680 units" (the foot of nothing). The rows are now pinned to the arena with `WorldPin.arena()` (the world is put back after, `WorldDefault.setForTest`).
2. **The effect itself on the planet, tested rather than pinned.** New `_biome_fx_planet` finds a desert, a forest and a mountain or highland on the planet's dry ground by the fine biome the planet really has there, and runs the plume rows there: a crater makes one column and one star; the puffs are Art's desert colours (16 of 25); the column stands up (312 units); tier 4 builds a taller plume and 22 chunks against 12; a forest bounce sheds leaves (6); a mountain slide throws rock (21 chunks); debris lands, stays, and is gone in nine seconds. **All pass: no effect fault on the planet.**
3. **The ring's turn (meteor trail): by design.** On the planet a field world's points are planet directions and the turn moves the frame, not the points (`VfxMeteor.on_turn`, `has_q`), so "30 to 30 points" is correct there. The row now says that for the planet (every point kept, the burning head too) and the "drops the points beyond the zone" row runs on an arena-pinned second sim.
