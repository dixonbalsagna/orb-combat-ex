The EP's `s422` (GW1a stack, a read-only copy) fails `effects_check` with six rows and a SCRIPT ERROR. Each is read below under GW1a's data: Orb's 9 November game with World's water floor and surface on, and the one energy model on. After these edits the same copy passes: 1,169 ok, 0 failing, no script error (`docs/vfx/runs/1ee3262c/gw1a-s422-effects-check.txt`).

**Apply:** `git apply docs/vfx/pending/gw1a-pins/apply.patch` against main (clean on main 1ee3262c; sha1 10a9c3d855d23c9613bd3d62496da93d960d3bc8). Two files: `render/vfx/tools/effects_check.gd` and `render/vfx/meteor.gd`. It applies on main and on the stack (disjoint from meteor-default).

## What each row was
1. **Water, three rows** ("a plunge throws more than a skip (29 > 37)", "the plunge schedules its rebound", "with the floor off a splash on the sea is the plunge"). **Not a wrong splash in Orb's game.** World's floor is on in the 9 November data (`WorldFill.floorOn()`), so a body that meets the sea lands on the sheet: a slap, no plunge column, no rebound (Game Design's 2026-10-30 ruling, `docs/world/lake-floor.md`). The three rows read the floor-OFF path (the plunge as it was). The check now pins the floor off for those rows and restores the data's own setting after, and a new row reads what Orb plays: with the data's floor setting a splash on the sea is a slap on the sheet (floor on), a plunge (floor off). The slap's own rows were already passing.
2. **Flight lines, two rows and the SCRIPT ERROR** ("the Anti-hero's line is drawn from 95 knots where the Protagonist's takes 95", "with the switch on the hub lays the line (0 points)", then `Trying to assign value of type 'Nil' to a variable of type 'Vector4'` at `effects_check.gd:7164`). **A real bug in `VfxMeteor.flat_out_of`, latent because the flight lines are off by default** (`VfxLook.FLIGHT_DEFAULT` false). `flat_out_of` assumed the old traversal dash: the close dash times `TRAV_FREE` over a boost taken from the distance to the rival. With the one energy model on (`S.powerOn`) the flight speed is `SimPower.flatSpeed(close, ramp)`, and with the LT dash on at any range (`SimPower.dashAnyRangeOn`) the ramp is always 1, so near the rival the old formula returned ten times the true flat out (97,538 units a second where the speed is 9,752). The check moved its fighters at that speed (1,625 units a tick: a knot every tick for both, 95 against 95), and the hub's ribbon was never drawn, so the check's read of the ribbon's material was Nil (the script error). The fix: `flat_out_of` takes the far end over the close dash as `k` (the traversal factor, or with the energy model his level's flat out over his close dash, from `SimPower.flatGrowth`, `level` and `S.powerFlat`) and divides by `1 + (k - 1) * ramp`, with the ramp 1 when the dash works at any range. Without the energy model it is the old formula exactly. In Orb's game this would have shown only if the flight lines were turned on: near the rival the line's speed ramp would have read zero, so no line while he fights close.
3. **The real-cue tell** ("each real cue's tell draws (a ground line and its tick) the tick it starts (70 of 71)"). One of 71 real cues (seed 4, tick 2560, a speed lunge, wind-up 10, move 4) arrives straight above or below its start, so it has no ground line to draw: its tick alone is its tell. **Not a wrong picture:** there is nothing to draw between the points. The check now needs two quads only when the zip has ground distance, one otherwise.
