# Powering up on RT: three answers of our own (VFX, paper, 2026-11-01)

The ask (through the EP): Legal's RL-239 (5) clears RT powering up with a binding cap, since it is the genre's best-known scene. **At most two of the seven marks at any moment**, with no scream, no lightning, no flame aura, no hair change, no flash with a shouted form name, no crouch with clenched fists, and no standing, screaming fighter. The sky **pales or parts and never darkens**; any ring is **thin, lane colour and slow** (k03); the ground is **a gentle lift with weight, not a ring of rocks with wind round him**; the pose is upright or braced with hands low (Animation sketches it); a long hold, up to the first step in 17.5 s, is read every tick. It needs an answer that is ours. Nothing is built: this page is three sketches and a line each. The picture is `docs/vfx/img/power-up-ideas/three-ways-to-power-up.png` (shapes only: the pose is a placeholder for Animation's).

Counting, for Legal to confirm: mark 4 is one bundle (rubble rising, ground cracking, dust, wind, and a changing sky all count as it), so option 1's ground lift and option 2's sky are the same mark; a thin lane ring is the second.

## 1. The ground gives
A shallow dome of pale, pressed earth rises a hand's width round his feet, and loose pebbles lift slowly and hang at uneven heights; one thin, slow lane-colour ring breathes out along the ground once a beat (about every 2 s), and a low dust breath follows it. **Marks: the ground (1) and a ring.** Built pieces: the levitating rocks (idea 1, with its stand-down gate), hover pressure's pressed disc and the standing cracks of `react.gd`, the pressure ring's quad; what is new is the dome and the beat. Cost: small (existing pool pieces and one ring a beat). **Risk:** the closest to the genre's famous picture, so it is the one most needing Legal's look: rocks held to a few, no wind lines, no ring of rocks.

## 2. The sky opens
No change on the ground except a soft pool of his lane colour. Over a few seconds the cloud overhead draws back from a point above him and the sky there pales to a clear, lane-tinted gap, held while he holds and closing as he lets go; it never darkens, and no column of light connects it to him. **Marks: the sky (1), and the ground's pool is light, not a mark.** It is Rendering's sky term (VFX has nothing to draw: idea 12), so the cost to VFX is the cue and the numbers. **Risk:** it is the one that changes the whole screen, so the flash and area rules bind (a slow rise over many seconds, a pale target, never white), and it must be read in both day and night; at night the gap should read as a clearing with a few stars and no brighter than the day sky.

## 3. The world takes notice (ours)
Nothing on him at all. The landscape answers, which is the game's own pillar ("the world answers back"): dust trickles off nearby roofs, window glass trembles, trees and flags lean away, a pool dishes in and rings, civilians in the town stop and turn to look, and a slow low pulse shivers dust off the ground in step with Audio's thud. The more he gives, the farther the effect reaches (a body height at first, four at the top). **Marks: none of the seven** (it is the world, not his body), which leaves room for either option above as a second effect. Built or buildable: `react.gd`'s window blow-outs and roof dust (idea 13), footfall prints and sound-linked pulses (16 and 17), water parting (8); the civilians turning is Encounter's and World's. **Risk:** subtle, so it needs a strong first second and a camera that frames some of the town; it must never read as damage to people, so nothing falls, breaks or hurts here.

## What else is ours (a few more, if none of the three lands)
- **Light that arrives:** a soft pool of his lane colour on the nearest ground and building faces, breathing slowly (idea 5; not a mark).
- **Stillness:** the wind and the dust within a few body heights go quiet for the length of the hold, and only he moves; sound and the lack of motion do the work (a still room is the opposite of the genre's roar).
- **The shadow:** his own cast shadow lengthens and sharpens toward the ground as if lit from straight above, a small quiet tell that costs a shape.
- **The scale:** the far hills and the horizon draw a few pixels toward him and settle, a slow parallax breath, nothing on him.
