# The other fight's effects (2v2, VFX, 2026-11-02): read only, behind a switch, off

The ask (through the EP): in a `{"fights": 2}` match Rendering draws the other fight's two bodies (Simulation's fight swap, `docs/architecture/fight-swap.md`, and cross-fights, `docs/architecture/cross-fights.md`). Hits, sparks, dust, beams and shots need that fight's events, and the host drains only fight 0's. Plan how the hub reads fight 1's events read-only, with Simulation's small part, then build.

## The finding: the events are already there
`SimCore._stepFights` runs each fight's tick with that fight entered into S (`SimStage.enter`), and for fights past the first it clears `S.out.fx` and `S.out.feed` **at the start of that fight's own tick**, then puts fight 0 back. The swap moves `out` with the rest, so after the step fight i's last tick's events sit in `S.stage.fights[i].out.fx`, and stay there until fight i's next tick clears them. Nothing reads them; nothing drains them. So **Simulation's small part is mostly a contract, plus one accessor**: those events are not cleared, drained or reused between a step and the next step of that fight, and a hold (hit-stop, a pause) leaves them empty because the clear still happens. If Simulation wants it explicit, the one useful addition is a read accessor (`SimStage.eventsOf(S, i)` returning a duplicate) so no reader reaches into `st.fights[i].out`; I do not need it to build.

## The plan, as built (read only: nothing is swapped into S)
The first cut entered each other fight into S (`SimStage.enter`) to read it and put fight 0 back; that is the view writing sim state, even restored, and was ruled out. The second built its own alias of the state. This is the third: **there is one view of another fight in the game, Animation's `RenderAnim.fight_view(S, k)`** (`docs/animation/fight-view.md`): a `SimState` whose per-fight fields (the bodies, director, clock, beams, shots, events and the rest `SimStage._swap` lists) are fight k's record from `S.stage.fights[k]`, and whose every other field is S's own by reference; `stage` is null on it and `S.stage.cur` is never touched. VFX reads it and builds nothing of its own.
- **One hub for each other fight** (`render/vfx/stage_feed.gd`). Fight k's events go to its own `VfxHub` on its own clock (its `tick` events carry its own `dt` and `frozen`), read through that view, so its debris pool, shots, beam plays, flashes and trail state are its own.
- **The host pushes the events; that is the only way in.** Rendering's host reads each other fight's events (`host.other_events()`: one fx list for each fight past the first, behind `SimHost.draw_others`), sets `hub.host_pushes = true` and calls `hub.consume_others(S, lists)` once a tick after `consume`. `consume` never reads the other fights itself. Nothing is drained or cleared: the lists are copied.
- **The other hubs copy the main hub's switches** (every `*_enabled`, quality, reduced motion and flashing, the screen numbers) at the start and every 30 ticks. **The colour-blind table** is the main hub's alone (`is_other` hubs skip `_sync_vision`), and it reads every fight's bodies (`SimStage.bodies`, a read).
- **Switch:** `VfxHub.other_fights_enabled`, `VfxLook.OTHER_FIGHTS_DEFAULT` false, `?otherfights=1` on a local page. Off, nothing runs; a 1v1 has `S.stage` null and never reaches it.

## Drawing it (Rendering's)
The views read the state at draw time as well as the hub (S.fighters, S.beams, S.shots, S.T), and take a `host` whose `S` they read. So a pane that shows fight k draws its VFX views with **fight k's hub** (`feed.hubs[k]`) and **Animation's view of it** (`RenderAnim.fight_view(S, k)`) as the state, through a host-shaped holder whose `S` is that view: nothing is entered, nothing swapped.

## Not built, and not mine
- Drawing the other fight's bodies and giving its pane the views (Rendering).
- The shot with owner -1 (a shot from outside) draws its shooter from `SimCross.shooter`, not the fight's last fighter; a beam drawn only to `b.stopAt` (Rendering, noted in `cross-fights.md`). The hub's own readers of `S.fighters[owner]` for a shot from outside need the same: not yet touched.
- Effects that cross between fights (a hit from outside landing in fight 1 shows in fight 1's hub, as it is met in his fight; the shooter's own muzzle effect is in fight 0's): that follows from the sim's rule and needs no code.
- Cost: a second hub's work per other fight, roughly one more fight's effects (a hub's consume is a small share of a tick; measured only in the test: 900 ticks of four computer fighters, 1,230 events, 70 pieces made in fight 1's hub). Performance should bench it with a pane that draws it.

## Check
`effects_check` (`_other_fights`): two computer pairs, 900 ticks, switch on against off. On: the second fight has its own hub, fed every tick, with its own events (1,230 of them, hits, sparks, dust and cues among them) and making its own pieces; the sim's hash, the main hub's pieces and ticks are the same on and off; off, no other hub exists; feeding drains none of the other fight's events; and the alias carries fight 1's own bodies, events and clock and S's shared world by reference while S, the fight's record and the current fight are as they were.
