## Places a transformation in progress is protected from interruption

1. Full and short versions freeze the whole sim: "SimCore.step consumes no input ... nothing in the sim runs" (`sim/core/pause.gd:4-6`); the frozen tick returns before the fight runs (`sim/core/sim.gd:398-402`); the break lands inside the pause (`sim/core/pause.gd:142-156`). Lengths: full 3 s (final 4 s), short 1.5 s (`data/fight/pause.json:9-19`).
2. Live version, data comment: "live: otherwise; no pause, the transformer cannot be interrupted for lengthS" (`data/fight/pause.json:3`; `lengthS` 0.8, gather 10 ticks at `data/fight/pause.json:20-23`); code comment: "the caller plays its uninterruptible live version" (`sim/core/pause.gd:12`).
3. Live version, code: director cooldown raised to the hold (`sim/director/exchange.gd:966`), players held to it (`sim/director/exchange.gd:967`; `sim/director/press.gd:65-71`), transformer's `stunTicks` raised to the hold (`sim/director/exchange.gd:968`), velocity zeroed (`sim/director/exchange.gd:963-964`). TRANSFORM_HOLD 0.8 s "no exchange starts and the transformer holds still" (`sim/director/exchange.gd:927`).
4. The cooldown gate on starting an exchange, with its listed exceptions (an opening, a bury follow-up, a player's request free of the cooldown): `sim/director/exchange.gd:201-202`; player hold rule `sim/director/press.gd:75-86`; AI attack readiness requires `S.dirS.cool <= 0.0` (`sim/director/ai.gd:258`).
5. A transformation can only start between exchanges (`sim/director/exchange.gd:948`), so no exchange is running at its start.
6. In `SimDamage.hurt` / `hit` (`sim/core/damage.gd:21`, `:46`): no check on `breakIn`, `formReady`, pause or a transforming state (grep: not found).
7. Any hit-ignore or armour flag tied to a transformation in `sim/`: not found (the armour hits in `sim/director/brawl.gd:1351-1371`, `sim/director/beam_move.gd:5-6` and the barrage `immune` in `sim/director/blast.gd:476,514` are tied to a heavy, a stick charge and a barrage, not to a transformation). The release's "armour" is listed as withdrawn in data: `data/fighters/PROTAGONIST/forms.json:61`.
