- Files beyond the top 15 per term are counted (file count and hit count in each term's header line) but not listed.
- Substring terms also match longer words containing them (for example `pride` inside an identifier, `hidden` as a Godot visibility word, `menace` inside `menace_*` keys); they are counted as hits, with no sorting by sense.
- Lines longer than 140 characters are cut with "..." in the examples.
