# Alpha audit: the adversarial review, and every finding that was lowered

Judgment session, phase 6, 2026-11-13. One round. Five reviewers each attacked the register, the path and the briefs through one lens; a separate verifier per lens checked every criticism against the files, told to refute by default. A criticism survived only if the verifier confirmed it, in full or in part. **58 criticisms: 36 confirmed, 22 confirmed in part, 0 refuted.** Every survivor changed the plan; the last column says where. Where the verifier offered a smaller fix than the reviewer, the smaller one was taken. The reviewers' full text is in `06-adversarial/<lens>.md`, the verifiers' in `<lens>.verify.md`, the brief amendments in `06-adversarial/brief-amendments.json`.

**What this round did not do.** A second round on the changed parts was dropped for budget (`07-skipped.md`), so the amendments themselves have not been attacked. The reviewers read all of A1's and A2's briefs that touched their lens and sampled the rest; the "missing" reviewer says which briefs it only searched by keyword (`06-adversarial/missing.md`). Brief amendments were appended under an "Amendments" heading that overrides the text above it; the older text was not rewritten line by line, so a brief must be read to its end.
