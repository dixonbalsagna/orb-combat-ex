## Notable

- sim/world/settlements.gd:4 says "NOT YET WIRED", but WorldSettle is called by sim/core/replay.gd:184 and sim/director/arc.gd:125. The header is stale.
- sim/world/planet.gd:2 says "Window 1: nothing in the game reads it yet", but WorldPlanet is named in 54 files, for example sim/core/planet_load.gd and render/core/far_land.gd:47.
- sim/world/planet_world.gd:2 is marked "DRAFT, scratch", yet it is in live use (audio/audio_world.gd:74, 20 files).
- The backburnered menace and anguish meters are still written in shipped code: sim/world/collateral.gd:407-467, reached from sim/world/structures.gd:477.
- The far-anguish switch is still in place (sim/world/plan.gd:49), and planet_world.gd:286 feeds it.
- The hiding cover system is still reached: sim/core/hiding.gd:22 calls WorldCover.coverAt, and sim/world/cover.js is imported by sim/director/ai.js:8.
- Signature-variant data is read by World: crater.gd:439 (data/combat/sigvariants.json scorch) and structures.gd:579 (variant `trees`).
- WorldWrecks (wrecks.gd) and WorldProps (props.gd) are reached only by their own check scripts.
- The comment at .github/workflows/ci.yml:384 says "the 48 scripts" and the `_why` in tools/world-checks.json:2 says "all 48 pass". The `run` list now holds 85, so both comments are stale.
- tools/world-checks.json `run` includes `floor_walk_probe`, a script named as a probe but run as a check.
- The world job's timeout was raised to 45 min after the job ran 23-25+ min (ci.yml:390).
- The frozen JS twins (terrain.js, structures.js, cover.js, biomes.js) are imported by sim/core/fighter.js:9-10, sim/core/sim.js:9 and sim/director/ai.js:6-8, and tested by sim/core/test/wrap.test.js:9-10 in CI job `sim`.
- Many World features ship off by default in data: after, campaign, craters, struggleMeet, fire, rivers, skate, water (all three switches), land.b and contact.settle are false; climate.json:4 has gen=1.
- No TODO, FIXME, KAI or VORR marks in sim/world or data/biomes (git grep). The only "vorr" is a variable in the caller sim/core/tools/parity.gd:4355.
- The word "strip" (the retired flat arena) appears in 13 runtime files, most in plan.gd (13), crater.gd (7), after.gd (7) and store.gd (5). Some uses may mean the ring's line rather than the retired arena; not evidenced either way.
