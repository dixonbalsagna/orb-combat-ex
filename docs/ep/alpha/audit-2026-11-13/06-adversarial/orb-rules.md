## What I looked at, and what I did not

Read in full: `04-path-to-alpha.md` (all 552 lines); `docs/ep/vision.md` from line 1396 to the end (2026-10-16 evening onward), plus a keyword search of the earlier part for the standing rules (AI disclosure at 723-725, in-progress character art at 842-850 and 1399, forward-facing text at 951, cooldowns and caps at 1412); `docs/ep/taste.md`; `docs/decisions/0010-look-gate.md`; all six `05-directives/*/orb.md`; `questions.md`; `07-skipped.md`; the specification's lines 133-163 and the X-02, X-12 and X-17 rows. Read the acceptance criteria and Orb's-rules lines of these briefs: A1 ui-ux, game-design, legal-ip, narrative-identity, encounter-systems, simulation-engine (the meters row); A2 vfx, camera, tools-pipeline, community-marketing, legal-ip, and the Orb lines of ep and rendering; A3 ui-ux, ep, game-design, encounter-systems, simulation-engine, controls-feel; A4 ep, and the gate lines of legal-ip and performance-platform; A5 community-marketing and the gate lines of ep and legal-ip. Ran keyword searches over all 100 briefs for marks on the fighters, fades, dissolves, blur, wipes, cooldowns, caps, lock-outs, slow motion, control taken from the player, the campaign and spending, and a script over `03-gap-register.json` for twenty rows named in the criticisms.

What the searches did not turn up: no brief adds a health bar, a cooldown, time manipulation, a dissolve or fade, or a marker over a fighter (most briefs forbid them by name); the AI disclosure is carried on the first screen, the package and the itch.io page; in-progress character art is kept off the restricted page, cover image included; nothing is posted or spent without Orb; the hero and villain meters and the biome signature variants are not counted as Alpha features.

Not done: the remaining briefs (about seventy) were searched by keyword, not read; the 22 area reviews were opened only where a criticism needed them (COMBAT, RENDER); `docs/ep/vision.md` before line 1396 was searched, not read; the proposals behind the stamina loop and the gravity well were opened only for the one default cited in ORB-04; no code was read, so register facts (for example that the marks in ORB-07 are live by default) are taken from the register and are no stronger than it. No Godot, browser or web was used, and no other file was changed.
