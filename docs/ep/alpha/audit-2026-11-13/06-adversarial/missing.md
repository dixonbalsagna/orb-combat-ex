# Adversarial review: what is missing

Lens: what the plan leaves out. Read-only review of the audit's plan at baseline 8901d351, 2026-11-13. Paths are relative to `docs/ep/alpha/audit-2026-11-13/` unless they start with `docs/`, `.github/` or `tools/`. "Path" means `04-path-to-alpha.md`; "spec" means `docs/ep/alpha/alpha-audit-specification.md`.

## What I looked at, and what I did not

Read in full: the path (all 551 lines); the spec's Alpha definition and criteria (lines 133-203), the 22 areas' questions (337-551) and sections 8 to 11 (576-706); `questions.md` and `07-skipped.md`; the register as one line per row (all 270, by script over `03-gap-register.json`), with a script that maps every criterion X-01 to X-34 to its rows and milestones and confirms that all 174 required rows are placed. Every criterion has at least one placed row except X-12, which is a decision and is in the path. Read the exit-criteria tables of all 22 area files and checked that the reduced-depth areas (ANIM, TEXT, ACCESS, AUDIO) and CONTROLS, CAMERA, NET, UI, QA, PROCESS and MARKET each answer every question the spec set; they do. Briefs read whole or through their acceptance criteria: every `orb.md` (A1, A2, A4 in part, A5 in part, A6); A1's EP and QA; A2's Performance, QA, Tools, Legal, Controls, Community & Marketing; A3's Legal, Netcode and Tools (in part); A4's Animation, Art, Combat, Game Design, Rendering, UI, Camera, Legal, Tools, EP and every Order line; A5's QA, Community & Marketing and Tools (in part); A6's QA and Tools (in part). Checked two project files directly: `.github/workflows/desktop-builds.yml:95-119` and the content-screen workflow's header.

One suspicion was tested and dropped: that committing the 14,805 uncommitted paths (A-PROCESS-03) could put a personal file path into the public repository. A scan of the untracked text files for a user-profile path found none, so it is not reported.

Not done: no brief in A1 beyond the EP's, QA's and Orb's was read whole (the other nineteen were searched by keyword only); A3's fight briefs (Encounter, Simulation, Game Design, World) were not read, so a row there whose acceptance criteria would not close it could have been missed; the verify files, `04-drafts/`, `reads/` and `01-inventory/` were not opened; nothing was run in the engine or a browser and no web page was read, so claims about the Deck, itch.io and Discord rest on the audit's own notes. Section 10.5's Steam items were checked only as far as: the timeline has a row (A-MARKET-08, A5) and "Beta in sight" is listed as able to wait.
