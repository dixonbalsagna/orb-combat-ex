# Adversarial review: what is under-costed

Lens: the plan's sizes against the project's own past costs. Read-only; 2026-11-13; baseline 8901d351. "Path" is `04-path-to-alpha.md`; briefs are under `05-directives/`.

## What I looked at, and what I did not

Read whole: the path (all 551 lines), `questions.md`, the `orb.md` of A1, A2, A3 and A6, `A1/ep.md`, `A6/ep.md`, `A3/simulation-engine.md`, `A4/combat-choreography.md`, `A4/animation.md`. Read in part (token lines, acceptance lines, order lines): `A1/tools-pipeline.md`, `A1/qa-balance.md`, `A3/world-environment.md`, `A3/ep.md`, `A3/legal-ip.md`, `A4/ep.md`, `A4/legal-ip.md`, `A5/game-design.md`, `A5/qa-balance.md`, `A5/legal-ip.md`, `A5/rendering-techart.md`, `A6/qa-balance.md`. Counted by script over `03-gap-register.json` and `04-milestone-rows.json`: size classes per milestone, rows that change the fingerprint, lead and support load per director, the cost basis of every placed row (234 of 270 rows say "estimate"), and across all 100 briefs the grant lines and look-gate lines. Comparables read: `docs/ep/proposals/terrain-globe-plan.md:315-334`, `gravity-well.html:431-444`, `combat-variety-packet.html:80, 207, 223, 473`, `co-op-2v2.md:28-40, 86`, `controller-vision.md:163`, `docs/qa/chain-3198301f.md:3`, `docs/tools/gauntlet-build1.md:16`, `docs/ep/runs/6a30b4c9/README.md`, and `git log` from 2026-10-25 (landing batches, dates of the energy model, the right-stick scheme, the Rail menus, terrain steps 0 to 2, the LAN folder, and every commit whose message names flashing).

Not done: the other 80 or so briefs were not read line by line; no brief's file or line evidence was re-verified; nothing was run in Godot or a browser; the area reviews in `02-areas/` were only searched, not read; no token meter was read, so every figure below is the project's own estimate set against the plan's, not a measurement. I did not criticise the weekly pace itself: 35M of register work a week is more cautious than the EP's own night figure (13M briefed for about 10% of the week, `questions.md:17`), and the path says the record is thin.

**What the total should say instead.** With COS-02, COS-03, COS-05, COS-06, COS-10 and the gate re-runs of COS-01 applied, the base is roughly 265 to 280M before rework, not 201M, and the people steps of COS-04 with the ranges of COS-07 and COS-08 put the candidate at about 12 to 20 weeks, not 9 to 16, before any holiday pause.
