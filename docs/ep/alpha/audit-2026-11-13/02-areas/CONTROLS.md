# CONTROLS: controls and feel (Alpha audit, phase 2)

Baseline 8901d351; game code as in the last played build, 6a30b4c9. Read-only: no game, engine or browser was run. "(uncommitted)" marks a path listed in `baseline-status.txt`. "Main" means the files on disk at the baseline; "the played builds" are `homework/Files/builds/orb-first-play-6a30b4c9-6627cb9d` (solo, browser) and `orb-lan-6a30b4c9` (LAN), read from their own `tree/data`.

## Summary

- **Works:** the base pad scheme (Arena preset, right-stick strikes and beams, signature on RT only) is the default in data; two pads each drive their own fighter; remapping, left-handed layouts and saved settings exist in code and passed their tests in the saved local gate.
- **Does not work:** the owner's two signature inputs cannot be pressed at all (RT switches the right stick off); four pads on one PC are impossible (the input hub has two slots); a pad pulled mid-match neither pauses nor says anything.
- **Not as played:** eleven input switches, and the 3500 flight speed, are on only through the private page's switch table and slider, not in data.
- **Stray beams:** one beam fired by itself in the owner's last match, 33 ticks after the stick was let go.
- **Biggest gap:** X-03 and X-13 cannot be met while the move table has empty and repeated cells and the signatures are not on the inputs the owner named.

## Questions and answers

**Q1. The pad scheme as Orb plays it: default in data, or only on through page switches?**
Both. The base is default in data: the pad preset `arena` is the default (`data/input/layouts.json:5-9`), and `rightStick`, `beamAim`, `quickRead`, `specialsOff`, `signatureRtOnly`, the haptic switches and `stallKeep` are true on main (`data/input/timing.json:41-49`, `:59-60`, `:63`). Eleven more input switches are false on main and true in both played builds: `driverOnRb`, `flightStrikeB`, `chordPick`, `dashHandover`, `flickKept`, `inertKnock`, `chargeLostCue`, `bNoBeam`, `bNoBeamKeepRt`, `hapticStages`, `dodgeHeld` (`data/input/timing.json:50-62`; each build's `tree/data/input/timing.json` `switches` block, read for this audit). They are flipped by rows of the private page's switch table (`tools/preview/switches.mjs:127-204`). So the rule the owner asked for on 2026-11-01, that B never fires a beam, is a page switch (`bNoBeam`), not the data's default. Flipping input switches in data changes the sim's state hash: the earlier flip needed the golden record regenerated (`docs/controls/pending/landing/README.md:16`, uncommitted). F-CONTROLS-05, F-CONTROLS-19.

**Q2. Keyboard: can a keyboard player do everything a pad player can?**
By data, on the solo layout, yes for every action: `kb-solo` binds move, dodge, guard, mode, power, light, heavy, signature, context, the transform chord, and the strike on the four arrow keys (`data/input/layouts.json:246-250` and the bindings below it). A left-handed keyboard preset exists (`:383-387`). Two players on one keyboard get less: the strike is one key plus the movement direction, and the project's own page says "Capability is lower than a pad's" (`docs/controls/right-hand-attacks.md:144`). Whether a keyboard can aim a beam between the four directions: not evidenced. No keyboard play, ledger or probe run on the played build is on record: not evidenced. The keyboard cannot press the owner's signature inputs either, since they do not exist (F-CONTROLS-01). F-CONTROLS-16.

**Q3. Remapping, left-handed layouts, and whether choices persist.**
All three exist in code. Remap: `sim/input/remap.gd` (rebind, swap, remove, checks), per player (`sim/input/input_data.gd:335-342`), saved to `user://input.json` (`sim/input/remap.gd:13`, `sim/input/input_data.gd:372-380`) and read at start-up (`render/core/main.gd:153`). Left-handed: a whole-pad mirror per pad preset and `kb-solo-swapped` (`sim/input/hub.gd:682-699`, `data/input/layouts.json:383-387`); the option is `hand_layout` (`ui/data/options.json:62-63`). Options persist through `user://ui_prefs.json` (`ui/hud/ui_hud.gd:2478`, `:2485`; `ui/core/ui_prefs.gd:7`) and are loaded at start-up except for scripted runs (`render/core/main.gd:212-215`). Tests passed in the saved local gate: `remap_test` 147 checks, `left_hand_test` 27, `hold_toggle_test` 88 (`docs/ep/runs/6a30b4c9/gate-full-s415.txt:105`, `:96`, `:90`). Not evidenced: a restart on Windows desktop or on a Deck with settings kept (X-20); whether the Remap screen is reachable with a pad in the played build's menus (UI's area). F-CONTROLS-15, F-CONTROLS-21.

**Q4. Two pads on one PC; four pads for couch 2v2.**
Two pads: built. The start menu claims the first connected pad for player one and the second for player two (`render/core/main.gd:1350-1361`); a pad's events reach only the slot it drives (`sim/input/hub.gd:266-290`, `:308-316`); `join_test` 53 checks and `hub_test` 60 passed in the saved gate (`gate-full-s415.txt:94`, `:91`). A manual two-pad checklist exists (`docs/controls/local-two-player.md:51-69`); a signed-off run of it by a person: not evidenced. Unplug: no pause and no notice; player two's fighter goes to the computer and player one moves to the keyboard (`sim/input/hub.gd:294-304`; `docs/controls/local-two-player.md:19`). "Player 1 starts on the left": not evidenced (no input test asserts it). Four pads: not possible; the hub keeps two slots (`sim/input/hub.gd:26-29`) and the 2v2 page says so (`docs/controls/two-v-two-input.md:30`). F-CONTROLS-02, F-CONTROLS-06, F-CONTROLS-20.

**Q5. The Steam Deck's built-in controls: do Godot's pad mappings cover them?**
The host reads pads only through Godot's standard button and axis positions (`sim/input/names.gd:7-15`; `render/core/main.gd:1444-1492`), so coverage is the engine's bundled mapping database (`docs/controls/platform-plan.md:29`, a claim). No run on a Deck, or through Steam's pad layer, is on record; the project's Deck page says "Untested" (`docs/tools/steam-deck.md:17`, `:25`). The host has a "deck" glyph family keyed on the pad's name (`render/core/main.gd:1075-1076`), but the Deck page says nothing detects a Deck through Steam's layer (`docs/tools/steam-deck.md:16`). Back buttons and trackpads: not read by the host (not in `sim/input/names.gd:7-12`), and not needed, since every action fits the standard buttons (`data/input/layouts.json:5-143`). F-CONTROLS-10, F-CONTROLS-22.

**Q6. Input latency: is there a measured budget from press to picture? Is it current?**
A budget is written: first visible reaction within 3 ticks, 50 ms before the display (`docs/perf/input-latency-2026-10-22.md:30`). Measured then: guard and dash react on the next tick, 32 to 44 ms to a changed picture at the median; the light attack's body moved 5 ticks after the press, about 130 ms, with the HUD's press mark at about 32 ms (`:16-20`, `:60-64`). It is not current: the record is the browser build, keyboard presses, 22 and 23 October, before the right-stick scheme (pitch dated 2026-10-30 in `data/input/timing.json:66`). No pad press, stick strike, desktop build or Deck was measured. A newer tick-count page exists (`docs/controls/responsiveness.md`, uncommitted: its header gives a stick strike 15 ticks from thumb to landing); it is a doc claim with no saved run found. `t1_latency_test` (62 checks) passed in the saved gate (`gate-full-s415.txt:115`). F-CONTROLS-11, F-CONTROLS-23.

**Q7. The quick-tap step and the getaway (landed 2026-11-09): what is still open?**
Landed in 6a30b4c9 behind switches (`git log -1 6a30b4c9`: "a quick LT tap is a short step that eases in to the rival (slip + arrive + dashHandover)"); `dashHandover` is false on main, true in the played builds (`data/input/timing.json:53`). Open, from the saved matrices: (a) far from the rival the shortest tap still carries 9.9 body heights against the ruled bound of 6.5 (`docs/controls/dash-handover.md:85`; `docs/controls/runs/cc958c18/lt-tap-probe-slip-arrive-matrix-cc958c18.txt`); (b) a tap away from the rival is now a short step by design, so the getaway needs a 12-tick press or a hold (`docs/controls/dash-handover.md:89`; `runs/cc958c18/lt-tap-probe-direction-cc958c18.txt`); whether the owner has felt and accepted that: not evidenced; (c) no measurement against a moving rival (`dash-handover.md:85`, "Not covered"); (d) the Executive Producer's note that docs say `underTicks` 6 where data says 12 (`questions.md:16`, a pointer; data: `data/fight/power.json:113`). F-CONTROLS-12.

**Q8. Flight speed: the default in data, and what changing it would touch.**
The default is 5000: `data/fight/power.json:108` (`"flatOut": 5000.0`, range 3500 to 7000), read by `sim/core/power.gd:66` and `:119`. The model that uses it is off in data on main and in the solo build's own data (`data/fight/power.json:4`); the page and the LAN setup turn it on per match (`tools/preview/switches.mjs:922-932`; `net/lan.gd:30`). The owner's 3500 came from a slider on the private browser page, kept in the browser's storage (`tools/preview/blackbox.mjs:462-474`); the LAN setup sends no flight speed, so LAN plays 5000 (`net/lan.gd:30`). Changing the default touches: the state hash when the model is on (`sim/core/hash.gd:106`), flat-out speed at every level (base times growth per level, `sim/core/power.gd:249`), Simulation's check that pins 3500 and 7000 as the range ends (`sim/core/tools/power_check.gd:97-104`), the dash's closing-time table keyed to absolute speeds (`data/fight/power.json:113`), the trail that starts at speed 3500 (`data/vfx/meteor.json:6`) and the glow at 4500 (`data/fight/power.json:107`), and any scripted player or probe that sets 5000 by hand (`sim/director/tools/dash_probe.gd:28`). 3500 is also the bottom of the allowed range, so "keep tweaking" downward needs the range moved. F-CONTROLS-07.

**Q9. Presses that do nothing with no word or buzz: is there a count?**
Yes, but not for the last played build. The newest saved count is Tools' ledger of the owner's 7 November play replayed on build 1931a0f8: 760 presses, 225 not honoured (30 percent); by press, the "silent" column sums to 97 (dodge 8, signature 27, heavy 40, light 8, stick 8, guard 2, power 4) (`docs/tools/runs/1931a0f8/b1-ledger-1229.txt`). The tool is `tools/feel/ledger.mjs`. A ledger of the two 9 November play files on 6a30b4c9 is not on file (the Executive Producer lists it as owed, `questions.md:16`). An older soak found 56 stick requests become 23 strikes (`docs/controls/controls-audit.md:27`, uncommitted, 1 November tree). The telling exists: pad knock on in data (`data/input/timing.json:47`), reason codes (`sim/input/hub.gd:445`), the director's refused cue off on main and on in the played build (`data/director/interrupts.json:611`). F-CONTROLS-08, F-CONTROLS-24.

## Decisions for Orb

1. **Should 3500 become the default flight speed in every mode (solo, LAN, the Deck), instead of a slider only you have?** Recommendation: yes; put 3500 in data and keep tuning from there.
2. **When a controller drops out mid-match, should the game pause with a plain notice?** Today it hands the fighter to the computer or the keyboard and plays on. Recommendation: yes, pause in every local mode.
3. **Once RT + RB + flick and RT + flick are built, should RT + B stop being a signature?** Recommendation: yes; then no beam can ever start from a face button, which is your rule of 1 November.
4. **For Alpha, is "the keyboard works, a pad is recommended" enough?** Your first answer was "keyboard and gamepad equally". Recommendation: yes for Alpha, since the outside testers play on a Deck; hold full equality for Beta.
