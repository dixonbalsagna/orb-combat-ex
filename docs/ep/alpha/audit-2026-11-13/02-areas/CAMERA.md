# Alpha audit, area 8: CAMERA (camera and split screen)

Read-only review at baseline 8901d351 (game code = the build Orb last played, 6a30b4c9). No Godot or browser run was made; every statement cites a file, a saved record, a commit or a build's own data. "The rig" below means `render/camera/split_rig.gd`, the one script that decides one picture or two, the framing, and the cuts. "(uncommitted)" marks a path listed in `../baseline-status.txt`.

## Summary

- **Works:** the hard-line merge Orb prefers is on main; the 70/30 split against the computer is built and was in both of Orb's builds; every cut in code carries a written reason; the chase check and the split sweep passed on the code Orb played.
- **Does not:** the camera Orb played is switched on by a launch word, not by default; nothing is checked at the Deck's 1280 by 800; no ally picture-in-picture exists; only 1 of 26 camera test files runs in CI; two camera flash safeguards are still parked.
- **Biggest gap:** the jolt that holds terrain step 2 back has no written cause (it doubles the picture's jolt on four rows), so the terrain work and the camera are stuck on each other.

## Questions and answers

**Q1. The hard-line merge Orb prefers; more stylised versions to try.**
The hard line is on main. `eased_merge` is `true` (`render/camera/split_rig.gd:118`): the feather is forced to 0 and the line stays whole until the two pictures are one, then goes (`split_rig.gd:4632-4634`). The cut cross-dissolve is off (`render/camera/split_view.gd:47`). Orb's words: "hard line looks better I think, lets try some more sylized versions perhaps" (`docs/ep/vision.md:1714`).
Stylised versions: **none found.** The only commit touching `render/camera`, `docs/camera` or `data/camera` since 2026-11-08 is the Monday batch (commit 6eec6a75, 2026-11-09), and no page under `docs/camera/` proposes line styles after Orb's remark (F-CAMERA-10). The pages are behind the code: `docs/camera/split-vision.md:22` (uncommitted) still says "Today's merge has a dissolve", and the park that holds the fix (`docs/camera/pending/split-vision/README.md:7`) is already on main (F-CAMERA-11).

**Q2. Picture-in-picture for an ally: what exists?**
Nothing for an ally. What exists near it:
- The panel cut-in: a slanted strip over the live view drawn from an inset viewport, for the pair on screen only (`split_rig.gd:1988-1996` refuses a slot outside 0 and 1; `split_view.gd:344-346`; settings full, still, off at `split_rig.gd:175` and `render/core/main.gd:1115-1118`). Built and reachable (F-CAMERA-21).
- The damage inset (a live window on a building hit by a dodged beam): designed (`docs/camera/damage-inset.md:3`, uncommitted edits), built as a park, **not on main** (`git grep damage_inset 8901d351 -- render sim ui data tools/schemas` returns nothing; `docs/camera/pending/damage-inset/`, uncommitted).
- The wider frame that takes in the other pair of a 2v2 when near: parked and "paused at Orb's word before the look gate" (`docs/camera/pending/others-frame/README.md:1-6`, uncommitted).
- Camera's page argues **against** an ally window: "No corner window by default" (`docs/camera/split-vision.md:68-72`, uncommitted), written 2026-11-01, before Orb's 2026-11-09 words "PIP for cool moments from your ally" and "PIP needs smart implementation to keep track of your ally ... unobtrusive ... minimal impact on framerate" (`docs/ep/vision.md:1715-1716`). The later words stand; the page has not been revised (F-CAMERA-05).
Frame-rate cost of an ally window: not evidenced. The only figure is the panel strip's, one extra world render at 749 by 144 px, 0.45 ms on desktop (`docs/camera/damage-inset.md:10`, a claim in a doc, not a saved record).

**Q3. The camera in LAN and against the computer; the 70/30 split against a computer.**
- Against the computer: built. `split_share` gives the lone human's pane the share (`split_rig.gd:357`, `:895-908`, `:3271-3283`), eases to even during a beam struggle (`:636-638`), and reads `splitShare` from `data/camera/swing.json` only when the tile ground is on (`main.gd:249`). Main's data says 0.0 (`data/camera/swing.json:4`); both of Orb's builds say 0.7 (`homework/Files/builds/orb-first-play-6a30b4c9-6627cb9d/tree/data/camera/swing.json:4`, and the LAN folder's copy is identical). UI's fit switch for the narrow pane is on (`ui/data/features.json:33`). Orb, after playing it: "the bigger screen share against an AI opponent can use some further tuning" (`vision.md:1715`); no tuning since (F-CAMERA-10). The setting that turns the split off against the computer reaches the rig (`main.gd:1105`).
- LAN 2v2: each PC holds its own fight in the sim state, "so to the game it's a 1v1 against the computer: the hub's first slot, its camera, its HUD and its drawing" (`net/lan.gd:7-8`); a PC whose player is out shows the partner's fight after the KO close-up (`net/lan.gd:11-12`). So the 70/30 applies per PC, and the ally is never framed while both play. This matches Orb's report of the first 2v2: "never saw all four fighters on screen together" (`vision.md:1662`). These are code comments and Orb's words; **no saved camera record of a LAN match exists** (`docs/camera/runs/` holds three folders, none for 6a30b4c9).
- LAN 1v1: **not evidenced.** The rig has no input naming this PC's player; it picks the large pane by "exactly one of fighters 0 and 1 has no computer driver" (`split_rig.gd:895-908`). With two humans that rule gives no share, so by the code each PC would show an even split; no record shows the picture (F-CAMERA-06). Camera's open question to Orb, "Your screen is your fight, on two PCs: is that right?" (`docs/camera/split-vision.md:95`, uncommitted), has no recorded answer beyond `vision.md:1715`.

**Q4. Every camera move eased; every instant cut justified in writing. Is the list of cuts current?**
In code, yes; on paper, no.
- Code: `CamParams.CUT_REASONS` lists 13 reasons (`render/camera/camera_params.gd:428-442`); every `_mark_cut` call passes one (`split_rig.gd:671, 685, 1067, 1377, 1771, 2225, 2256, 2294, 2361, 2495, 2702, 4289, 5056, 5288, 5306, 5593`); `launch_shots_check` fails a cut whose reason is empty or unknown (`render/camera/tests/launch_shots_check.gd:103-104`). Two places set the cut flag without a reason: the match's first frame (`split_rig.gd:593`) and a relocation or replay seek (`:719`).
- Paper: the list is `docs/camera/director.md` section 11 (`:293`, table at `:321-334`), dated 2026-10-23, file last committed 2026-10-30 (commit b4b9e599). The reasons `motion_setting` (the player chose cuts in place of swings) and `travel_safety` appear on no page under `docs/camera/` (a search for both keys finds nothing; `docs/camera/swing-view.md:51` describes the setting but not as a listed cut). So the written list is two reasons short (F-CAMERA-08).
- Enforcement: `launch_shots_check` is not in CI and is not named in the last gate record (`docs/ep/runs/6a30b4c9/gate-focused-s419.txt`); whether it passes at the baseline is **not evidenced** (F-CAMERA-07).
- "Every move eased": not evidenced as a whole. The check that measures it in play, the chase check, limits the picture's step and jolt per tick (`chase_rush_check.gd:4-9`) and passed on the landed tree (`gate-focused-s419.txt:98`). Under reduced motion the divider slam is 0.04 s, "a near-cut" (`camera_params.gd:165`), which is an instant move by design for that setting.

**Q5. The jolt at a split that held terrain step 2 back: cause known?**
**No.** What is known: with terrain step 2 ("globe-step2") applied, `chase_rush_check` fails 4 of 130 rows, each "a jolt of 0.137 to 0.153 (tick 96 split)" against a limit of 0.10 (`docs/ep/runs/6a30b4c9/chase-rush-fail-with-globe.log`; `chase_rush_check.gd:5`); a bisect showed step 2 alone fails and the hit-stop change alone passes (`docs/ep/runs/6a30b4c9/README.md:4-5`). The same rows read 0.053 to 0.068 at commit b34e32e1 (`docs/camera/runs/b34e32e1/sunday-energy/chase_rush_rows.txt:2-6`), and their worst tick moved (92 to 142 there, 96 here). No page says why: World's park README does not mention the failure (`docs/world/pending/globe-step2/README.md`, uncommitted), no page under `docs/camera/` or `docs/world/` names it, and the EP's answer lists it as owed by World with "nothing started" (`../questions.md:16`). Note also that the fifth base row passes at 0.095, inside 5% of the limit, in the same log (F-CAMERA-02).

**Q6. Camera at the Deck's 16:10 screen.**
**Not evidenced.** The rig takes the view's width and height each tick and has no fixed aspect; the project sets no window size or stretch mode (`project.godot` has no such key). The peak shot corrects for screen shape against 16:9 (`split_rig.gd:5261`, `:5444`), and one width is commented "at 16:9" (`camera_params.gd:410`). But every camera test fixes 1280 by 720 (`chase_rush_check.gd:75`, `split_sweep.gd:33`, `launch_shots_check.gd:8`, `split_share_check.gd:24`, `cross_ko_check.gd:43`), and no saved record shows a split, a merge or a peak shot at 1280 by 800. UI's paper on the 70/30 split says that at 1280 by 800 a plate "crosses by 70" px into the other pane when the line leans most (`docs/ui/split-unequal-fit.md:13`); the fit switch meant to cure it is on (`ui/data/features.json:33`), with no still to show it (F-CAMERA-04).

- **The intent feed is off (F-CAMERA-14),** so the director's plan does not reach the camera in play, though Orb asked for a camera "that works with the combat choreographer" (`vision.md:1474`).
- **The look gate's reference is old (F-CAMERA-15)** and the known-bugs row for the jolt is stale (F-CAMERA-16).
- **Leftovers and convention (F-CAMERA-12, -13):** rejected dissolve code and the hiding hook remain behind switches; about 500 camera numbers are constants in code.
