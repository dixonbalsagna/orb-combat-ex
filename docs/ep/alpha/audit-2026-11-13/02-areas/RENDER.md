# RENDER: rendering, VFX and look (Alpha audit, baseline 8901d351; game code of build 6a30b4c9)

Read-only review, 2026-11-13. Nothing was run in Godot or a browser. Every line cites a file, a saved record, a commit or a CI listing; "not evidenced" means no file or record settles it. "(uncommitted)" marks a path listed in `baseline-status.txt`. Reading answers RR-07 and RR-08 were used and spot-checked. CI on 8901d351 (run 43554356718) was still running when this was written.

Words used here: the **globe ground** is the newer ground drawn as tiles of a true planet (`render/core/tile_ground.gd`); a **launch word** is a switch passed on the command line or in the page's address (`--tiles`, `?tiles=1`); a **park** is a finished patch waiting under `docs/*/pending/`.

## Summary

- **Works:** each fighter's beam has its own data-driven look; the beam struggle reads from the beams with no marker; the start-up shader compile is spread over frames with a warm-up list; the renderer is Compatibility everywhere and the shaders avoid the features that renderer lacks.
- **Does not:** the picture Orb plays (globe ground, crater look, meteor trail) is not the game's default; it exists only through launch words, and CI never draws it.
- **Not measured:** frame rate on 6a30b4c9; anything at all drawn on Linux or a Steam Deck; the flash reading of the globe ground on 6a30b4c9.
- **Biggest gap:** the last flash reading taken on the globe ground failed in both normal and reduced modes, and the standard clips never use that ground (F-RENDER-01; handled off Orb's page).
- Six look bugs from Orb's plays: two have a cause and a fix, one has a cause and a parked fix, three have no cause and no owner.

**Q2. Re-entry heat trails: what exists?** A built effect, the meteor trail: a ribbon laid along a sent fighter's real path, strength from altitude, speed and tier, cooling over seconds (`render/vfx/meteor.gd:3-7`, `:273-274`; numbers in `data/vfx/meteor.json`). It is off by default (`render/vfx/vfx_look.gd:177`) and on in Orb's builds by a launch word (`docs/tools/runs/6a30b4c9/switch-list.txt:35`). Its colours are the fighter's own energy tints (`render/vfx/meteor.gd:625-629`), with a pale neutral when the fighter's colour is already in the fire range (`render/vfx/vfx_look.gd:57`). Orb has seen it ("as a placeholder its fine") and asked for a friction-and-heat trail in place of the fighters' colours, smoke particles and heat waves round the fighter (`docs/ep/vision.md:1677`, `:1699`). None of those three is built: there is no smoke or heat-wave code in `meteor.gd` or `meteor_view.gd` (grep). A park that makes the burn read launch speed is still waiting (`docs/vfx/pending/power-burn/README.md:1`, uncommitted). The matching flight lines are also off (`vfx_look.gd:174`).

**Q3. Quality levels from performance to maximum.** Decided as a rule (ADR 0012, `docs/decisions/0012-turntable-and-quality-range.md:9-10`), designed on paper as five levels with budgets (`docs/perf/quality-range.md:115-120`), and shown in a research prototype where keys 1 to 5 pick a level (`docs/world/ring-any-angle/quality-range.md:5`). In the game:
- Effects have three levels and an automatic step: down below 42 frames a second for 2 s, up at 57 (`render/vfx/vfx_hub.gd:156-158`, `:227-228`). Animation follows it between two of its four levels (`render/core/main.gd:786-788`).
- The Settings screen lists "Effects quality" (`ui/data/options.json:52`), but no game code reads the choice (F-RENDER-07).
- There is no level picker, no default for the Deck, and the pool caps for the lowest level are off (`render/vfx/vfx_look.gd:178`).
- For Orb's ask to extend the top on their PC (`docs/ep/vision.md:1700`): nothing above today's picture exists in any build. Research notes there is room above its top level (`docs/world/ring-any-angle/quality-range.md:118`).

**Q4. The look gate.** *Is the reference build still the right one?* The page still names d3f1ca0e, the demo of 2026-10-15, for camera and split screen (`docs/ep/taste.md:7`). It predates the globe ground; 66 commits have touched `render/core`, `render/shaders` and `render/vfx` since the gate was adopted (git log since 2026-10-18). The side-by-side tool refuses two clips that are not the same match (`tools/flash/side-by-side.mjs:17-22`), so a same-tick comparison of today's game with that build is not possible. Only Orb can move it (`docs/decisions/0010-look-gate.md:9`). *Is a side-by-side saved for each visible landing?* Not evidenced, and for the last five builds no: their gate records name no side-by-side (`docs/ep/runs/` for 091dfac9, 1931a0f8, 4890bdd9, 6eec6a75, 6a30b4c9; grep), and landings come as batches (commit 3c9005ac: 133 parks). Pictures do exist: `docs/rendering/img/lookgate/` holds 229 files, 214 of them uncommitted, with no index from picture to commit.

**Q5. Steam Deck on the Compatibility renderer.** *Any shader or feature that won't run?* None found by reading: the renderer is Compatibility on all platforms (`project.godot:18-19`); no shader reads the depth texture (grep: 0 files); flat varyings were removed (`render/shaders/building.gdshader:407`); one shader reads the screen and has a fallback (`render/vfx/clash_view.gd:8-9`). The things a first Deck run should watch are the float data textures and texture arrays the globe ground reads (`render/core/tile_field.gd:30-31`). **Not evidenced in practice:** nothing has been drawn on Linux (`docs/tools/steam-deck.md:25`). *Shader compile stutter and the warm-up:* the boot spreads the compile over frames (`render/core/boot.gd:3-14`) and draws a list of effect shaders once (`render/core/warmup.gd:18-38`). The list does not name the clash, meteor or blast-head shaders, and the park that warms first draws in the real pane is unlanded (F-RENDER-14). On record: a cold native start took about 15 s on Orb's PC on 2026-10-27, before the globe ground (`docs/perf/desktop-boot-2026-10-27.md:46`); the browser build's cold shader warm-up was 28.8 s (`docs/perf/page-load-2026-10-31.md:40`). No record on 6a30b4c9.

**Q6. Beams and auras against the taste page.** Beams: each fighter has a flavour in data, the struggle is carried by the beams, and the code holds the "never a white core" rule (F-RENDER-22, 23). Whether they read as "crisp" and not as "soft, blurry blocks" (`docs/ep/taste.md:26`) is a judgment of pictures, which this audit did not open: not evidenced. Orb's latest word on beams is about how they play, not how they look (`docs/ep/vision.md:1677`). Auras: the page asks for three-dimensional, frothing auras unique to each fighter and inverted between the two (`docs/ep/taste.md:15`). Main has one shared aura setting (`data/vfx/aura.json:4-7`); the 3D aura is parked since 2026-10-18 awaiting Orb's look (`docs/vfx/pending/aura3d/README.md:3`). Orb's Alpha definition says current looks are fine, so this is a decision, not a blocker (F-RENDER-15).
