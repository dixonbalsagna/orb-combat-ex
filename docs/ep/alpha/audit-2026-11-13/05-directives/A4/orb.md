# A4: Orb

Leads: none
Supports: none

This is the short list of what only you can do or decide in milestone A4 ("Horde, teams, your own planet, the whole fighter"). Everything else is the team's. Nothing here is built until you have approved the plan.

## Before A4 starts: four questions

Each has one recommendation. A plain "yes to all" is enough.

1. **How complete must each fighter's move table be for Alpha?** (decision 15; row A-COMBAT-04)
   Recommendation: the smaller table. Every button combination does something; the plain martial row and the energy row are complete and different for each fighter; the rows on LB, LT and RT may share moves where the design page says so. The full table (55 moves a fighter) is for Beta. Saying "full table now" adds about a week and moves the Alpha date with it.

2. **How big is the first Horde, and what are the 2v2 line-ups and the planet dials?** (decision 16; rows A-MODES-02, A-MODES-03, A-AI-08, A-WORLD-02, A-CONTROLS-01)
   Recommendation, in four parts:
   - Horde: at most three weak enemies at a time, coming back in waves, for one or two players, with a timer and a result screen. Bigger waves after Alpha.
   - Couch 2v2: two people as a team against two computer fighters. Four pads after Alpha.
   - A computer partner: yes, one line-up (you and a computer partner against two computers), so a 2v2 can be played alone.
   - Custom planet: a seed and three dials (how many cities, the climate, how much water) at today's one planet size. Size choices after Alpha.

3. **What changes when a fighter transforms, and how do you tell two copies of the same fighter apart in a 2v2?** (decision 17; rows A-COMBAT-06, A-ANIM-01, A-ANIM-02)
   Recommendation: one cheap body change per step (hair or accent colour, and each fighter's own pose at the moment of change), with two forms beyond base for each fighter. Bulked-up bodies after Alpha. For two copies: a second body colour per fighter, picked by team; no marker over anyone.

4. **Should the computer opponent use the right-stick strikes?** (row A-AI-06)
   Recommendation: yes, at Medium and Hard, built now so the difficulty levels are tuned once, in A5, with it in.

## During A4: things to look at

5. Look over the short lists the team sends of anything a player will read: the names of the new modes, the names of the forms, the names of the planet dials, the result-screen words. Change any you don't like.
6. Look at the side-by-side pictures the Executive Producer sends for each visible change (each form, the second colours, the four-fighter picture). Say if anything looks worse than the build you compare against.

## When the A4 build arrives: things to play

Allow two to four days for the play and for your reports to be read and sorted.

7. Horde: last eight minutes alone, then again with a second pad.
8. Couch 2v2 with your second player on one PC, and a 2v2 alone with a computer partner.
9. Custom planet: make a dry planet with one city, and check the second PC gets the same planet from the same choices.
10. With each fighter: hold nothing, then LB, RB, RT and LT, and press X, Y, B and A (tap, mash, hold). Write down any that feel the same or do nothing.
11. Reach both forms with both fighters.
12. LAN 2v2 with the trusted second player on your two PCs: five full matches as a team together and five apart. Send both PCs' report folders to the Executive Producer after each session. Say whether you saw all four fighters when the fights were near.

## Good to know

- After A4, the rules of the fight are closed for Alpha. A new idea that changes a rule is written in the ideas log and waits until after Alpha unless you lift it in as a change of scope, with its cost shown.
- If a must-pass line is missed, you will get one question with three choices: wait, ship the milestone short with that line carried forward by name, or cut something.
- Token cost, roughly: about 48M for the milestone at full size, about 10M less if you accept the smaller versions in questions 1 to 3. Two to three weeks.
