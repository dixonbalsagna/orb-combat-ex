# A3: Orb

Leads: none
Supports: none

What only you can do or decide in milestone A3, "The fight's new rules, and LAN inside the game". Nothing here is decided until you say so. Each question has one recommendation.

## Before A3 starts: questions

1. How much of the stamina, energy and body loop is in Alpha, and is "body" a new number? Recommendation: the whole loop as shared rules with each fighter's numbers in data, switched on only after QA has measured how often the fighter who falls behind still wins; reuse the old hidden health number as the deep number behind the body parts, with the core kept as its own most important part.
2. How much of the pull into a close fight (the gravity well) is in Alpha? Recommendation: its size, its zones and the snap into a close fight. The long-range moves you get outside the pull, catching a beam, batting one away and the taunt wait until after Alpha.
3. Up close, should your stick move only your own fighter? Recommendation: look at the short trial clip from A1 first, then answer; until you do, today's rule would be built (your stick moves both fighters).
4. Once signatures are on RT + a flick, is RT held still the charge, or the High Intensity switch? Recommendation: keep the charge for Alpha.
5. Beam stages: in Alpha or after? Recommendation: decide from the beam trial's page; build them only if they fit inside the beam rework.
6. LAN at Alpha: for outside friends, or for you and Orb 2? Recommendation: inside the game behind a Host and a Join row, played only by you two, on Windows; the package your friends get has no LAN row. Offering it wider costs about 6M tokens and one to two weeks.
7. How fast must "the other PC has gone" appear when a cable is pulled? Recommendation: within 10 seconds, keeping today's 8-second rule.
8. On two PCs in a 1v1, should each screen favour its own player, as it does against the computer, or stay an even split? Recommendation: favour the player sitting at that PC.
9. The small marks drawn on a fighter during a dash, and the ring on a striking hand, were approved before your "nothing on the fighters" rule. Keep or remove? Recommendation: watch one short clip of each mark and decide mark by mark.

## During A3: actions

- Play the A3 build on your PC. Hold the right stick to aim a beam; have the other fighter fly off its line at near, middle and far range and see it miss. Throw both signatures: RT + RB + a flick of the right stick, and RT + a flick. Fight with stamina empty and see what a hit does. Get a transformation (the game now tells you the buttons). Knock someone into a building and see whether they still bounce back like a ping-pong ball.
- With Orb 2, on two Windows PCs: one presses Host, the other presses Join and types the address shown. Play five full matches. Play three in a row without closing the game, using rematch and "back to menu". Then pull the network cable and note how long each screen takes to say so. Press the report button on both PCs afterwards.
- Do the firewall step on each PC once, from the written steps.
- Send the A3 update to your two friends with Steam Decks and ask each for the file the five-minute speed test saves.

## If something slips: one question you may be asked

- If the pull into a close fight is not playable halfway through A3: ship A3 with its size and zones only and move the snap to the next milestone? Recommendation: yes, so the beam and the stamina loop are not held up.
- Any other must-pass line that is missed comes to you within a day as one question with three choices: wait, ship the milestone short with that line carried forward by name, or cut something.

## New ideas during A3

- Say them whenever you like. Each goes into the ideas log that day in your words, and within a working day you get one line back: now, at the next milestone, or after Alpha, with its rough cost. Nothing is built from the log until you say yes. A3 is one of the two milestones that can take a rule change, if it fits inside work already open.
