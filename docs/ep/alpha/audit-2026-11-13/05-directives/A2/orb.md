# A2: Orb

Leads: A-LEGAL-07
Supports: A-TOOLS-02, A-PERF-01, A-AUDIO-03, A-LEGAL-05, A-CONTROLS-03, A-ACCESS-02, A-MARKET-02, A-QA-02, A-TOOLS-05

This milestone has no new things to press in the fight. It makes the game safe to hand to a friend, and it puts the game on two real Steam Decks for the first time. Below is what only you can do, then the questions, each with one recommendation. Nothing is built until you approve the plan.

## What only you can do

1. **Set up the report place (A-LEGAL-07).** Make a new private Discord server (not one that already has members or history), with one channel only people with a "tester" role can read, invites by you alone, no public invite link. Name it with "(working title)" or "Alpha testers". Legal's ten points (a short list the Executive Producer will show you) are in `docs/legal/alpha-discord-flags.md`; the first three matter before the first friend gets a build. Tell the Executive Producer in one line when it is done.
2. **Make the itch.io page, in one sitting.** Tools will hand you a checklist. With it open: create the account, make a page that is restricted and key-only, and install itch.io's upload tool. Any password or key stays with you; nobody on the team types or stores it.
3. **Ask two friends who own a Steam Deck.** Each gets a key, a draft guide and a one-page sheet: install, play two matches, run the one-press speed test, post two files. About 30 minutes each. They must be adults, and you keep a private dated note that each has read the tester note and agreed (never in the project folder).
4. **Record one line of approval** for desktop test builds for those named friends. Only after the Executive Producer tells you every safety check is green on that build, not before.
5. **Play the A2 build.** Install it on the old PC from the itch.io test page using only the draft guide. Pull a pad out mid-match (the game should pause and say so). Force a crash from the hidden test row and send the report. Save a report with the pad alone.
6. **Listen for ten minutes** with Audio's sheet beside you and mark what sounds wrong (A-AUDIO-03).
7. **Read the words a tester will meet** before they are used: the tester note, the first-screen notice with the AI line, the guide and the sheet.

## Questions, each with one recommendation

1. May the team make desktop test builds (Windows and Linux) for named testers? **Recommended: yes**, with your one-line approval recorded when the checks are green.
2. Where do reports go? **Recommended: a new private server**, a channel locked to a tester role, and keep the promise to delete a tester's reports if they ask. (A-LEGAL-07)
3. Should a tester's report be one small file they post themselves, with clips left out? **Recommended: yes**, one file under 10 MB saved by one press in every build. (A-TOOLS-05)
4. May a restricted, key-only itch.io page go up under the working title before the paid title search? **Recommended: yes**, if Legal writes that it is unlisted and unpromoted, with one key per named friend. (A-TOOLS-02)
5. The licence line for the papers in the package. **Recommended: all rights reserved through Alpha**, with a short friendly permission page; decide about public code at Beta.
6. When a pad drops out mid-match, should the game pause with a plain notice instead of handing the fighter to the computer? **Recommended: yes**, in every local mode. Keyboard: "works, a pad is recommended". (A-CONTROLS-03)
7. Is a quiet fight with no music acceptable for Alpha, and will you do the ten-minute listening pass? **Recommended: yes to both**; one quiet background bed and the building and menu sounds come later in the plan. (A-AUDIO-03)
8. The Hit pause slider on the Accessibility page does nothing today. Hide it, or make it work? **Recommended: hide it** until the hit freeze is switched on in the game; making it work changes the fight's rules and would wait for the next milestone. (A-ACCESS-02)
9. Where is the progress archive (each milestone's shots and builds) kept safe? **Recommended: a second disk or a private folder you already have**, copied at each milestone. It blocks nothing, but today everything is on one disk. (A-MARKET-02)
10. Will one of the two friends later leave the game running computer against computer for two hours on their Deck and send back the result file? **Recommended: yes, ask one friend**; not needed for this first session. (A-QA-02)
11. After the two Decks report: does "30 frames a second held, 40 as the aim" stand for the Deck? **Recommended: wait for the real reading, then confirm 30 held with 40 as the aim** unless the reading says otherwise. (A-PERF-01)

## What you will get back

A PLAY THIS page for the build; the two friends' sheets and what they showed (did it start, did it draw, how fast, did the Deck's controls and sound work); and a fresh estimate of the Alpha date made from real Deck numbers instead of guesses.

Order: actions 1 and 2 can be done any day and are what the top of this milestone's time range waits on. Action 3 can start now. Action 4 comes last, after the Executive Producer's word. Questions 1 to 5 are needed before this milestone can close; 6 to 8 at its start; 9 to 11 can wait.
