# A5: UI & UX

Leads: A-TEXT-03, A-UI-05, A-UI-08
Supports: A-TEXT-04, A-AUDIO-02, A-RENDER-05, A-RENDER-06

Goal: A newcomer holding a Steam Deck reads only words Orb has approved, is told to press buttons the Deck has, is taught only the marks that are really on screen, can read every screen at the Deck's size, and sees the result panel without it covering their own fighter's corner.

Context:
- Read first: `docs/ep/alpha/audit-2026-11-13/04-path-to-alpha.md` (section A5, exit lines X-22 and X-20; decision 22); `docs/ui/strings.md` (the head and Batch 1); `ui/tools/strings_inventory.py`; `ui/data/howto.json` lines 1 to 9 and 70 to 80; `ui/widgets/ui_howto.gd` lines 176 to 184 and 477 to 483; `ui/widgets/ui_pause.gd` lines 206 to 212; `ui/widgets/ui_rail.gd` lines 40 to 46; `ui/hud/ui_hud.gd` line 771; `ui/data/options.json` line 52; `docs/ep/vision.md` line 951 and lines 1191 to 1198; `docs/decisions/0010-look-gate.md`; `docs/ep/taste.md`.
- A-TEXT-01: no record shows Orb has seen the menu, how-to, hint, notice or settings text. Evidence: `docs/ep/vision.md:951` (forward-facing text is seen by Orb before it is used); `docs/ui/strings.md:3` (1115 strings); `docs/ui/strings.md:11` (the Mark column, empty in every row). - Supporting: A-TEXT-04 (Legal leads; "(working title)" beside the name); A-AUDIO-02 (Audio leads; menu moves have no sound); A-RENDER-05 and A-RENDER-06 (Rendering leads; the "Effects quality" row does nothing and has no Deck default).

You own: `ui/`, `docs/ui/`.
- Rewritten lines are drafted by Narrative and reach you through the EP. The sound a menu move makes is Audio's; the level a setting applies is Rendering's.
- ask under NEEDS FROM EP for a grant: `data/input/layouts.json` (no owner), only if the how-to hint needs a binding the layout does not name.

Acceptance criteria:
1. (A-TEXT-01) A short list of the text a tester meets, about 150 strings (the notice, the start menu, the how-to card, the match-over card, the report panel, the settings a tester is asked to change), is generated by `ui/tools/strings_inventory.py` into `docs/ui/`, in the order a tester meets them, with a Mark column (keep, rewrite, cut). Checked by: the file; the EP hands it to Orb.
2. (A-TEXT-01) Every row has Orb's mark; every "rewrite" has Narrative's line, seen by Orb, applied in `ui/data/`; every "cut" is gone. Checked by: the marked list saved in `docs/ui/` with the date, and a script that fails if any row of the short list is unmarked or if a marked "keep" differs from the data. The three hints already marked are carried over, not asked again.
3. (A-TEXT-03) With a pad and no keyboard, the how-to card's corner hint and its reopen line name the pad's own buttons, drawn from the player's layout as the controls page already does; with a keyboard they name keys. Checked by: a `hud_check` case per device, and a still at 1280 by 800 with a pad.
4. (A-UI-08) "Reading the fight" describes only what is on screen in the build a tester gets: each line about a ring on a body, finishing-move rings, the top box or the planet strip is either filtered out while `minimal_hud` is on or rewritten (Narrative drafts, Orb marks) to say what the fight itself shows. Checked by: a `hud_check` case that fails if a how-to line names a mark the HUD does not draw under the shipped switches.
5. (A-UI-05) At match over the result panel does not cover the corner of the player who is looking at it: for one player it sits off their own plate, portrait and spoken lines; for two players the rule Orb picked is applied (recommendation: keep it off the winner's corner). Decide once, since it moves every Rail screen's frame. Checked by: stills of match over for one player, two players and 2v2 at 1280 by 800 and at 1920 by 1080, saved under `docs/ui/runs/<sha>/`.
6. (exit X-22) The 1280 by 800 sweep built under A-UI-09 runs on the A5 build and finds no clipped or overlapping text on any screen and nothing under 9 px; every string under 12 px is listed with a reason. Checked by: the sweep's output and stills under `docs/ui/runs/<sha>/`. Must pass.
7. (supports A-RENDER-05, A-RENDER-06; exit X-20) The "Effects quality" row offers the levels Rendering built, in plain words Orb has seen, shows the Deck's default on a Deck, and the settings round-trip test from A-UI-10 passes on Windows and Linux with the quality level included. Checked by: the saved test output.
8. (supports A-TEXT-04) "(working title)" shows beside the name on the start menu if Legal's ruling and Orb's answer say so. (supports A-AUDIO-02) Each menu move, choose and back calls the one hook Audio names. Checked by: a still, and a `hud_check` case that counts the calls.
9. (all) Look gate (ADR 0010): every change above that alters the picture ships with a side-by-side against the reference build named in `docs/ep/taste.md` on the day, seen by the EP before commit. `hud_check` passes with the Rail on, and where it compares a changed string word for word the check is updated in the same commit. Results saved under `docs/ui/runs/<sha>/` (CD-22).

Constraints:
- Tokens: about 1.0M (four S rows), an estimate; the supporting items are small and counted in their own rows.
- State hash: no change. The UI reads the sim and never writes it; a "getter" that resizes a sim field counts as writing.
- Machine: headless Godot only; `hud_check` in a clean scratch copy; at most 3 Godot processes; stills only through Tools' launcher (`tools/machine/capture.mjs`), one capture at a time, never your own browser; check nothing you started is still running before you end a turn.
- Determinism: every word is data in `ui/data/`; a change to a data file's shape updates its schema in `tools/schemas/` in the same commit.
- Originality: no borrowed phrases, button art or menu layouts; the neutral glyph style stays.
- Orb's rules: nothing drawn on, over or between the fighters; no dissolves, fades or slow wipes on any panel; no debug or key-help text in a tester's build; plain words (what you press, what you see); forward-facing text is seen by Orb before use; the AI disclosure stays on the first screen; English only at Alpha, and the guide says so.

Return: the standard report format (SUMMARY, CHANGES, DECISIONS, NEEDS FROM EP, RISKS, NEXT).

Order: Starts when the menus stop changing at the end of A4 (the Rail default A-UI-04, the mode list A-MODES-01, fighter select A-MODES-06 and the sweep tool A-UI-09 landed in A1; A-UI-10's round-trip test in A2). Item 1 goes first because Orb's reading time is the longest wait in the brief. Item 7 waits on Rendering's levels. The tester guide's screenshots, the A5 rehearsal and A6's X-19 wait on items 2 to 6.
