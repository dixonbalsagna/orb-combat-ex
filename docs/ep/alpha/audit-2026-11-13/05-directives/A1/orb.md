## Questions to answer first (they hold up everything else)

1. **May the team make the build you played on 9 November the normal game, piece by piece, before any new feature work?** Today that game only exists as a special browser copy. Recommendation: yes. (A-CODE-01)
2. **The default world: one fixed planet, or a new random planet each match?** Recommendation: a new random planet each quick match, with its seed number shown and saved in reports so a problem can be found again; one fixed planet for a newcomer's first match and for tests; the current planet kept as a preset. (A-WORLD-14)
3. **Do the round ground, the crater look with the black fill removed, and the meteor trail become the normal look? And is the terrain workshop of 9 November part of Alpha?** Recommendation: yes to the three looks. The workshop waits until after Alpha; Alpha gets craters and trenches drawn where you stand.
4. **Which build do new looks get compared against?** Recommendation: the round-planet build of 9 November, with its known picture faults listed so they are not treated as "the look to keep". (A-RENDER-11)
5. **Flight speed 3500 as the normal speed in every mode, with the slider removed until there is a real setting?** Recommendation: yes.
6. **The old hero and villain meters: off, folded in, or retired?** Recommendation: keep the comeback as a rule every fighter has, set every other effect to zero now, remove the leftover code after Alpha.
7. **The licence line.** Do you agree to keep everything all rights reserved through Alpha, with a short friendly page saying what a tester may do, and decide about public code at Beta? Recommendation: yes. (A-LEGAL-10)

9. **How much of the pull into a close fight is in Alpha, and does your stick move only you up close?** Recommendation: its size, its zones and the snap into a close fight. Answer the stick question after you have seen the trial clip. RT held stays the charge for Alpha.
10. **Beam stages and the other "workshop this" items: before or after Alpha?** Recommendation: decide beam stages from the beam trial, and build them only if they fit inside the beam rework. After Alpha: the terrain overhaul, High Intensity, the taunt, walking into a beam, the sound toolset.
11. **How full must each fighter's move table be for Alpha?** Recommendation: no button press that does nothing; the martial and energy rows complete and distinct for each fighter; the other rows may share moves where the design says so. The full table at Beta.
