The Driver and mines on a big ring

    Try it with a pad (press any button first so the browser sees it). Left stick flies. LT flat out. RB + B: tap for the shot, hold for the Driver. LB + RB + Y: a blast mine; hold to throw it out along the stick. LB + RB + X: a spark mine. The small button left of centre on the pad (two squares) switches the Driver and the old beam. D-pad up and down change tier, left and right change fighter. The small button right of centre (three lines) restarts. Keyboard: WASD fly, Shift flat out, E RB, Q LB, L B, K Y, J X.
