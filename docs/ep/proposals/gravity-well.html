The gravity well

Your stand-in's design packet, 2026-11-08, for Orb and the EP. Design only: nothing is built, every number is a first value for Game Design, and the speed tables model the code's formulas (not a Godot run). References are to main unless a build is named. Revised after three checks (code and rulings, feasibility, Orb's ask): what they changed is in section 8.

1. Orb's words, and the short version

"overhaul the magnetic combat: by this I mean there were some instances during the fights where the LT high-speed didn't quite feel fast enough but sped up when we were further apart, and sometimes engaging in close-up fights it felt like we weren't in control of our fighter. make sure the combat choreography system is adaptive to what the player is trying to do with their controller. I have an image in my head of a 'gravity well' where when fighters are in each others 'gravity well' they are drawn together and attacks tend to snap them towards each other, when fighters are outside the influence of another gravity well they have long range attacks, charge attacks, taunts." (Orb, 2026-11-08 21:52, docs/ep/vision.md)
In plain words: what you press and what you see.
  • Every fighter carries a gravity well. It's bigger when he's heavier (transformed), at a higher power tier, or holding a charge.
  • Inside his well, any blow you throw snaps you in to him. You both drift together a little, gently: never more than a quarter of your flying speed, and he's only drawn to meet your snap if his stick isn't pulling away. Right up close it's the brawl, which keeps its own rules.
  • Outside his well, you get the far kit: taunts, charges, energy bolts, beams (RB + the right stick), and the long strike (hold LT flat out at him, then B or a flick). A taunt is a challenge: if he answers with a press, you both rush in and meet.
  • Hold LT and you move on the next tick, at the same speed at any distance: full speed by tick 12, easing only as you arrive (Game Design's ruling (c)). A faster LT inside a well is an option, off until Game Design rules on it and you try it.
  • Your left stick always moves your fighter, and your right hand always throws a blow. In a brawl the stick moves the brawl as you ruled on 10-06, and the director's pulls never beat it. The director picks how each blow looks and bends it toward what your sticks are saying. It never takes your fighter unless you pressed for a set piece.

What it looks like: hold LT at him from across the city at full speed, and one press snaps you onto him. Once he transforms his well reaches further: you're inside it sooner and your taps snap in from further off, while he has his taunts, charges, bolts and the long strike across that range.

2. The two problems, traced to the code

Both come from the code doing what it was written to do. The maps read main and the switch list of build 1931a0f8, which Orb played with the kick, flight strikes and the energy model on. On main's data all three are off; the LAN setup turns the energy model on (net/lan.gd:24-27, "power": true).

Problem 1: "LT high-speed wasn't fast enough, but sped up further apart"

  1. The traversal ramp, the main cause. Held LT is the dash, about 1,032 u/s at base. Its boost starts only past 1,500 u (20 bh) and is full at 12,000 u, so everything from contact to 20 bh runs at melee-dash speed, slower than the director's own lunge (4,000). sim/core/fighter.gd:622-631; the constants are code, not data (sim/core/constants.gd:16-19).
  2. keepTop rewards starting far. It keeps the most boost reached, drops it at 1,500 u, and the lag pulls him back to 1,032 just as he arrives (sim/core/kick.gd:163-176).
  3. The handover. The dodge's ×1.25 ends on tick 12, the tick the sprint starts (act.gd:21, 82), so high speed begins 20% slower. With the kick off (main), the response reaches 90% only after 19 ticks (fighter.gd:733). In 1931a0f8 the kick was on: from below half speed it starts at 80% of top speed on tick 1 and reaches it by tick 9 (SimKick.move; kick.json instant 0.8, climbTicks 8). But inside 20 bh that top speed is the 1,032 melee dash, so it starts quickly at a slow speed.
  4. Flight strikes take a fixed time. Travel is max(4, ceil(gap ÷ 3 bh)) (sim/director/zip.gd:516-518): 4 ticks for any gap up to 12 bh. With the tell (6, 10 or 14 ticks for X, Y and B; interrupts.json) press to contact is 10 to 18 ticks at any gap. So X averages about 1,000 u/s from 3 bh and 5,050 from 12 bh, and the travel alone runs 2,505 to 12,630 u/s. The tell zeroes velocity first (zip.gd:499): a fighter sprinting at 5,000 stops dead. Ruling (c) leaves flight strikes untouched, so this packet does too and puts the question to Game Design (section 7).
  5. A dead zone from 12.5 to 20 bh: no flight strike (it ends at the middle band's edge, zip.gd:256-258), no boost, and a press there is the far band's taunt or charge (DirBands.farOn, bands.taunt).
  6. Vertical gaps get no boost (fighter.gd:626), and every rush ends at zero velocity (fighter.gd:335).

The result (the speed lane's model of 1931a0f8): closing to 3 bh takes 0.70 s from 1,000 u (1,110 u/s average) and 2.72 s from 12,000 u (4,330 u/s). From far away he closes four times faster.

Problem 2: "Close up, we weren't in control of our fighter"

  1. The stick moves the pair, not you. In a brawl it moves the pair's centre at 40% of flight speed (24% guarding), carrying the rival along (sim/director/brawl.gd:2865-2992, nudgeMul at :2896). That is Orb's own rule of 10-06 (docs/design/brawl-second-pass.md §1: "nudge the center of attraction"). On top of it, velocities are zeroed at the start (:311), the arrival's carry runs 20 ticks (:296-307, ruled so nobody stops dead), and the step-in and magnet place you (:2666, :3899-3925). You move alone only while he winds a slow blow, as ruled (giving ground, :2517-2545).
  2. You can't leave alone, by Orb's rule. A walk-out needs both sticks apart for 12 ticks (:3029, :3865). Orb: "if both players move their sticks in the opposite direction they should be able to walk out"; Game Design then withdrew its one-sided walk-out: "To leave alone he pays" (the escape, a dodge, a zip away or a burst; brawl-second-pass.md §1). Orb's Sunday play: 27 brawls, 0 walk-outs, 11 flight-strike exits and 14 dodge-cancels at 15 ki (docs/qa/runs/1931a0f8/slot0-cue-summary-orb-play-20261108-1726.txt). Whether one player may now leave alone is Orb's call (section 7, question 2), not this packet's.
  3. Set pieces take the body. A close press against a rival in stance 2 or 3, rushing, not free or giving an opening becomes a locked exchange (brawl.gd:267-272; exchange.gd:179-180, 363-364). "Locked" topped Orb's 11-07 ledger at 47 (docs/tools/runs/1931a0f8/b1-ledger-1229.txt).
  4. Side swaps the defender didn't pick. The meteor flip is ruled out (11-08 (a)). The held "away" flick carrying him past you is the ruled "past you, the sides swap" (docs/design/stick-lands.md; stick.gd:388), the attacker's choice, so it stays.
  5. Presses may wait. In 1931a0f8 a press behind a busy line waited for the blow in hand (pressWait on; brawl.gd:3834-3846). Its 600 ticks are "only a backstop", and a stun or the brawl's end clears it. Sunday's 189 "held" acks against about 200 blows count presses that waited, not how long they waited. A kept flick fires up to 90 ticks later (stick.gd:536-546), as ruled (gauntlet-plan.md §5, lever 1). Increment 0 measures the waits before anyone changes them.
  6. The flight strike drives the body for 30 to 50 ticks, and the take-back undoes the slip without a word (zip.gd:372-381).

The gauntlet: Orb's-scheme arm wins 5% on easy and takes 2.6 blows per blow landed (docs/tools/gauntlet-build1.md). The "seconds without control" meter (47 to 58 a minute) counts brawl time as locked (tools/feel/feel.gd:209); increment 0 splits it.

What already points the right way: the bands (close to 3 bh, mid to 12.5; sim/director/bands.gd:60-66) already split near from far. But they're three hard steps by distance alone, blind to weight and power. The well makes them a field.

3. The gravity well

How big a well is

The radius grows with what makes a fighter hit harder in the physics packet's model (§9):

radius = 12.5 bh × √(heft ÷ 100) × (1 + 0.1 × (tier − 1)) × (1 + 0.25 × charge held)    capped 4.5 to 20 bh
  • 12.5 bh at base is today's mid band edge, where the lunge already reaches. The 20 bh cap is today's BOOST_NEAR, closing the dead zone.
  • Heft is the physics packet's weight, 100 at base and rising with each form. It isn't on main, so until it lands the well reads 100 plus a per-form step.
  • Charge held runs 0 to 1 from the strike stages, B's hold and a beam's gather.

The zones

Each fighter asks one thing: am I inside his well? If yes, my blows snap. If not, I have the far kit, as Orb's picture has it. Wells differ in size, so the two can be in different zones. The zone is worked out once a tick for every fighter at once, and kept with hysteresis as the band is, so it doesn't flicker on an edge. A press uses the zone held on its tick.

Under ruling (c) the speed on each tick after the press is the same at every distance until the arrival's ease. Average closing speed from 12,000 is still 1.55 times that from 1,000 (today about 3.9 times), because a short dash spends more of itself starting from rest. Not a Godot run: QA's rows confirm it.

Flight strikes stay as ruled. Ruling (c) leaves them untouched and playable-build.md §1 sets their travel (the tell, then at least 4 ticks and at most 3 bh a tick). That keeps a gap Orb may still feel: LT + X takes 10 ticks from 3 bh or from 12 bh, so it looks about five times faster from far. Closing it means shortening the tell up close or counting its braking toward the travel. Either changes a ruled piece and B's counter window ("arrives 10 to 19 ticks after its press", playable-build.md), so it's Game Design's call, with QA's rows from 3 and 6 bh.

Control inside the well: four rules

1. The left stick moves you. Outside the brawl, your own body, always, except in hit-stop, reels, knock-backs and launches. In the brawl it moves the brawl's centre, as Orb ruled on 10-06, and moves you alone while he winds up or charges. Up and down change your height.
2. Pulls never beat the stick. No draw, magnet, carry or snap moves you against a held stick by more than a quarter of your flight speed (about 107 u/s at base). Leaving a brawl alone stays paid, as ruled: the escape, a dodge, a zip away or a burst. A free lone walk-out is Orb's call (question 2).
3. The right hand strikes. A press is a blow on your line's next free tick, or refused with a word ("each press is a blow"). Held presses keep their ruled waits (gauntlet-plan.md §5). Increment 0 measures how long they wait before anyone proposes a cap, and no cap may drop a press queued behind your own B's 28-tick wind-up.
4. The director bends, it never takes. It picks the piece and bends delivery up to 20° toward your sticks. It moves your body only in a set piece you chose: a full hold's send, a flight strike, a finisher, a grapple you started, a beam struggle you entered. Sides change only as ruled: the away flick's "past you" and a send's direction. Never a flipped launch (ruling (a)).

5. Adaptive choreography: reading what the player is trying to do

Today a template is chosen by attack kind and the defender's stance alone (sim/director/data.gd:128-176), and the stick picks only a cosmetic piece name (recipe.gd:3-8). The well adds an intent read from both your hands and the distance. Each tick it names one intent, and logs the reason to the debug feed whenever it changes.

  • It turns when you turn. Pushing one way clears the other side's count at once, so a player who swings from away to toward reads "press in" by tick 4, not after the old count drains.
  • Both hands count, each for its own job. The right stick names the blow: a flick's direction and hold stage pick its family, as stick-lands.md rules. The left stick moves the body and bends delivery only within that blow's side. When the two disagree, each keeps its job. RB's beam aim stays the beam's own; the read doesn't bend it.

It may

  • Choose which blow of the press's cell you see (the composer), reading intent, terrain and the well.
  • Bend a snap or launch up to 20° toward the sticks' intent or a dramatic target, within the blow's side.
  • Set the snap's reach by intent, within the cell's timing. The attacker covers the gap.
  • Schedule beats, windows and the held moment, as today.

It may never

  • Change what a press is worth: a variant changes delivery only.
  • Move a fighter against his held stick past the pull cap, or take his body outside physics and the set pieces he chose.
  • Flip a launch's side (ruling (a)), add clout, or fire a beam from B (ruling (b)).
  • Delay a press past its rule, or drop one without a word.
  • Pull civilians. Wells act on fighters only.

6. Pseudocode

GDScript-shaped, float64 throughout (no Vector2: it's float32). Names not on main are marked (new). The well draws no randomness; the composer keeps its keyed draws. New state lives in its own object, f.wel, made and hashed only while the well's switch is on, as the kick's f.kik is (hash.gd:195-204). Nothing joins the director's always-hashed ints, so a match with the well off hashes as today. One function writes it, once a live tick; every other function is a pure read.

# ---- sim/core/well.gd (new): class_name SimWell (new). No randomness. Off unless S.wellOn (new: data/fight/well.json "enabled", off).
## State: f.wel, a WellState (new), made only while S.wellOn and hashed only then, like f.kik.
## One writer: SimWell.tick(S), once a live tick before the fighters step, for every fighter at once (as DirBands._bandTick).
## Everything else here is a pure read, so the view (meteor.gd:306), the AI (ai.gd:363) and swing.gd:952 may call it freely.
enum Zone { BRAWL, BOTH, DRAWN, HOLDER, EDGE, OUT }
const HASHED: Array = ["zone", "of", "r", "prevGap", "open", "toward", "away", "round", "presses", "intent"]

static func radius(S: SimState, f) -> float:                     # pure
	var c: Dictionary = cfg()                                   # data/fight/well.json (new) + schema
	var x: float = f.heft / 100.0                               # (new) physics packet; until then 100 + c.formHeft * steps
	var hs: float = sqrt(x) if float(c.heftExp) == 0.5 else SimDetMath.pow(x, float(c.heftExp))   # IEEE sqrt rounds exactly
	var r: float = float(c.wellBh) * DirInterrupt.BH * hs       # 12.5
	r *= 1.0 + float(c.tierStep) * (f.tier - 1.0)               # 0.1
	r *= 1.0 + float(c.chargeGrow) * chargeShare(S, f)          # (new) 0..1; chargeGrow 0.25
	return SimMathx.jclamp(r, float(c.minBh) * DirInterrupt.BH, float(c.maxBh) * DirInterrupt.BH)   # 4.5 .. 20

static func _in(d: float, r: float, h: float, was: bool) -> bool:
	return d <= r + (h if was else -h)                          # hysteresis, as DirBands._bandAt

static func _zoneAt(d: float, rf: float, ro: float, h: float, was: int) -> int:
	if _in(d, float(DirBrawl.cfg().breakBh) * DirInterrupt.BH, h, was == Zone.BRAWL):
		return Zone.BRAWL
	if _in(d, ro, h, was == Zone.BOTH or was == Zone.DRAWN):    # inside his well
		return Zone.BOTH if _in(d, rf, h, was == Zone.BOTH) else Zone.DRAWN
	if _in(d, rf, h, was == Zone.HOLDER):                       # he's in mine, I'm not in his: the far kit and the long strike
		return Zone.HOLDER
	if _in(d, ro + float(cfg().edgeBh) * DirInterrupt.BH, h, was == Zone.EDGE):   # 3
		return Zone.EDGE
	return Zone.OUT

## The one writer. Reads every fighter as the tick began, then writes them all, so slot order changes nothing.
static func tick(S: SimState) -> void:
	if not S.wellOn:
		return
	var h: float = float(cfg().hysteresisBh) * DirInterrupt.BH * 0.5
	var n: int = S.fighters.size()
	var rs := PackedFloat64Array(); rs.resize(n)
	var zs := PackedInt32Array(); zs.resize(n)
	var js := PackedInt32Array(); js.resize(n)
	for i in n:
		rs[i] = radius(S, S.fighters[i])                        # each radius once a tick
	for i in n:
		var f = S.fighters[i]
		var j: int = _deepest(S, i, rs)                         # (new) the rival whose well he's deepest in, by a loop over S.fighters
		js[i] = j                                               #   (never SimRoster.opp: 2v2 has its place, and the ratchet holds)
		if j < 0:
			zs[i] = Zone.OUT
		else:
			var was: int = f.wel.zone if f.wel.of == j else -1  # hysteresis per pair: a new rival starts fresh
			zs[i] = _zoneAt(DirBands.dist(f, S.fighters[j]), rs[i], rs[j], h, was)
	for i in n:
		var f = S.fighters[i]
		var w = f.wel
		w.zone = zs[i]
		w.of = js[i]
		w.r = roundi(rs[i])
		var g: int = roundi(DirBands.dist(f, S.fighters[js[i]])) if js[i] >= 0 else 0
		w.open = _leak(w.open, g > w.prevGap)                   # the gap's trend: one int, not a ring
		w.prevGap = g
		DirIntent.step(S, f)                                    # reads only his own pad, his own f.wel and positions

static func zone(f) -> int:                                     # pure
	return f.wel.zone if f.wel != null else Zone.OUT

## The draw: added to the free branch's steering target in SimFighter.stepFighter. Pure. Every rival whose well
## holds him adds to it, and the sum is capped once (Rule 2): two rivals hold tighter, and the stick still wins.
static func drawTarget(S: SimState, f) -> PackedFloat64Array:
	var out := PackedFloat64Array([0.0, 0.0])
	if f.wel == null or f.rush != null or f.wel.zone == Zone.BRAWL:
		return out                                               # the brawl keeps its own rules
	for g in S.fighters:
		if g == f or _mates(S, f, g):                           # (new) teammates don't draw each other (first value)
			continue
		var d: float = maxf(1.0, DirBands.dist(f, g))
		var r: float = float(g.wel.r)
		if d > r:
			continue
		var depth: float = SimMathx.jclamp(2.0 * (1.0 - d / r), 0.0, 1.0)   # full by half his radius
		var mass: float = 2.0 * g.heft / (g.heft + f.heft)      # (new heft) 1 when equal; a heavier rival pulls harder
		out[0] += SimWrap.sdx(f.x, g.x) / d * depth * mass
		out[1] += (g.y - f.y) / d * depth * mass
	var m: float = SimDetMath.hypot(out[0], out[1])
	if m <= 0.0:
		return out
	var cap: float = float(cfg().drawCap) * SimFighter.flightSpeed(S, f, false)   # 0.25: about 107 u/s at base
	if _awayHeld(S, f):                                         # (new) his stick held away this tick, from his pad, no counter
		cap *= 0.5
	var s: float = cap * minf(1.0, m) / m
	out[0] *= s
	out[1] *= s
	return out

# ---- sim/core/fighter.gd: LT by ruling (c) (increment 1; needs no well). Pure, like the flightSpeed it sits in.
## Replaces the ramp in flightSpeed's dash branch (fighter.gd:622-631). sp is today's speed after his tier, legs, stance
## and hold, times 2.4. With the energy model off the branch stays as today (the LAN runs it on: net/lan.gd SETUP).
static func dashTop(S: SimState, f, sp: float) -> float:
	var top: float = SimPower.flatSpeed(S, f, sp, 1.0)          # ramp 1 at any range: far by his level times close / plain,
	                                                            #   so guard and battered legs still slow it (power.gd:182-185)
	if S.wellOn and bool(SimWell.cfg().ltOption.enabled):       # THE OPTION, off: amends ruling (c); Game Design rules, Orb tries
		var z: int = SimWell.zone(f)
		if (z == SimWell.Zone.BOTH or z == SimWell.Zone.DRAWN or z == SimWell.Zone.EDGE) and SimWell.toward(S, f):   # (new)
			top *= 1.0 + (float(SimWell.cfg().ltOption.mul) - 1.0) * SimWell.edgeShare(S, f)   # 1.35; 0..1 across the edge band
	return minf(top, 3.0 * DirInterrupt.BH / SimConst.DT)      # RL-076: 3 bh a tick. The sea's 0.55 follows, as today

## The held dash's response, in place of free flight's 11% a tick (with the kick on, its instant and climbTicks give this
## shape). A rate from his own velocity, which is hashed already: no new state, no f.kik, nothing kept from an earlier
## dash. Returns his speed along the stick; the caller sets vx = mx * v and vy = my * v * 0.85.
static func dashRise(S: SimState, f, i: SimIntent, top: float, lag: float) -> float:
	var d: Dictionary = SimPower.cfg().dash                     # power.json "dash" (ruled 11-08, not in data yet)
	var first: float = float(d.firstTick)                       # 1000
	var full: int = int(d.fullByTicks)                          # 12
	if S.wellOn and bool(SimWell.cfg().ltOption.enabled) and SimWell.zone(f) <= SimWell.Zone.DRAWN:
		full = int(SimWell.cfg().ltOption.fullTicks)            # 6, the option: only the slope changes, so no step mid-dash
	var n: float = SimDetMath.hypot(i.mx, i.my)
	var cur: float = (f.vx * i.mx + f.vy * i.my) / n if n > 0.0 else 0.0   # his speed along the stick: a reversal starts from first
	if cur > top:
		return cur + (top - cur) * lag                          # above top (the option's well left behind): eases down, never a step
	return minf(top, maxf(first, cur + (top - first) / float(full - 1)))   # 1000 on tick 1, top by tick 12

# ---- sim/director/snap.gd (new): class_name DirSnap (new). A blow pressed inside his well or the edge band, outside the brawl.
## Worth and the cell's timing untouched, and the attacker covers the gap. The brawl is left out: its own step-in (2 ticks
## a body height, at most 4; it moves only the attacker) and its contact ticks (a light: 2) stand.
static func plan(S: SimState, A, D) -> Dictionary:             # a read; the caller applies it on the press's tick
	var z: int = SimWell.zone(A)
	if z != SimWell.Zone.BOTH and z != SimWell.Zone.DRAWN and z != SimWell.Zone.EDGE:
		return {}                                               # the brawl keeps its own; the holder and outside have the far kit
	var c: Dictionary = SimWell.cfg()
	var gap: float = maxf(0.0, DirBands.dist(A, D) - float(DirData.contact().get("offset", 58.0)))   # old profiles have no contact block
	var speed: float = SimFighter.flightSpeed(S, A, true)       # flat out for his level (increment 1), at most 3 bh a tick
	var ticks: int = maxi(int(c.minTicks), ceili(gap / (speed * SimConst.DT)))    # RL-076: 4
	var his: float = 0.0                                        # his share: only inside both wells, only within Rule 2's cap,
	if z == SimWell.Zone.BOTH and not D.input.guard and SimWell.along(S, D, A) > -0.5:   # (new) read from his stick this tick
		his = minf(gap * 0.5, float(c.drawCap) * SimFighter.flightSpeed(S, D, false) * float(ticks) * SimConst.DT)
	var mine: float = gap - his                                 # held away or guarding: none, and the attacker flies it all
	var bend: float = DirIntent.bendDeg(S, A, float(c.bendMaxDeg))   # at most 20, toward his sticks, inside the blow's side
	SimEvents.feed(S, A.name + " SNAP", SimMathx.jstr(SimMathx.jround(mine / DirInterrupt.BH)) + " bh in " + str(ticks)
		+ " ticks; " + D.name + " drawn " + SimMathx.jstr(SimMathx.jround(his / DirInterrupt.BH)) + " bh")
	return {"mine": mine, "his": his, "ticks": ticks, "bend": bend}

# ---- sim/director/intent.gd (new): class_name DirIntent (new). Both hands, once a live tick, from SimWell.tick's write pass.
enum { HOLD, PRESS, CIRCLE, GIVE, LEAVE, CHASE, STANDOFF }
const NAMES := ["hold", "press in", "circle", "give ground", "leave", "chase", "stand off"]

static func step(S: SimState, f) -> void:
	var i: SimIntent = f.input
	var w = f.wel
	var o = S.fighters[w.of] if w.of >= 0 else null
	var side: int = 0 if o == null else (1 if SimWrap.sdx(f.x, o.x) >= 0.0 else -1)
	var along: int = roundi(i.mx * 127.0) * side               # SimIntent.mx is -1..1 (intent.gd:21): + toward him, - away
	var up: int = absi(roundi(i.my * 127.0))
	if along > 64:                                              # a reversal clears the other side at once:
		w.toward = mini(64, w.toward + 4); w.away = 0           #   away to toward reads PRESS by tick 4
	elif along < -64:
		w.away = mini(64, w.away + 4); w.toward = 0
	else:
		w.toward = maxi(0, w.toward - 1); w.away = maxi(0, w.away - 1)
	w.round = _leak(w.round, up > 64 and absi(along) < 48)      # +4 when true, -1 when not, held 0..64
	w.presses = _leak(w.presses, i.light or i.heavy or i.sig or i.rsStart)   # the face buttons and the right stick's strike
	var it: int = HOLD
	if w.away >= 48 or (i.dash and along < -64):
		it = LEAVE                                              # 12 ticks of away (partTicks), or LT + away
	elif w.away >= 16:
		it = GIVE
	elif i.guard:
		it = HOLD                                               # LB: holding ground behind a guard (no snap share on him)
	elif w.toward >= 16 and w.open > 0:
		it = CHASE
	elif w.toward >= 16 or w.presses >= 8:
		it = PRESS
	elif w.round >= 16:
		it = CIRCLE
	elif w.zone >= SimWell.Zone.HOLDER:
		it = STANDOFF
	# The right stick names the blow: a flick's rsDir and its stage (rsLevel) pick the family, as stick-lands rules.
	# The left stick moves the body and bends delivery only within that blow's side. When they disagree, each keeps its job.
	if it != w.intent:
		SimEvents.feed(S, f.name + " INTENT", NAMES[it] + " (" + _why(w) + ")")   # every decision explainable
	w.intent = it
