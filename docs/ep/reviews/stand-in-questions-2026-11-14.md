## What that leaves

- **Question 4, and the second part of question 2, are still open.** "Recommendation on all" cannot settle a question that carried none, and the stand-in cannot supply one after the fact and call it answered.
- If the EP wants to put them back to Orb with a recommendation, these are the stand-in's, offered now and not yet seen by Orb:
  - **Beam speed:** wait for the trial's clip, as the draft intended. Build with Game Design's first speeds, and ask Orb under the clip. I think Orb would want to see it before choosing.
  - **A1 and the sizes:** count A1's line as met when all five play and can be picked, and small, medium and big are drawn and measured. Tiny and huge follow as soon as they are drawn well, with their open problems written down. I think Orb would want the sprint to close on what is solid, since Orb's own words tie sizes to seeing "how the impact to framerates work".
- **On question 1,** the trial was designed to build both versions of the close fight so Orb could feel them. Orb's slight lean toward the magnetic feel is worth keeping in view: the new version still holds a fight together (attacks close distance, and the magnet works while both sticks are at rest), and the trial's clips should show that. I would still build both for the trial, with the new one as the default.
