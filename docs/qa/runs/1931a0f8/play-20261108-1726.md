## 1. "The first meteor launch knocked the opponent in the opposite direction of the punch": confirmed, and it is the rule

Slot 0's first meteor is the launch at tick 2044 (34.1 s): a full-charge B (held ticks 1992 to 2037, released) with **the left stick at rest**. The sent body stood to the right of Grist, Grist faced right, and the meteor went **left and up** (set direction ux -0.71, uy 0.71; the body moved 1,911 units left and 3,171 up in 26 ticks).

Cause, in `sim/director/arc.gd` `heavyFull` (lines 195 to 200): the side starts as the way of the punch; with the stick not choosing a side (`aim` not 2 or 4) it is flipped to the other side when `landingScore` says that landing is better. At that tick (probe, tick 2043): going with the punch scored **-8.0**, against it **+11.0** (the landing the punch's way is water or empty, the other way has ground and buildings), so the director sent him backwards. Every other arc launch in the match went the punch's way (ticks 6122 and 8995: sent right and left of the sender, along the punch). So with the stick at rest a meteor goes where the director likes the landing, not where the punch pointed. Orb's ask (the arc should go the way the knock was intended) means the flip should go, or the stick should be the only thing that picks a side. That is Game Design's and Encounter's.

## 2. "The trail arc doesn't match the arc the fighter flew; the trail is too long"

The meteor is an authored curve and not physics (header of `arc.gd`: "one authored curve, not physics"; Orb asks for physics). The sim's own landing prediction (`DirArc.landing`) is close to what happens: at the end of each curve the body was 626, 482 and 482 units from the predicted x (still 1,700 to 2,100 units above the ground, from where it falls as an ordinary body). So a drawn trail that disagrees with the flight is not the sim's prediction being wrong by that much. The length is real: the first meteor travels 25,400 units sideways and peaks near 15,700 up over 372 ticks (6.2 s); the other two 15,400 sideways over 289 ticks. How the trail is drawn (its colour in the fighters' colours, its length, its match to the body) is Rendering and VFX's; the replay cannot show pictures. Orb's wish for a burning, smoking trail of friction and heat is also theirs.
