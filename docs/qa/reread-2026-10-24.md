## The script fix (rulings §9d)

`gp.gd`'s guard-and-grab was compared with Encounter's probe (from git history, docs/director/pending/balance-19/probes/gp.gd). Differences found and removed: (1) the guard reads the AI's wind-up with `DirBrawl._windOn`, and is **exclusive** (while he guards he presses nothing else; mine could press a catch in the same tick); (2) the **stick is held down on the claim's tick**, so the throw is down and the bury opens the sure launcher (mine held none); (3) **B is thrown until a live tick has run**, then held two live ticks (a hit-stop can swallow a one-tick press). Both guard-and-grab and grab-and-follow use them.

**This is a finding, not a pass.** The newcomer who presses B 0 to 20 ticks after it lights **wins every match** against medium and easy, launching 144 and 98 times a match from half a second in.
