# Weekly token record

**Status: draft for the Executive Producer to own and commit** (row A-PROCESS-08, a supported row: Production Operations drafts it, the EP supplies every figure). Started 2026-11-13. One row a week; a week runs from Friday 19:00 (Pacific) to the next Friday 19:00, when both meters reset. The date of the Alpha is re-estimated from the first two weeks of A1 (the path says: at the end of A1 from two weeks of real token figures, and again at the end of A2).

**Why it exists.** The weekly limit is the binding constraint on the Alpha, and until now there were no clean weekly figures (the EP's own words: none for the last three weeks). Every length in weeks in the path is a size-class estimate; this page is what replaces the guess with a measurement.

**Words.** _All-models meter_: the weekly meter that every model draws on. _Fable meter_: the separate meter for the largest model; its use also draws down the all-models meter. _Tokens briefed_: the size, in millions of tokens, of the director work the EP sent out that week (the EP's estimate at briefing time, not a bill). _Rows landed_: register rows whose work reached main that week with a commit (the tracker, milestones.md, is where each is traced).

## The rate basis the EP gave (to convert tokens to meter points)

- About **1.6 all-models points per 1M tokens on the largest (Fable) model**; about **1 point per 1M on the smaller models** most directors run on. (The audit measured the first: 11 points for 7.0M tokens.)
- The audit's own blend, from the row above: 26 points for about 18.5M tokens, about 1.4 a million.
- These are rough. If a week's computed figure differs by a lot from this basis, note why (a heavy Fable week reads high; a week of cheaper models reads low) and use the computed figure from then on.

## What the path assumes, and the arithmetic it implies

Arithmetic from the path's own numbers; none of it is a forecast.

| Case | Finished director work a week | A1 at 55M | A1 at 67M (with the planet scope the EP added, about 12M) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| The path's planning pace | 35M (range 25M to 50M) | about 1.6 weeks | about 1.9 weeks |
| The slower case | 20M to 30M | about 1.8 to 2.8 weeks | about 2.2 to 3.4 weeks |

The path gives A1 two to four weeks. The planning pace is "roughly 60 to 80% of the weekly allowance" with about a third of each week spent by the EP on briefs, reviews, gate runs and rework. The record will show whether 35M is real: if two weeks of A1 land well under 25M of finished work, every range in the path sits at or beyond its upper end and Orb is told so at the end of A1.

## How to fill a row (the EP sends the figures; Production Operations enters them)

1. At each status message (every two days), the EP sends: the all-models meter, the Fable meter, tokens briefed since the last message, rows landed with their commits.
2. At each Friday 19:00 reset, close the week's row with the end readings and start the next.
3. Never fill a figure that was not given. An unknown figure is written "not given" so the gap stays visible.
4. Computed points per 1M tokens = (end meter minus start meter) divided by tokens briefed in millions, per week. It is a rough blend, not a rate for a single model.
