# Risk register: the Alpha push

Rewritten 2026-11-13 by Production Operations (row A-PROCESS-04), from the audit's path to Alpha ("The critical path, and the three risks", "What the adversarial review changed", the decisions table) and the register's own risk lines (docs/ep/alpha/audit-2026-11-13/03-gap-register.json). It replaces the starting set of 2026-09-28; every one of the 11 old rows is kept at the end with its fate. Nothing here is decided: the path is the audit's recommendation until Orb approves each milestone.

**How to read it.**
- **Standing:** _open_ (could happen in the current milestone, guard in place), _watching_ (not likely yet, guard in place), _happened_ (it has happened; the section says what changed). A closed risk is moved to the old-rows table with its date and reason, never deleted.
- **If it happens:** what moves, from the path's own numbers where it gives them. No risk here has a probability: the path gives none, and this page invents none.
- **Early sign:** the first thing visible, taken from the path or from the register row's own risk line.
- **Owner:** one director, or the Executive Producer (EP). Owners answer for the guard, not for the risk coming true.
- Words: **park** is finished work waiting outside main; a **golden window** is a landing that changes how the game plays, so saved replays stop working; a **spike** is a small throwaway trial that shows how big a row really is; **gate** is the EP's check before anything lands; **X-nn** is an exit criterion.

**Watch in A1 (this fortnight):** R-01, R-12, R-16, R-10, R-24 and R-04. The tracker (milestones.md) carries the count that R-01 is watched by.

## 6. The critical path (what waits for what)

From the path, so a slip can be traced to what it moves:

1. **Commit or ignore the working tree** (A-PROCESS-03), then move the played build's patches and the switch groups onto main (A-CODE-03 and the rows A-CODE-01 needs), then A-CODE-01 itself and a golden record (A-CODE-02). Nothing else lands with confidence before this. (R-01, R-12)
2. **The one-command desktop build** (A-TOOLS-01, which also needs A-LEGAL-02).
3. **The model page** (A-ENERGY-02, landed), then the stamina, energy and body loop (A-ENERGY-01) and the signature inputs (A-COMBAT-02), in GW2. (R-03)
4. **The move table** (A-COMBAT-04), which needs both, with transformations (A-COMBAT-06), in GW3.
5. **Tuning on the finished rules** (A-AI-02, A-QA-05, A-QA-08), GW4 at the end of A5.
6. **The candidate's long checks** (the soak, the bands) and two friends' Deck sessions (X-01).

Two chains run beside it and are nearly as long: the build to the itch.io rehearsal to the tester guide to the testers (A-TOOLS-01, A-TOOLS-02, A-QA-01, X-01); and the mode list to the horde to its computer control to the team and horde bands (A-MODES-01, A-MODES-02, A-AI-01, A-QA-06). The safety chain (A-LEGAL-01 and A-LEGAL-08, then A-LEGAL-03, then A-PERF-01 on a real Deck) is not on the path to the candidate's date but is on the path to learning the Deck's answer (R-04, R-02).
