# Ideas log

**Where a new idea waits.** One line per idea, in the speaker's words, with the date. The Executive Producer adds a price within one working day; nothing here is built until Orb has said yes to its line. The rule is [change-control.md](change-control.md). Orb's earlier note on special moves stays at docs/ep/ideas/special-moves.md and is not copied here. Production Operations keeps the page tidy; the Executive Producer prices the lines and records Orb's answers. Started 2026-11-13 (row A-PROCESS-01).

**How to read a line.**
- **EP's line:** the recommendation: _now_, _at the next milestone_ (named) or _after Alpha_.
- **Size:** S about 0.25M tokens, M about 1.2M, L about 4M, XL about 8M (the register's classes; sizes, not quotes). "To price" means the Executive Producer has not sized it yet.
- **Moves the fingerprint?** Does it change how the game plays, so that a golden window is needed and saved replays stop working? ("Likely yes" means it is a rule; the Executive Producer confirms when pricing.)
- **Orb's answer:** Orb's yes or no to the EP's line. An idea Orb liked is not an answer to the line: it stays "not asked yet" until Orb has seen the price.

## How to add a line

1. Copy the last row of section A or B; give it the next number and the date it was said.
2. Put the speaker's words in quotation marks, as written, with a few words in brackets saying what the idea is. No names of existing games, films or characters, even when quoting.
3. Leave the EP's line, size and fingerprint cells as "EP to add" and "To price" until the Executive Producer prices it. Orb's answer stays "Not asked yet" until Orb has seen the price.
4. Never move a line to a milestone's row list yourself: step 5 of the rule says the list changes only at a milestone's start, and the EP does it.
