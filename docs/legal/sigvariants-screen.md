# Ten biome signature variants: name screen

Owner: Legal and IP Compliance. 2026-10-23. Screens the ten names in `docs/combat/biomes.md` and `docs/combat/pending/biomes/sigvariants.json` (Combat, paper only). Method: each against the genre's famous move names and the common creature-battler and fighting-game move lists from general knowledge, a web search for the exact names (no exact hit for any; the engine is weak on rare phrases, so this is a screen and not a trademark search), and the plain meaning of each phrase. Combat's own four renames stay as made. A screen, not legal advice. RL-174.

| Name | Verdict | Reason |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| ICEFIELD THAW | **CLEAR** | Plain geography words. "Ice field" is also a combo term in one online game, a different phrase; no famous move is named this |
| FLOE SPLIT | **CLEAR** | Plain words (a floe splitting is what sea ice does); no known move or mark |
| VINE SHEAR | **CLEAR** | Near the creature-battler move "Vine Whip" in shape (vine plus a verb), but a different verb and name; "vine shear" is also a gardening phrase. Keep away from "Whip" |
| GRASSLAND BLAZE | **CLEAR** | "Blaze" is a very common fire word (and an ability name in one battler series); the whole phrase is unused and descriptive |
| FROST HEAVE | **CLEAR** | A real geological term for ground lifted by freezing; descriptive and not owned by anyone |
| PINE BREAK | **CLEAR** | Plain words, no known move or mark |
| MIRE BOIL | **CLEAR** | Plain words, no known move or mark |
| SHORE SCOUR | **CLEAR** | A real coastal-erosion phrase; descriptive |
| SCREE RUN | **CLEAR** | Descriptive (a scree run is also a hillside term); no known move |
| CRUST SEAR | **CLEAR** | A cooking and surface phrase; "sear" is common in move names but this pair is not one |

**All ten are CLEAR; no rename is needed.** The naming pattern (a place word plus a plain verb) is ours and generic, which is why none echoes a signature move; the genre's famous names (the wave, the final flash, the cannon names and the like) look nothing like them.

**For when they are built (not a name issue):** the fire and ember variants (GRASSLAND BLAZE, CRUST SEAR) and the instant flood (MIRE BOIL, SHORE SCOUR) are new light and area events. They join the flash registry and the required set as their own worst-case clips in both modes on the commit that carries them (c01, c03): amber-orange, slow glows, no saturated red (fx05), no whole-screen step, the burn spreading at the stated column rate and not as a flash. Nothing ships with the names yet, so nothing is needed now.

## Adoption view: TIDE CLEAVE and LANE SWEEP (2026-10-23, RL-175)
Both are **CLEAR to adopt**, and TIDE CLEAVE should replace HORIZON CLEAVE. They were already GO in the earlier name screen (Steam: no hit for either; `name-screening.md`). Today's web search found no move, skill or mark with either name; the nearest is an unrelated trading-card move called "Tidal Sweep", a different phrase. "Cleave" and "sweep" are common move words. HORIZON CLEAVE stays a retired working label (a companion-app brand word, RL-006). The new effects are fine on this screen: TIDE CLEAVE's parted sea and falling walls is a generic image, and its surge and LANE SWEEP's low, wide scar join the flash set when built, as the other water and area variants do.
