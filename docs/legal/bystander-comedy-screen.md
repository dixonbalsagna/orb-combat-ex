# Bystanders, soldiers and the comic collateral style: ruling (RL-247)

Owner: Legal and IP Compliance. 2026-11-02. From Orb's direct decisions (the owner, in a private discussion, then released to the EP): bystanders and scientists thrown by blasts are shown as harmless slapstick, soldiers are an openly hostile scenery enemy, and there is no death counter. A screen, not legal advice. **Update 2026-11-07: Orb's follow-up has arrived** ("No civilian deaths going forward. Civilians, scientists, bystanders, VIPs, will bounce harmlessly off the ground, or scatter when impacted", docs/ep/vision.md). It is consistent with everything below; the hold is released and the open points are re-ruled in **RL-257**: the toll is retired (no death, kill or people-lost counter anywhere a player or the public sees; the sim's civilian-death scalar and the meters fed from it come out), hero anguish and the villain's menace may not read a person being struck, the older people rows stand only as the guards listed in RL-257, and the lint row c11 is added. Where this page says the toll and hero anguish are "to come", RL-257 is the answer.

## What Orb decided

1. **Civilians, scientists and bystander soldiers** hit by collateral are flung and bounce on the ground like rubber. At most they are knocked unconscious (stars or flashes over the head) and wake in a few seconds.
2. **Lava and magma** get the same comic treatment: no burning, smoking, scorching or charring.
3. **No death counter.** Bolus will have a scanner. The evacuation mechanic is far off. **No children.**
4. **Hostile soldiers** may be vaporized by blasts. They never surrender. They are openly hostile to the players, are treated by the game like scenery objects, and are never the target of any minigame or achievement.

## Ruling: fine, with limits

**Ratings and stores.** Cartoon slapstick with no injury is the mild end of the violence scale, and bloodless instant removal of armed enemies is ordinary in Teen-range games. Expected shape: ESRB Teen with cartoon or fantasy violence descriptors, PEGI 12, USK 12. The whole build is still driven by mass destruction, so the Mature working assumption in c09 stands as a ceiling. A screen, to be re-checked on the real build when the rating questionnaires are filled in; keep a copy of the answers.

**Bystanders (civilians, scientists, bystander soldiers)**
- Nobody stays hurt: no blood, no limb or body damage, no body left lying, no dead idle pose. A knocked-out figure wakes within about 3 seconds (a number in data, never longer) and gets up.
- No children appear anywhere in the thrown set, or in any harm scene.
- No reward: no score, achievement, bonus, counter or voice line for flinging people, and the AI and any picker never aim at them (c08 stands). No score term reads people (RL-213 to RL-217 stand).
- Schools, hospitals and places of worship stay barred as staged targets and stay spared by every blast and fall (c09, RL-242, RL-243).
- Real-world marks: no real military insignia, flags, agency names or weapon brands on soldiers or scientists.
- Lava and magma: no burning, smoke from bodies, charring or screaming; the figure pops out harmlessly. Nothing burning on a body at any time.

**Hostile soldiers (the vaporizing)**
- Hostile means armed and attacking the fighters, always. No surrender, no fleeing, no unarmed or knocked-out soldier is ever vaporized. A soldier never harms a civilian or scientist on screen.
- The vaporizing is instant and clean: a flash into a puff or a brief cartoon x-ray skeleton, then nothing. No remains, ash pile, charring, screaming or lingering burn. Stylized, never realistic.
- **Never a body imprint or shadow on a wall or the ground** (a scorched human outline echoes real nuclear imagery), and the blast-cloud wording rule (c07) stands.
- Orb's "scenery" is a design word and not a rating category: a soldier on screen is a person to a board and to our own rules. So no AI, picker or score term reads soldiers (RL-213 to RL-217), nothing aims at them to gain anything, and the scenery label is not a way around those rules.
- Nothing is gained from vaporizing: no energy, health, power, menace, drop or counter. No kill counter, no achievement or minigame on soldiers, no kill voice line or celebration.
- A hostile soldier is told from a bystander by what they do (firing at fighters), never by a marker over anyone (ADR 0010).

**The toll and the stats**
- There is no death counter and no kill counter. The sim's old civilian toll scalar and the words "civilians lost" and "casualties" are retired in anything the player or public sees; what replaces them is Game Design's call and comes back to me as a new row.
- The people-terms regime (RL-213 to RL-217, RL-219, RL-228, RL-244 to RL-246) was written around a toll of people lost. With nobody dying, those rows are not withdrawn: they stand as design guards until Orb's follow-up, and I will re-rule them once the toll's new meaning is known (they may simplify, since no one is lost).
- Pillar 5's hero anguish loses its old input. Whether it comes from buildings, bystanders knocked out, or something else is Orb's and Game Design's decision, and it returns to me.
- Public copy and the stores: nothing may say "nobody gets hurt" while hostile soldiers can be vaporized. Say what is true, with the AI-disclosure line unchanged.

**Bolus's scanner**
- Drawn on his own body, never as a marker over a fighter. It reads fighters' output and real objects and machinery only, never people, soldiers included (ln08 stands). It adds no kill or toll reading and gives no benefit for soldiers.

**The evacuation mechanic** is far off. When it is designed it comes to me first (the old "mass evacuation" menace term in pillar 5 is a people-adjacent input and must be re-read).
