# The fixed lines for the public page and the gate, in American spelling

Owner: Legal and IP Compliance. 2026-10-22. For Marketing's page, UI's gate and Tools' word-for-word checks (RL-166). These replace the "fixed parts" in `docs/marketing/public-page-drafts.md`. The gate and the page's flash box carry the **same words**. Spelling follows Orb's rule (p03). A screen, not legal advice.

## One change beyond spelling: the fix promise
Addendum 11's words say "we are working to fix them". **Orb paused flash work on 2026-10-18**, so that sentence is no longer true as a statement of present work. A public notice must be true on the day. The honest version is **"and they are not fixed yet."** It is also what Orb's renewal records. If flash work restarts, "we are working to fix them" may come back. This change goes in the same commit as the spelling change and the check update.

## The flash notice (page box and gate, identical)
> This game contains flashing effects. Our own automated check found some scenes that go over the recommended limit of three flashes a second, and they are not fixed yet. No independent analyzer has been run. If you or someone in your family has photosensitive epilepsy, please do not play this version.

## The lock line (the small line under the gate's button, and under the page's box)
> Reduce flashing is on and cannot be turned off in this version. It lowers some effects but does not remove the flagged scenes.

Buttons: **"I understand, start"** and **"Not now"**. Never "safe", "tested" or "compliant". The Settings entry "Flashing effects notice" describes the notice and says "photosensitivity **analyzer**".

## The other fixed lines (unchanged except spelling)
- **Title:** Orb Combat EX (working title)
- **AI line:** "Orb Combat EX is a one-person project. Orb directs it, and AI tools write the code and draft the art, animation, dialogue and design documents. Nothing in the game is generated by AI while you play: the fight director and the world are ordinary game code."
- **In development:** "Everything here is in development and may change."
- **Privacy:** "This game collects and sends no personal data. The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps its own server logs. The feedback button copies a report to your clipboard for you to paste to Orb; nothing is sent by the game."
- **License:** "License: not chosen yet, so all rights are reserved for now." Link: "Licenses, notices and privacy". Third-party notices keep their official spelling ("MIT License", "SIL Open Font License 1.1").
- **Order on the page:** title; AI line; the flash box with its lock line; the description; the play button ("Play in your browser: a notice comes first"); the small lines.

## Checks for Tools
Compare word for word against these strings; scan the public strings for British spellings (colour, behaviour, grey, centre, artefact, programme, catalogue, neighbour, organisation, recognise, licence, analyser) and fail on a hit; fail the interim export unless the lock flag is true. The settings ids that contain "colour" are internal and may stay; their visible labels are American.

## Addendum (2026-10-27, RL-192): the game's own notice strings (ui/data/terms.json, UI's file)
The in-game notice still carries the **old Addendum 11 words**: "We are fixing them" (untrue since flash work paused on 2026-10-18), "analyser" (British) and a "reach ... and a few that go over it on stricter readings" sentence that differs from the cleared notice. **Not cleared as they are.** The in-game notice and the page's gate carry the same words; use these exactly (keys under `prompt`):

- **`menu_notice`** (the whole notice in one string):
  > This game contains flashing effects. Our own automated check found some scenes that go over the recommended limit of three flashes a second, and they are not fixed yet. No independent analyzer has been run. If you or someone in your family has photosensitive epilepsy, please do not play this version.
- **`photo_title`:** unchanged ("This game contains flashing effects.").
- **`photo_body_a`** (the card version that names the scenes; the named scenes are true and may stay):
  > Our own automated check has found some scenes, such as a camera fly-past of collapsing buildings and some beam impacts, that flash more often than the recommended limit of three times a second, and they are not fixed yet.
- **`photo_body_b`:**
  > No independent photosensitivity analyzer has been run. If you or someone in your family has photosensitive epilepsy, please do not play this version.
  ("we recommend not playing" becomes "please do not play", the cleared words.)
- **`menu_rf_locked`** (also the lock line under the button while the lock flag is true):
  > Reduce flashing is on and cannot be turned off in this version. It lowers some effects but does not remove the flagged scenes.
- **`ui/data/settings.json`, the "Flashing effects notice" help:** "...and that no independent photosensitivity analyzer has been run." (replaces "has not yet been tested with a photosensitivity analyser": no "tested").
- **`ui/data/options.json`, label "Colour-blind mode":** "Color-blind mode" (visible labels are American; the id stays).

Conditions:
1. `menu_rf_on`, `menu_rf_off` and the options help ("Turning it off restores full effects") are true only while the toggle exists. **In the interim build the lock flag is true and the toggle is hidden, so these must not show;** the picture in docs/rendering/img/lookgate/lookgate-boot-notice.png shows the toggle and `menu_rf_on`, so confirm the interim export sets the lock flag (Tools' check already fails the export otherwise).
2. Tools' word-for-word check covers the in-game strings as well as the page, and its British-spelling scan runs over `ui/data/*.json`.
3. This is a words change in the live build, so it lands with the boot park and before the pin moves, and the nightly runs on the new commit (pin rule).
4. No other word changes; no "safe", "tested" or "compliant".

## Addendum (2026-10-28, RL-201): the phone note (shown to a phone before the flashing notice; ADR 0011)
Exact public words; Tools' check holds them word for word. American spelling, no promise about any other platform or date.

- **Heading:** Best on a computer
- **Body** (one string):
  > This demo is made for a computer with a keyboard or a controller. It may not run well on a phone.
- **Button label:** Continue anyway

Conditions:
1. **The note comes before the flashing notice and never replaces, shortens or skips it.** After the button the visitor sees the notice and the lock line in their cleared words, exactly as on a computer. The note's button is not the notice's button and does not count as seeing the notice.
2. No other words: no "coming soon", "later", "mobile version", "supported", "optimized", "tested", "free" or any price or date. A change to any word is a new ruling.
3. The note appears on a phone only; computers see no note. It is not a take-down and not a block: the button always continues.
4. The pin rule applies: this is a page change, so the nightly runs on the new commit before the site pin moves.
