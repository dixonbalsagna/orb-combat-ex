# Core four names: Grist, Loupe, Empress Orza, Bolus (stages 0 and 1)

Owner: Legal and IP Compliance. 2026-10-30. RL-211. A screen, not a clearance and not legal advice (name-screening.md). Source: Orb's lock in `character_names_finalization_directive.md` (outside the repo), read as Orb's approval of the four names only. The supporting-cast candidates and the home-ground names in that file are not approved and are not screened here.

**Provenance (ai01):** the naming session was Google Gemini, model unconfirmed, date 2026-10-30; Orb's part is the lock and the choice of the title form. The record needs the model name and the brief file when Orb or the EP has them.

## Verdicts (as in-game names)
| Name | Role | Verdict | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Grist | Protagonist | **CLEAR** | A plain English word. No fighting-game or anime character of this name turned up. A trading-card game has a legendary character card with the same first word and a title after it (an insect theme; our hero has no insects), so keep the hero's look, powers and title away from it. |
| Loupe | Rival | **CLEAR, low flag** | A plain word (a jeweler's lens). A minor named character in a long-running hunter-themed manga shares the word as a first name; he is not a fighter anyone would mistake for ours. Keep the Rival's design, role and any title clear of that manga's setting. |
| Empress Orza | Empress | **CLEAR** | No character named Orza turned up. Near neighbours differ by a letter and in role (a male non-player character in a weapon-fighting game, an Atlantean soldier, an orc cook). "Empress" as a title is a generic court rank. |
| Bolus | Cyborg | **CLEAR** | No character named Bolus turned up. The word is a medical and anatomical term. See the condition on its meaning. |

How it was checked: web searches on 2026-10-30 for each name against game, anime and manga characters, plus a second look at Grist, Orza and Bolus. No trademark register was searched (stage 2); that is for any name that will be marketed.

## Conditions
1. **Proper nouns only (the directive's rule 1 stands).** No line, caption or note plays on what a name means: Grist as milling, Loupe as a lens, Orza as fabric or a jar or a sailing term, Bolus as chewed food or a medical dose. The Cyborg's hunger lines (RL-181, RL-210) must never connect to his name. The craft anchors stay behind the scenes: they are not written in any player-facing text, store text, caption or the in-game credits.
2. **Titles are exempt from the length guideline** (Orb's words); no Legal limit on name length.
3. **Spoken forms** may vary by attitude (Grist calls her "Orza", she says "the Empress" or "We"); no slur, joke or diminutive built on a name.
4. **Franchise names stay out of files.** Only the first-name sameness above is recorded here, without the work's name.
5. **Private until committed:** the names enter the repo and the game only after the EP commits this screen.

## Product-name cautions (not about the in-game use)
These are four ordinary words, and each is already used as a product or organization name: a spreadsheet and database tool and a climate news site (Grist), an image viewer app and optics products (Loupe), medical products and calculators (Bolus). None is a conflict for a character's in-game name. **None should become the game's title, a subtitle, an app or store tag, a DLC or a merchandise line without a formal search (name-screening.md stage 2).** Orza had no use found. The game's own title stays "Orb Combat EX (working title)".
