## The page (plain words, for testers)

**What you may do with Orb Combat EX during the Alpha**

Thank you for testing. Here is what you may do, in plain words.

**You may:**
- play the build on your own computer or Steam Deck;
- tell Orb what you think, in reports, chat or conversation;
- record or screenshot a problem to show Orb, and send it to Orb only;
- suggest ideas, fixes, drawings or sounds (see "If you send something", below).

**You may not:**
- post, stream or share footage or screenshots of the build anywhere public, or send them to anyone else, without asking Orb first;
- give the build, its files or your download key to anyone;
- copy, sell, rent or re-upload the game or any part of it;
- change the game and share the changed version;
- take its art, writing, sound or code and use it somewhere else;
- use its name, characters or look to promote or sell anything;
- feed its art, writing or code to an AI model to train it.

**Why it is this way.** Orb Combat EX is an early, private test, and some of it is not ready for the public. All rights in the game, its art, writing, sound, code and name are reserved for now. That is the plain default, and it keeps every option open for later. It is not a reflection on you.

**What comes later.** Orb will decide about public code at Beta, the next stage. A later version of this page will say what players may do when the game is public, such as streaming and making videos. Nothing here promises any of it.

**If you send something.** If you send an idea, a fix, a drawing or a sound, you let Orb use it in the game, and Orb will credit you if you wish. You keep your own copy and your credit. You are not owed payment unless you and Orb agree it first. For anything bigger than an idea (code, art or sound you made), Orb will ask you to agree to a short contributor note first.

**Credits.** The game runs on the Godot Engine (MIT License) and uses the Open Sans font (SIL Open Font License 1.1), among other parts. Their notices are in the build, on the licenses screen, and in NOTICES.txt in your folder.

**How the game was made.** Orb Combat EX is a one-person project. Orb directs it, and AI tools write the code and draft the art, animation, dialogue and design documents. Nothing in the game is generated by AI while you play: the fight director and the world are ordinary game code.

**Questions or a problem.** Tell Orb through the private report channel that came with your key.
