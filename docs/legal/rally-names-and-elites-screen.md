# Rally names and call-outs, and the elite henchman's six tricks

Owner: Legal and IP Compliance. 2026-10-25. Screens `docs/narrative/rally-names.md` (Narrative, committed) and the elite henchman's six tricks (`docs/combat/henchmen-grades.md` section 2, Game Design's rulings 2026-10-25 item 10) against mc01 and the earlier rules. Paper only. A screen, not legal advice or a patent clearance. RL-184; new row mc05.

## 1. The twelve rally names and the eight call-outs: **CLEAR**
- **Names:** Palm Turn, Net Haul, Pillar Lift, Plate Chase, **Ring Hold** (the rename of Ring Hang, adopted), Stair Spike, Rope Return, Banner Drop, Mantle Wall, Crane Reel, Wreck Toss and Slag Catch are plain place-plus-verb words with no franchise echo (general knowledge; no web search this round). The alternates (Palm Steer, Hand-Over-Hand, Stone Lift, Straight Chase, Loop Hold, Step Spike, Rope Snap, Banner Slam, Braced Wall, Crane Fling, Scrap Hurl, Slag Bounce) are fine too. Narrative's two renames (Mantle Wall, Plate Chase) are good: they keep "guard" out of her lines and avoid an echo of Rank Breaker.
- **The call-outs:** one line, once, at the moment the rally turns, only when the fighter it concerns is in flight and not dazed or bound, never the move's name, never a multiplier or countdown: **this follows ln06 and passes.** All eight lines are ordinary taunts or reactions; the insults (rat, insect, amateur) follow ln01.
- **"My turn now! Hang on!"** (the Protagonist taking over a chase): **pass.** "Hang on" is the idiom for "hold on tight", said to a fighter who is flying free; it does not describe a bound fighter and is not about hanging. With Ring Hang gone, nothing nearby uses the word in a held context. If Narrative wants the line to stay clear of any doubt, the alternate "Nice chase! Now it's mine!" is equally good.

## 2. The elite henchman's six tricks (mc05, a clarification of mc01): **Pass, with conditions**
GD's ruling (a trick is a type picked for each spawn, never kept, never named, no memory) is the right line. mc01 bans a procedurally generated enemy **individual** (a name, a history, traits that persist or change from play, ranks); it does not ban **types of anonymous enemy** with a fixed kit drawn by the stage's seed, which is ordinary enemy variety. To keep it there:
1. **The trick is picked from the fixed list of six by the stage's seed and stage data only** (the column, the boss, the difficulty). It is **never picked or changed by anything the player did**: not by how he beat the last elite, not by his habits, not by the ledger, not to counter him.
2. **An elite does not persist.** He appears in one stage, is never kept, never returns, never remembers the player, and is never promoted. **The grades are types, not ranks:** no henchman becomes a veteran or an elite because of what happened to him or to the player, and no survivor grows stronger.
3. **No name, no insignia, no story** (as the henchmen's rule already says); his one-sided telling part (one big shoulder, one long arm) is a silhouette cue, not an identity.
4. **The tricks themselves pass:**
   - **The counter-catch** (a perfect block turned into a catch and an across throw): arms and torso, a planner throw, never the throat (g05, e12).
   - **The wall return, the meet and the blitz:** pass. **The blitz's tell** (a 20-tick crouch with the aura flaring on his body) is a held low stance and a power-up mark together: keep it a lunge-low stance with one limb loaded and the other hand up, never fists clenched at the sides, no scream (h06), the aura a thin outline that brightens over at least 0.1 s (fx06), and nothing else at once (k01's cap of two marks). It is a held pose over 12 ticks, so the held-pose lint runs on it.
   - **The thrown henchman:** a henchman (a combatant) is thrown, never a civilian, with no gore. **The car:** a car that is empty with nothing living near it (ln04). Both throws are launches through the planner.
5. **Item 13** (a downed henchman pays a little ki and counts toward nothing else) is fine: henchmen are combatants, shown knocked out and not gored, with no score, meter, achievement or unlock; menace and anguish stay the civilians' (c08).
