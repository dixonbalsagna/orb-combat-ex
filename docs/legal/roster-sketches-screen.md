## 2. The stomp (rows s09, RL-134)
- **On a standing opponent: clear.** A sole or heel from above on the chest, gut, legs or shoulder is the cleared stomp and axe kick, and the head by the heel is an ordinary axe kick.
- **On a downed opponent, torso and limbs: clear** under the Mature target.
- **On a downed opponent's head, face, neck or groin: out of the base moveset until the rating decision.** This is a ratings point, not an originality one: it is the image most likely to attract a violence-towards-defenceless-people descriptor (PEGI 18's own term) and to move a listing toward Adults Only or a refused classification in stricter markets (general knowledge, not checked today). If Orb wants it, make it a **finisher only**: a rare, non-repeatable state, never in a public trailer or screenshot, and run through the IARC and Steam questionnaires on the real build first.
- The Hopper's card can say "never a blow on a downed opponent's head" until then.

## 3. The barrage as the Anti-hero's signature (sg01, RL-135)
**Pass on paper, with conditions; it must be seen drawn.** It is assembled from cleared parts: a sweep that fans shards, a thrust that sends three, a flick that sends one shot that splits (all screened in waves 5 and 6), thrown from an unhurried walk (the cleared "walk through"), closed by a heavy from his own pool. A walk forward under fire is a staple of the genre, not a signature; the three-volley schedule is ours. Conditions (sg01): each volley is one motion of one hand and each differs; never both palms in turn or one hand pumping (e05, e06); the hand lit as a thin edge, never a ball; **the walk is upright and unhurried with the hands open and low**, no crouch, no aura, no charge pose (k01, k02, h01); no shouted syllable repeats across the three; and **the flash plan from VFX is right**: at 24 ticks apart the three volleys sit inside one second, so **one full flash on the last volley and two lesser ones**, counted over the whole screen with everything else (k05 and the release margin), no whole-screen flash, a rim on the rival on a landing. The name is not chosen; "The Barrage" as it stands in the data is a plain descriptive phrase.
It is also Orb's question as a design choice. The Legal view: clear to build once the walk and the three release poses and the flash strip are drawn.
