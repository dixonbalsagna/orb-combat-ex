## 1. A

- **The shove:** two hands, no damage, a 3 bh push. Clear. P1 holds: palms or forearms on the chest, **hands at least shoulder width apart**, no light, no grip, one motion, never held. Two palms thrust forward with a glow would read as a beam release (b01, b09).
- **The clinch, trip and throw:** clear. A collar-and-elbow or waist hold is the cleared tie-up. g01 holds point, g02 no locked or laced hands between the fighters and nothing glowing at the hands, and new **g05**: never the throat, hair or tail, and the trip and throw are drawn. "How a flying fighter takes control" is fine: a hold and a turn in the air is a staple. The throw is a launch the director stages; through a shell he finishes it: ordinary.
- **The tackle:** conditional. **g04:** a hold at the waist or shoulders; the carry of 6 to 16 bh is drawn travel (m01 to m04: at least 4 ticks, the carried body in view every tick, no more than 3 bh a tick); the arrival crater is local, with debris falling, and **no rising ring of rubble, cracks, wind, lightning or aura** (a held 16 to 36 tick coil plus that bundle would stack marks 1 and 4); a buried rival is shown partly in the ground, never hidden. **The coil** is a held pose (h01 to h03): open hands or one hand down, never a crouch with fists clenched at the sides, no scream. Must be seen drawn: the coil, the carry and the crater.

## 2. B

- **Burst (tap):** a short flash from one hand at 5 bh. Clear: one open or blade hand, shoulder height or lower, the flash a thin edge or short cone at the hand, **never a ball** (e01, e02, e06).
- **Ki flurry (mash, one every 8 ticks):** conditional. Rapid blasts from the hand are the shouted-barrage image if they are the same hand pumping, or two palms firing in turn. **e05, e06:** vary the piece (hand state, release, body) from the screened hands, never pump one hand or both palms in turn; **f03** no repeated shouted syllable. The generated B moveset gives that variety.
- **Blast at 12, art at 24, signature at 48:** conditional. A held B passes three tiers, so it shows three flashes over 48 ticks. **k03:** each flash is a thin ring or edge flash on the hand, forearm or plate in the lane colour, 4 ticks or fewer, never body-wide, never gold, white or red, never with a shouted name; **at most two of the seven marks at any tick through the whole hold; no scream at any tier.** The held pose follows e01 (no sphere growing), e02 (one open hand, shoulder height or lower, never both hands from the chest, never a hip), h01 (emitter test) and h03. "Armoured from the first flash" shows as a plate or edge glint, not an aura. Must be seen drawn: the tell at each tier and each flash.
- **A beam held and steered:** clear. The beam as built was screened; **e07:** it stays a plain straight line, steering moves the aim or origin only (no spiral or whip), and the firing pose does not change while it is held. e04 (a bracing hand never cupped or gripping the wrist) stands.

## 3. The charged blows

- **Held X (charged light):** clear. The hold frame obeys L6: an arm drawn back at shoulder height, **an open hand never drawn to the hip** (b02, h04: wrist at x 9 or more); a closed-fist chamber at the hip is allowed. No armour, so no armour cue.
- **Held Y (charged heavy, 12 ticks charged, 40 full, armoured against flurry blows):** conditional. The pose is a 40-tick held pose: h01 to h03, never a crouch with clenched fists at the sides, no scream, no aura, no rising rubble ring with cracks. "Armoured" shows as plates or an edge glint. **The full flash is k03:** a thin lane-coloured ring or edge flash, 4 ticks or fewer, not body-wide, not gold, white or red. **The just release** is subtle and has no visual: clear. Must be seen drawn: the hold pose and the flash.

**Names:** the lift, the slip, the breaker and the seize are ordinary combat words. None is a problem. Do not pair "breaker" with the repository's former name in any public text.

## 5. The rest

- **Even mash, both thrown back 4 bh:** clear. k04: a slide and a little dust, **no shockwave ring of rubble, cracks and lightning**, no body-wide flash.
- **The taunt as a gamble (45 ticks):** clear if it uses the screened taunts (no beckon, no two fingers, no hand to the forehead; held-lint class gesture). Its finish (a meter gain and 10 ki) is a thin lane-coloured pulse at most: no aura, no gold, white or red flash (k04).
- **A zip at a launched rival:** clear under **m10**: the zipper follows the rival's drawn body on a continuous path (m01 to m08), never a snap to where he will land.
- **Zip chains until ki runs out:** conditional. A chain of hops is close to the flicker-teleport barrage, so **m09:** every hop meets m01 to m08 alone; the strike pose is held at least 4 ticks at contact with the body drawn; the ghosts on screen across the whole chain are at most 5 and none is left behind or placed round the target; no camera cut or flash between hops. Orb's ki limit is fine as a limit.
- **The spark-lock** between two heavy commitments (VFX): sparks at the contact point (fist to fist, plate to plate), **no locked or laced hands, no body-wide glow** (k04, g02).
- **Fire on the move (LT+RB):** clear in principle: the bodies already listed (backpedal, kiting turn) and e01 to e07 apply. Its **tell and its B are new vocabulary** and need my screen drawn: a lean into flight with the lead hand lit would stack mark 3 if the glow streams upward, so keep the cue small and in the lane colour (k02).

## 6. Does a heavy flurry or an X and Y mix raise anything the light flurry did not?

Yes, three things. The light flurry rules (s01 to s03) stand; these are added.
1. **A blur that multiplies limbs (f01).** Heavy blows have large wind-ups and are thrown up to five a second; at ten a second a mix invites a smear with extra arms or fists drawn at once, the many-armed barrage. The smear must follow the one striking limb's real path, at most two ghosts of it per blow, never extra limbs or a fan round the target.
2. **Repeat rules must count the string, not the button (f02).** A mix of X, Y and B changes the piece every tap, so s01 (same limb, tip and path) can be dodged by alternating buttons while the target stays the same. s01 and s02 count the whole string: no more than two blows in a row to the same target, never two gut blows running, no more than three of the same limb, tip and path.
3. **Voice (f03).** Up to ten blows a second with a vocal each is a chanted barrage. No repeated shouted syllable, no shouted word, and at most one grunt for every two blows, varied. Audio owns this.
Nothing else is new: the heavy flurry's guard chip and ki cost are balance, not originality.
