# Contributor note: if you want to add code, art or sound (A-LEGAL-10, RL-265)

Legal's draft, 2026-11-13. A screen, not legal advice; a lawyer should read the final text once before the first sale. For anyone outside the project who sends more than an idea. Not needed for bug reports or feedback.

---

## The note (plain words)

Thank you for wanting to help with Orb Combat EX. Before Orb can use something you made, please agree to this:

1. **It is yours, and you made it.** What you send is your own work, or work you have the right to share. You did not copy it from a game, a show, a website or anyone else, and it does not contain anyone else's art, music, code or characters. If an AI tool helped, tell Orb which one.
2. **Orb may use it.** You give Orb the right to use, change, copy, show and sell your contribution as part of Orb Combat EX and later versions, forever, anywhere, and to put it under whatever license Orb chooses for the project, now or later. You are not giving up your credit or your own copy.
3. **Credit.** Orb will credit you by the name you choose, in the game's credits, if you want that.
4. **No payment unless agreed first.** You are not owed money for a contribution unless you and Orb agreed it in writing before you sent it.
5. **No obligation.** Orb does not have to use what you send, and may change or remove it.
6. **You may stop.** You can withdraw an offer before Orb uses it. After the contribution is in a build, it stays in that build.
7. **Adults only,** or a parent or guardian agrees for you.

To agree, say so in writing to Orb (a message in the report channel is enough) and name what you are sending.

---

## For Orb and the EP

- Without a note like this, someone else's code or art in the project belongs to them, and Orb could not later change the license or sell it freely. That is why the note lets Orb relicense (point 2), which the usual sign-off line (the Developer Certificate of Origin) does not do by itself.
- Keep each agreement privately, with the date and what was sent; testers' names are never put in the repository (RL-260 and the A1 brief).
- A lawyer reads this once before the first sale, and again if outside code or art becomes a large part of the game.
- AI-written parts may carry no copyright (guide-licence-rundown.md); the note still matters for what a person wrote or drew by hand.
