# Originality rules

Version 1, 2026-09-28. Owner: Legal and IP Compliance. For everyone who adds anything to this project, human or AI. A working guide, not legal advice.

## The rule

We take inspiration from a genre. We never take material from a franchise.

The test: if someone who knows Dragon Ball could look at a name, design, sound or line and say "that's the one from the show", it does not ship. Calling it a homage, a parody or a tribute does not change the test. Ideas and mechanics are free to use. The way a franchise has expressed them (names, characters, art, music, words, code) is not. An open repository is easy to find and to report, so we stay well clear of the line.

## What we may take from the genre

Ideas and conventions that many games and shows share:

- Fighters who fly, dash and fight across a huge sky and ground.
- Energy blasts and beams, charge-ups, beam struggles (clashes) and shockwaves.
- Power-up auras, power tiers, and ground that cracks when a fighter powers up.
- Destructible arenas, collateral damage and world-scale stakes.
- Hero against villain, rivalry, comebacks, hiding and ambush.
- Naming a move out loud or on screen. The habit is fine. The names must be ours.

Already ours, so keep building on them: the wrapped planet, stances chosen by intent, the procedural fight director, signatures that change by biome, menace and anguish.

## What must never be copied

Not exactly, not lightly altered, not translated, not recoloured.

| Never copy | What that means in this project |
|---|---|
| Names and lore | No character, move, place, race or item names from the franchise, no puns or near-spellings of them, and none of its lore (afterlife, tournaments, wish-granting objects). Our beam names follow our own pattern, a place or material plus what the beam does to the land: HORIZON CLEAVE, GLASS TRENCH. Not: Kamehameha, Final Flash, Spirit Bomb, Galick Gun. |
| Characters, designs, costumes | Fighters come from our art bible. Not: spiky upswept hair with an orange and blue martial-arts uniform, a golden-hair transformation, a tail, a pale horned emperor. |
| Catchphrases and lines | Barks are written by Narrative. Not: "over 9000", quotes or speech habits from the show. |
| Music and sound | Original score and effects, or licences on file. Not: sound-alikes of the themes, charge-up or beam sounds ripped from shows or games, voice clips. |
| Logos and UI | Our own HUD and type. Not: a scanner overlay, another game's HUD, logos, title cards or fonts. |
| Code and assets | Our own, or licensed and listed in `licence-register.md`. Not: anything from other games, ripped sprites or models, unlicensed mods. |

## The fan games

"Spiritual successor" means we may share ideas, such as destructible terrain. It does not mean we may share files. Search-result summaries of its public listings (IndieDB, DBZGames.org) describe Lemming Ball Z as a Dragon Ball Z-based game with characters from other franchises. We could not open those pages, so who made it, what it contains and on what terms are unverified. So we treat everything in Lemming Ball Z and Lemming Ball Z 3d (names, characters, art, sound, code, text, levels) as off limits. Something can come across only if Orb can show they made it, it contains nothing borrowed, and Legal has reviewed it.

**Orb decides:** who made the fan games, what rights or permissions exist, and whether anything is meant to carry over.

## Grey zones: Orb decides

Until Orb decides, the default in the last column applies.

| Item | The worry | Default until decided |
|---|---|---|
| Hair-colour transformations | Golden spiky hair on power-up is the franchise's best-known image. The prototype hero has gold hair (review-log RL-014). | No hair-colour change as a power cue. The prototype's gold hair does not carry into art. Show tiers with aura, silhouette, markings or eyes. |
| "Power level" readouts | A scanner reading out a number is a signature gag. | No scanner device and no numeric "power level" text. Tiers show as bars or pips. Avoid the phrase "power level". |
| Iconic poses | Cupped hands at the hip then thrust forward, two fingers to the forehead, and arms raised for a giant orb are recognisable from a silhouette. | Animation designs original charge and fire poses. Generic poses (arm out, palm forward) are fine. |
| Attack-name patterns | "Final ___", "Big Bang ___", "Spirit ___", "Special ___ Cannon" and "-ha" coinages. | Our own pattern only. Plain words such as Wave, Beam and Lance are fine. |
| Named transformations | "Super ___", "Ultra", "God" or colour names for forms. | Original tier names from Narrative. |
| The word "ki" | An everyday martial-arts word, but the genre's signature word for energy (RL-015). | Fine as an internal stat. Narrative proposes a game-specific name for players. |
| Nods and Easter eggs | A wink is still a copy if it uses a name, pose, line or sound. | None. |

## Checklist before submitting a name, design, dialogue or audio

Send it to Legal through the EP with the answers to these.

1. Origin: who or what made it, with which tool, on what date. For AI output, keep the prompt and the model name.
2. Clean process: no franchise reference images, audio or footage, no tracing, no franchise words in briefs or prompts.
3. Names: search the exact name in quotes on the web, Steam and itch.io. Say it aloud to catch sound-alikes. It is not built from franchise words or puns.
4. Designs and poses: shrink the design to a silhouette, then to three flat colours, and ask "what does this remind me of?" If a specific character comes up, redo it. Poses are built from our own atoms, not traced from footage.
5. Dialogue: no catchphrases or plot beats. Search the boldest lines in quotes.
6. Audio: original, or a licence on file. Never "in the style of" a named theme. No samples from shows or games.
7. Third-party items (font, library, asset, music): a row in `licence-register.md` before it is merged.
8. Any doubt about a name, a whole character, a trademark or a copied asset, or "it reminds me of a specific character or work": stop and send it to Legal. Doubts about a pose, a gesture or a look (hand shape, arm angle, glow strength, how many marks) are creative direction and go to the creative rulebook's owners, Art and Animation under Orb (Orb's ruling of 2026-11-07, audit CD-15); they are never a Legal flag.

Legal answers GO, CONDITIONAL or NO-GO in `review-log.md` for the items in Legal's scope (names, whole-character likeness, trademarks, copied assets and licences, and player safety: flash and light, data and privacy, ratings and stores). A flag in that scope cannot be locked or shipped until it is closed, but only at the gate its horizon tag names (private, web or store): a flag tagged for a later release does not hold an earlier gate. Pose, gesture and look rows are advice from the creative rulebook, are tagged Moved in the log, and **never block a phase gate or a release**. See "How to read it" in `review-log.md`.

## Words we use in public

No franchise names in the game's name, store tags, keywords, repository topics or hashtags. How the homage is described in public text is Orb's call, reviewed by Legal with Marketing. A "not affiliated with" line helps but does not make a lookalike safe.
