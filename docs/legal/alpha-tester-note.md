# Orb Combat EX: a note for Alpha testers

Please read this before you play. It is one page. (Legal's draft of 2026-11-09 for Orb to send; the working title may change.)

## What this is

A private Alpha test of an early fighting game. It is rough, it will break, and it is not for sharing. **Orb Combat EX is a one-person project. Orb directs it, and AI tools write the code and draft the art, animation, dialogue and design documents. Nothing in the game is generated by AI while you play: the fight director and the world are ordinary game code.** Music, if any is in your build, is credited in the build.

## Who can test

- **Adults only.** By taking part you are telling Orb you are 18 or older.
- **The build is private.** It was shared with you personally. Please do not pass it on, upload it, post it, stream it or share clips or screenshots of it publicly, and do not give your download key to anyone. You may record or screenshot a problem to show Orb, and send it to Orb only. If you want to show the game to someone, ask Orb first. The permission page that came with your key says the same thing in more detail.

## Your safety

**This game has flashing effects and bright, fast scenes.** It is still being checked and some scenes may flash more than the recommended limit. **If you or anyone in your family has photosensitive epilepsy, please do not play.** Read the warning the game shows before you start, keep **Reduce flashing** on, and stop playing at once if you feel unwell. You can stop testing at any time, for any reason, with no explanation needed.

## What you agree to

- You may play the build, on your own devices, and tell Orb what you think. That is all the permission you have. **All rights in the game, its art, writing, sound and name are reserved**; nothing is open source or free to reuse.
- You will keep the build and anything in it private, as above.
- You will not copy, change, sell or share the files, and you will delete the build if Orb asks.
- If you send an idea, a drawing or a fix, **you let Orb use it in the game**, and Orb will credit you if you wish. You are not owed payment unless you and Orb agree it first.
- Orb may end the test, take the build back or change it at any time. It is a free test, offered as is, with no promise it will work.

## Your privacy

- **Nothing leaves your PC unless you send it.** The game collects and sends nothing about you. (The two-PC mode opens a link between two computers on your home network only when you start it.)
- **Reports go to Orb's private Discord server.** You choose what to send and when. Discord is a separate service run by another company, with its own rules and privacy policy; it will see what you post, and the people in the server can see your Discord name and your report. Please put no personal details in a report: no real name, address, email, or account names, and nothing you would not want other testers to read. If you attach a file, glance at it first.
- If you want your reports deleted, tell Orb and they will remove them.

## Credits and notices

The credits, the licences of the tools the game is built on (the Godot Engine, the Open Sans font and others) and the third-party notices are in the build, on the licences screen. They are there so everyone gets the credit they are due.

Thank you for helping. Every report, small or large, makes the game better.
