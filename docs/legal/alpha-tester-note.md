## Your safety

**This game has flashing effects and fast, bright scenes.** Some scenes may flash more often than the recommended limit of three flashes a second. **If you or anyone in your family has photosensitive epilepsy, please do not play.** Reduce flashing is on and cannot be turned off in this build. It does not remove every flashing scene.

**To stop:** if you feel unwell, look away from the screen first. To close the game, choose **Quit** on the first screen, or pause (☰ on a controller or Steam Deck, P on a keyboard) and choose **Quit**, then **Close the game**. You can stop testing at any time without explaining why. **[Orb: new lines, ok?]**

## Before you play

This is a private test of an early fighting game. It is rough and it will break. **Orb Combat EX is a one-person project. Orb directs it, and AI tools write the code and draft the art, animation, dialogue and design documents. Nothing in the game is generated by AI while you play: the fight director and the world are ordinary game code.**

- **Nothing leaves your computer unless you send it.**
- **itch.io.** You download the game through itch.io, a separate service run by another company, with its own rules and privacy policy. It will see your download. If you claim your key with a free itch.io account, Orb will see your account name, so use one with no personal details if you prefer. The game itself collects and sends nothing about you. **[Orb: changed since you saw it, ok?]**

The game removes folder paths, your account name and your PC's name from those copies, but it can't promise to catch everything, so check before you send. **[Orb: ok?]**

- **Deleting.** If you want your reports deleted, tell Orb. Orb will delete your report files and messages from the report server, and Orb's own copies, within 14 days.
