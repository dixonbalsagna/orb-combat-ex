## The nudges

- **A raised arm in the follow-through** is a `held_raise_overhead` (b09, h05): at contact an arm may pass above 80 as part of the swing, but **by 6 ticks after contact the hand is at or below 76**, and the follow ends as the table in `super-heavy.md` already says: plate, "the forearm across his centre"; hook, "the fist across his centre"; hammer, "sunk low, the fist low"; elbow heave, "the elbow lowering at once"; blade jaw, "a pace past, back in guard". The sheets show the arm still up at 10 ticks. **Animation's lint should read the follow key at +6 ticks:** report the measured heights; if they are at or below 80 and the render is misleading, say so and I will close it.
- **`pu.su_blade_jaw` follow:** two forearms crossed above the head is also h02. Uncross: the striking arm across the centre, the other hand back to guard.
- **`pu.su_ball_heave` load:** hands together at the face reads as hands clasped or a prayer pose when held. Keep the hands at least 10 apart (the guard fists beside the face).

## Other rulings

- **The kneeling look** of the four heaves (the rear knee on or just above the floor, the loaded limb low, the other hand up): **no legal reason to ease it.** It is a lunge-low stance, not a crouch with fists clenched at the sides (the fists are in front and in guard), and nothing is held overhead. Whether it reads as a kneel is a design call; if Animation wants a lunge, ease by a hand's width. Keep it **grounded**: the rise goes to the **chest or gut** (never the jaw with a leap, b03), with **no fire or glow, no scream and no leap**, so it stays an ordinary uppercut and not the well-known jumping rising uppercut.
- **Redrawn live mediums:** the uppercut's loaded fist now in front of the belt, and the hammer and dropping elbow chambers at 78: **pass** (h05).
- **Drives:** the checked drives (D1, D2, D3, D4, D5, L1, L8, h07) read as required except where noted above. The rival's open tells keep the limb drawn back (h07).
