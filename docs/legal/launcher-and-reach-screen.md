## 1. Q1: the lit fist
**Yes.** e06 was not meant to ban it. The lit fist, the light on the plate and knuckle edges and never a ball round the fist, was cleared as one of the six energy hands and the 13 emitting strikes (RL-062), and a closed hand that emits is not banned by e02 or e06 as I wrote them for the open-palm blast. I re-worded **e06 to name three hands: open, blade, or the rival's lit fist with edge light only.** Combat may generate the lit-fist bolt and blast. Conditions: shoulder height or lower, never at the face, the shot leaving the knuckles at contact as a thin edge or short cone. (Design point for Combat: rows 8 and the bolt 2 give the rival an open palm; he has none as a strike hand, though the open palm is a cleared energy hand.)

## 2. Q2: R1
Adopted as **e08**. Two additions: **the blade hand is a flat blade, never a pointed finger or two fingers** (row 16 has the light "running off the fingertips", which must read as a thin edge off a flat hand, e03), and **the lit-fist hand is allowed** under Q1. Everything else in R1 stands: lit as a thin edge before it lands, the shot a thin edge or short cone, a rim of each body's colour on the near side and never a flash over the whole body, never at the face, a changed piece on every mashed blow, at most one full flash in 20 ticks, and the blast's 12-tick wind-up a held pose with a thin ring or edge at the palm (h05, k03).

## 3. The 8 bolts and 4 blasts
Pass. A palm set on the chest or gut with a short cone is a close-range palm shot, a staple, and it is one hand, shoulder height or lower, never both palms, never cupped, never at the hip. The four bolts differ in hand or release, which satisfies e05 and e06 (no pumping one hand). Conditions: no scream and no repeated shouted syllable (f03); the Protagonist's heel-of-the-palm placement is "placed, never swung". **Must be seen drawn:** the contact of a thrust, a flick and a sweep, the cone and the rim, and a mashed run of bolts (VFX is building it).

## 4. The two manners
- **The Protagonist (row 15):** pass. The palm set on one point and a short cone that moves him; never at the face.
- **The rival (row 16):** pass. A blade hand driven in with the plate behind it and the light along the plate's edge: a thin edge, a flat hand, no pointed finger.

## 5. The launcher
Pass, no new shape. A heavy thrown on half of B's wind-up at a staggered rival, tell cut to its last third and the drive whole. It is judged as a held pose of 14 ticks (h05) and by its drive's conditions. A spin or a leap is never thrown this way, which keeps b04 and L1 intact. The mediums borrowed into the launcher's pool were screened as mediums.

## 6. Q3: vicious blows on one arm
**s02 stands and is applied by place.** An arm has one place only because the grammar's targets are coarse. Split it for this purpose into **shoulder, elbow and wrist,** with two blows at most running to one place, and the head, core and legs as two places each (head and jaw, chest and gut, legs and shins). Four taps on a region then alternate places (**s08**). The wound-looks (the heel of the palm into the elbow joint, a blade hand inside the wrist, a plate ground down the arm) can be assigned a sub-place by Animation. Further conditions: vicious blows are **strikes, never a grip on the throat, hair or tail, never a clawed hand on the face**; the four-fingertip jab under the ribs is a flat blade hand (b10); f03 holds across the taps.
**This does not clear the design.** The plan has no bite, gouge or claw for the launch pair, which is right. Orb has not confirmed vicious blows in a clinch, and the **rating question stays open** (the wounds system and a held fighter taking up to four blows are exactly what the questionnaires ask about). I will screen the poses when the design is confirmed.

## 7. The flash rate
Game Design's hard limit (three full flashes a second) and Combat's (one in 20 ticks, which is three a second) are the same ceiling, and it is the standard one for photosensitivity (no more than three flashes in any one second). **k05** makes it explicit: **counted over the whole screen**, both fighters and every effect together, each flash small (a thin edge or short cone), **none red**, a reduced-motion setting that halves the rate. What I need to see: a recorded **3-second mashed sequence of point-blank bolts by both fighters at once** on the real build, with a flash count taken from the recording (a flash analysis, an accessibility check rather than a legal one). I cannot run that analysis myself; if it shows more than three, VFX drops sparks to the hand for the rest as planned. A person with photosensitivity should not be the test.
