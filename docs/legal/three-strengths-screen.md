## 1. Combat's questions

**Q2: the words "heavy" and "held".** Confirmed. Applying b02, b09 and b12 to mediums and heavies and to the form `charged` is the intended reading, and I made it explicit: **b12 now names `medium` and `heavy` and `held` and `charged`**, so the new words loosen nothing. The test that matters is duration, not label: **a wind-up of 12 ticks or more is a held pose** (h05).

**Q3: W1 and U1.** Both **adopted**, as h05 and f05.
- **W1 (h05)** with additions: the loaded limb at shoulder height or lower (a leg may be chambered); **never at a hip for any hand state** (this is stricter than b02, which is for open or clawed hands: a closed-fist punch chamber at the hip passes b02, but a 12-tick wind-up is held to the shoulder, which is also the tier's own tell); never both hands together; no limb above 80 except in the last 6 ticks before contact; no held crossed forearms; no light, no aura; both-hand blows keep their hands at least shoulder width apart; a stand-in is judged on the longer wind-up it is played on.
- **U1 (f05):** the burst never repeats the last blow's limb, tip, path or place; no more than two on one path; no piece twice; the hands alternate; one breath of sound for the eight; **and no slow-motion close-up or camera cut on the burst.**

**Q4: what f01 becomes.** The old wording ("at most two ghosts of the limb per blow") does not hold at 15 blows a second, because ghosts of successive blows overlap. **f01 is now by instant:** at any instant at most **2 ghosts of any limb and 4 limb-ghosts in all**, each following the limb's real path; never extra arms, legs or fists drawn at once, never a fan round the target. The rate itself is not the problem; the many-armed storm with a chanted voice is. So a new **f04:** a gap of **under 6 ticks never runs for more than 3 blows in a row, and only inside a burst's opening.** The held-X burst (gaps 4, 4, 5, 6, 8, 11, 15) is inside that: it opens fast for three blows and slows, which is also what keeps it from reading as the sustained fist storm. f02 (the string counts across buttons) and f03 (voice) stand.

**Q5: the two study rows.** Confirmed; pass to Animation.
- **`ru.su_ram`** (the lunge's fist): the flags `hip_chamber` and `drawn_to_hip_then_thrust` are the right ones to stop on. By the letter, b02 is for open or clawed hands and a closed fist chambered at the hip is an ordinary punch chamber, but h05 holds a heavy's 12-tick-plus wind-up to the shoulder, and so does the tier's own tell ("the striking side drawn back at shoulder height"). **Redraw the chamber at shoulder height.** No other change.
- **`pu.su_drive`** (both palms overhead, then driven down): **fails.** Two hands held raised overhead is b09 and b12, L3 and h05, and the tier's falling heavy is **one arm**, the limb at shoulder height until he starts down. Redraw as one arm. (`pu.su_turn`, the spinning blade hand, is fine as a study.)

Row-level notes: **row 8** (the Protagonist's falling palm): one arm only, never both palms (see `pu.su_drive`); **row 12** (heaving palm to the chest) and **row 23** (heaving fist to the gut): L1, no leap, no turn, the arm lowers at once; **row 10** (stepping blade hand to the jaw): a strike, never a grip (L8); **row 16** (the Protagonist's turning elbow to the jaw) and **row 22** (the rival's heaving elbow to the jaw): **b03 now covers elbow and knee** as well as hands, so a rising elbow is held to the same rule: no leap, no spin, the arm lowers at once. Neither is a problem as drawn in the table.

## 3. The 11 stand-ins

All pass as shapes (each was screened as a heavy of the old kind). Played on a 28-tick wind-up they become held poses, so h05 judges them again:
- **Double hammer** (row 33) and **double palm** (row 28): both hands held in the gather for 28 ticks. The hands stay **at least shoulder width apart, at or below shoulder height**; a double hammer's raise overhead may happen only in the **last 6 ticks** before the fall, never held. If the gather cannot be drawn that way, drop it as a stand-in.
- **Crossed-arm rams** (rows 32, 35): the forearms cross **only for the strike**, never held crossed through the wind-up (h02, L7).
- **Spins** (27, 29, 31, 36, 37): a single turn, in place; the longer wind-up is the turned-away tell, fine (s04).
- **Drop kicks** (30, 34): the long gather is not a crouch with fists clenched at the sides, and the leap is the cleared drop kick's own.
- Encounter's fallback (the oldest piece may repeat when a stand-in flurry runs dry) is fine; no repeat runs more than three (s01).

## 5. The two manners

- **The Protagonist, pointed not cruel:** pass. Compact tell, turn does the work, back in guard; no flourish. His `never` row on bringing a heavy down on a head stands.
- **The rival, a show-off:** pass with **h07**: the open tell keeps the limb drawn back, **never arms spread as an invitation and never a beckon**; "chin up" is fine as a posture; the limb he **leaves out after the blow lasts 12 ticks or fewer** (a beat, not a held pose); no taunt pose is built into a blow.
