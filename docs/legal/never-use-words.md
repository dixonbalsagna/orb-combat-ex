# Never-use words for public text

Owner: Legal and IP Compliance. 2026-10-05. The single list for store text, tags, keywords, hashtags, handles, alt text, captions, repo topics and devlogs. Marketing and others refer to this file instead of copying the words into their own, so the words stay in one place. This file names franchises as analysis, as the rest of `docs/legal/` does. Not legal advice.

## Franchise words (never, in any public text, tag or handle)
- Any franchise, character, move, place or technique name from the genre's best-known series, and its signature words: "Z", "Super" followed by a form name, "Saiyan", "Dragon", "Ball" as a title word, "Kai" as a character or title word, "Final" followed by a move or form name.
- Power-system vocabulary: "ki" as a player-facing word (the interface says CHARGE), "power level", "scouter", "transformation" names from the series.
- Fan-game and web-series names: "Lemming Ball Z", "Madness Combat" (and the word "Madness" as a title word).

## Our own working names (never public)
- "Breakers", "Meridian", "KAI", "VORR", "OrbCombat" (another project's name: never used as ours, never as a tag).

## Relationship words (never about the game)
- "spiritual successor", "tribute", "parody", "clone", "homage" (in store text), and "like", "inspired by" or "for fans of" followed by a franchise or character name.
- A genre phrase such as "inspired by the classic anime energy-brawlers" is allowed once in the README only, never in the store pitch, tags or keywords.

## Claim words (never, unless the record supports them)
- "human-made", "handcrafted", "hand-drawn", "composed by", "no AI", "AI-free"; "created by Orb" for anything a tool made.
- "open source" for the whole project; "official", "licensed" or "exclusive" for the music; "first" and "best" claims; "balanced" or "fair"; "registered" or the ® symbol before a registration; "Steam Deck Verified" before the badge; "approved by", "in partnership with" or "powered by" for any platform, engine or AI tool.

## Free to use
Genre and descriptive words: fighting game, energy brawler, anime-style (as a style word), side-on, local versus, destructible, flight, planet, stance.
