## 1. UI's lock wording (RL-157): **Pass; one claim to drop**
1. **Settings help, "at most one large flash a second, and the small ones off": drop it.** It is a specific, measurable claim, and our own reads of the calm mode go up to 2.5 and 3 flashes in the worst second (ai 43 at 2.5, PR-8 at 3). A reader could hold us to "one a second". Say only what is true and general: **"Fewer and gentler flashes. Turning it off restores full effects, which flash more. Reduced motion is a separate setting."** This is the same position as `writing-note.md`: a hedge is needed against a claim, and the cure is to claim less.
2. **Off-state line, "Reduce flashing: off. Full effects are on, and they flash more.": acceptable as written.** It is true, plain and gives the same information as the sentence. No need to repeat the longer sentence.
3. **Dropping "does not remove the flagged scenes" from the shown-toggle menu line: acceptable only when the toggle is shown**, which my ruling allows only after full effects have a recorded pass, so nothing is left to name. Keep the first-screen sentence 1 in every case.
4. **The locked state's line stays**, with one condition: it says "flagged scenes", so it is true only while the pass file shows scenes at or over the limit. If the second interim's pass has none over (clips at exactly 3 only), I will give the words when I see the pass file; the likely line is "Reduce flashing is on. It lowers some effects."
5. **The capture hook's `set_option` route** (local host only, no screen, key, link or saved file reaches it): fine, and necessary to measure the other mode.
6. **The flag check: yes, make it a condition.** Tools' `check-site-interim.mjs` fails the export when the exported `features.json` does not have `lock_reduce_flashing` true, **unless** a full-effects pass is recorded for the published commit. ## 3. Art's finisher styles (RL-159): **Pass with conditions (h10)**
I read the text only (the sheets are on Art's tools and Orb's homework).
- **Held Breath** (head bowed, looks back for the people, a push-in then a cut wide): **pass.** Nothing to taunt; the flash is one steady pulse in the road colour (check it with the rest).
- **Open Palm** (an open hand held out low, as an offer): **pass if it reads as an offered hand and not a beckon.** My existing rule bars the beckon (the palm-up finger curl, "come on"). Conditions: **one hand only; fingers together and still (no curling, no wiggle); the palm toward the fallen rival or sideways, not at the camera; held low** (hip height or below, forearm out, never a hand drawn to the hip); **no smirk and no taunt line**; **never both arms** (that is the held arms-wide invitation, h02).
- **Both Roads** (one open hand raised, "a promise in the future tense"): **pass if it stays at shoulder height or lower.** A hand raised above the head with the palm up or open is the raised charging pose (banned), and any hand above 82 fails the held-pose emitter test in the signature and tell classes. Conditions: **hand at shoulder height or below, palm out like a pledge, fingers together; no light, glow or ring in or above the hand; no ball; one hand only.** The slow half-circle camera is fine.
- **Taunt test for all three:** the closing pose follows a blow, so it must not be a held pose of a gesture aimed at the beaten rival that mocks (a beckon, arms wide, a shrug, a point); the offered hand is a "get up" gesture and reads as respect. The Protagonist's established voice is warm, not scornful, which helps.
- **The other three finishers' styles** (The Verdict, Clean Cut, Last Orders): same method; send them when drawn.
