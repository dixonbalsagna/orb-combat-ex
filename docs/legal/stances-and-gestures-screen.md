## A. Intro gestures

All ten new motions are ordinary, small, two-pose gestures; every hand is open and outside the hip zone; none is a taunt, a beckon, a point, a hand to the forehead or a crossed forearm. Clear as written, with conditions on two:

- **`pi.claim`** (feet widened, chest up, hands open and away from the body at waist height, 2.6 shoulder widths apart, a breath out): the nearest thing to the power-up stance. Clear **if** he does not crouch, the hands do not clench, nothing rises or shakes round him (no aura, no cracking ground, no wind), no shouted line and no flash goes with it, and it stays a 14-tick peak that plays only as the `claim` intent. That keeps it at one mark at most.
- **`ri.check`** (a glance down at his own forearm plate, the forearm raised to chest height): clear **if** nothing is drawn on or over the plate: no readout, numbers, display or lens flash (no scanner look). Chest height, never to the eyes.
- **`ri.defer`** (the pose I was asked to look at: one open hand laid flat on the coat): clear. A courtly bow with one hand on the chest is a staple. Keep the hand at the chest or below the collar line (never to the forehead or face), open and flat (never gripping the coat), and not both hands.
- **`pi.defer`, `pi.dismiss`, `pi.appraise`, `pi.check`, `pi.soften`, `ri.acknowledge`, `ri.soften`:** clear as listed. `pi.dismiss` has no hand movement at all, which is what keeps it from being a taunt. The reused gestures (RL-067) stand.

## A2. Far-side pass, the check's guard layer, the push style

- **Far-side pass** (an arc dive over the rival, a pivot round him): clear under m01 to m04, which Animation built to. Condition for the arc dive: the leading arm never punched ahead with the other trailed straight back (the dash rule), and the dive's body is never head-down hidden behind the rival at the strike tick. The zip-entry sheet shows every entry drawn on every tick. The fastest turns (flips at 2 rad a tick) are a blur and are not hiding the body.
- **Guard layer a check is thrown over:** clear. The resting arm is the set guard: a forearm up with the hand open (the Protagonist) or the plate forward (the rival). Keep it **never a held X of crossed forearms** (h02, L7), and the layer ends with the blow.
- **Push style** (no impact snap, a drive of 4 units, a 10-tick follow-through): clear under P1: a shove on the chest or gut, no light, hands apart when it is two.

## B. The four generated stances

### B1. The five signature frames (generic)

All five are clear as generic frames. A person screens each fighter's version (the defining piece) before art. Conditions on each frame:

| Frame | Condition |
| :-- | :-- |
| **Martial B** (tell 30, a lunge, a rush of 4, a launcher) | The tell is a held pose, so h01 to h03 apply at every tick. The rush of 4 follows s01 to s03 (never a run at one place, no repeated shouted syllable). The launcher is a planner launch. |
| **Manoeuvre B** (tell 20, zip in, take hold, into the landscape) | The zip is m01 to m04. The take-hold uses g01 to g03. The terrain art is a set piece: one of the seven marks at most (a crater with a rising ring of rubble and lightning would be three). It must differ by biome, as the signatures do. |
| **Energy B** (the beam as built) | Clear as screened. A new variant or a new hand is new vocabulary and is screened. |
| **Defensive B** (a counter, a guard zone, an interpose) | **Counter:** he catches the next blow on his own guard and answers. **Guard zone:** a thin, lane-coloured arc or plates on the guarding side, never a sphere or a dome of light round him and never a hand-held shield. **Interpose:** he steps in front with a braced guard, never arms spread wide to take the hit (that is the rival's On the Chin and the take-it stance, h02). |
| **Charging B** (a 45-tick tell, a release, the 75 ki ultimate) | The charge pose and the release are where the stacking rule bites, so k01 is checked at **every tick of the 45**: planted and drawn in, open hands forward and apart, a thin outline or short streaks in the lane colour, no scream, and at most one more mark (ground cracking, but then no rubble ring and no lightning). The release is the fighter's own (e01 to e05). No shouted form name and no gold, white or red flash. Each fighter's ultimate is screened as its own set piece before art. |

### B2. The travel kinds

- **Zip, zip away:** m01 to m08 (RL-076, RL-077). The moves on the sheet are clear: every blow on arrival already passes the banned rows.
- **Step:** up to 2 bh drawn inside 6 ticks: at or above the 4-tick floor. Clear.
- **Charge, pursuit:** a flight at the rival: the dash rule (not both arms trailed straight back, not one fist punched ahead), drawn the whole way. Clear.
- **Directions** (around, over, under, through, back, away): clear. **Through** must be a pass beside or over the rival's body, never his body passing through the rival's (b08), and **over** and **round** keep the rival in view (m04).
- **Entries** (`spiral`, `arc_dive`, `cartwheel_step`, `air_roll`, `rising`, `coil_spring`, `skid`, `dash`, `rooted`, `fade`, `lane_step`, `pivot`): clear as posed. The name `fade` and any pose that makes the body fainter is not allowed (m01): the pose is a backward step, drawn solid. The prone flight in the entry sheet's first row must keep to the dash rule above.

### B3. Energy hands, releases, deliveries

Clear as written, because the six hands, the emitting strikes and the volley, arc, lob, charged, lit-fist and crown releases were screened earlier (RL-041 to RL-052, RL-062). The generator's combinations add no new hand pose. **Conditions that stay with them (e01 to e05):**
- the **ring hand** and **flat palm** (Protagonist) and the **pinch** (rival): one hand, open, no higher than the shoulder; the pinch is thumb to forefinger, not two fingers; no ball in or at the hand;
- the rival's **fist glow** and **clawed palm**: light on the plate and knuckle edges, never a sphere round the fist or in the claw; the claw never at the face;
- **volley** (a fan of three, and the rival's fan of shards): from one sweep or flick, never both palms pumping in turn, no shouted barrage;
- **charged shot:** plates stack along the forearm (the rival) or a thin charge line on the forearm (the Protagonist); never a sphere growing in a palm, arm at shoulder height or lower;
- **split, spray, lob, the drop (palm down) blast:** clear as screened.
- **Short beam (not drawn yet): conditional.** A plain straight line (no spiral or drill), one hand thrust at shoulder height or lower, never both hands from the chest or wrists together, a bracing hand never cupped under or gripping the firing wrist, no scream. I must see the braced pose and the beam drawn before it goes live.

### B4. Check, push, guard

Clear: the checks are short strikes over the guard, the pushes are shoves (palm, ball of the foot, sole, shoulder, forearm wedge). Conditions as the sheet's L1, L2, L7, P1: a palm check is a blow (no light), the crossed-arm ram never held crossed.

### B5. Specials, and the Protagonist's three placeholders

- **The rival's cutting step** (a change of level then a wide cutting blow): clear. **Barrage volley** (arms open through a half turn and a fan of shards, ending in the ring): clear if the arms sweep and are never held open and apart as an invitation, the ring sits round the shoulders or body and never above or behind the head, and no shouted barrage. **Grip and drag:** g01 to g03.
- **The Protagonist's placeholders** (a turning step with arcs, three curving shots, an open-hand turn aside): clear as written, being staples (a spinning approach, curved shots, an aikido-style redirect). Screen again when they are designed: the three curving shots must not become three glowing spheres circling the hand, and the turn aside is an open-hand sweep, not a held palm-forward stop.

### B6. Grabs

Tackle, carry, lift, throw, snatch, aimed and guard throws: clear under g01 to g03. The hold point is the collar, shoulder, arm, body, waist or ankle, never the throat, hair or tail. No locked or laced hands, and nothing glowing at the hands in a hold. A snatched civilian is lifted clear, not choked or dragged.

## C. The roster of twelve: what I screen per niche

Every template needs three screens before it gets art: the design (silhouette, colours, costume, mask or face, name), the kit (a generator frame plus a person screen of the set pieces) and the identity (name, lines, catchphrases), and the four stand-alone rules apply to all: the stacking rule, no vanish, no hip-cupped or wrists-together hands, and no named technique. **Speedster:** the zip is his identity, so the motion rules m01 to m08 are the limit; avoid copies in a ring or a decoy, a lightning bolt on the body or chest, and a red-and-gold running suit. **Stealth, with the parked mask:** hiding in cover is fine, but an ambush from cover is a drawn dash, never a vanish and reappearance (m01); the mask is a new design, original in shape and markings (no skull or face-paint pattern, no known hooded vigilante look), and is screened as a design before it is drawn. **Armoured heavy:** super armour against mashing is a staple; keep the armour silhouette original (no horned, caped, plated tyrant look and no famous green or gold scheme), and a catchphrase of his own. **Terrain shaper:** walls, pits and thrown ground are staples, but raising ground with a rubble ring, cracks and wind is the stacking rule's mark 4, so never at a power-up with an aura or scream; mending repairs structures only and never revives a fighter or civilian (Orb's no-revival rule); no named elemental-bending vocabulary. **Grappler:** hold points per g01 to g03, no laced-hands lock, no throat, hair or tail; avoid a known wrestler's trademark finisher, gesture or entrance, and a mentor with a known trope costume (a particular master with sunglasses, a shell or a staff). **Zoner:** wide beams and hot lanes are staples; plain straight lines, no spiral or drilling beam, no two-hand wrists-together push, and the lane is a thin edged strip, never a sphere. **Puppeteer:** helpers walk or fly in as bodies, never a summoning circle or glowing sigil, never a self-copy ring (m05), no creature-collecting look and no known duo. **Wildcard:** each of the nine specials is its own set piece and needs my screen (three fighters' worth); do not imitate a real entertainer, stage persona or catchphrase, and a mimic copies only our own fighters' moves. **Juggernaut:** a silent, relentless walk through attacks is cleared; avoid a tall dark conqueror in cape, horns or armour that stands near a known tyrant, keep the clock and the marked region on the world or the rival's body as a plain mark (no numeric readout or scanner look), and no shouted menace line that borrows a known villain's.
