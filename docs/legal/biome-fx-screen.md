## Window 1, the plumes (RL-176): **conditions met; RL-176 closes for the close-framing clips**
The look is ours: faceted puffs in a column, biome ejecta, steady orange embers, one pale burst star. VFX's re-cut meets fx07:
- **The luminance floor:** a puff stepping 10% or more of linear luminance from the ground counts at weight 1.0 in the area budget (under 10% it counts 0.3 to 1), tested per puff colour in all fifteen biomes. **Right.**
- **The star:** never repeats within a second (tested), refused under Reduce flashing (the locked page never draws it), counted at the disc as drawn at the framing in use. **Right.**
- **The clips:** `plumedesert`, `plumeice` and `plumevolc`, a crater every 150 ticks at the game's close framing, tier 3, both modes, through the launcher at below-normal priority: **primary 2, general 0, red 0, margin 0 of 4 events, dips 0, all PASS**; the volcanic reduced clip's area-15% run reads 3, which is at my line and not over it. The embers read red 0. **Condition 2 of fx07 is met for the close framing.**
- **Still open (not a blocker for the parked state):** a plume at the middle and far framings, and a plume inside a beam or a clash. Add them to the release set and read them in the nightly seeds' matches before any public pin.

## Window 2, ambient life (new, RL-187): **Pass with conditions (fx10)**
Light, sparse drift made about the fighters by biome: snow, blown snow, heat motes (particles, never a distortion), ash, leaves, pollen and petals, midges, spray, dust motes; small faceted flecks of 4 to 20 units from the plume pool in Art's colours, rates 0.5 to 9 a second under a cap, halved in reduced motion, nothing while the sim is held, nothing in the city. The sheet shows a few flakes and flecks and no screen-level effect. **Passes** on originality (generic weather and dust) and on the flash side as drawn: flecks this small are nowhere near the area threshold, and VFX built it against full-screen blur and distortion.
**Conditions:**
1. **Flecks never blink or twinkle:** no per-fleck luminance flicker above 2 Hz, and no midge or heat mote that pulses on and off. A fleck fades in and out slowly or simply moves.
2. **Small and sparse stays small and sparse:** the live fleck cap holds, and the whole ambient layer covers a small fraction of the screen. If the desert and savanna heat motes (nearly invisible on sand as drawn) are made larger or brighter in a look pass, they stay modest: never a field of bright glints, never white-hot.
3. **Heat shimmer stays particles,** never a distortion or a ripple of the image (a spatial pattern risk and a look Orb already ruled out).
4. **One clip with the ambient on** (any plume clip or an AI match in tundra, desert or volcanic), both modes, before any public pin; VFX did not re-run the flash clips with window 2 in.

## Window 3, beam scars (new, RL-187): **Pass with conditions (fx10)**
As a beam scorches the ground, the biome throws up what it is made of: glints and dark glass chips on sand, melt steam and slate chips on ice, pale fused flakes and steady orange chips on volcanic ground, black char on soil, a little grey smoke in wet ground, dark chips on rock; a fleck or two a sample, at most six a tick, halved in reduced motion, nothing in the city. The sheet shows a few flecks at the contact beside a beam. **Passes** on look and originality.
**Conditions:**
1. **A "glint" is a steady fleck, not a sparkle:** no blinking or twinkling above 2 Hz and nothing brighter than the beam's own light; glass chips are dark or mid-tone, not bright flashes.
2. **Orange chips steady and amber-orange,** and the analyzer reads red flashes 0 with a beam over volcanic ground in it (fx05, fx09).
3. **The cap of six a tick holds,** and the flecks add no flash event of their own: the beam's start and end flashes stay registered as before.
4. **A fixed clip for each ground** (a beam along sand, ice and volcanic ground, both modes, margin rule, red 0) before any public pin. VFX ran none with a beam over these grounds; the beam is the brightest thing on the screen, so this is the clip that matters.
5. The ten new biome variant names ride on this later (the look follows the names once Encounter wires the table); they were screened as names in `sigvariants-screen.md`.
