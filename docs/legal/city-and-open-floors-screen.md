## Ratings, against the Mature target
The vision's own target is **Mature with graphic violence, including the Cyborg's consumption** (`docs/ep/vision.md`). My earlier "expect Teen or PEGI 12 to 16" assumed non-graphic casualties and **no longer applies**; a graphic game should expect the top of the scale (ESRB Mature or, depending on depiction, Adults Only; PEGI 18) with blood-and-gore and intense-violence descriptors. Two things the target does **not** settle:
1. **The Cyborg's consumption** (cannibalism-like content) and **graphic harm to children** are the depictions most likely to push a rating past Mature or to refused classification in some countries (my general knowledge, not checked today). Decide them knowingly; answer the questionnaires from the real build; ask counsel before a console or a German or Australian listing.
2. **The count** is no longer a separate rating driver once the violence is graphic. It stays in the game as designed. In public it is a taste choice, not a legal one: no rule of mine forbids showing it above zero. Do not add a celebratory sound or bonus to it (a design choice).

## What a Mature target frees, and what it does not
- **Frees:** showing graphic destruction, injury and death in the game and in trailers and screenshots, on platforms that allow mature content with an age gate (Steam and itch.io both do; each has its own marking and answers: platform rules not re-read today).
- **Does not free:** the **labelling** (descriptors, age gate, the content survey answered truthfully); and **where it is hosted.** **GitHub's rules bind the repository and GitHub Pages whatever the rating:** content that "gratuitously depicts or glorifies violence" is not allowed, and a Pages site's primary focus should not be advertising (GitHub Acceptable Use Policies, section 2 and section 10, read 2026-10-12). So keep graphic imagery and sales copy on the store pages and video platforms; a holding page and a plain description on GitHub are fine. (Rows p01, p02.)
- **Video and social platforms:** use their age restriction and mature-content marking for trailers.
