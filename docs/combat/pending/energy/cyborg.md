# The Demon Cyborg: energy moveset (parked)

Combat and Choreography, 2026-10-23. Fitted to Game Design's frame (`docs/design/energy-overhaul.md`, E1 to E117, kit sheet §14), the README's templates and Legal's screen (e11 to e14, fx08). Data: `energy.cyborg.json`. The grammar, ki, hit-stop and rules are everyone's (E10, E100, E102). This page fills only the cells E81 leaves to him. Nothing here is built.

**Identity: appetite, on wreckage and metal only.** His energy is a chest furnace that burns scrap, never people (Legal ln02, ln04). His hands are plates and hammers. **His hand** (where energy leaves him) is the slatted furnace grille across his chest plate, plus the rivet line on his wrist plates. **His colour** is Art's lane: molten slag oranges with scrap-metal grey (`data/art/energy-lanes.json`, bolt main #f58a22), clear of the Protagonist's cyan and the Rival's violet, and under the red line. Before his final form a limiter shows in how it all looks and sounds: rough, rattling, sputtering. In the final form his technology comes online and the same moves run clean.

## Light, RB + X: Rivet Spray
- **Tap.** No wind-up (E14). A backhand snap of the forearm, and a hot rivet pops out of the wrist plate's rivet line and seeks the foe. It is a bolt by E20, at speed 54 (×0.9, heavier) and 14.3 damage (×1.1). Mashed: one a press, at most one every 6 ticks (E21).
- **Hold: the stream** (E23). He holds the forearm level across his chest and rivets pop down the line: 8 in the first second, then one every 8 ticks. The stick sprays it up to 15° either way. His cone is a little wider than most (8°).
- **Flash (fx08):** the rivet line holds one steady glow while the stream runs, with no flash per rivet, and hits build one steady scorch glow where they land. Answers are every bolt's: guard, dodge, trade shot for shot.

## Medium, RB + Y: Slag Roll
- **Tap.** Tell (12 ticks): a hammer fist cocked high, the chest grille's slats rattling open. He hammers his own chest plate and the grille throws a jagged clot of slag up and over, on a high, slow arc (40 ticks, inside 36 ± 6). It is the frame's lob (E30): a 1.5 bh burst worth a medium, a 3 bh knock-back, 6 ki. Mashed: a barrage, one every 12 ticks, a hammer blow each.
- **Hold** (from 16 ticks, E14). He leans back with his off hand braced flat on the plate, and the grille steps brighter at level 1 (28 ticks) and level 2 (48). The stick leads the landing up to 4 bh (E33), read in his shoulders.
- **Level 2 rolls** (his template). The clot lands short of the foe (or at the lead) and rolls along the ground toward the point under him as molten slag: up to 4 bh in about 20 ticks, burning a glassy track. It bursts 2 bh when it touches a fighter, reaches the point under him or runs out, throwing slag 3 bh up, so a low flyer is caught. Worth ×1.4, a 4 bh knock-back, 12 ki, trade power 5. On water it sinks hissing and goes off as a steam column. Each burst is one event (fx08).
- **Answers** (E34): guard at the guard's rate; a dodge tap in its last 8 ticks (for the roll, as it reaches him); a shot that meets it sets it off by trade power.

## Heavy, RB + B tapped: Wrecker Swing
- **Tell: the gather** (16 ticks, a hold from 20, E14; shared with the beam until level 1, E40). Both clawed hands grip the edges of his chest plate and prise it apart, and the grille steps brighter between them. Nothing gathers in or above his hands (e12).
- **Release before level 1.** He swings his right forearm round from behind like a wrecker's boom, feet planted (he never spins), and slams it onto the grille. The blow knocks a jagged slab of molten scrap out, and it tumbles end over end at the foe. It is the frame's heavy blast (E41): worth a heavy, a 5 bh knock-back, a 2 bh crater with a column of flame, 10 ki, trade power 4. The crater is studded with scrap.
- **Answers** are the frame's: a blow in the gather (E45), a dodge, a guard, or a beam that bursts it (E90).

## Beam, RB + B held: Furnace Vent
Held past the gather, the grille opens one pair of slats per level, each a brightness step at the grille with a click and a rumble tick. He flies at half speed (E45). Loaded at level 3 (E47), the grille holds a steady light. It never fires by itself: when his ki can't pay for the level, a pair of slats closes and the charge steps down a level, and below level 1 it holds as the heavy blast. Only letting go fires it. Release fires the level reached, with the frame's numbers (E43, E44):

| Level | Charged at | Length | Worth | Ki | Clean hit |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | :--- | ---: | :--- |
| 1 | 30 ticks | 20 ticks | ×1.0 of a heavy | 12 | 4 bh knock-back |
| 2 | 54 | 36 | ×1.6 | 20 | 6 bh knock-back |
| 3 | 84 | 54 | ×2.4 | 32 | Carried along it, dropped at its end |

- **Shape.** Ragged jets from the slats merge side by side into one rough, uneven column, ×0.9 width (narrower than the Protagonist's 1.2). Its edges tear and wobble on a seeded draw, and embers fall away (render only). The wobble and sputter live in the edge and the width, never in brightness, or at most a step under 10% and under 2 Hz (Legal fx09). It never rotates: no spiral, drill or helix (e11). He is rooted and sweeps it by turning his torso, at 26° a second, slower than the reference's 32.
- **End.** Where it ends on the ground it sinks a pit with a slag rim at the end of its trench. Its last 6 ticks sputter out in three uneven surges, each a step under 10% luminance.
- **Pulses: mashing B** (E50). Each press, he lets go with one hand and hammers his chest plate, and a swell runs down the column to the front, under a 10% brightness step. With B held he hammers by himself every 8 ticks.
- **Struggle look: the heave** (push unchanged, E96). His surges come as swells heaving down the column. Losing, his beam bulges in lumps and sputters back toward the grille, quivering, never thinning.
- **Answers** are the frame's (E45 to E48): hit him before level 1, a dodge tap, guard it, or meet it with a beam.

## Grab, RB + A: Crane Hook (a lash; payoff: the throw)
- **Tap** (10-tick wind-up). Tell: he unclips the crane hook from his forearm and lets it swing once behind him on its cable. An underarm-to-overhead heave casts it, and the cable pays out from a winch drum inside his forearm plate. It seeks the foe and arrives within 30 ticks from any range (E60). In reach he hooks him by hand at once (E68). 10 ki.
- **Hold (from 14 ticks, E14): aim, the long cast** (E61). The hook is lofted ahead of the foe's flight, up to 4 bh by the stick, to catch him where he is going. 14 ki.
- **The catch** (E63, at most 40 ticks). The hook catches round his arms or torso, never throat, face or hair (e13), with hit-stop 5. The line goes taut and the winch ticks. The foe struggles against it, keeping half his nudge, and can't guard or fire. No slow motion, zoom or taunt over him.
- **The payoff, A: the throw.** He takes the line in both hands and heaves his chassis through a third of a turn, feet planted, swinging the foe round and letting go. It is a launch through the planner, 6 bh along the stick (E66). X, Y or B instead fires that shot into him (E65). No finisher starts on a bound fighter.
- **Answers** (E62, E64): it beats a guard; a dodge tap in its last 8 ticks slips it; a shot of trade power 2 or more knocks it down in flight; a blow in his wind-up stops it. Caught, each press takes 4 ticks off (one counted every 6 ticks), and RT's burst breaks it after 12. It never reels anyone in.

## Trap, LB + RB + B: Scrap Pit (a field that holds and drops)
- **Lay** (E71 to E73). His guard drops for the lay's 12 ticks; 12 ki. He strips a fistful of wire from his forearm wrap and drops it with a clutch of scrap plate (laid on rubble, it wires the rubble already there). On the ground it sets as a flat wired frame of plates 2 bh across; in the air it hangs as a wired knot. It arms in 30 ticks: dim, then a slow glow along the wires.
- **Trigger.** A foe enters its field: the column 3 bh above the frame, or 1 bh round the knot. **It flares for 6 ticks** (E74): the wires light and the plates rattle. Then the plates fold down into a pit, clamps close on his torso, hold him for the 8-tick hit-stop, and drop him into the ground. It is worth a level-1 beam (×1.0 of a heavy) and is a down send in place under the spike's rules. Machinery only: plates and clamps, no teeth, nothing that chews, no swallowing.
- **Answers** (E78): avoid it; set it off with a lob or heavier (the clamps close on nothing); guard (the guard's rate, no drop); a dodge tap in the flare; or send the Cyborg through it. One live at a time; it fizzles after 12 s (E76) and never moves.

## Snare and mines (looks only)
- **Snare, LB + RB + A:** the crane hook laid on a coil of cable. In its flare the coil twitches and the hook jumps. It catches as the grab does, with the bind as payoff, or the throw if he presses A (E75).
- **Spark mine (X):** one hot rivet hanging in the air; it arms with one soft tick, never a repeating beep (Legal e15). A mashed string of them flares staggered (fx08). **Blast mine (Y):** a crusted clot of slag, glowing at its cracks. All fizzle after 12 s and never move toward people.

## Forms
The grammar and every number are the same in every form (E10). **The limiter shows in looks and sound only.** Before the final form: dull, uneven rivets with a dry rattle; the slats open partway, the beam wobbles ±20% and sputters embers, with a governor click at each level; the winch grinds and its cable smokes; the pit's plates are rusted. Final form, online: the rivet line opens into clean rows of fins; a steady white core runs inside the column (±8% wobble, no sputter); a silent winch; new metal plates. His hidden weapons show here only as these looks. The weapons themselves are Art's and Game Design's to design.

## Collateral
Near occupied buildings his beam and his lob's burst widen to 1.3 (E113), and his mines don't hold for people (E79). Nothing else touches people: no energy move targets, feeds on or uses bystanders, and no death feeds his hunger (Legal ln02, ln04). Deaths go on his account under the budget (E110).
