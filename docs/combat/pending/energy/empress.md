# The Empress: her energy (parked)

Combat and Choreography, 2026-10-23. Fitted to Game Design's frame (`docs/design/energy-overhaul.md`, E1 to E117, kit sheet §14), README §2's templates and Legal's conditions (`docs/legal/energy-overhaul-screen.md`, e11 to e13, fx08). **Only what E81 leaves to a fighter is hers. The buttons, gestures, ki, hit-stop and every other number are the frame's.** Names are Narrative's re-fits. Data: `energy.empress.json`. Nothing is built. 60 ticks a second; 1 bh = 75 units.

**Identity: dominion.** She stands still and rules the space in front of her. Small shots leave the edge of her blade hand; big ones leave the bladed hem of her mantle, swung by her arm. Every shot is a line, never a ball, and lands where it was aimed: a cut swept through the petitioner, a crown that clings to him, banner staves stamped down round him that drive him to one knee, a rope of light he may not cross. She never fires from a fingertip, never spins, never turns her back. Her mantle never wraps, coils or grabs, so it never reads as a tail. **Colour:** Art's lane, ivory with a deep gold rim (lines, not balls; `data/art/energy-lanes.json`, bolt main #fffefa), clear of the Protagonist's cyan-azure, the Rival's violet-magenta and the Cyborg's oranges, and under the red line. **Hand:** the blade hand's edge and the mantle's hem.

## X: Silk Slivers (E20 to E26)
- **Hers:** slivers, short upright slits of light flying broadside: not needles, sparks or crescents. 15% faster than the frame (69 a tick), the frame's damage. A tight 4° fan that the stick sprays in the vertical only, so a missed stream scores a neat row of cuts behind him.
- **The frame's:** tap one bolt at once; mash one a press (6-tick pace); hold for the stream (8 in under a second, then one every 8 ticks at 85%).
- **Tell and pose:** blade hand at her chest, edge forward; each bolt is one short stroke from the wrist; the other hand stays at the small of her back. The stream shows as a steady light along the hand's edge, not a flash per bolt.

## Y: Crown Toss (E30 to E36)
- **Hers:** a crown of light, five points on a ring, tumbling end over end. It never spins flat and has no cutting edge. A high, unhurried toss: a 40-tick arc whose last stretch falls almost straight down.
- **Level 2, Cling** (at 48 ticks; the frame's 12 ki, power 5): the crown lands upright against his torso, back or arm (never his head, neck or face), points out, and goes off 20 ticks later: a 2 bh burst worth 1.7 lobs, a 4 bh knock-back. He keeps control while it clings. A guard takes the burst at the guard's rate; a dodge tap in its last 8 ticks shakes it off and it bursts where he was. A miss settles where it lands and goes off on the same fuse. The throw's burst and the later burst are two events.
- **The frame's:** tap, barrage, a hold from 16 ticks, level 1 at 28 ticks, the aim, the answers in flight; a loaded level 2 never fires by itself (E47).
- **Tell and pose:** her palm turns up at her shoulder, never above her head. Charging, the palm's edge brightens a step at each level, and nothing is held on it. A tip of the wrist throws, and the crown forms as it leaves the hand (Legal e15).

## B tapped: Throne Quake (E41)
- **Hers, the look:** B's gather is her blade hand drawing the hem across her body, a brightness step along the hand's edge, her heel lifting. Let go before level 1 and she stamps. A hairline seam races from her heel to him (the seeking shot), and at him splits open into a lens of light 4 bh tall that slams shut. The ground bows into the frame's 2 bh crater and column of flame.
- **The frame's:** 16-tick wind-up, a hold from 20 ticks, let go any time before level 1 for the blast (E40), worth a heavy, 5 bh knock-back, 10 ki, power 4.

## B held: Hem Sweep, the beam (E42 to E50, E90 to E97)
- **Shape:** one thin straight cut that leaves the hem's edge at the end of an arm swing from the shoulder (never a body turn, never a hand-frame or spiral). Width ×0.4, the thinnest in the roster. **Sweep** 38° a second, steered by the stick (E48): the fastest, at the 20% band's edge over the reference's 32 (E81).
- **Charge:** the edge light at her hand steps brighter at 30, 54 and 84 ticks with the frame's click and rumble; she turns side-on and sets her heel down. Held at level 3 it stays loaded and fires only when she lets go (E47). Lengths, worth, ki and power are E43's (inside §14's bands).
- **Pulses (mashing B):** each is a swell, under a 10% brightness step, that runs down the line; where it meets the ground the gash deepens a notch. E50's numbers.
- **End:** the line snaps off the hem like a cut thread, and the path it swept hangs as a scored hairline that cools for 60 ticks. The trench is narrow and straight-edged (E111's depths).
- **Struggle look, the edge:** no change to the push (E96). A surge drives the meeting point in as a wedge; only the point moves, and the other beam keeps its own look and never parts or thins. Losing, the line bellies into a bow and quivers like an overdrawn wire. It buckles and never thins.

## A: Banner Snare, the grab (E60 to E69)
- **Look, the cage of bars:** she takes the clasp at her throat and flings the mantle open (the frame's 10-tick wind-up), and the hem casts one furled bundle of four banner staves that tumbles end over end to him. At him it bursts apart: the four staves stamp down into the ground round him (in the air they stand planted in the air), and their banners of light, in her colour, wrap his arms and torso; never his throat, face or hair. He strains against them. Never a prison or a cell, and gone within the 40-tick catch.
- **Payoff, the slam** (A in the catch): her blade hand chops down once and stops dead at the hip. The staves drive into the ground and their banners drag him down: a down send in place. He lands on one knee in a crater under him, and recovers by the slam's rules. The kneel is the landing, never a held pose: no slow motion, zoom, taunt or finisher over it.
- **Held, place (E61):** held past 14 ticks, the release sets the furled staves where the stick points, up to 6 bh off, standing furled as a snare that springs when he comes within its reach (her snare's arming, reach and flare). It counts as her one snare.
- **The frame's:** throw within 30 ticks; catch at most 40; X, Y or B in the catch fires that shot; the way out is his presses (4 ticks each) or RT's burst (E64); 10 and 14 ki; the lockouts.
- **Answers (E62):** a dodge tap in the throw's last 8 ticks; any shot of power 2 or more that meets the bundle in flight; a blow on her in her wind-up. It beats a guard.
- **Reads:** one bundle tumbling in flight, then four staves standing still in the world. Broken, the banners tear loose and the staves topple and burn out. Never swords, rods or strings, never a dome, and it never shrinks.

## LB + RB with B: Velvet Rope, the trap (E70 to E79)
- **What:** a line across the air: two hem plates hang 4 bh apart with a rope of light between them. It is laid across the way between her and him, at her hand or flicked by the stick (E71).
- **Crossed:** his chest crosses the armed rope. It flares once, for the frame's 6 ticks, then fires her level-1 beam along itself, post to post, and swings shut like a gate: 60° about the post nearest him, toward the side he went, over the beam's 20 ticks. Its hit is worth 0.9 of her level-1 beam, with the level-1 end's 4 bh knock-back, so he is sent back across.
- **The frame's:** guard drops for its 12 lay ticks (4 wind-up, 4 set, 4 recover); 12 ki; arms at 30 ticks; one trap live; fizzles 12 s after arming and never moves; hit-stop 8; the answers (go round its ends, a lob or heavier sets it off, guard, dodge in the flare, send her through it).
- **Mines, the look:** the spark mine is one upright sliver hanging still; the blast mine is a small crown resting upright. A mashed string of spark mines flares staggered, one after another and never all at once. Every mine fizzles 12 s after arming and never moves toward people. **Her snare** is the four furled staves, standing, that spring and wrap as the cage. Lays take E72's frames: spark 4, blast 8, trap 12, snare 8.

## Collateral (menace, assumed)
- E113: near occupied buildings her beam's width and her lob's burst widen to ×1.3. The budget caps deaths, with no borrowing at tiers 1 and 2 (E110).
- Casualties and fleeing people feed menace as for every villain. Her sweeps don't angle away from people as the Protagonist's do, but nothing aims at people for their own sake, nor ever at schools, hospitals or places of worship (E117). Her scars are straight: a city she swept reads as cut, not cratered.
