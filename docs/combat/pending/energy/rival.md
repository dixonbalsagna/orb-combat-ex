# The Rival's energy (parked)

Combat and Choreography, 2026-10-23. Fighter: the Rival, the Anti-hero (roster id `RIVAL`). Data: `energy.rival.json` (`combat.energy/1`). **Frame: Game Design's `docs/design/energy-overhaul.md` (E1 to E117).** The grammar, ki, hit-stop and every number not named here are the frame's; this page fills only the cells E81 leaves to a fighter, inside their bands, with the templates in `README.md` §2. Nothing here is built. Names are Narrative's, re-fitted to this kit (`docs/narrative/energy-names.md`).

**Who he is in energy: precision.** He wants you to stand still and know your place. His energy is small, exact and many: needles, hexagonal slabs and plates, violet to magenta (Art's lane, `data/art/energy-lanes.json`, bolt main #4420c4), all off his plated forearm. He fires one-handed with the other hand behind his back, and he lets you see everything coming because he doesn't think you can stop it. The ring holds you still, the stair walks onto you if you stay, and the plate denies the ground you stand on. Then he walks in and finishes by hand, but never off the bind: the ring lets go before any finisher (Legal e12).

## X, the light: Pin Volley (E20 to E26)
- **His bolt is a needle:** a thin violet dart, blade-edged. Faster and lighter than the reference: speed ×1.15 (69 a tick), worth ×0.9. Its own spread is tight, 4 degrees (precision).
- **Tapped,** one needle on the press, flicked off the edge of the blade hand. **Mashed,** one a press at the 6-tick floor.
- **Held, the stream** at the frame's pace: the opening 8 leave as a fan from one backhand sweep of the arm, then the blade hand flicks back and forth in a short arc, a needle off each end every 8 ticks. The stick sprays it up to 15 degrees. Never both palms pumping in turn. One steady light at the hand, not a flash per needle, and its hits build one steady scorch glow (Legal fx08).
- **Answer:** as every bolt (trade, guard, dodge). The tight spread makes his stream easy to read and easy to leave sideways.

## Y, the medium: Stair Split (E30 to E36)
- **His lob is a thick hexagonal block tumbling end over end,** thrown overhand with one hand. It is never a thin spinning slab, and never held, charged or raised overhead (Legal e15). **Low and quick:** a 32-tick arc. Worth ×1.0. Tap, barrage, the hold from 16 ticks (E14), level 1 at 28 ticks and the aim are the frame's.
- **Level 2 at 48 ticks splits into three, stepping toward him.** The block lands where aimed and bursts (×0.6 of a lob, 1.5 bh). It cracks into three smaller blocks that hop to staggered spots on the line toward him (near, mid and far, the far one on him, at most 4.5 bh out) and burst together as they land, 12 ticks after the first burst (×0.35 each, 1 bh). That landing knocks back 4 bh, once. All on him: ×1.65 of a lob, inside 15% of the reference's 1.5. 12 ki. Two burst events in all for the flash count (fx08).
- **What it means:** the spots are set when it splits, so a fighter who moves in those 12 ticks is clear, and one who stands, guards or hovers takes the lot. A dodge tap as they land slips all three, and a shot of trade power 2 or more sets a piece off early.

## B tapped, the heavy blast: Rank Breaker (E41)
- The frame's blast, his look. **Worth ×1.1** (brutal, inside the band). In the 16-tick wind-up the plates on his forearm stand up and stack toward the fist, with a thin edge light along them, and the fist draws back at shoulder height. A straight punch sends a ram of stacked hexagonal plates, point first. On contact the stack bursts open flat, its plates shattering outward in a hexagonal ring over the 2 bh crater and its column of flame. Never a ball of light round the fist.

## B held, the beam: Lance Sheaf (E42 to E50, E90 to E97)
His barrage made one: a straight, parallel, unrotating sheaf of straight beams, thin lances, one off each plate on his forearm (7, 19 and 37 by level). The violet and magenta alternate along each lance, never around it (Legal e11 to e14). A sheaf, not a cut, so it stays distinct from the Empress's beam (Narrative re-fitted the name from "Straight Edge", which Legal flagged).
- **His numbers:** width ×0.85 (a tight bundle), sweep 34 degrees a second (slower than the reference: straight lines). Levels at 30, 54 and 84 ticks; lengths, worth and ki as E43, so it sits inside the 15% and 25% bands. B is a hold from 20 ticks (E14); let go before level 1 and it is Rank Breaker. Loaded at level 3 (E47), it never fires by itself: when his ki can't pay, it steps down a level.
- **Charge:** side-on, the firing arm drawn back at shoulder height, the free hand behind his back, as in his stream (Legal e10, e15). Each level lights one more plate on the forearm, a step brighter, never a ball. **Release:** the arm straight at him and the lances leave together on one line.
- **Pulses (E50):** each B press, his firing fist clenches tighter and a swell runs up the bundle, under a 10% brightness step. His free hand stays a shoulder width away or behind his back, never touching or bracing the firing arm (Legal e15).
- **Its end:** the lances run out from the outside in, and the centre lance goes last. Lances that missed stand in the ground along the trench and smoulder, with no flash; the whole line counts as one event at most (fx08). That is the trench's mark, with no extra damage to a fighter. Split by a perfect block, the bundle forks straight.
- **Struggle trait: the press** (push ×1.0 by E96: the look changes, never the push; a straight, unrotating press, per Legal e14). On each surge the sheaf presses straight and tightens into a narrower bundle, its tips stepping forward one after another. Losing, the bundle bulges and quivers as the lances bow (never thins), and loose tips fall and scorch the ground.

## A, the grab: Rank Ring (E60 to E69)
- **The look:** a thin open loop of violet light. In the 10-tick wind-up his blade hand draws back level with his chest and the forearm plate's edge lights. On the tap he thrusts the arm straight at the foe and the loop leaves his hand. It flies facing forward at hand height, never spinning, and arrives within 25 ticks (narrow and fast, inside E81's 20%). It snaps shut round his arms and torso, pinning the arms to his sides. A thin loop, never a disc, a hoop or an edge. It binds, never cuts, and throws no sparks.
- **The catch** is the frame's: hit-stop 5, half his nudge, no guard and no fire, 40 ticks at most. The loop dims in six steps as the time runs out. He struggles and braces against it, never hangs limp.
- **Payoff, the bind (A in the catch):** the loop pulls tight and he is held where he is. The Rival's next shot lands as on an open fighter, full worth and reel. The bind ends when that shot lands, or when the catch's 40 ticks end, whichever comes first. X, Y or B in the catch fire straight into him instead (E65). No slow motion, zoom or taunt over a bound fighter, and no finisher on one.
- **Held variant, aim (E61):** A is a hold from 14 ticks (E14). The stick leads the throw up to 4 bh ahead along his travel, as a lob's landing, and the loop flies to where he will be. If he isn't there it closes on empty air and fizzles. 14 ki.
- **Answers (E62, E64):** it beats a guard. It loses to a dodge tap in its last 8 ticks, to a shot of trade power 2 or more that meets it in flight, and to a blow on the Rival in his wind-up. Caught, each press takes 4 ticks off the hold (one counted every 6 ticks), and RT's burst breaks it after 12 ticks. The aimed throw is answered by changing course after it leaves his hand.

- **The call-out (Legal ln06):** one per grab, on the throw or in the first 10 ticks of the catch, inside the hit-stop and never extending it. Nothing is said over the rest of the catch, the payoff or a kneel.

## LB + RB: the Pressure Plate, his snare and his mines (E70 to E79)
- **B, his trap: the Pressure Plate.** A flat hexagonal plate set into the ground, or hanging flat in the air. Its zone is 2 bh round it and 6 bh up. Laying, arming, flare and limits are the frame's (guard down for 12 ticks, 12 ki, armed at 30, a 6-tick flare, one trap, fizzles after 12 s). Crossed, its facets light for the flare, then a fan of needles erupts upward, up to 50 degrees either side of straight up. One caught in it takes up to 8 of the 12 needles, worth ×0.9 of a heavy at most (inside 15% of a level-1 beam), hit-stop 8, and a pop of 2 bh up. It denies the air above a spot, where the Protagonist's pillar throws and the Empress's line cuts across.
- **Answers (E78):** go round or above 6 bh, shoot it with a lob or heavier, guard at the guard's rate, dodge tap in the flare, or send him into it.
- **A, his snare:** the loop laid open in the air or flat on the ground, dim until armed. It catches as the grab, and the payoff is the bind.
- **X and Y, the spark and blast mines:** everyone's, in his look. The spark mine is a single needle standing point up. The blast mine is a small slab hanging flat (Narrative's Hanging Plate). A string of spark mines flares staggered, and every mine fizzles after 12 s.

## Collateral and menace
- Near occupied buildings his beam and his lob's burst widen to 1.3 (E113). His mines, plate and snare don't hold for people (E79), and none ever moves toward them.
- Casualties and fleeing people feed menace (the shared rule). The collateral budget caps the deaths at every tier (E110), and nothing scores deaths (E117).
