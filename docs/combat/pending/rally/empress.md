# The Empress in a rally: dominion

Combat and Choreography, 2026-10-25. Game Design's frame is `rulings-2026-10-23d.md`. The data is `rally.empress.json`; the names are working labels.

**Who she is in a rally.** She rules the space the body flies through. She draws a line it may not cross, drives it down where she chooses, and sends it back off her own braced mantle. Her energy links come with revision 9, as her energy kit does (Game Design §20 item 6).

## Her links (each its own kind for item 8; the base link's price and tech)

**Rope Return** (a world link; revision 9 on)
- **Input:** she lays Velvet Rope across the body's coming path in the rally (LB with RB and B). After that there is no press: the send carries him across it.
- **Tell:** two hem plates set in the air with a rope of light between them.
- **What happens:** the armed rope fires her level-1 beam along itself as he crosses, and knocks him back across her line (a knock-back, Game Design §20). It is a ping-pong across a line she drew.
- **Price:** the trap's 12 ki, and her guard is down for its 12-tick lay.
- **It is a read, not a reaction:** the rope arms 30 ticks after it is laid, so she lays it while he is still on his way.

**Banner Drop** (the grab in flight; revision 9 on)
- **Input:** RB with A at the flying body.
- **Tell:** she takes the clasp at her throat.
- **What happens:** four banner staves close round the body in the air, their banners wrapping his arms and torso. Her payoff, the slam, drags it straight down into the ground under it from any height: a spike from anywhere, not only from above. He lands on one knee, which is only the slam's landing. On hard ground it glances up as a spike does.
- **Price:** the grab's (10, or 14 held).
- **Legal:** no lines of light join the staves into a box, and there is no dome or cell (e16). No taunt, zoom or finisher over the kneel (e12, e16). One call-out (ln06).

**Mantle Wall** (a bank; Narrative renamed it from Guard's Wall)
- **Input:** no press. A send toward her while she braces (the stick held toward the body with LB).
- **Tell:** she plants her heel and swings the mantle forward, rigid as a door.
- **What happens:** the body strikes her braced mantle and rebounds back along its line: a ricochet, with the force the send's, counting toward the two-ricochets-in-a-row limit. **It works both ways:** a sent fighter can also wall-kick off her braced mantle as off any face. The mantle stands. It never wraps, coils or grabs, so it never reads as a tail.
- **Look:** the hem plates lock edge to edge, a ring of force where the body meets them, and she is pushed back a step.
- **Ruled** (Game Design, 30d6539a): Mantle Wall stands in every form, and the guard version is withdrawn. Narrative keeps "guard" out of her lines, and no guard closes, launches or knocks back (`empress-guards.md` §4 is met).

## Her sell, a size larger (item 4)
- **A medium:** her head turns with the blow, the mantle flaring and settling. She is rocked back onto one heel, and her chin comes up as she is pulled in.
- **A heavy:** her head is thrown back and the whole mantle billows as she is driven a step back. She laughs through it, then straightens her shoulders royally. She never calls her guards.
- **The send:** launched with the mantle streaming like a banner, laughing.
- **Voice:** a laugh, loud and unbroken.
- **Scale:** snap ×0.9, fold ×0.9, step 1.0 bh.
