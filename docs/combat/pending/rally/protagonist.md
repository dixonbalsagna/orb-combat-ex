# The Protagonist in a rally: control

Combat and Choreography, 2026-10-25. Game Design's frame is `rulings-2026-10-23d.md`. The data is `rally.protagonist.json`; the names are working labels.

**Who he is in a rally.** He decides where the fight goes. His links bend the body's line, bring it back to his hands, or lift it clear of people. He would rather turn a fight into the sky than through a street.

## His links (each its own kind for item 8; the base link's price and tech)

**Palm Turn** (an overtake variant)
- **Input:** the chase (LT with X or Y), with the stick held across the body's flight, up or down. Held back against the flight, it is the plain overtake.
- **Tell:** his hips turn as the zip passes the body, the palm edge-forward.
- **What happens:** an open palm meets the body side-on and turns it 90° onto the stick's line, up into the sky or down to the ground, at the overtake's power. With a neutral stick it turns up and away from people.
- **Price:** the zip's (20 or 30).
- **Look:** a turn of the wrist, the line bending at a right angle in a burst of cyan.

**Net Haul** (the grab in flight)
- **Input:** RB with A at the flying body.
- **Tell:** his open hand draws the tow line back to the shoulder.
- **What happens:** Harbour Net catches the body round the arms and torso, and the rival struggles in it. His payoff, the yank, hauls the body into his reach mid-air, and his next press lands a palm that sends it on along the stick, with no zip to pay.
- **Price:** the grab's (10, or 14 held).
- **Legal:** one call-out, on the throw or in the catch's first 10 ticks, and nothing said over the haul (ln06).

**Pillar Lift** (a world link)
- **Input:** no press. A send carries the body across his own armed Trip Pillar (TRAP LANDING).
- **What happens:** the seams flare for 6 ticks, then a solid stone slab rises under the body and throws it up and away from people. It is a knock-back of at most 6 bh (Game Design §20), and it keeps the body in the air for his next link.
- **Price:** nothing at the time; the trap cost 12 ki when he laid it.

## His sell, a size larger (item 4, as re-read: two sold mediums, then the send; lights aren't sold)
- **The first medium:** his head snaps aside and rolls through the neck; his eyes come back first. He gives a half step on sliding feet with his arms open, and is pulled in again grinning.
- **The second medium,** a step bigger: he folds over the blow, breath forced out in a visible gust, and is driven a step back. He recovers with a short laugh and a nod: he grades it.
- **The send** (B, the heaviest blow of the sell, decisive hit-stop 20): launched with his arms flung wide, still grinning, turning in the air to watch who sent him.
- **Voice:** a grunt, then a laugh. Never an apology.
- **Scale:** snap ×1.0, fold ×1.0, step 1.0 bh.

**Settled** (23d item 21): "back" is within 45° of the reverse of the flight and "across" within 45° of either perpendicular, so the sectors touch only at their edges. Controls adds a dead band if the capture shows misreads.
