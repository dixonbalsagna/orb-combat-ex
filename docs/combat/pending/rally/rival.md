# The Rival in a rally: precision

Combat and Choreography, 2026-10-25. Game Design's frame is `rulings-2026-10-23d.md`. The data is `rally.rival.json`; the names are working labels.

**Who he is in a rally.** He puts the body exactly where he means it to go and holds it there to be ranked. His links are straight lines, a hold in mid-air, and a staircase down.

## His links (each its own kind for item 8; the base link's price and tech)

**Plate Chase** (his chase with Y; Narrative renamed it from Plated Ram so it doesn't echo Rank Breaker)
- **Input:** LT with Y. With X it is the plain chase strike.
- **Tell:** the forearm comes up square, its plates lit in a line.
- **What happens:** the zip drives him into the body forearm-plate first. The body is sent exactly along the stick's line, snapped to one of eight directions, with no bend from the planner: it lands where the line says. With the stick neutral, the line is straight on along the chase. An empty line is his choice, and the choreographer scores it low for variety, which is his trade. The tier's collateral budget still caps the deaths on that line, and a surface on it is met by the ricochet rules (23d item 19).
- **Price:** the zip's with Y (30).
- **Look:** a straight ram with no flourish, and a clean violet line behind the body.

**Ring Hold** (the grab in flight)
- **Input:** RB with A at the flying body.
- **Tell:** a short closing pull of his fist.
- **What happens:** Rank Ring snaps shut round the body's arms and torso. His payoff, the bind, holds the body where it is in the air while it struggles in the loop, never dangling. His next shot (a needle stream, a heavy blast) lands as on an open fighter, inside the catch or within 6 ticks of its end (Game Design §20), and that shot sends it on.
- **Price:** the grab's (10, or 14 held).
- **Legal:** named Ring Hold, never "hang" (RL-183). The ring is a thin loop that closes: never a disc, never raised overhead, and it never cuts (e12). No zoom, slow motion, taunt or finisher over him, and the shot on him is a normal hit. One call-out (ln06).

**Stair Spike** (a spike)
- **Input:** B from above the body.
- **Tell:** he rises over it with the fist cocked at the shoulder.
- **What happens:** one press, one attack. He drives the body down in three short steps of 1 bh each (plate, plate, plate), and the third makes the crater. On hard ground under the plant speed it glances back up as every spike does; on soft ground the rally ends.
- **Price:** none beyond the spike's.
- **Look:** three stepped impacts in a straight vertical line, merged into one glow that brightens only at the last: one burst event (Legal mc04, to be proven in a clip).

## His sell, a size larger (item 4)
- **A medium:** his head snaps with the blow and is snapped straight at once, jaw set. He barely folds, and his feet skid back further than anyone's.
- **A heavy:** his glare comes back to the attacker before his body follows: a short fold and a long skid. He recovers with a sneer (it was luck) and straightens a plate on his forearm as he is pulled in.
- **The send:** launched rigid and straight, arms in to his sides, refusing to flail until the flight turns him.
- **Voice:** a hiss through the teeth, never a cry.
- **Scale:** snap ×1.2, fold ×0.8, step 1.3 bh.

**Settled** (23d item 19): the exact line is allowed.
