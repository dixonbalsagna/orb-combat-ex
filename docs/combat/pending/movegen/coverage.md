## What the generator cannot express

- **A limb it does not have.** The rival's tail strikes (`strike.tail_*`, waiting in today's older pools) need the `own` limb in `parts.json` (design section 7). Until it exists they stay hand-kept.
- **A shape his identity or Legal refuses.** Today's older pools still name pieces his `never` rows or Legal's rows now refuse (above). The generator never offers them; the hand-kept pools are older than the rows.
- **A pool that is not a cell.** The blur patterns, the combo links and accents, and the power pools are hand-kept tables of strike ids from before the generator; they are not written from cells, and `cells.json` `pools` has no rule for them. The pools the brawl reads are all written.
- **Set pieces and frames.** Context actions, signature frames and the specials' frames are lists or frames, not generated (`cells.json`).
