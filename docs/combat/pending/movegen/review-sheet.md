## For Legal's person screen

Legal's banned rows and sequence rules are applied to every strike on this sheet: the martial cells, the checks, the pushes, and the blow of every zip, step and charge. They cannot judge what follows, which is new vocabulary or a set piece (`docs/legal/movegen-screen.md` section 3).

- **martial arts B, the signature:** a person screens the frame and its hand-picked piece (docs/legal/movegen-screen.md section 3).
- **manoeuvre B, the signature:** a person screens the frame and each fighter's version; a terrain art is a new set piece.
- **energy arts B, the signature:** screened as built; a new variant or a new hand is new vocabulary.
- **defensive B, the signature:** a person screens the frame and each fighter's version: the rival catches on his plates, the Protagonist turns it aside with open hands.
- **charging B, the signature:** a person screens it: the charge pose and the release are where the stacking rule bites.
- **New vocabulary:** the travel kinds (zip, zip away, step, charge, pursuit) and their directions; the energy hands, releases, bodies and deliveries; the push; the guard layer; every special's frame, and the Protagonist's three placeholder specials.
- **New grabs:** the aimed throw, the guard throw, the zip tackle and the snatch (condition G1).
