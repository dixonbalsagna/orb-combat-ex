# The Protagonist: his moves on one page

For Orb's yes or no. Combat and Choreography, generated on 2026-10-12 from the parked movesets and recipes. **None of this is in the playable build yet:** today both fighters draw on four old pools that they nearly share. It lands with the recipes window.

**Who he is in a fight.** An earnest martial artist with the widest set of moves in the fight: open hands, the edge of the foot, arcs and turns that come at you from the side. Pointed, not cruel. Each blow goes to one point and stops there, and he is back in his guard at once. The head is the last place he goes.

## What each press throws

Each line names three of the pieces that press draws on, his own first. A star marks a piece only he has. Within a press the game draws evenly, so a player sees them all.

**X, the lights**
- **Tapped:** 24 to draw on, 9 his alone. Back chop to the arm*; Hammer fist to the head*; Rising palm to the jaw*.
- **Tapped with the stick toward the opponent:** 5 to draw on, none his alone. Shoulder check to the chest; Round knee to the legs; Rising elbow to the jaw.
- **Mashed, the flurry:** 11 to draw on, 3 his alone. Rising palm to the chest*; Rising palm to the jaw*; Rising palm to the gut*.
- **Held, the burst's quick first blows:** 8 to draw on, 4 his alone. Rising palm to the chest*; Rising palm to the gut*; Back chop to the arm*.
- **Held, its middle:** 12 to draw on, 4 his alone. The slower gap lets in hooking elbow to the jaw; round knee to the legs; driving knee to the gut.
- **Held, its slow last blows, and the pace it settles to:** 24 to draw on, 9 his alone. The slowest gap lets in hook kick to the chest*; downward chop to the arm*; hammer fist to the head*. His burst ends on an arc.

**Y, the mediums**
- **Tapped or mashed:** 10 to draw on, 3 his alone. Palm strike to the chest*; Crescent kick to the chest*; Rising palm to the jaw*.
- **Held, the charged medium:** 14 to draw on, 4 his alone. It adds the ones that need the longer wind-up: spinning heel kick to the gut*; push kick to the chest; crossed-forearm ram to the chest.
- **Out of a perfect block:** 4 to draw on, 2 his alone. Palm strike to the chest*; Rising palm to the jaw*; Uppercut to the jaw.

**B, the heavies**
- **Tapped, mashed or held:** 10 to draw on, 10 his alone: the 7 launchers listed below, and spinning blade hand to the chest*; falling hammer fist to the arm*; turning knee to the legs*.
- **On a staggered opponent, the launcher:** 7 of those heavies, the ones that read on half the wind-up. Which one depends on the stick (below).

**RB held in reach, the energy pieces**
- **RB + X, the point-blank bolt:** 4 to draw on, 4 his alone. Palm thrust on the chest*; Palm flick on the chest*; Flat palm thrust on the gut*; Flat palm sweep on the gut*.
- **RB + Y, the blast:** 2 to draw on, 2 his alone. Palm blast on the chest*; Palm blast on the gut*.

## Where his launcher sends you

- **Stick up, he lifts you:** heaving palm to the chest; heaving ball of the foot to the gut.
- **Stick down, he drives you under:** falling palm to the chest (both arms).
- **Stick toward, he drives you back:** stepping edge of the foot to the gut; stepping blade hand to the jaw.
- **Stick away, he turns you past him:** unwinding edge of the foot to the gut; turning elbow to the jaw.

## What only he has

30 of his 67 pieces are his alone: his 10 heavies, his 6 energy pieces, 14 lights and mediums. The other 37 he shares with the Anti-hero.
- **The lights and mediums that are his alone:** back chop to the arm; crescent kick to the chest; crescent kick to the gut; overhand punch to the arm; hammer fist to the head; hook kick to the chest; downward chop to the arm; rising palm to the chest; rising palm to the jaw (two of them); palm strike to the chest; rising palm to the gut; inward chop to the head; spinning heel kick to the gut.
- **His energy hands:** palm, ring hand, flat palm.

## What he never does

- He never strikes with a forearm plate. He has none.
- His straight light blows are open hands, never a fist.
- He never brings his whole weight down on a head.

## Three things that set him apart

Counted over the 56 strikes of his three buttons.

- **Open palms.** 10 of his 56 are open palms. The Anti-hero: none of 56.
- **Kicks.** 20 of his 56 are kicks. The Anti-hero: 12 of 56.
- **Arcs.** 21 of his 56 come round in an arc. The Anti-hero: 15 of 56.

**How much of him is drawn.** Across all five stances he has 128 moves: 85 are posed, 28 borrow a nearby pose, and 15 wait to be drawn, his specials mostly.
