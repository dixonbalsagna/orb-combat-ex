# The Anti-hero: his moves on one page

For Orb's yes or no. Combat and Choreography, generated on 2026-10-12 from the parked movesets and recipes. **None of this is in the playable build yet:** today both fighters draw on four old pools that they nearly share. It lands with the recipes window.

**Who he is in a fight.** Brutal and showy. He lets you see it coming: the wind-up held open with his chin up, the blow on the straightest line there is, and the limb left out for a beat after it lands. Anything with weight is a closed fist or a forearm plate. The quick blade hands are only there to keep you busy until it arrives.

## What each press throws

Each line names three of the pieces that press draws on, his own first. A star marks a piece only he has. Within a press the game draws evenly, so a player sees them all.

## Where his launcher sends you

- **Stick up, he lifts you:** heaving elbow to the jaw; heaving fist to the gut.
- **Stick down, he drives you under:** falling heel to the chest; falling hammer fist to the head.
- **Stick toward, he drives you back:** stepping fist to the chest; stepping knee to the gut.
- **Stick away, he turns you past him:** turning forearm plate to the head; turning fist to the jaw.

## What only he has

30 of his 67 pieces are his alone: his 10 heavies, his 6 energy pieces, 14 lights and mediums. The other 37 he shares with the Protagonist.
- **The lights and mediums that are his alone:** downward chop to the arm; dropping elbow to the chest; overhand punch to the head; uppercut to the chest; backfist to the arm; forearm drop to the arm; uppercut to the gut; backhand hammer fist to the arm; rising knee to the gut; push kick to the legs; forearm drop to the chest; forearm drop to the head; spear hand to the chest; uppercut to the jaw.
- **His energy hands:** blade hand, lit fist, pinch, clawed palm, open palm.

## What he never does

- Anything with weight is a fist or a plate, never a blade hand.
- He never strikes with an open palm.
- He never swings a kick outward. The crescent and the hook kick are the Protagonist's.

## Three things that set him apart

- **Closed fists.** 12 of his 56 are closed fists. The Protagonist: 2 of 56.
- **The head.** 20 of his 56 go to the head or the jaw. The Protagonist: 15 of 56.
- **Dropping blows.** 13 of his 56 come down from above. The Protagonist: 9 of 56.
