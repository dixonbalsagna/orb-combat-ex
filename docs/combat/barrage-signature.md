# The Anti-hero's barrage as his signature: what it needs

Owner: Combat and Choreography. Date: 2026-10-12. A sketch on paper, half a page, no build. The signatures are a question with Orb; this is what a yes would ask of Encounter and VFX.

**What it is.** Where the Protagonist fires one beam, the Anti-hero walks you down. Three volleys from a slow walk forward, each thrown by one hand in one motion and each a different motion: a sweep that fans five shards, a thrust that sends three in a line, a flick that sends one shot that splits. If it pins you, he closes the last of the distance and ends it by hand. That last blow is the point of him: he bombards until you give way, and then he finishes it himself.

**The pieces exist.** The shards, the fan and the splitting shot are shot kinds today. His release poses and the charged brace are drawn. The shape is already written as his finisher's opening (`launch-pair-movesets.md`: three volleys, then the walk in and the blow by hand).

**From Encounter.**
- **A second kind of signature,** at the beam's price and the beam's tiers: a schedule of volleys in place of one beam. First values: volleys at 12, 36 and 60 ticks, 24 apart.
- **Each volley counts as one landed shot,** as a split's shards already do, so the three can close with the barrage's knock-back and never with a launch.
- **The defender's answers, as the beam has them:** a guard takes chip and is pushed a step each volley; a dodge beats one volley and the next is aimed where he went; an answering beam or blast is a clash, volley by volley; an escape is his gamble as it is against the beam.
- **He can be hit between volleys.** He is walking, not braced. That is the difference in risk from the beam, and what the price buys the rival.
- **The close by hand.** When two of the three land, or the third knocks him back, the director sends the Anti-hero in for one heavy from his own pool. When fewer land, he is left in his recovery.
- **By place,** as every signature adapts: over a town the misses wreck what is behind the rival, which is his pride and nobody's mercy; over water and open ground the fan is wider.

**From VFX.**
- **Three shapes a player can tell apart at the fight's zoom:** a fan, a line, a shot that bursts. Thin edges and shards in his colour, never a ball.
- **The hand lit as an edge** before each release, along the blade hand or the knuckle edges of the lit fist.
- **Three volleys inside the flash rule.** At 24 ticks apart the three fall inside one second (48 ticks from the first to the last), and the release rule does not pass at exactly three flashes a second. So one full flash, on the last volley, and two lesser ones; or the volleys spaced wider.
- **No whole-screen flash,** and a rim on the rival when a volley lands.

**From the others.** Audio: no shouted syllable repeats across the three volleys. Animation: nothing new for the volleys; the walk between them is the one new thing.

**Legal first.** Its rules already shape this: each volley is one motion of one hand, never two palms in turn and never one hand pumping, and the three volleys differ in hand or release (`docs/legal/movegen-banned.json` e05 and e06). A signature built on them still wants Legal's screen as a whole before anything is built.
