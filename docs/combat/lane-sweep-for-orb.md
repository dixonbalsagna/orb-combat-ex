# LANE SWEEP: the village's own signature

1. Today a signature fired over a village plays exactly as it does over the city: the beam tears up through the buildings behind the defender.
2. LANE SWEEP would give villages their own version: the beam grazes low and wide along the lane, partly flattening a whole row of houses instead of gutting one, so the scar runs along the ground where the city's runs up through the towers.
3. It needs your yes and Legal's clearance of the name. Game Design is setting how many people it costs, so it still weighs on the Protagonist, and the village stays as it is until then.
