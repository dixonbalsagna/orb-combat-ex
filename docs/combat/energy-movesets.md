# Energy movesets: four fighters, and how the director composes energy exchanges

Owner: Combat and Choreography. Date: 2026-10-23. Paper and parked data (`pending/energy/`); nothing is built.

**Orb** ("Energy attacks overhaul", `docs/ep/vision.md`, eab5a846) asked for:
- energy attacks per fighter along light, medium, heavy and grab: rapid-fire small shots, explosive lobs, the most devastating techniques, and a grab each fighter treats differently ("maybe someone has an energy net, someone has an energy ring");
- guard combined with energy leaving mines or traps;
- a choice between beam and blast;
- chargeable beams of each fighter's own, replacing the one shared beam;
- the light's rapid fire carried into both.

**The frame is Game Design's** (`docs/design/energy-overhaul.md`, E1 to E117). Its grammar, numbers and ki are the same for every fighter (E10). This page and the four kits fill in only what E81 leaves to a fighter, inside its bands. Narrative's provisional names (`docs/narrative/energy-names.md`) are the starting points for every name.

## 1. The frame, in one table (Game Design's E15)

| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tap** | A bolt, at once | A lob on a 36-tick seeking arc, bursting over 1.5 bh | The heavy blast | The grab, thrown |
| **Mash** | One bolt a press, one per 6 ticks at most | A barrage, one lob per 12 ticks | One heavy blast per 24 ticks | (In a catch, A is the payoff) |
| **Hold** | The stream | Charges the lob: level 1, then the fighter's own level 2 | Charges the beam through three levels (30, 54, 84 ticks) | Charges or aims the fighter's own variant |
| **While his beam is out** | | | Each press a pulse; held, a pulse every 8 ticks | |
| **With LB held** | A spark mine | A blast mine | His trap | His snare |

**Beam or blast is how long B is held:** let go before level 1 and it is the blast. Level 3 carries him along the beam. The biome variants are every beam's aftermath at level 2 and up (E114).

## 3. How the director composes energy exchanges

The rule of the brawl holds: **each press is one request, and the director composes the exchange** from the attacker's move, the defender's state and what is in the world. The defender's answers are his own presses. No energy move takes a player's hands beyond its hit-stop and reel (E1).

### 3.1 What meets what (Game Design's trade powers)

The trade powers: a bolt is 1; a lob 3, with level 1 at 4 and level 2 at 5; the heavy blast 4; a beam 6, 9 or 12 by level.

| Incoming, against | A bolt or the stream | A lob | The heavy blast | A beam |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A bolt or the stream** | **Trade:** bolts cancel in pairs (E22) | The lob goes off where they meet, by trade power (E34) | The blast eats four bolts and goes on (E41) | Eaten; the beam loses that trade power (E48) |
| **A lob** | — | Both go off where they meet | The blast eats one lob and goes on | Eaten, as above |
| **The heavy blast** | — | — | The bigger power goes on, at its remainder | It bursts on the beam and pushes its front back 1 bh (E90) |
| **A beam** | — | — | — | **The struggle** (E90 to E97) |

**The director's part is the staging, not the rule:**
- It times a trade so the meeting point sits between the bodies, where the camera can see it.
- It sends what is left of the winner on to its target.
- It lets each fighter's struggle look show: a wall pushes, a sheaf of straight lances presses and tightens, a ragged beam sputters and surges. No struggle look rotates, spirals or drills (Legal e11).

The losing beam bulges and quivers as it buckles, and never thins (Orb, 2026-10-22).

### 3.2 The rapid fire, carried in

- **Projectiles:** a held X is the stream, and a mashed Y the barrage. Each shot costs its ki, and both stop when the button is let go or the presses stop.
- **The barrage's flashes:** at 12 ticks a barrage makes 5 bursts a second, so the 4th and 5th in a second draw as smoke, dust and ember, or the bursts share one glow that never falls 10% between them (Legal fx09).
- **The beam:** each press of B while it is out is a pulse, a surge that runs to the front (E50). Held, it pulses every 8 ticks at 85%.
- **A struggle** counts a pulse on its beat (every 15 ticks, 2 ticks either side) double, and no press voids anything (E92). Mashing pushes, and timing pushes harder.
- **The pulse's look is each fighter's own,** and the rule is the same for all.

### 3.3 Grabs as openers (Game Design's E60 to E69)

Every energy grab shares the frame:
- a throw that seeks and arrives within 30 ticks;
- a catch of at most 40 ticks;
- a way out by the defender's own presses, each taking 4 ticks off the hold, or RT's burst after 12 ticks;
- a payoff the grabber picks by pressing A. Pressing X, Y or B instead fires that shot into the held foe.

| Fighter | The look | The payoff the director stages |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Protagonist** | A net: wide and slow, a long catch | **The yank:** the net is hauled in, and the brawl starts with his blow at the arrival |
| **The Rival** | A ring: a thin loop that flies facing forward at hand height and snaps shut round arms and torso. It never spins like a disc, is never raised overhead, and never cuts (Legal e12) | **The bind:** the foe is held where he is, and the Rival's next shot lands as on an open fighter |
| **The Empress** | A cage: four banner staves stamped into the ground round him, their banners wrapping his arms and torso | **The slam:** he is dragged down into the ground under him and lands kneeling: a down send in place, under the spike's rules. The kneel is the landing, never a held humiliation (Legal e12) |
| **The Demon Cyborg** | A lash: a crane hook on a line | **The throw:** a swing on the line flings him 6 bh along the stick |

**Four looks and four payoffs, one of each, so no two grabs read alike.** Every bind takes arms, torso or body, never throat, face or hair. The held fighter struggles or braces, and nothing slows, zooms, taunts or finishes over a bound fighter (Legal e12). **One call-out per grab** (ln06): on the throw or in the first 10 ticks of the catch, inside the hit-stop and never extending it, and nothing said over the rest of the catch, the payoff or a kneel. The Empress's staves are never joined by light into a box, a barrier or a dome (e16). The answers are the frame's (E62): a dodge tap in the throw's last 8 ticks, a shot of trade power 2 or more meeting it in flight, or a blow on the grabber in his wind-up.

### 3.4 Traps as defence that bites (Game Design's E70 to E79)

A trap is laid from the guard (LB + RB), so the director stages the laying as a **guard beat**: the guard is up, and the free hand sets it in the world. The guard drops only for the lay's own frames (E72).

| Fighter | His trap (B with LB) | What it does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Protagonist** | A pillar | A solid slab of ground or stone a few bh tall rises over several ticks and throws whoever trips it up and away (never a light beam, never around a body; Legal e14). It moves the fight off people, and as the carer's it holds while a person is in its burst (E79) |
| **The Rival** | A pressure plate | Crossed, it fires a fan of needles up from the ground: area denial |
| **The Empress** | A line | Crossed, it fires her level-1 beam along itself |
| **The Demon Cyborg** | A field | **Scrap Pit:** clamps and plates around a pit that holds and drops a passing fighter (no chewing, no teeth; Legal e14) |

The spark and blast mines (X and Y with LB) are everyone's, and only their look is a fighter's. The snare (A with LB) is each fighter's grab, laid.

**How traps compose the next exchanges:**
- **Tripped:** walked, dashed or knocked into. Every trap flares for 6 ticks first: the answer window (E74). A trap's throw is a knock-back, never a send, at most 6 bh on either axis, and no trap goes through a guard (§20 items 2 and 9).
- **Shot:** a shot that touches a trap sets it off where it is (E78), so a volley can clear a path.
- **Fed by a send:** a send that carries a fighter through an armed mine or trap sets it off at once with no flare, and that includes its layer (E74).
  - **TRAP LANDING, accepted by Game Design (§20 item 12):** the launch planner scores a send of the foe onto the launcher's own armed trap, at base 12 plus the trap's bonus, inside the stick's win and freshness by kind.
  - It is how the director composes "defence that bites" into a combo.
- **Limits:** 4 mines, 1 trap and 1 snare per fighter. Laying another fizzles the oldest, and an armed mine fizzles after 12 s (E76).

### 3.5 The beats the camera can frame

No energy move has a pause or a camera of its own (E4). The camera eases to frame each move's three beats, and an event is sent at each:
- **The anticipation:** the gather in the hand, each charge level a step brighter with a click and a rumble tick (`energy_charge`, with the level).
- **The impact:** the release, the catch or the flare (`energy_release`, `grab_catch`, `trap_fire`).
- **The follow-through:** the burst, the knock-back, the carry of a level-3 beam, or the trap's effect.

Nothing is drawn on, over or between the fighters (E6): no aim line, no charge bar and no mine marker. A charge reads in the hand, and a mine reads in the world.

### 3.6 The hero and the villains

The director aims within the stick's cone:
- **The Protagonist's energy leans away from people:** lobs angled high, and a beam tipped skyward when people stand behind the target. Near people his beam narrows to 0.6 of its width and his lob's burst to 0.7 (E113).
- **The villains lean through people:** their beam and lob widen to 1.3, and their traps sit where people will run.

The collateral budget caps every death (E110). The hero's casualties feed his anguish, and the villains' feed their menace or hunger. The Cyborg's whole set runs on wreckage and metal, never on people (Legal).

### 3.7 The AI

The AI uses every cell the player has (E18), by its personality and level, with the same answers. The hero's AI lays traps to shield a settlement, and the villains' lay them where people run. Every share is QA's band to set.

## 4. The data and its schema

| File (parked in `pending/energy/`) | Target | Schema |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `energy.protagonist.json`, `energy.rival.json`, `energy.empress.json`, `energy.cyborg.json` | `data/combat/energy/<fighter>.json` | **`combat.energy/1`**, draft `combat-energy.schema.json` |

**What the schema enforces:**
- the bolt's speed and damage multipliers, within 20% and 15%;
- the lob's arc (36 ± 6 ticks) and its levels (28 and 48, ± 10%);
- the beam's levels (30, 54 and 84, ± 10%), its damage and its length;
- a struggle trait's push, which is always 1 (E96);
- the grab's arrival (30 ticks) and catch (40 ticks);
- the templates: grab looks and payoffs, level-2 forms and traps.

The top level is closed, and each cell is open past its checked keys, because each fighter names his own look.

**Proven with Tools' validator** in a scratch root, with the draft and a map row added:
- all four kits read 0 errors and 0 warnings;
- four refusals fail at their pointer: a bolt speed past its band, a "drain" payoff, a trait that pushes, and a 45-tick catch.

**Cross-checked:**
- every atom a cell names is defined;
- the names are Narrative's;
- the templates are the README's;
- every pair differs in five cells.
