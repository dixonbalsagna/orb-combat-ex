# Collapse flash attribution (Rendering, 2026-11-23): why the collapse clip fails at small and tiny, and passes on main's medium

Builds: the GW1c tree (main 74c03fdb + World's stage 2 + planet-sizes + drawn-at + relief-snow; cap off unless said) and main 2e3407dc for medium.
Readings: Tools' run-pixels, tiles on (the default since 2026-11-13), worst of 4 readings unless said "960 only". Flash readings are for the EP only.

## Medium on main 2e3407dc (Orb's current default)
- collapse-normal PASS 2, collapse-reduced PASS 2 (worst of 4). The old rushcity stretch (ticks 1450 to 1475) reads 2. The 4 quoted earlier was on
  older commits (calm-tones, e56d372c/ed1591cb). Not the cause below; no fix needed.

## Small (cap off): collapse-reduced FAIL 7 (normal FAIL 5); cap on: reduced FAIL 4
- The worst second (ticks 1016 to 1069) is near ground in front of the line flipping between two looks in big patches every 1 to 3 ticks.
- The same build is bimodal run to run: 3 of 3, then 2 of 3, failing runs read exactly the same 7 events; the others read 2 to 2.5. Single runs of a
  switch therefore prove nothing; several "fixes" seen once were clean runs (the anchored foreground line, nocover, the rim rule, the held re-check).
- A failing run against a passing run of the same build, same tick: the failing run draws the near ground from coarse data (big smooth facets),
  the passing run from fine data (many small facets). Debug views of the tone, the spared marks, the bowls and the rows held still; the street-paint
  switch (paint only on slopes under 0.45) flipped on exactly the flicker ticks over the big coarse facets.
- Headless at tick 1016 (the same state as the failing runs): the tile pool is 142 of 800 layers, yet 60 of 67 near tiles draw level-2 data (128-unit
  nodes) for their level 3 and 4 meshes. A forced fill of 80 tiles takes that from 60 to 29; on medium, 57 fills take it from 26 (level-3 data) to 0.
  A tile costs about the same at both sizes (about 25 ms); the queue is the difference: the fill takes the coarsest wanted level first, and small
  wants many more tiles before the near ones.
- Cause: on small (and tiny) the near ground is drawn from 128-unit data for long stretches, because its finer tiles wait behind every coarser one in
  the fill queue; its big flat facets near the lens cross the look's hard steps (street paint by slope, tone, rock) in big patches as the camera moves.
  Whether a clip's stretch comes before or after the fill catches up depends on timing, hence the bimodal runs. Owner: Rendering (the tile fill,
  render/core/tile_field.gd, under planet-sizes).

## Tiny: cap off normal FAIL 3.5, reduced PASS 2.5; cap on normal FAIL 6, reduced FAIL 8
- Headless at tick 760: 53 near tiles drawing coarser data than their level (mostly level 2). The same mechanism; not confirmed by repeated runs.

## Ruled out along the way (each by repeated or headless evidence)
- Tile level changes (the tile set is constant through the flicker), the crater bowls' selection (all 11 drawn every tick), the cover (FAIL 7 with
  nocover on a full run), the foreground tone (FAIL 7 with nofore), the morph (FAIL 7), the anchored foreground line (full clip FAIL 7; its stretch
  pass was a skip run that starts with the fill caught up).

## Side finding (a separate bug, not this flicker)
- Two craters dug at tick 0 (x 40,510 and 41,410) are drawn twice, in the strip's rows and as the store's bowls: the ground's first build runs before
  the tile field is attached, so GroundField._store_holds finds no tile field and never holds them. A re-check as the store grows fixes it headless
  (scratch held_recheck.py); not parked.

## Update (2026-11-23, later): the coarse-data reading was wrong; the cause is still open
- near-fill (the near ground first in the fill, a capture's slice fixed at 5,000 us): small, cap off, reduced, 3 runs: FAIL 7, 7, 7 (the control the
  same session: PASS 2.5, FAIL 6, FAIL 7). Not a fix.
- An on-screen label of the tile fill in the web build (scratch dbg_label.py), 3 runs: the pool 80 to 118 of 800, the slice 4,500 us, two panes (the
  split view) in every run. Near tiles on coarser data than their level at tick 1015: the passing run 33 of 49, the failing runs 26 of 43 and 54 of 69.
  So coarse data doesn't separate a failing run from a passing one. The tile layout does differ (the first pane at tick 540: 26 tiles, 13 on coarse
  data, with zigzag shapes on the near ground, against 14 and 0), set by when data arrives against the camera's moves; the runs part from about tick 140.
- No mesh finer than its data at any level (a split waits for its children's data), 3 runs: FAIL 3, 7, 3.5, with 0 coarse tiles in the label. Not a fix.
- What stands: the flicker is the near ground in front of the line (big facets near the lens, over the collapse's crater rims) flipping in big patches;
  whether a run has it is decided by timing early in the clip, about 2 runs in 3 at small. Single runs of a switch prove nothing until a capture's
  tile fill is reproducible (its slice is wall-clock time).
