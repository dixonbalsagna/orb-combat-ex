# The whip haze (parked, 2026-10-15)

The fallback for signaturecity if Camera's whip-pan speed cap does not clear it (Orb: "Camera's cap first"). In every match, while a pane's camera travels along the ground faster than 1.5 screen widths a second (whole at 2.5), every building melts into the sky and ground behind it: the plain wall in place of its windows and marks, then fully the colour behind. It comes in over 0.1 s and clears over 0.6 s. A rush under 1.5 widths a second is untouched. The whole town's pan haze (`--panhaze`, 70%, from 0.35 widths a second) stays behind its switch at its own strength.

- **Files:** `render/core/look.gd` (`PAN_WHIP_ON`, `PAN_WHIP`, `PAN_WHIP_MIX`), `render/core/pane_world.gd` (`pan_whip`), `render/core/mats.gd` (sends it), `render/core/planet_view.gd` (`pan_whip_mix`), `render/shaders/building.gdshader`, `render/tools/flash_reg_check.gd` (three checks: on with the switch off; none in a rush of a screen width a second; whole within 6 ticks of four; a cut leaves it; one fall; both building materials hold its strength). `whip-haze.patch` is against main d3cd19d5; check it with `git apply --check` before applying.
- **Gated** on d3cd19d5 with the patch: all 15 of render's gates pass (flash register check 35).
- **Measured:** a build with the same whip haze and the front-row form read signaturecity 2 / 3 / 2 normal and 2 / 2 / 2 reduced (main 4 / 5 / 4 and 4 / 4 / 4). The patch alone has not been captured. Before shipping: signaturecity both modes, collapse normal, ai-4, ai-38 and ai-39 normal, and clash and signature both modes (they launch), on Tools' path.
- **The look:** in a launch or a whip through a city, the town is gone into its sky and ground for the length of the whip, and comes back over about half a second.
