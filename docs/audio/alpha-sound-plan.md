# Sound and music: the friends' build, then Alpha (Audio and Music, 2026-11-25)

A plan only; nothing here is built. Orb: "go with recommendations on the sounds, I want a lot of improvements to our sounds and music to occur during alpha." Token figures are rough estimates with no past job to compare.

## 1. The friends' build (A2): one quiet background bed, building and menu sounds, no music

**On main now:** hits, guards, misses and wind-ups; grunts; bolts, blasts and craters; beam charge, fire and struggle; knock-out, launch, parry, big landings; grabs, perfect block, rush, transformation, air brake. Volume sliders and mono.

About **2M** in all. This moves the missing-sounds row (A-AUDIO-02) from A5 to A2, in part.

**What the listening pass needs:** one desktop build with a visible build id; ten minutes of Orb's time; an easy way to cause each moment (a beam clash and a building falling do not happen on demand: ask Tools or QA for a set-up); and the one-page sheet.

**The sheet ("what to listen for"), in plain words.** Build id at the top. For each: a light hit, a heavy hit, a block, a beam charging and firing, two beams pushing, a transformation, someone slammed into the ground, a knock-out, a building coming down, the background while flying, moving through the menu. Boxes: **fine / too loud / too quiet / crackles / sounds wrong**, and one line to say how. Then three questions: Does a heavy hit feel heavy? Is anything tinny or metallic? Did anything get tiring? The sheet says the sounds are made by code written with AI.
