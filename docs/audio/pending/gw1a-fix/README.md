# Park: the bed's gain and pitch are in range (Audio and Music, 2026-11-14, GW1a blocker)

**Cause.** `drone.bed.hi` is the beam struggle's high drone. Its data is designed to run from -28 to -10 dB with the point's place and up to +5 semitones (`audio/data/tug_cues.json` `bed`), and its pitch/gain updates ride on the point. The two checks' generic "every cue in range" rule allowed -20 to +8 dB, which holds for hits and voices but not for a bed. On main's sim data no beam struggle ever happens (the struggle is off there), so the beds never reached those checks and they passed. On the 9 November fight data the struggle runs in the seeded matches, so a bed's update (-20.3 and -20.4 dB, pitch 1.14 to 1.15, both inside the bed's own design range) tripped the generic floor. Neither my sets nor the fight data push the cue out of its design range: the cue is right, the check's range was wrong for a bed. `host_check.gd` already had the right rule for loops and ambience (floor -40) but missed updates.

**Fix** (`range.patch`, tests only, four files, applies to the s422 copy with `git apply --check` and is in the working tree): the floor is -40 dB for a looped cue, an ambient cue and an **update** cue (the struggle bed's moves), and -20 dB for everything else, in `moments_check.gd`, `coop_check.gd`, `cue_sweep.gd` and `host_check.gd` (the last two only for the same reason; neither failed). No sound or data changed.

**Proof** (`docs/audio/runs/51b840f5/gw1a-fix/`): on a copy of the GW1a stack (s422): both checks failed before with the two quoted lines (`*-before.txt`) and pass after (`*-after.txt`), as do `host_check` and `sets_check` (records kept) and `tug_check`, `carry_check`, `status_check` and `web_policy_check` (exit 0, not kept). The copy holds s422's sim, data, audio and net folders, the part these checks read. On main (HEAD plus the A1 working-tree files) `moments_check` and `coop_check` pass. Headless, one Godot process at a time.
