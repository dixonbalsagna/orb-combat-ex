# Rulings, 2026-10-30 (b): what opens the damage inset

Owner: Game Design. Design only, no build. Camera built Orb's ask ("dodging a beam that hits a skyscraper behind you should give a live picture-in-picture view of the damage, depending on how significant it is"; `docs/ep/vision.md`) behind a switch, off (`docs/camera/pending/damage-inset/README.md`). In 12 AI matches it never opened.

**Why it stays shut.** From reading the code, not from a measurement. Most signatures start at brawl range (signature was the top brawl ending at 9.43 a match in `docs/qa/chain-3198301f.md`, an older build). There the attacker rises only 90 units (`templates.json`, the rise is `dist x 0.22` clamped to 90 to 300), so the beam comes down steeply and is in the ground just past a dodger at ground level. So it can only damage buildings near him, and today's rule calls any building 60 px wide with its centre in the pane "already in the picture". At the fight's framing that is nearly every building near him. The AI's dodge count (one in 12 matches) is a separate problem: it says nothing about how often a person would dodge.

| 1 | **The in-picture rule** | **Change it. This is the main fix.** "Already in the picture" means the player can already see the damage well, not that the building is somewhere on screen. So the gate stays shut only when the centre is in the pane **and** the building is drawn at half the pane's height or more. A tower small in a wide shot, or one off the top of a close shot, opens it. A collapse that fills the view still doesn't. No sim change and no hash change | `seenShare` 0.5, replacing `visibleMinPx` | Camera |
| 2 | **Which outcomes count** | **Every beam the defender avoided that carries on: DODGE, ESCAPE and DEFLECT.** The build already takes the first two. A perfect block's swat and split send the beam into the world, which is the same picture, earned harder. The swat's beam belongs to the defender (`beamplay.gd` 288) and leaves on a new line, so the check must read the live fork's line and accept that owner. HIT, GUARD and CLASH never open it | `avoided`: those three kinds | Camera |
| 3 | **The beam's reach past the dodger** | **Not now.** Longer reach is a sim change: it moves the replay hash and the collateral bands, and it makes every dodge cost the town behind him, which is a new rule rather than a camera fix. It's the one lever held in reserve (row 7). **Camera's `reach` (400) isn't a design lever.** It only decides which damage counts as this beam's, and it stays at whatever credits all of a dodged beam's damage. The blast's radius is about 336 units at tier 2, and it keeps biting below ground past the point where the line meets it, so Camera checks that 400 doesn't lose real hits | none | Camera (the check) |
| 4 | **Other triggers** | **None in this version.** A blow's building is already in the picture, and a corner picture in the middle of a brawl takes the eye off the play (control first). A beam avoided is the one moment with a beat to spare, and it's what Orb asked for. The significance score stays as built (open at 1.0, big at 2.0), so a scratch on a tower opens nothing, which is Orb's "depending on how significant". It reads the building only and never casualties (Legal c08) | none | none |
| 5 | **The target rate** | **About one a match: 0.5 to 2, in a fight of 5 to 8 minutes, for a player who avoids a third of the beams.** Rare enough to be an event, and common enough that a player who fights near towns sees it. **Camera's 0.5 to 1.5 a minute goes:** that's 3 to 12 a match, about as many as there are signatures (the 9.43 above), and it can't be met from avoided beams. **Camera reads it:** at least 30 matches on a scripted defender that avoids a third of the beams, because the AI's own rate can't measure this. Gen 1 and gen 2 are read separately, since the buildings differ. The ration (15 s apart) stays as built and shouldn't be what holds the rate down | the band, in `balance-targets.md` when it goes on | Camera, QA |
| 6 | **Under Reduce flashing (the default)** | **Live, if it passes.** Orb asked for a live view, and a held frame is what most players would otherwise get. A falling tower moves; a still of one for up to 6 s doesn't say "falling". **The price:** VFX's calm flag (`inset_calm`) on the inset's view, then three readings: the flash set must show the inset adding nothing to the count against the same clip with it off, in both modes (the test Camera already used), the look gate (ADR 0010), and Performance on the laptop budget (ADR 0011), since a live inset is one more world render for up to 6 s and Orb's rule is no frame drops. **If a reading fails, the still is the fallback, and that goes to Orb** rather than being decided here. Reduced motion, the `camera_panels` still setting and VFX's lowest quality stay still whatever this says | `flash.stillWhenReduced`: false once the readings pass | VFX, Tools, Performance, Camera |
| 7 | **If row 5 reads under 0.5** | **Lower `seenShare` is the wrong way to go, so it comes back here.** The next lever is the reach of row 3, or a fall of any cause out of the picture. Either would be a ruling of its own with QA's collateral read, and neither is designed yet | none | Game Design |

**Order.** Rows 1 and 2 are small edits to Camera's park, so the rate can be read before anyone spends VFX work on row 6. Orb sees it, live, before the default flips: Camera has flagged that the picture is a first look, with fighters crossing it as dark blobs and one stage of damage hard to see.
