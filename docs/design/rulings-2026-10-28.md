# Rulings, 2026-10-28: buildings on a planet ring, and the biome launches' bands

Owner: Game Design. **One decision per item, routed by owner.** Paper only; every number is a first value.

## 1. Structures on a planet ring (QA's gen 2 baseline, `docs/qa/cloud-reports/2026-10-28-gen2-baseline.md`)

A 2,400-column ring stands about 40 structures (38.5 on gen 2, 43.4 on gen 1 rings), against the hand-made world's 118. Structures lost at the KO read 59.9% against 31.5%, and structures levelled a minute at tier 2 read 4.01% (band at most 4%). In absolute terms a ring fight levels about 23 structures a match against the hand-made world's 37. Rings with 50 or more structures read 2.85 to 3.94% a minute, and planet 11, with 8, loses 81% of them.

| # | The question | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Re-base the share bands (15.str, §21) for a ring, or stand more buildings on it?** | **More buildings. The bands stay as written.** A ring is the world the player loops, so a share of its structures is still the honest measure of "no fight destroys the planet at low tiers". Re-basing would accept a ring that is 60% gone at the KO, or 81% on a sparse planet. **The real cost is escalation with nothing left to break.** A ring fight levels fewer buildings than the hand-made world (about 23 against 37) and runs short of them before tiers 3 and 4, when pillar 4 needs them most. More buildings also give BUILDING SMASH and the rally's world links their places, and more walls to stop a long journey (QA's journeys row, 20.8% against at most 20%). **The target: at least 60 structures on a Half ring (2,400 columns), aiming for 80. A ring of another length scales with its columns, never under 40.** QA's own rings with 50 or more already pass the tier-2 row, and the hand-made world runs 118, so 80 is within what the game draws today. **How is World's call.** My preference is that settlements stand more (denser towns and cities, outskirts that read as suburbs) rather than more sites, so the route's site rules hold. The kinds stay as today (Legal c08: no schools, hospitals or places of worship as targets). **QA re-reads,** on the new rings: the structure rows, the tier-1 and tier-2 rates, long journeys and civilians lost | World, then QA, Performance |

## 2. The biome launches (Encounter's W3, `docs/director/cloud-reports/2026-10-28-biomes-w3.md`)

All eight candidates sit behind `biome.enabled` (off). In a 192-match batch with it on, no candidate takes over 30% of launches. TREE LINE is taken in 37% of the launches where it is offered, PROP SMASH in 1.6% (band 5 to 30%, `rulings-2026-10-23b.md` item 1), and MARSH SWALLOW was offered 7 times. ICE SKID and VENT LIFT were never offered in the batch.

| # | The question | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | **VENT LIFT's `onUppercut: true`:** it scores on top of UPPERCUT's own score there (alone, its 12 never beat the uppercut's 22 near the ground: 0 of 16; with it, 4 of 16) | **Confirmed.** VENT LIFT is an uppercut over a vent, with the landscape answering back, so the vent's value is an addition to the uppercut, not a rival to it. 4 of 16 is inside the band. The stick still wins: a held stick that isn't up never becomes VENT LIFT (23b item 1) | Combat, Encounter |
| 3 | **TREE LINE and PROP SMASH: tune now, or after World's flight hooks (W6)?** | **Tune the pick now, and read again before the switch goes on.** The band is a share of picks, and the planner's pick doesn't wait for the flight. The hooks change what follows a pick, not the pick itself. **TREE LINE:** `perTree` from 2 to 1.5 (13 to 19 for two to six trunks, from 14 to 22). **PROP SMASH:** fix what it can see before its score. Its `props` list names `outcrop`, which matches no World kind, so it becomes World's own rock kinds (`lichen_rock`, `lava_rock`). Bergs and lone trees wait for World's provisional props. Its score stays at 10 until it can find its props. **The rare ones** (MARSH SWALLOW, ICE SKID, VENT LIFT) are read with seeded slices placed over mud, sea ice and a vent, not tuned for how rarely the AI fights there. **Before `biome.enabled` goes on,** QA reads every candidate's band again with World's hooks in | Combat (data), Encounter, QA |
