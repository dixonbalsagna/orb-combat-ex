## 2. The rally's first read, and right-stick v2's timing

### 2a. Encounter's first read of the rally (`rally.enabled`)

**The read:** dazes 24.5 a match (band 2 to 8), rallies 14 (3 to 10), a median of 0 links a rally (2 to 4), 3.6% of rallies turning (15 to 40), and the lit-B newcomer winning 88 of 100 against medium (10 to 50). **The cause of the links' zero:** the attacker stays locked in the exchange after his own send, so the body lands before any chase can start.

| # | The question | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 7 | **Free the attacker after the rally's opening send?** | **Yes.** The send's contact and its hit-stop end the exchange for him. A link press made during the hit-stop is queued and starts on his first free tick, inside the glide, which exists for exactly this (`fight-feel.md` §1). **Each link's own send frees him the same way,** so links can chain | Encounter |
| 8 | **Should a perfect block daze only when it turns a heavy or an ender?** | **Yes.** A perfect block dazes only when it turns a heavy, a charged blow or a string's ender. A perfect block of a light or a medium is the reversal as before, a plain opening. **A second source is tightened too, for the newcomer:** a heavy's whiff dazes only when the punish is a tapped medium or heavy, or a perfect follow-up, and never a mashed light. That holds 23d's own rule that mashing alone never dazes. 23d item 1 is amended to match | Encounter |
| 9 | **Which band gives way, the turn's or the links'?** | **Neither, yet.** With no links, there was nothing to tech, so the turn's 3.6% says little. The two fit together: with a medium defender's tech landing on about **15 to 20% of links,** and about half of those counter-rushes landing, a rally of three links turns about a quarter of the time. Tune Encounter's AI tech share to that. **If they still pull apart, the links' band holds,** because Orb asked for the attacker to "continue to bounce him around". The turn's band then widens to 10 to 40% for AI v AI, since players turn more than the AI does | Encounter, QA |
| 10 | **Should the AI land a sold blow or two before it presses B?** | **Yes, the sell is the point.** The AI lands at least one sold blow (two on medium and hard) before B, and presses B with at least 8 ticks of daze left. On easy, it may press B at once | Encounter |
| 11 | **Should QA's full-launch row count a rally's links?** | **No. A rally is one full launch:** its opening send. Its links are their own row (links a rally, a median of 2 to 4). So the full-launch row reads opening sends only | QA |
| 12 | **The newcomer's 88 of 100** | **Read it again after items 7 and 8.** If he still wins over half against medium, Encounter reports his dazes and rallies by source. Any daze his line (mash, then B) reaches is a reading of 23d item 1 to close, since mashing alone never dazes | Encounter, QA |

### 2b. Right-stick v2's timing rules (Research's prototype)
