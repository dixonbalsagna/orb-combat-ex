# Rulings, 2026-10-26: the campaign's route and the liftable wreck

Owner: Game Design. **One decision per item, routed by owner.** Paper only; every number is a first value. It answers World's `docs/world/campaign-route.md` and `docs/world/wreck-toss.md`, for `campaign.md` §2.3 and `rulings-2026-10-23d.md` item 20.

| # | The question | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **The flight speed behind "10 to 25 s of boosted flight."** World read it at the melee dash (1,032 u/s) without the traversal multiplier, which gives 322 to 806 columns each way. With the multiplier a lap takes 15 s, so no place could be 10 to 25 s away | **Confirmed, and the band is now stated in columns: a column's places lie 322 to 806 columns from the pair, each way.** The seconds were a way of saying "a real choice of direction, never a long trip". At the speed players actually travel, the trip is a few seconds, and that is welcome: a partner who finishes first reaches the other's stage quickly (`campaign.md` §3.2). **Columns 1 to 4 run on Half rings** (2,400 columns), where the band fits well. On a Close ring the band ends at the far side, 600. `campaign.md` §2.3 is amended to read in columns | World |
| 2 | **A side story finds no place in reach** (11 of 32 test rings) | **Widen first, then turn.** First, any named place on the current ring qualifies, since at travel speed even the far side is a short trip. If the ring holds none, the ring turns at the episode's end, a calm moment, to bring one along it. That is a campaign turn, outside the fight's four. **Never an unnamed site,** since the templates read the place's name (`side_place`). If both steps fail, nothing is offered and the draw is spent, as World has it | World |
| 3 | **Wreck Toss's lift by tier,** on World's wreck masses (houses 0.5 to 6, median 1.1; towers 2 to 57, median 16). World's placeholders were [0.8, 2, 5, 14] | **[2, 6, 16, 57].** Tier 1 lifts most house wrecks. Tier 2 lifts any house and the smallest tower pieces. Tier 3 lifts a median tower piece. Tier 4 lifts any piece, the rule of cool's tower chunk. It follows the throwables' ladder in spirit (a sedan, a bus, then ships), and **Combat checks it against the throwables' table** so a tier lifts things of comparable heft. Wrecks are his identity, so he should find one often from tier 1. What the hit does to a body stays the throwables' rule, damage by size, and the burst's damage to structures stays World's brunt ladder with the tier factor | Combat, World |
| 4 | **"Nothing living near it"** read as civilians, with fighters not counted | **Right from the design side:** the thrower and the target are fighters, and both are always near. **Legal confirms the reading** (RL-183, ln04) | Legal |
| 5 | **Rings turn in about 15 of 16 columns** with World's data bonus | **Too often. Aim for about half.** "The route is the ring" should usually feel like flying on to the next place on the same ring, with a turn as the occasional reveal of new country. **Target: 40 to 60% of columns 1 to 4 turn.** Raise `minGain` first, so a turn must clearly improve the offer, then lower `turn.bonus`. The climax (column 5) changes the ring anyway | World, QA |
