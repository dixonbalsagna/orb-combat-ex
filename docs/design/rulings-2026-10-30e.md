# Rulings, 2026-10-30 (e): the AI and a beam's dodge; the struggle's aim and its AI ends

Owner: Game Design. Design only, no build. From Encounter's read, 40 matches and 310 signatures: CLASH 128, HIT 104, GUARD 51, DEFLECT 26, ESCAPE 1, DODGE 0. `beamDodge` is only the chance a dodge is in time for an AI already in Dodge stance. The AI re-picks its stance only while free, so it stays frozen through the tell, and it was in Dodge stance at fire 3 times in 310.

| # | The question | The decision | Number | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Should the AI dodge beams?** | **Yes.** The levels already carry a `beamDodge` that almost never gets used, so 0 of 310 isn't what the data meant. An AI that never dodges never shows the player that a beam can be dodged | none | Encounter |
| 2 | **How** | **Encounter's lever: a draw at the tell's start to take Dodge stance for this beam.** It's drawn only if the AI isn't answering the beam and hasn't planned a perfect block, so it comes out of HIT and GUARD and leaves the struggle's share alone. `beamDodge` stays as it is, and a dodge that isn't in time is a HIT, so an easy AI visibly gets it wrong | **`beamDodgeStance`: easy 0.15, medium 0.25, hard 0.35** (`ai.json` levels) | Encounter |
| 3 | **At what rate** | **DODGE as a share of all signatures: easy 2 to 5%, medium 5 to 10%, hard 10 to 15%.** Worked out, not run: about half of signatures are unanswered (155 of 310, a read I take to be medium and assume for the other levels), so those draws times `beamDodge` give about 3, 7 and 12%. Most signatures should still connect or struggle. QA reads it on each level, with the standing rows beside it: signatures at most 30% of a match's damage, and match length. If DODGE misses its band, Encounter moves `beamDodgeStance` alone | the three bands | Encounter, QA |
| 4 | **`overwhelm.stickUy`** | **Accept 0.25.** On a level beam the launch's line runs from about 7° down to 20° up with the stick held fully down or up (`uy * 0.6 + 0.12`, plus or minus the aim). That's a visible choice at this launch's length, and still a nudge of the line rather than a new direction, which is what `rule-of-cool.md` asks for. Worked out from the code, not played: Orb's play of the struggle can move it | `stickUy` 0.25 | Encounter |
| 5 | **AI v AI ends lean to time** (48 time, 35 detonate, 3 overwhelmed, median 305 ticks) | **Not a problem, and no lever.** Two AIs at one level land the same share of pulses, so the point wanders near the middle and the 5 s runs out, as it should. The struggle is built for a person: the reads show a player on the beat overwhelming medium 99% of the time, and a masher losing in about 157 ticks. A time end with the point well over still has a loser (E93). **The cost is to reads, not play:** QA's AI v AI arms will show few overwhelm launches, and they say nothing about how the struggle feels. With the struggle on, QA re-reads match length and signature damage before it goes on by default, because it changes how every clash ends | none | QA |
