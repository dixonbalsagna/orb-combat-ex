# Rulings, 2026-10-30: where a fighter stands in a lake or the sea

Owner: Game Design. Design only: nothing is built, and no player sees it yet. Rendering found that a lake's interior doesn't draw, and that drawing it shows the pair on the bed with their feet under the sheet (`docs/rendering/pending/2026-10-30a/README.md`, `docs/rendering/img/lookgate/lake-interior-tundra-sbs.png`). The picture's own column is 46 deep. World's lakes run 50 to 900 deep.

| # | The question | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Surface, wading or bed?** | **By depth, on one curve, with no new number.** A fighter under his own control is on a floor at `surface - feet`, where `d` is the water's depth at his column and `feet = max(0, min(d, wade.below - d))`. With `wade.below` at 75, one body height: he is on the bed in water up to 37.5 deep, so the water reaches his waist at most. From there he rises out of it as the bottom falls away, and from 75 down he stands on the sheet. The curve has no step, so walking in from the shore never pops. Tundra's 46-deep column has 29 of him under the sheet, so that picture is close to right | World (the floor), Simulation and Encounter (the lines that read it) |
| 2 | **Can he fly under?** | **No. To a fighter under his own control, a lake's water is ground.** Stick down stops at the floor. Orb has complained twice about fights underwater (`docs/ep/vision.md`, the greybox notes and the flight round the planet), and hiding is out of the base game. So the 0.55 water speed never applies to him in a lake, which also keeps row 15's rule that water never slows the brawl | Simulation |
| 3 | **Item 7 (23b, surfacing set within 30 ticks)** | **Unchanged, and it's how he gets back to the floor.** Only a launch or a drop goes in. A launch skims or sinks by the sea's rules, a body that reaches the bed slams as now, and he rises to the floor over the 30 ticks with no down state. **So the surfacing rule has to be on wherever this floor is on, and the floor never snaps a body up:** it applies once the surfacing ends | World, Encounter |
| 4 | **`wade.below`** | **It is the one line for a lake.** Today a column from 75 to 100 deep is neither waded (`WorldFill.wading`) nor sea (`seaAt` and `seaTop` use `HIDE_MIN_DEPTH`, 100, a leftover from hiding). For a lake, those read `wade.below` too. `wading` is also false whenever water v2 is off, so the lake's read can't depend on that switch | World |
| 5 | **Fills (23b rows 14 and 15)** | **Unchanged.** This floor is for the ring's own lakes and rivers and for the sea (row 7). A fill rising in a hole the fight dug still never lifts or holds him | World |
| 6 | **Should a lake that deep be in a fight lane?** | **Yes. Don't shallow or move them.** Depth was only a problem while a fighter could stand on the bed. Now it shows only in a launch that goes in and in the colour of the water, and the landscape answering back is pillar 6. `lakes.json` stays as it is. Rendering should make deep water hide the bed, so that a deep lake never reads as a shallow one | World (none), Rendering |
| 7 | **The sea** (EP's word, 2026-10-30: design ruling only, the builds are routed later) | **The same floor, rows 1 to 3 as written.** Water is water (23b row 13), and a gen 2 ring shouldn't teach two rules. The game already treats the sea this way: `data/director/location.json` calls it "a destination at its surface", and the AI comes up when it's under. Only a human can still fight on the sea bed, and hiding, the reason to be down there, is out of the base game. Orb's two complaints about underwater fights were about the sea. **`d` is the depth at his column, so a beach wades in to the waist and then stands on the sheet with no special case.** **Rules measured from the ground stay measured from the ground** (a power-up's crater, `y < g + 140`): only the floor moves, so a fighter on the sheet is as far above the bed as one at the surface is today. **One switch, every world:** off keeps gen 1 and the hand-made world bit for bit, and on changes the replay's hash. QA reads the whole table before it goes on | World, Simulation, Encounter, QA |
| 8 | **What the sea's floor touches** | **Three small things, none lost.** **A finisher's `submerged` variant** (`data/combat/finishers.json`, the drive into the seabed with a geyser) stays, since a blow can still send a body in. Combat keys it on the sent body or on deep water under him, never on him already being under. **The `submerged` band** (`data/combat/styles.json`, y < -60) stays true during a launch and the surfacing and at no other time, and its gates still read right. **The cap on underwater time** (`balance-targets.md` section 10) stays as a QA row, and now it should count only those ticks. **A future stealth fighter** (pillar 6) may get going under as that fighter's own ability. This ruling doesn't close that | Combat, QA |
| 9 | **A waded body under the floor** (Simulation's review of World's park `docs/world/pending/lake-floor/`: in water 37.5 to 75 deep, a drop or a get-up ending lifts him 16 to about 73 units in one tick). A rise over ticks, or the bed as his floor until he leaves the column? | **A rise over ticks, and he keeps the stick.** Below the floor he rises at most `wade.below / riseTicks` a tick, 75 / 10 = 7.5 units, the surfacing's own rate over its full depth, with no new number and no new state: his height is the state. So the deepest lift takes 10 ticks and a 16-unit one takes 3. It isn't a rush and holds nothing, since water never slows the brawl (23b row 15). World's walk probe peaks at 5.57 units a tick, so an ordinary walk stays on the floor as before. **Not the bed:** a column is 32 units wide, so that only delays the same lift by a step, needs a memory per fighter, and leaves a fighter who stands still with his head under the sheet | World, Simulation, Encounter |
| 10 | **The load error** requires `surface.enabled` but not `interrupts.json` `water.enabled`, and with that off a drop into 96 deep rises 100 units in one tick. Should the floor require both? | **Yes, both, and the error names both.** They are two halves of item 7: the first brings up a launched body, the second a drop, and only the second counts him as landed (`rulings-2026-10-26.md` row 6). Row 9's rate doesn't stand in for it, because at 900 deep it would take 120 ticks against item 7's 30 | World, Encounter |
| 11 | **A bury or a grapple's slam over water** (Encounter: `bury.gd` 143 and `grapple.gd` 451 place a body on the bed; in a wade that's the snap again, and on the sheet a slam would bury him on water) | **A burial needs him on the bed: water under 37.5 deep at his column.** Deeper, no burial starts: no embed and no follow-up. An impact there still digs the bed and he comes up by item 7 or row 9, with no down state, as item 7 always said. **The grapple's down throw keeps its reward everywhere,** so a throw is worth the same over the sea. `grapple.gd` reads the floor for both its height test and its placement, so he is slammed flat on the floor for the same `tc.bury` ticks of opening, with a splash and no crater on water. This moves that line off World's "left on the ground" list. **`bury.gd` 143 stays on the bed,** because it only runs inside a burial, which is the one hold that keeps him below the floor. If the deepened crater takes the water past 37.5, he rises by row 9 when the burial ends. **MARSH SWALLOW's always-embed** holds only where a burial can start | World (the embed), Encounter (the throw) |

**The look.** Rendering's interior line can go ahead with this, through the look gate (ADR 0010). Standing on the sheet is a new picture and a rule-of-cool call, so it belongs in Orb's look at gen 2 before the default flips.
