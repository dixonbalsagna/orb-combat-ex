# Pitch for Orb: B as a third kind of blow

Owner: Game Design. Date: 2026-10-06. **Nothing here is decided.** It is four options and one recommendation.

**Orb** (answering `brawl-second-pass.md`, question 3): "I like the body blows suggestion, but it should read more as a new axis to the 'light / heavy' paradigm, I'm thinking 'vicious' or something similar, depending on the character it might be but not limited to headbutts, bites, clawing, gouging. please pitch some more alternatives."

**The test for a third axis.** Light is the fast blow and heavy is the strong one. A third button earns its place only if it does a job neither of them can. Each option below names that job first.

## What is the same in all four

- **B is a blow, with a tap, a mash, a hold and a beat,** like X and Y. It joins the flurry and the mix-up.
- **Signatures leave the single button.** The fighter's defining signature goes to **RT + B:** the charging stance's B, on the hold ladder that is already ruled (his art at 24 ticks, his signature at 48, his ultimate in acts 3 and 4). That is where the control scheme first had it. If a pair is preferred, the alternative is a held Y+B.
- **The other stances keep their own B:** the guard signature on LB + B, the movement signature on LT + B.
- **The energy moves go to the energy arts stance.** There a tap of B is the Charge burst, and a hold is the Charge blast and then the beam, live and held by the player. That is the first draft's B, moved one stance over, so nothing written for it is lost.
- **The four power attacks stand.** Y+B and A+B take the colour of whichever axis is picked.

## Recommendation: option 1, vicious, with the Protagonist's version read as "pointed"

- **It is the only one that uses what only this game has.** Wounds by region are the game's own system, and today a player can't aim at them: the stick only leans a blow. A button whose whole job is the wound gives him that, and gives a fight a plan: work the arm, then wrench it.
- **It is a real third choice.** It loses a damage race to X and Y on purpose. A player presses it to set something up, and not to win a trade.
- **It fits all four fighters without bending one.** The same rule reads as cruelty on the Cyborg and the rival, as cutting on the Empress, and as a guardian's disabling strike on the Protagonist.
- **It takes option 3's best idea:** on the beat, it steals the rival's beat.
- **What it costs outside the build:** the Cyborg's bite, and any clawing or gouging, go to Legal and to the rating question, which Orb hasn't answered yet. The option works with none of them drawn graphically: a bite can be a clamp.

**If Orb picks it, these follow:** the limb-break band moves (0.3 to 0.5 a match today); the crippling moment's trigger moves from a heavy that lands on a battered limb to the wrench; and QA gets a row for how often a region a player worked is the one that breaks.
