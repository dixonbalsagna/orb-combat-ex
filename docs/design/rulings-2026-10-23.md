# Rulings, 2026-10-23: flips in AI play, balance-19's misses, and the Whole ring's turns

Owner: Game Design. **One decision per item, routed by owner.** Paper only; every number is a first value.

## 1. Recoil flips in AI play

| # | The read | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | QA: 0.42 flips a minute in AI play (634 in 200 matches) against the band of 2 to 6, and 47% of flips lead to a counter-rush | **The flips come from knock-backs, and the AI is short of knock-backs, not of flips.** Read it on the build with `rulings-2026-10-22.md` §1a (a clean tapped heavy recoils 1.5 bh), which adds a knock-back to every clean heavy. **The band for AI play becomes 1.5 to 6 a minute,** since an AI's heavies land less often than a human's. **If it is still under 1.5 with 1a in, the AI's flip shares rise from 20, 45 and 70% to 30, 60 and 85%.** The counter-rush after a flip (47%) is in line with its shares (50, 70 and 90%) and stays | QA, Encounter |

## 3. The Whole ring's calm turns

| # | The read | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3 | Half turns 1.2 times a match and Close 1.1, which pass. Whole turns 0.6, or 0.7 with the ground rule at 450 | **One more moment, at every size: the end of a set piece.** A beam struggle, a clash exchange or a throw's landing ends with both fighters apart. When both are set and nothing is in flight, the 45-tick window opens from there. The list grows from the form pause, the landing (from the journey's end) and the cutaway, to these as well. **The ground rule goes to 450 units at every size.** Whole's beam struggles (2 to 5 a match) are the likeliest new moments. **Bands:** Half and Close 1 to 3 a match. **Whole 0.8 to 3:** the biggest ring changes most from turn to turn, and its far side is already the most varied, so its floor is lower. If Whole still reads under 0.8, it is accepted at what it reads, and no hitch is ever the lever | Encounter, World, QA |
