# Rulings, 2026-10-30 (d): the damage inset also opens on a skyscraper's fall

Owner: Game Design. Design only, no build. Follows `rulings-2026-10-30c.md` row 6, whose clause fired. Camera's read (`docs/camera/pending/damage-inset/README.md`): 67 to 84% of avoided beams have no building in reach, so openings stay at about one in every two to four matches. Meanwhile 76 to 80% of all building falls, 17 to 29 a match counted per building, happen off every pane. This replaces 30b row 4's "no other triggers" and c's row 6's "a fall of any cause stays out".

| # | The question | The decision | One number | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Instead of, or as well as?** | **As well as.** The avoided beam stays and opens as built, because it's what Orb asked for. A fall of any cause out of the picture is a second way in. That earlier "no" was a guess that a corner picture in a fight costs more than it gives. The read now shows the planet's biggest moments mostly go unseen, and pillar 4 doesn't work if nobody sees them. The strip already opens in the middle of a fight, so this is no new kind of interruption | none | Camera |
| 2 | **Which falls?** | **Skyscrapers only.** A fall opens it if the building stood at least 20 body heights, about where the damaged buildings in Camera's read average (21 to 26). A hut isn't what this is for, and most of those 17 to 29 falls are probably small. A landmark gets no exception. "Out of the picture" is 30b row 1's rule, with `seenShare` 0.5. In a split it goes in the pane of the fighter nearer the building | `fallMinBh` 20, read from the height the building had before the fight, on every world; 0 turns this trigger off | Camera |
| 3 | **Grouping per blast** | **One opening, on the first qualifying fall, and no new number.** The ration's 15 s gap makes the others in that blast open nothing, and a running inset is never replaced (as built). So a blast that drops six towers gives one picture, and the first of them is a skyscraper by row 2. **Camera's rate counts openings, so its fall counts need no grouping of their own** | none (`ration.gapS` 15, as built) | Camera |
| 4 | **The target rate** | **Both triggers together: 1 to 3 a match on every world, with no opening in under a quarter of matches.** This replaces c's floor of 0.25. Same script and scaling. **Camera moves `fallMinBh` to land in the band, between 15 and 30, with one value for every world.** If no value in that range lands it, it comes back here. The read also reports openings by trigger and the share that open during an exchange, since that's the risk to control first | the band | Camera |
| 5 | **The rest of the gates** | **As built.** The blocked list (intro, transform, finisher, KO, launch, lunge) and a fighter strip still come first. A fall scores 2.0, so it opens as the big strip in a merged view and as the thumbnail in a split. The score still never reads casualties, and nothing is awarded for a fall | none | Camera |
