## 1. Repeated launches: destruction escalates, and freshness is gentle

Orb: "it's expected that each time you launch an opponent against something, the more destroyed it will be. I don't necessarily think a handicap should be applied just for repetition, though I could be convinced either way."

| # | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1a | **Repeats escalate the destruction.** A send into a building already marked meets it one tier higher for each mark on it, up to two (`buildings.md` §6.1). So the second launch into a tower breaks more, and the third may go through. A send into ground already cratered deepens and widens that crater, rather than digging a new one beside it | World, Encounter |
| 1b | **Freshness stays, gentle.** A kind repeated on the same fighter within 60 s is ×0.9 a mark (it was ×0.75). The general term is ×0.97 for each launch of him in the last 60 s (it was ×0.9). The floor is ×0.7 (it was ×0.4). Air sense is 10% sooner a mark (it was 25%). It nudges toward variety, and no longer handicaps the player for repeating | Encounter, Simulation |
| 1c | **The pacing rows follow.** Full launches by the route's player and on him are watched, not banded. The route's edges are the dominance test: at most 80%, and 0 to 25 points over its masher. AI v AI keeps its band of 6 to 12 full launches a 7-minute match | QA |

## 2. The slingshot: the next single form is stronger (working rule)

Orb ticked "Waiting only makes your next single form stronger", and asked to settle the lore before transformations are final.

| # | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2 | **One step a transformation:** a release takes only the next step, and the steps still ready stay ready. **The coil makes that one form stronger:** its step gains × (1 + c/100), at most ×2, for as long as he is in it. The coil, patience and Trial's road stay as ruled. **Withdrawn:** the jump of several steps, the burst, the 10 s surge and its armour, the lasting gain. It is the working rule until Narrative's lore paper, and is read again then (`hero-forms.md` §4b) | Simulation, Encounter, Narrative |

## 4. Rare boons: a base form that is a transformation

Orb, beside the recommended rule: "possibility of unlocking rarer boons that could potentially set your base form to a transformation?" A draft, for Orb to react to in play:

| Boon (working names; Narrative names them) | What it gives | Its price | When it is offered |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Born ready** | Every fight starts in his first form above base: step I for the Protagonist, the tier-2 form for anyone else. Power starts at that step's floor | That step can't be coiled, since he is never in base. It counts against the ceiling: with the ceiling at tier 2, he can't climb further until a form boon | Rare: from fight 3, in at most one offer a run, on a seeded share of offers (one in three) |
| **Second skin** | Every fight starts one form higher still: II for the Protagonist, tier 3 for anyone else | The same, and the opponent's Trial fills at once, since he starts below. Collateral's budget is the higher tier's from the first second | Rarer: from fight 4, only with the ceiling at tier 3 or more, and only if Born ready was taken |
| **The borrowed form** (a temptation) | Every fight starts at the highest form unlocked | Each fight's first broken region comes a quarter sooner. **It changes the final arc,** like any temptation | Only as the temptation between fights 2 and 3, on the runs that offer one |

- **Rarity is shown, never rolled.** The offer is drawn from the run's seed, as every offer is, so a seed shows the same rare boon to everyone (`unlocks.md` rule 2: no random reward rolls).
- **It never touches versus.** Every boon is a run-only gain.

## 5. Rewards: generous, and always something new

Orb: "rewards should feel generous, there should be tons of different rewards so players feel like there's always something new."

| # | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5a | **Something new after every fight, win or lose:** a won fight gives its keepsake and **one item the player doesn't own yet,** from his fighter's pool or the shared one. A lost fight gives its mastery points, and every second loss in a row gives an item, so a hard run is never empty-handed | UI, Art |
| 5b | **Many more kinds of reward** (`unlocks.md`, the new section 6). Beside the outfits, patterns, trails, beam tints, titles, keepsakes and finisher styles: taunt and emote variants; victory poses and KO camera styles; intro variants; voice-line variants; HUD themes and menu backdrops; **music tracks** (each arena theme as it is made); place **postcards** (a still of every place defended); aura tints inside Legal's palette; fighter-card badges and banners; and **lore entries**, a codex filled by events in runs and matches, written with Orb's universe | Art, Narrative, Audio, UI, Legal |
| 5c | **The size:** about 1,000 items across the first four fighters, so a player sees something new for many runs. Art's pool (122 items a fighter of its own and 38 shared) is about 530; the run's kinds and the new ones above add the rest | Art, Narrative |
| 5d | **Still earned, never rolled, never for a death.** Earned through play, no purchases, no random rolls, nothing missable (`unlocks.md` rule 2), and nothing for a death (Legal c08). "Always something new" comes from long seeded sequences with choices in them, not from chance | all |
