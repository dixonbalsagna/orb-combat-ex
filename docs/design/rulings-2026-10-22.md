## 2. Combat's choreography and `fight-feel.md`, reconciled

Combat's four pieces (recoil exits, ricochets, clash meets, turns) and the rules in `fight-feel.md` describe one system. Combat draws how each looks, and these are the rules they play by. **The build order is one piece at a time:** 1) exits and flips (flips built), 2) ricochets, 3) meets and the clash exchange, 4) turns.

| # | The piece | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2a | **Exits: ticks and distances** (Combat's 10, 18 and 24 ticks; 1, 2 and 3 to 4 bh) | **The exit fits inside today's reel or knock-back, which doesn't change length.** The distance and time are the sim's (the reel, the 1.5 bh heavy recoil of 1a, `knockBack.distBh`). Combat's 10, 18 and 24 ticks are the animation's nominal lengths, scaled to fit. Combat's exits are the look of every separation by weight (skip, handspring, backflip, tuck). **The recoil flip is the player's cut of it:** a dodge tap in the first 10 ticks lands him set 12 ticks later, with the counter-rush | Animation, Encounter |
| 2b | **Ricochets: the speed line, the counter, the count** | **The line is 1,500 units a second into the surface** (the speed along its normal). **Below it, Combat's plant:** he plants on the surface (a 4-tick stop, a tell of at least 6 ticks) and springs back toward the rival. **A press of X, Y or B within 6 ticks of the plant makes the rebound a blow of that strength** (Combat's counter), and a dodge tap aims the spring by the stick instead. **At or above it:** a glancing contact (under 40°) glances off at 0.7 of the speed, and a steeper one is the wall slam, the lodge or the chain, as ruled. There a dodge tap within 4 ticks either side is the wall kick (half the slam's damage, a kick-off at 0.6 of the speed). **At most two ricochets in a row,** of either kind, then the next surface is a plain stop (Combat's count, where `fight-feel.md` had three). **A rebound heavy launches only into an opening,** as any B: otherwise it is a heavy, with 1a's recoil | Simulation, Encounter, World |
| 2c | **Meets: the window, who wins, the escalation** | **The window is 3 ticks.** Contacts within 1 tick of each other meet; 2 or 3 ticks apart, **the earlier breaks through** at half its worth (Combat's "break by timing"). **Equal strengths meet:** X knocks wrists and both bounce 0.5 bh, and pressing again meets again; Y bumps elbows and both slide 1 bh; B is the heavy clash ring and both backflip 3 bh. **Unequal: the heavier goes through** at full worth, and the lighter takes his exit (the triangle, visible). **The third meet in a row escalates** into the clash exchange on the pulse (`fight-feel.md` §4). **A B meet breaks glass near it from tier 2,** and its shockwave damages structures from tier 3, as rule of cool item 15 has it | Encounter, World, VFX |
| 2d | **Turns: the deflect's window and the count** | **The deflect:** a fresh guard press in the last 16 ticks of an incoming blow's wind-up that misses the perfect-block window turns the blow aside, with no damage and no stagger. His next press within 8 ticks is **the return,** a quick blow from his stop and reversal pools. A turn is the deflect plus the return, 12 to 16 ticks. **2 to 6 turns,** ending when one misses a deflect (he takes the blow and his exit), or escalates (a heavy is a meet, a launch is a link). A press inside the deflect window never starts the perfect block's lockout. The perfect block stays the better read: its reversal and stagger | Encounter |
| 2e | **The AI's shares,** by level | Recoil flips 20, 45 and 70%, and their counter-rush 50, 70 and 90% (1e). The plant's counter press 30, 55 and 80%. The wall kick 15, 35 and 55%. Deflects 20, 40 and 60%, and the return after a deflect 60, 75 and 90%. The clash exchange's pulses 40, 65 and 85%, as in every clash. All of them start no sooner than its reaction time after the cue that starts them | Encounter |

**QA's rows, piece by piece:** flips 2 to 6 a minute; plants and glances 2 to 8 a match, wall kicks 1 to 4; meets 3 to 8 a minute, clash exchanges 1 to 4 a match; turns 2 to 8 a minute, and runs of turns 2 to 6 long.
