# Rulings, 2026-10-30 (f): the next round on launches and high speed

Owner: Game Design. Design only, no build, and no number moves until the read in row 3 is in. Orb, after the preview: "lets definitely keep rebalancing the launches and how high speed is handled." Standing word: speed should feel great, and the camera stays smooth.

**The finding that sets the order.** The levers for this were built and are off on main: the launch's curve (`data/fight/flight.json`, `enabled` false, its hit-stop `hitstop.json` too, from Orb's "rebalancing the speed on launches may be a way to handle it"), the AI's approach cap (`ai.json` `approachCap`, off, against a return Camera measured at 10,000 units a second) and the camera's travel budget (off in the game). So unless the preview's overlay turned them on (the EP to confirm), Orb has judged the uncurved launch. **Try what's built before designing anything new.**

**QA's table doesn't call for a rebalance on its own.** Long journeys (14.9 to 17.4%, at most 20%) are in band, and bounces (1.64 to 1.93, 1.2 to 2.0) are in band on the point. The table measures how far and how often. Orb's complaint is about how it reads, and the table has no row for that yet.
