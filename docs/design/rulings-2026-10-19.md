Owner: Game Design. **One decision per item, routed by owner.** Paper only; every number is a first value. Orb's words are in `docs/ep/vision.md`, "Orb's answers of 2026-10-19". Each design page named below carries the same change.

## 1. Launch speeds for the camera

| # | The decision | Owner |

## 2. The fight's feel, in place of the rest

| # | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2a | **Recoil flips:** a dodge tap in the first 10 ticks of a knock-back backflips him out of it. He lands set 12 ticks later, and his next blow within 20 ticks is a counter-rush. Free: no ki and no cooldown (`fight-feel.md` §2) | Encounter, Animation |
| 2b | **Ricochets:** a glancing contact (under 40°) with a surface that holds keeps 0.7 of the speed and reflects. **The wall kick:** a dodge tap within 4 ticks of a wall contact kicks off it toward the stick at 0.6 of the speed, with the slam's damage halved. At most 3 ricochets a journey (§3) | Simulation, Encounter, World, Animation |
| 2c | **Clashes:** blows that land within 3 ticks of each other, of the same strength or one step apart, clash. Neither is hurt, both recoil, and the pull brings them back. Three clashes in 3 s become a clash exchange on the pulse (§4) | Encounter, VFX, Audio |
| 2d | **The rest is gone for good.** The brawl's pacing comes from impacts, recoils, ricochets, clashes and launches. QA's rows: flips 2 to 6 a minute; ricochets 2 to 8 a match; wall kicks 1 to 4; clashes 3 to 8 a minute; clash exchanges 1 to 4 a match | QA |

## 3. Smaller arenas

Orb: "every player might not like every fight to take place along the entire planet." An arena is a circle on the planet. A great circle is today's 153,600 units, and a small circle, a plane that doesn't pass through the centre, is shorter and loops round a centre point. Every size wraps, so pillar 1 holds.

| # | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3a | **Three sizes.** **Planet:** the great circle, 153,600 units. **Region:** about 51,200, holding one city, two towns and two biomes at least. **Local:** about 19,200, holding one city or one landmark | World |
| 3b | **Where each is used.** In versus it is a setup option, **Region by default**. In a run, by offer kind: a Proving ground is Local (a duel site), a Rampage and a Crossing are Region, and **the boss is Planet,** since the catastrophe tier and the exosphere need the room. Story mode's set pieces pick their own | World, UI, Encounter |
| 3c | **What scales with it.** The meters stay normalised to 1,800 people. The collateral budget is a share of the people present, as now. A launch's glide is scaled so a send never carries more than a sixth of the loop (`fight-feel.md` §1) | Simulation, World |
| 3d | **The shifting plane** (Orb's red loop that moves during a fight). **World illustrates it first, as Orb asked.** Game Design's rule for it, if Orb likes the picture: the plane may tilt only during a launch's flight or a set piece, when the camera is already moving, pivoting round the landing point, and never during a brawl. Off until Orb has seen it | World, Camera |

## 5. Balance, from Encounter's parks (c3f305b2) and QA's read

| # | The read | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5a | The one-light armour overshoots: answered 89.8% (band 20 to 50) | **The second light must land within 6 ticks of the first.** Only a real flurry stops an answerable launcher, not a busy string's spaced lights. The rule stays otherwise (`launch-anywhere.md` §3) | Encounter |
| 5b | The masher wins 52% (band 35 to 50) | **No lever.** It is two points over, inside the noise of 100 matches. Read it on 200 with 5a. 5a doesn't touch him, since his lights land inside 6 ticks | QA |
| 5c | The A-only player wins 0 of 10 again | **The row is replaced.** A grab-only player has no damage of his own by design, since Orb sees the grapple as "a way to set up combo chains". The new row is the **grab-and-follow** script: he catches, pummels within 3 ticks, throws down, and then presses B on the bury. It wins 25 to 50% against medium. The A-only row is retired (`grapple.md` §7) | QA, Encounter |
| 5d | The clinch's median is 85, with 46% timing out | **The clinch is 60 ticks at most: three pulses,** where it was 90. The median band becomes 30 to 55 ticks, with time-outs under 30%. The leader's first move at the time stays (`grapple.md` §2) | Encounter |
| 5e | QA on launch anywhere (77ca29a4): 45 launches a match at medium | **The route rows for launch anywhere:** launches a match by the route's player 20 to 50, on him 10 to 40, AI v AI full launches 6 to 12. **The route's edges stay the dominance test:** at most 80%, and 0 to 25 points over its masher | QA |
