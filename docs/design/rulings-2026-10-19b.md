# Rulings, 2026-10-19 (second batch): the turning ring, story stages, sizes, hit-stop and the charge hints

Owner: Game Design. **One decision per item, routed by owner.** Paper only; every number is a first value. Orb approved the shifting arena in World's calm-moment version (31b): "I want this approach to work... please begin work on engineering this system", and "I can see this being used to programmatically assign stages to opponents in the story mode". With World's `docs/world/homes-and-arenas.md` and `arena-shift-research.md`, and Research's `docs/research/cinematic-storytelling.md` (recommendations 9 and 10). It supersedes `rulings-2026-10-19.md` §3a, §3b and §3d where they differ.

## 1. When the ring turns

| # | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1a | **The calm moments.** The ring turns only on a calm tick, with nothing in flight (World's rule: no beam, shot, flight, slide, failing stack, magma or crowd on the move). It turns at one of three moments: **a form step that pauses** (a tier-up, or the Protagonist's II, IV or V), under the step's own camera; **the moment after a launch has landed,** with both fighters down or set and nothing moving, under Camera's hard cut back to the pair; and **a set piece's cutaway** (the inside chain's or a collapse's), under its cut. Never in a brawl, never under a dissolve or a wipe (`taste.md`), only a hard cut or a moment the camera already owns | World, Simulation, Camera |
| 1b | **How much, and how often.** A turn is at most 10° of bearing about the pair's midpoint (World's limit, so the front street stays the front street near the pivot). There are at most 4 a fight, at least 60 s of fight time apart, and the loop drifts at most 40° in a match | World |
| 1c | **The player feels it, and steers it through the fight.** Feeling it: the ground under the fighters stays, and the far side changes, so a city that was off to one side is the street behind them and the fallen tower is still fallen. Steering it: no button and no prompt (`minimal-hud.md`). **The turn leans the way the fight has been pushed:** the stick's drag of the brawl and the last launch's direction set its side. Inside that side, the choreographer picks the bearing that brings the coolest place into view by combat alchemy's score (a city, a landmark, an opponent's home) | World, Encounter |
| 1d | **Off by default until it is built and played.** Its switch is the arena's, and a replay's setup carries it | World, Simulation |
| 1e | **Encounter's three, 2026-10-22** (`docs/director/calm-turn.md` §6): a landing's settle is `landedTicks` 30, with "set" meaning both fighters under 60 u/s and nothing in flight. A landing in the water counts once he surfaces set; a skip is still flight. The hero's pick may bring a city into view: a turn changes what is seen, not where the fight is, and seeing the city he protects is a story beat. His care term only lowers its score | Encounter |
| 1f | **Re-ruled after Encounter's read, 2026-10-22** (`rulings-2026-10-22.md` §5a): the form pause is the first moment, with the pivot fixed at its start and the ring warmed during it. A landing turns less rather than not at all: the ground under both fighters moves at most 300 units, the angle scaled to keep to it, never under 2°. `landedTicks` is 45. No pivot is fixed at the swap, since its hitch would drop the frame rate. The aim is 1 to 3 turns in a typical match | World, Encounter, Rendering |
| 1g | **A landing's moment starts at the end of his journey** (`journey_end`), not at first contact, with the 45-tick window from there (`rulings-2026-10-22.md` §7b) | Encounter, World |
| 1h | **A set piece's end is a moment too** (a beam struggle, a clash exchange, a throw's landing), with both set and nothing in flight. The ground rule is 450 units at every size. Bands: Half and Close 1 to 3 turns a match, Whole 0.8 to 3 (`rulings-2026-10-23.md` §3) | Encounter, World |

## 2. Story stages: each opponent's ground

| # | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 2a | **Each opponent has his ground:** his home (Mendquay, Rankhold, Crownreach, Slagholt) and his thematic places. They are the Protagonist's harbours and coasts, the Anti-hero's mountains and terraces, the Empress's plains and avenues, and the Cyborg's deserts and yards. The polar outposts are kept for a later fighter. Narrative and World confirm the lists | Narrative, World |
| 2b | **A fight against him is drawn through his ground.** The stage picker's candidates for a fight against an opponent are rings through his home or one of his places, seeded per run. So every run puts each rival on his own ground, on a planet that is new each time | World, Encounter |
| 2c | **The run's shape on the ground.** The first fight is at the player's own home (World's proposal). The later fights are on each opponent's ground. **The final fight is a Whole ring through the boss's home and the most populous city still standing:** a great circle passes through both, so the boss fights at home with the most left to lose. This replaces "the boss's place is the most populous city" | World, Encounter |
| 2d | **A turn of the ring (§1) prefers to bring the opponent's ground into view** when it can: a fight on his ground that turns toward his home is a story beat | World, Encounter |

## 3. Arena sizes, reconciled

| # | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 3a | **World's sizes are taken:** **Whole** 153,600 units, **Half** 76,800, **Close** 38,400. They are measured: Close holds about 1.0 city and 1.5 towns, Half 1.4 and 2.4. Mine (Region 51,200 and Local 19,200) are withdrawn. 19,200 was a lap in under two seconds, too small to fight in | World |
| 3b | **Where each is used.** A run opens Close round the player's home, the middle fights are Half, and the boss is Whole (World's proposal). A Proving ground's fight is Close too: a duel site. A polar stage is Close round a pole or an outpost. **Versus:** Whole by default, today's arena, chosen at setup by player 1 (or at random) and kept in the replay's setup | World, UI |
| 3c | **Collateral by size:** the shares stay. The budget and the ceilings are shares of the people present, and the meters are normalised to 1,800. **So a Close ring is where collateral is loudest:** the same share falls on one town, which can fall in one fight from tier 3. That is the reason to want it ("collateral is the story", World), and it is read by QA, not tuned away | Simulation, World, QA |
| 3d | **The AI's roaming and the escape:** distances that are a share of the world scale with the loop. The hero's lure takes the least-populated stretch within a quarter of the loop. The escape stance's slip bonuses for distance (beyond 1,500 and 4,000 units) stay in units, so on a Close ring a long separation is rarer, as World says. A launch never carries more than a sixth of the loop (`fight-feel.md` §1): 6,400 units on Close | Encounter |
| 3e | **The match's length:** the floor (five minutes or more, the 10th percentile at 300 s) holds at every size, since a fight's length comes from the wounds, not the travel. QA reads the length per size | QA |

## 4. Hit-stop by weight, and the launch's ramp

Research's newer table (`cinematic-storytelling.md` recommendation 10) is larger than its earlier one (`readable-speed.md` row 1: light 2 to 3, up to launch 10 or more). The brawl's mashed lights land every few ticks, so the larger stops can't go on every blow.

| # | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 4a | *Re-cut 2026-10-22 (`rulings-2026-10-22.md` §5b): light 2 and 3, medium 5, heavy 8, launch 12 and 14, decisive 20 and the KO 24; the budget 18% with a second step.* **The table, in ticks:** a mashed or burst light 2 to 3; a tapped light 4; a medium 7; a heavy 10; a launch or a close 14, and a full launch 16; **a decisive blow** (a finisher's last, a duel of perfects' send, a blow that breaks a region) 24, with 30 as the cap, for the KO. Both bodies freeze, and the struck one shakes more | Simulation, Animation |
| 4b | **Freeze is budgeted:** the time frozen is at most 12% of a minute of fight. Overlapping stops merge and don't add. Over the budget, the mashed and burst lights go to 2 first | Simulation, QA |
| 4c | **No window gets shorter for it.** The perfect windows and the parry windows end at least 5 live ticks after the freeze ends, however long the freeze. A longer stop never shortens a window | Encounter |
| 4d | **The launch's curve meets recommendation 9.** No one-tick jump to full speed: the kick **ramps up over 5 ticks** to the peak, holds it for 3, then glides. The arrival's rise from 0.4 to 0.6 of the peak is a ramp over 10 ticks, not a jump. The stop is 14 ticks (16 for a full launch), from 4a. The AI's free-flight approach is capped near Camera's whip limit unless the camera rides with it (Encounter, with Camera's limit) (`fight-feel.md` §1) | Simulation, Encounter, Camera |

## 5. The charge hints

Orb's line "Full! Let go to knock him back." is true of no full charge today. A charged heavy knocks him back before it is full, and launches him at full. A full charged medium staggers him, which is an opening. So the line goes where its meaning is true, and the two full charges get lines in its pattern. Narrative polishes, and Orb picks.

| # | The decision | Owner |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 5a | **The charged heavy at its first level (32 ticks, the knock-back):** Orb's line, with one word changed so it is true: "**Charged! Let go to knock him back.**" | UI, Narrative, Orb |
| 5b | **The charged heavy at full (44 ticks, the launch):** "**Full! Let go to send him flying.**" It echoes Orb's own launch hint ("send him flying") | UI, Narrative, Orb |
| 5c | **The charged medium at full (24 ticks, the opening):** "**Full! Let go to open him up.**" | UI, Narrative, Orb |
| 5d | **Each shows once,** at the screen's edge with Control hints on (`minimal-hud.md` §3). If Orb keeps "Full! Let go to knock him back." word for word, it goes at 5a's moment | UI |
