# Rulings, 2026-10-30 (c): the damage inset's rate came in low

Owner: Game Design. Design only, no build. Follows `rulings-2026-10-30b.md` row 7. Camera's read (`docs/camera/pending/damage-inset/README.md`, scaled to a third of the beams avoided): gen 1 0.52 a match, gen 2 0.28, hand-made 0.35, against a band of 0.5 to 2.

**What the read says.** The gates aren't the limit: 40 of 41 avoided beams that reached the score opened. 74 to 86% of them never reach it, and nobody has measured why. **A hint, not a finding:** damage goes up as openings go down. Credited stages run 199, 313, 461 across gen 1, gen 2 and hand-made, while rates run 0.62, 0.33, 0.40 before scaling. That could mean many buildings each getting one stage (0.5 points, under the 1.0), but those stage counts include both fighters' beams, and they don't say how many beams hit nothing at all.
