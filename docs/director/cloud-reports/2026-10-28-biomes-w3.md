## SUMMARY
`chooseLaunch` now offers the eight candidates of biomes.md section 3 (CLIFFSIDE, ICE SKID, MARSH SWALLOW, TREE LINE, SKIM ASHORE, DOWNHILL, VENT LIFT, PROP SMASH). It also adds the terms on the landing's surface and the downBonus and distMul tables. All of it sits behind `biome.enabled`, which stays **off**. The data moved out of the pending folder to `data/combat/launch_biome.json`, with its schema and map row. With the switch off, the hand-made world and gen 1 give the same state hashes as origin/main (1515 hashes), and the golden is byte-identical. In a gen 2 probe, real launch beats chose each of the eight. In a 192-match gen 2 batch with the switch on, no candidate takes more than 30.0% of launches. Look evidence pending (local).

## DECISIONS
1. **The data is its own file, `data/combat/launch_biome.json` (Combat's),** not a block of launch.json.
   - This is the brief's "move it to data/ as Combat's file".
   - It keeps launch.json, and so the replay hash of every replay made so far, unchanged.
   - `DirData.dataHash()` folds the file in, as it does sigvariants.json.
2. **VENT LIFT scores on top of UPPERCUT's own score there** (`onUppercut`, noted in the data).
   - Alone, its 12 never beat the plain uppercut beside it, which gets 22 near the ground. It was chosen 0 of 16 times in the probe.
   - It is "an uppercut over a vent", so the vent's value is the extra. With this it was chosen 4 of 16.
   - For Game Design to confirm (NEEDS 1).
3. **The probes' reading of the gates.**
   - **CLIFFSIDE:** strides of stepBh. Any stride that rises minRiseBh is searched column by column for a run steeper than minTan rising minRiseBh or more. The landing is the face's foot.
   - **TREE LINE and PROP SMASH:** count only trunks and props that are taller than his height above the ground there, because the low line passes through them. A prop's height is its size times a body height.
   - **SKIM ASHORE:** predicts with contact.json's water skim, the same rule as `moveAir`.
   - **DOWNHILL:** reads the look class halfway down the drop.
4. **Only CLIFFSIDE joins the long-haul set** (break and finisher launches), as MOUNTAINSIDE does. The other seven are not long hauls.
5. **What the plan carries for World.** The candidates set direction, force (ICE SKID 0.8; VENT LIFT's lift ×1.25) and landing. The plan also carries the effects as fields for World: `embed` (MARSH SWALLOW 1.8), `trees` and `treeBrake`, `prop`, `soft`, `skims` and `land`. World's flight doesn't read them yet (NEEDS 2).

## NEEDS FROM EP

   - the props list's `outcrop` matches no World prop kind (World has lichen_rock and lava_rock).
2. **World (flight hooks, for the effects to play as section 3 says):** read the plan's fields in the flight:
   - MARSH SWALLOW always embeds, ×1.8 deep;
   - each trunk snaps and brakes him 12%;
   - the prop breaks and he stops at it;
   - a soft landing multiplies impact damage;
   - ICE SKID's slide on World's ice brake, and control at the rock stop (23b item 2).
   Today these play as plain launches in their direction.
3. **World:** a prop query the sim owns. Props are render-only today: propsIn has no match seed, and the renderer doesn't lay them in a match. The director draws them with `propsIn` keyed by an FNV hash of the fine biome map (`_propSeed`). When World or Render lay props in a match, they should use the same seed or give the director the query. Also, bergs and lone trees are provisional props (`provisionalProps` off), so PROP SMASH finds only boulders today.
4. **QA:** the batch tool is `sim/director/tools/launchbiome_batch.gd -- --on|--off --seeds=… --matches=…`. It prints a BATCH JSON line a process with the per-biome counts.
5. **Look evidence pending (local):** a side-by-side once the switch is turned on.

## RISKS
1. **The effects aren't played yet** (NEEDS 2). With the switch on, a MARSH SWALLOW is just a steep slam, a TREE LINE a low send, and so on. The scores assume the effects.
2. **ICE SKID's predicted travel uses the planner's generic slide** (predictFlight), not World's ice brake.
3. **The prop seed is the director's own** (NEEDS 3). Nothing draws props in a match yet, so the player can't see a mismatch today, but one is possible later.
4. **The candidates add work to each launch beat with the switch on:** CLIFFSIDE's probe, DOWNHILL's 40 bh walk, a scan of the trees, and propsIn over at most 80 bh. These run only at launch beats, a couple a minute.
