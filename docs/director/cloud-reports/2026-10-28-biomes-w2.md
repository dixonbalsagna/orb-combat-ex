## How it plays
- **The aftermath.**
  - **When it is laid.** On the tick the main beam's front first reaches its path's end, `WorldAfter.lay(S, row.aftermath, ox, the path's end, row.fineId, b.oz, the defender's x)`. That is "once the beam has carved" (after.md), not the beam's last visible tick (0.95 s): the twists need the groove while he is still there.
  - **Its gates.** It is laid only with `aftermathOn`, World's `after.json enabled`, a gen 2 planet (the fine biome map) and a row that has an aftermath and a fineId. A split's forks lay nothing.
  - **The onset cue.** `cue` kind `aftermath`, with text the aftermath kind, source the variant, x and x1 the path's ends, and n the columns.
- **The twists.** They are played only where the aftermath is live (the same gates). A twist outcome's `beamImpact` beat re-schedules itself each tick while the beam has not finished carving, at most 30 ticks.
  - **under:** if water stands above the floor at his column, he is launched straight down into it at the HIT's force, and World's surfacing rule brings him up set. That water is the sea, a flooded groove or a lead.
  - **skate:** on a glazed column, the push-back's horizontal speed is times slideMul.
  - **wall:** if the heaved plates lie ahead along the push within the pad, the push goes flat, at the force whose slide reaches them. That force is at most reachMul times the push's, and World's wall rule stops him there.
  - **The cue.** Each twist sends `sig_twist` (text the kind) and a feed line.
- **The personality.** On each beam sample, the firer's care sign picks the hero or villain row. Where `popNear(x, DirLaunch.CARE_R)` is above `whenPopAbove`, the groove's width and the structure radius are times widthMul, and the bite is times structMul.
- **crater_shape.** At the end of each beamStep, every crater event from this tick whose `surface` is set gets a `cue` kind `crater_shape`. Its source is the material, and x, y, r, depth, rim and owner are copied from the crater event. `DirSigAfter.craterShape(e)` returns World's `eventFor` record. The shaped craters dug by fighters, the director and beams are all in by then.

## DECISIONS
1. **Both switches off.**
   - `byPersonality` on changes the hand-made world's city beams, so gen 1 would no longer be bit-identical, and parity's JS oracle has no such term. That fails the acceptance criteria outright.
   - `aftermathOn` on is inert today, because World's after.json is off. But the twists' numbers are untuned (the skate is short, RISKS 1), and on would arm them the moment World flips its switch.
   - Both stay the gate, as the brief says. QA turns them on.
2. **The twists sit behind aftermathOn, not a switch of their own.**
   - Every rule line is about what the aftermath left: the lead, the flooded trench, the glaze, the plates and, by ruling 23b item 8, the sea parting.
   - Played only where it is live, the ocean's twist can never reach gen 1, where HORIZON CLEAVE fires today.
3. **The aftermath is laid at the carve's end, and a twist's impact waits for it.** This is at most 0.22 s (13 ticks) after the front reaches him, or the same with the beam plays on. Without the wait, the water and the glaze would not exist yet when he lands.
4. **Events ride `cue`.** The aftermath onset, sig_twist and crater_shape are `SimFx.cue` kinds, so `hash.gd`'s FX_FIELDS needs no new type and no Simulation grant. crater_shape carries the material in `source`, and `craterShape(e)` gives World's full record, because an FxEvent has no field for a dictionary.
5. **A defender above deep sea is the ocean's twist case.** A beam within reach of the floor carves the floor at his own column. The part then opens the trench under him and there is no water to drive him into, so that HIT plays as before (a plain launch along the beam).
