# The right stick's step 1 against today's director (notes, 2026-10-23)

Owner: Encounter Systems Director. A reading of `docs/design/right-stick-pitches.md` §11 (step 1's command set) against today's movement, brawl and zip code, for the EP and Game Design before the build. No code yet.

**Nothing in the director reads a right stick today** (no `rx`/`ry` in `SimIntent` or in `sim/director`). So there is no clash of inputs inside the director; the conflicts are of meaning, where a new command overlaps one that exists.

| Step 1's command | What already does that today | The conflict, and what needs a ruling |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The lunge** (flick toward from beyond reach, 10 ki) | An attack press from the mid band is already a lunge (`bands.gd`: the mid-band lunge, its wind-up crouch, the director's rush), and from the far band a tap is the taunt or the far charge. Pillar 3: attacks always close the gap | Two ways to close: the press's lunge (with its blow) and the stick's (no blow, 10 ki). Which wins when both come in one tick, and does the stick's lunge reuse the press's rush (`DirBands.begin`) or a move of its own? |
| **Step in** (in reach, 1 bh) and **step back** (2 bh in 8 ticks) | In a brawl the left stick already nudges the pair (`DirBrawl`'s movement, each stick through Controls' ramp, `nudge.guard`), and the brawl's give is "giving ground" (`give.leashBh`), the AI's walk-out too. `_stepIn` places the striker "on his own side and at the rival's height" as each blow lands | A stick step in a brawl moves him against the brawl's own pull, which holds the pair at striking distance and rests only for a rush. Steps would need to be director rushes (like the recoil flip's) so the pull rests. Step back overlaps giving ground; one of them should be the other |
| **Slip frames** (step back's first 4 ticks, the hop's and drop's) | The dodge is a tap: stance 2 for `dodgeWindow` after `act.dodgeTick`, which the exchange templates read (dodge and read, dodge and counter), and the tap is also the recoil flip's and the pop's tech. "Slip" today is only the pursuit template's escape branch | Slipping shots and the blow it clears is a dodge by another input. Should a slip set stance 2 (so every template that reads a dodge reads it), or a flag of its own for shots only? The dodge tap's other jobs (flip, tech) must not fire from a stick |
| **The dash on a hold** (toward or away) | The boost (sprint + dash, 12 ki a second) and the Escape stance, which is the sprint held away from him (`SimAct.stance`: 3) | A held flick away must say whether it is the Escape stance. If it sets `sprint` with the stick away, it is Escape, with its pursuit templates and hiding; if not, Escape keeps R3 (R24) and the stick's dash away is movement only |
| **The orbit and the cross-up** | The AI already circles in reach (`DirAI`'s ellipse). The brawl holds each fighter on his own side (`_stepIn`, the pull), and the blows' sides come from `sdx` | A cross-up inside a brawl swaps sides against the pull and the placement. Either the orbit ends the brawl (a part) or the brawl learns a side swap. Outside a brawl it is free flight and fits |
| **The wall run** | The wall kick (`DirWallKick`): a sent fighter kicks off a face that holds | Same faces, different states (free against sent). Nothing collides if the wall run is read only when he is free, and the kick only when sent |
| **The skim** (hold down near the ground) | World's skim is a sent body bouncing on water. Hiding: the Escape stance in cover, submerged in the sea, suppresses his signature; the new water rule brings a sent fighter up (`../pending/water-surface/`) | Over water, hold down "near the ground" is the skim or a dive to hide. A ruling on which, since diving is how he hides today |
| **The energy aim line** (RB family: latched up, down, behind; shots within 30° seek) | A blast fires from where he stands toward him (`DirBlast.press`), in any band | The line is new state on the fighter, and every shot's direction then comes from it. Fits, but the struggle (`DirBeam.startClash`) should ignore it once beams lock |
| **LB + RB, the lay** | Today LB and RB are exclusive in the input layer (the last pressed wins), and A with RB held lays a mine (`DirBlast.layMine`) | The energy overhaul's §19 already moves the lay to this chord with the guard kept; it needs Controls' chord first |
| **The beam sweep by either stick** | The signature beam follows its planned path (`planBeam`, `sampleBeam`); nothing sweeps it today | Fits. While two beams are locked in the struggle, the sweep should stop (the struggle's own presses rule) |
| **Repeats (R8: the third inside 3 s loses its slip)** | | A per-fighter count: two director slots |

**Today's zip (LT with X or Y)** is untouched: step 1 gives the stick nothing while LT is held. The chase (the zip at a launched rival) is untouched too.
