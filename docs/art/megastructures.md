# Megastructures: landmark concepts, version 2 (Art, 2026-11-01)

Brief: the EP's, from Orb's spitball after the placeholder towers in Research's globe prototype ("high-tech sci-fi megastructures"). Concept only, not for publication: Legal is screening the family (RL-233). Version 1 broke the limits in RL-233 (a twin pair with a bridge, a ring on a shaft, a saucer on a stalk, a stacked pagoda of dishes, pods on a shaft, lit windows); it is replaced, not patched.

Sheet: `art/concepts/megastructures/megastructures.png` (and `.svg`). Rebuild: `node art/concepts/megastructures/gen.mjs`.

Eight original silhouettes on one scale, from a 60 bh anchor to a space elevator that does not end in the frame. A downtown tower (30 bh) and a fighter (1 bh = 75 units) stand at every foot. Widths are stretched about 2.6 times so the facets read, as on the district chart; heights are true. Sky bands run haze, sky, high sky, thin air (to 300 bh, about the planet's radius), space.

All are unpopulated machine infrastructure (anchor, power, lift, tether), stout rather than slim, with nothing on top of a shaft and no lit window grid (only the dim machine lights under "Lights" below). Red is a static colour block only.

## How it meets RL-233

- No thin needle with anything on top; nothing on any top but a flat cap. No antenna, dish, saucer or ring. No clock, stripes or beams of light.
- No twin pair: the Pylons are three different forms, the Heat Wall's fins differ.
- No stacked pagoda, ring-world, cooling tower, dome-and-stack, reactor or religious reading.
- No lit windows, no figures, no crowd at a foot. Every fall breaks in sections or at a joint, never a pancake.
- Red is a flat block (Anchor, Heat Wall tab, Spine Lift, Long Rail muzzle, Tether), with no light on it.

## Where I am least sure (for Legal's look)

- The Pylons' A-frame reads as a generic power pylon; if Legal reads it as a known tower outline, the leg can become a plain slab.
- The Bobbin's drum on a column is the nearest to a saucer on a stalk; the column is stout (30 bh wide, 170 tall) and the drum is mid-height, not on top.
- The Anchor's two cables running up out of the frame are thin lines; if they read as antenna masts, they can be shortened to stubs.
- Names are working names; Narrative has not seen them (RL-233: names pass e11).

## Legal's RL-235 changes and the true-width check (2026-11-01)

RL-235 passed five (Heat Wall, Pylons, Spine Lift, Long Rail, Heavy Shaft, which stays smooth) and asked for three changes, all made:
- **The Anchor:** the two thin cables are now two short, heavy cable bundles, each ending in a cream fitting block.
- **The Bobbin:** the drum is a thick prism (33 bh wide, 16 bh deep, about 1.27 times the 26 bh column at that height), and the cap is no wider than the column.
- **The Tether:** no thin line above the top; the shaft is now heavy (46 bh at the foot of the shaft, 38 where it leaves the frame) and runs off the top of the frame, so it reads as endless; one red block between two thin cream collars, no repeated bands.

The sheet's strip shows every silhouette at true width (no stretch), one smaller scale (0.55 px per bh). The needle rule: the narrowest width in the top third must be at least a twelfth of the height. All eight now pass.

## Lights (RL-236, 2026-11-01)

Legal cleared lights on megastructures, with conditions. They are on all eight now, as steady, dim, warm-neutral machine lights (`#cdb374`, well under the tower windows' luminance cap): irregular slots, a few short service bands, in clusters on a few levels, never an even grid, never red, no lit crown (none in the top tenth of any structure; the Tether's top is cut off by the frame). They are densest on the Tether (seven clusters up the shaft) and the Heavy Shaft (five), where they help the scale: a lit service band the size of a street, a hundred bh up. No figure at a light, no silhouette. In a fall they go out slowly (each at least 0.3 s, no more than 2 changing a second, never as a count): that is for whoever builds the hit.
