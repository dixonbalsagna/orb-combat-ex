# The beach shell (cover shape for World's `shell` prop)

Art Director, 2026-10-29. Sheet: `shell-cover.svg` (and `shell-cover.png`); generator `art/concepts/biomes/shell.mjs` (the tones are checked by `shellcheck.mjs`). For Rendering's `RenderProps.KINDS` / `RenderCoverMeshes`: a new kind `shell`, beach only, size class 0.4 to 0.9 fighter heights (30 to 68 units; World's `shell` row is [0.4, 0.9]).

- **Shape:** a scallop standing on its hinge, in the strip's plane, leaning 6 to 14 degrees (the three on the sheet lean +10, -6, +14). A fan over 126 degrees from the hinge: eight lobe tips at the full height h and seven notches between at 0.93 h (a gentle scallop), 14 wedge triangles. A small flat ear below the hinge (0.4 h wide, 0.16 h tall), and one growth line across the ribs at 0.62 h.
- **Faces:** every wedge from the hinge to a pair of neighbouring edge points is one flat triangle, tone by direction: the left third lit, the middle mid, the right shade, as the cover's fan. The ear is the shade tone. Six ribs and the growth line are keyline strokes (0.8 px), not geometry. 42 triangles at the middle size counting the strokes as `covers.mjs` does, 16 without them.
- **Tones, all three fixed colours (not the palette slots):** lit #e4bcc4, mid #cc90a0, shade #94626e. They were chosen to be at least 20 CIEDE2000 from every beach tone (ground, rock, subsoil, accent: worst 20.7) and from every energy lane's main colour (worst 19.8, the lit face against the Empress's white, for a shape that small): the pale lit face of any prop sits near the Empress's ivory. No red (R share under 0.5). Under the colour-vision presets the distances were not simulated for this shape; the keyline carries it.
- **Depth and foot:** as the other props (a few percent into the ground on a slope). It is decoration and not a PROP SMASH target (World's smash list: lone trees, boulders and bergs).
- **Not changed:** `data/art/biomes.json` and the biome schema (the shell is not in the beach's cover list; it comes from the sim's props).
