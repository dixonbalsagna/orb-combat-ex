## What it does

| When | What | Where |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Fronts meet | The beams carve as they did until now, then **stop** | Encounter: each live beam's `stopAt` = its front (3 lines) |
| During | One **ring** at the point, 1 to 3 body heights (75 units each) across the struggle, at most one every 2 s. The buildings inside take the stronger beam's damage as one blast does (its structure factor under Legal's x1.5, its ring cap, keep 0.25). A first ring leaves a standing building at `leaveFrac` of its hit points; the next may take it. **No digging, no groove** (`perPulse` 0) | `WorldStruggleMeet.ring(S, ev, frac)`; cue `struggle_ring` |
| End, once, every ending | **One wave and the crater, in one call.** `E_meet` = both beams' `E(level) x stage / count` added, x9 if either is a signature; dug energy = `E_meet x f_end x f_height`; bowl `160 x sqrt(dug)` by `WorldCrater.dig` (special, so it is handed a ninth), depth 0.22 | `WorldStruggleMeet.meet(S, ev)` |
| End: buildings | Inside the bowl they **fall, falling off from the centre**: within 0.8 of the radius every standing building (the cap: the stronger fighter's `beamLevelCapShare` of the first ring, the rest left at 0.2); from there to the lip a building takes 0.4 of its hit points falling to nothing at the lip. **Glass:** every tower within the radius **before the height cut** is taken to 0.85 of its hit points (stage 1, windows out) and no lower, and counts no one. So an air burst shatters windows and fells nothing | same call |
| End: ground | `bowl`: dug. `scorch` (dug energy under 0.47, a dent): `WorldCrater.scorch` and nothing else. `air` (the point past twice the radius up): nothing dug and nothing scorched on the ground in the sim; the look is VFX's, from the cue's `shape` field | same call |
| Overwhelm | The winner's beam carves **from the point outward** through the loser and blasts him where he lands; the stretch hand to point gets nothing new | Encounter |

`f_end`: detonate 0.5, time 0.25, overwhelm and let-go 0.25 x (run / 5 s, at most 1). `heightReach` twice the radius before the cut, height the point's lowest above the ground in the last second. Every number is `craters.json struggleMeet` (Game Design's block, plus World's `wave` keys: `fallReach` 0.8, `dent` 0.4, `keep` 0.2, `glassHpShare` 0.85, `bodyHeight` 75).

## Needs from Encounter (`sim/director/beam.gd`; the EP routes it)

1. **At the struggle's start** (`_startStruggle`), when `WorldStruggleMeet.on()`: for each live beam of A or D, `b.stopAt = b.p * b.len` (it stops carving where its front is; `beamStep` already clips `s1` to `stopAt`).
2. **The end-of-struggle event**, one Dictionary built once and handed to `WorldStruggleMeet.meet(S, ev)` and also written on the `beam_clash_end` cue:
   `{x, y, z` (the point, world), `how` (`"time"`, `"detonate"`, `"overwhelmed"` or `"letgo"`: the cue's `source`), `run` (seconds the struggle ran, float), `lowY` (the point's lowest y over the last 60 ticks; else `y`), `a: {slot, stage, count, sig}`, `d: {slot, stage, count, sig}}`. `a` and `d` are the exchange's A and D (slots as `S.fighters.find`); `stage` and `count` are that fighter's beam's charge stage and stage count when he fired (the ladder, `beam-answers-2.md` §1); `sig` is true when it is a signature. A side that omits `stage` and `count` counts as the top stage of one (all of his energy). Nothing else is read.
3. **At the end** (top of `_resolveStruggle`, before the endings): `var res = WorldStruggleMeet.meet(S, ev)`. If `res.on`: skip the **detonation's old `_blast` at the point** (`meet` is that blast) and, on an overwhelm, fire the winner's beam **from the point** (`fireBeam` with the origin at (x, y), the same direction through the loser, the length from the point) with `_blast` at the loser as now. Add `r` (the bowl's radius, 0 for a scorch or an air burst), `rGlass` (the radius before the height cut), `shape` (a string: `bowl`, `scorch` or `air`; **not** `kind`, which is the cue's name, and **not** `ground`, which is `FxEvent`'s existing float: a debris event's floor) and `dug` to the `beam_clash_end` cue (the call's return calls it `kind`: copy it into `shape`). The returns also hold `fell` and `glass` (counts).
4. **During** (`clashTick`): every tick `st.ys` keeps the point's y for the last 60 ticks (for `lowY`); when `WorldStruggleMeet.ringDue(st.lastRing, S.tick)` call `WorldStruggleMeet.ring(S, ev, frac)` with `frac` = elapsed over the struggle's length and set `st.lastRing = S.tick` (start it at -1). The ring reads `cause.z` of the stronger fighter, which is on the plane near the point.
5. **Legal (RL-246 4):** the winner's beam, its blast at the loser, the meeting crater and the release in one ending are **one event with one envelope**: Encounter fires them on the same tick; measure the combined step.
