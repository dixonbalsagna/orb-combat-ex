# The surfacing rule (World, 2026-10-26)

For the EP's brief and Game Design's `rulings-2026-10-23b.md` item 7 (and rows 13 to 15): **a fighter dropped or landing in water surfaces set within 30 ticks, with no down state beyond that; he counts as landed once he surfaces.** Built as window 15 behind `data/biomes/water.json` `surface.enabled` (**off**: the water, the golden and every replay are as they were). Parked in `docs/world/pending/surfacing/`; check `sim/world/tools/surface_check.gd`.

**What it does.** A launched body in water that is not waded rises so as to reach the surface on the last of `surface.ticks` (30) ticks, at a speed of its depth over the ticks left (about 2 depths a second at the start, a body that has gone 300 units under rises at 600 a second), and the flight ends there: he is set on the surface, free, landed (the existing `sink` end, which the old rule fired wherever the body had slowed, at depth). Water that is waded (under 75 units, with water v2 on) is not touched; a body that skims off the surface is not touched. Rivers, lakes, filled trenches and the sea all count: the rule keys on the water's own surface (`WorldWater.surfaceAt`).

**Where.** World's `sim/world/contact.gd` (`moveAir`: the ground-contact flight, which is the default path) and `WorldFill` (the switch). **Simulation's lines (grant approved on Orb's word):** `sim/core/state.gd`, `Fighter.wetT` (ticks under the surface since a launch took him in, 1 line); `sim/core/hash.gd`, hashed only while not 0 (2 lines), so a dry state hashes as before. The data hash carries `water.json`'s surface block when the rule is on. The older `stepLaunched` path (`contactOn` false, off by default in the data) is not changed.

**Measured** (the hand-made world's deep sea, five launches: slow, steep, fast, glancing): each ends set on the surface 29 ticks after going under, free, never down; with the rule off the same launches end at another tick or depth. **Not built:** a fighter dropped (`SimFighter.drop`) into water: he ends free in the water where he is, controlled, with no down state, so the rule's "no down state" already holds and the AI's dash for the surface applies; a drop that should be lifted to the surface in 30 ticks is Encounter's call.
