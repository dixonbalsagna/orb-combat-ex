# Small-circle arenas: a loop round a centre point (World, 2026-10-16)

Owner: World and Environment. Orb (homework 22): "could the slice be along a smaller area, making the arena smaller and appear to circle around a center point? please provide prototype illustrations for this idea so we are on the same page." **Paper and prototype only; nothing in `sim/` or `data/` changed.** Pictures in `docs/world/planet-slice/`: [K1](planet-slice/k1-small-circles-planet.svg) the loops on the planet, [K2](planet-slice/k2-small-arena-strip.svg) the loops as strips, [K3](planet-slice/k3-small-arena-inside.svg) from above and from inside, to scale with fighters. Prototype: `scratch-build/small-circles/` (`smallcircle_dump.gd` on `WorldPlanet`, `gen_svgs3.py`, `small.json`); planet 7, the one in pictures A2 to F.

## 1. The idea

A great circle (a plane through the planet's centre) is the biggest loop on a planet: 153,600 units round, today's arena. **A small circle** is a plane that misses the centre: every point of it is the same angular distance from a centre point **c**. Its length is `2 pi R sin(theta)`: `theta` 14.5 degrees gives 38,400 units (a quarter), 30 degrees gives 76,800 (half), 90 degrees is the great circle. The loop's own radius is 6,111 units for 38,400 and 12,223 for 76,800; the centre is 6,177 and 12,800 units from the line, measured over the ground. The read is the same as the great circle's: column `i` is the point `cos(theta) c + sin(theta) (cos a u + sin a v)` at angle `a = i x 32 / r`; the planet function, the places and the wear list need no change.

## 2. What it looks like (K3)

- The side-on camera looks toward the middle. **The centre is always straight behind the fight** (a city's towers, a mountain, a lake, a pole) and **the far side of the loop is across the way** (12,222 units for the 38,400 loop: a skyline of the arena's own other half). That is what "appears to circle round a centre point" costs and gives: no new picture, only what the far land shows. Today the far land reads the same heightfield with a wander; for a loop it can read the real opposite arc and the real centre. Rendering's, and cosmetic.
- **A loop can go round anything.** K2: a ring of ice sheet round the south pole (98% ice), a ring of sea ice and open water round the north (64%); K1: a ring round a mountain, a city or a bay. The poles are the natural "round a point" arenas, and the research outposts (below) sit in them.
- **Scale:** a screen is 3,000 units, so it is 2% of the great circle, 4% of the 76,800 loop and **8% of the 38,400 loop**; a fight covers most of a small arena, and the lap, flat out, is 15 s (great circle), 7.5 s and **3.8 s**.

## 3. What it does to the sim (honest)

| Thing | Effect |
| :--- | :--- |
| **The wrap and the seam** | None in principle: a loop wraps exactly as the great circle does (the shortest arc round; nothing marks the seam, and the touch-down is any point of the loop). **But the length changes, and the length is a constant today**: `SimConst.W` (the wrap), `HALF`, `NC` (4,800 columns) and `COL` are read 83, 25, 102 and 115 times in 32, 6, 23 and 24 files. A match on a 38,400-unit loop has 1,200 columns and `W` 38,400 |
| **The cheapest way to vary it** | Make `W`, `HALF` and `NC` static variables set once at `newMatch` from a setup key (`arena`, the length in units; absent means today's values). The 210 reading sites stay as they are; **4 sites are compile-time `const`s built from them** (`location.gd:14`, `collateral.gd:45`, `structures.gd:32` and my `planet.gd`'s `TOUCH_COL`) and become functions. The golden does not move (the default is today's). Simulation's change, with one proof: a 1v1 golden byte-identical with `arena` absent. The frozen JS twin is not a gate. A static that every match sets is safe only while every match sets it: `newMatch` always does |
| **The hand-made Bellgate** | Stays at 153,600 (it is a hand-made 153,600-unit layout): a setup without a planet has no `arena` key |
| **Rendering and Camera** | The ground field, the far land's fade (`FOG_FAR` is 0.78 of the planet), the sky and the camera clamps read `W`; a 38,400 world is 4 times smaller than today's, so the fog and the zoom ceiling (a view up to 21,333 units wide: over half the smallest loop) need a look. Theirs |
| **The picker** | Choose a **centre** first (a city, a town, a landmark, a pole, or a random point), then a bearing, then the loop's length; the touch-down is a random point of the loop. Candidates in a seeded order, as now. The minimum **scales with the length** (data: at 38,400 one city, one town, two biomes, half land; at 76,800 one city, two towns, two biomes; at the full length the minimum of today). Measured on planet 7 (126 loops round its places at three bearings, plus 60 round random points): a loop meets the scaled minimum in **10 to 22%** of cases (38,400: 10% round places, 17% round random points; 76,800: 14% and 22%), about what a random great circle does, so the picker tries about ten candidates: tens of milliseconds |
| **What a small arena holds** | An accepted 38,400 loop holds on average 1.0 cities and 1.5 towns and is 48% settled (up to 66%: the best example, K2, is 1 city and 3 towns on 66% settled land); a 76,800 loop holds 1.4 cities and 2.4 towns (35% settled; the best has 2 cities and 4 towns). **A small arena is denser** than the great circle (32% settled): more to break per screen, the same 1,800 people, so a tier ceiling's share falls on fewer, bigger places. Game Design's collateral budget may want a smaller number for a small arena |
| **A loop round a city** | The loop of 38,400 round a city passes through the city's own outskirts (the city's radius is 6,500; the loop's 6,111), so the downtown is the "centre behind the fight" and the city is not counted by the picker's reach rule (60% of its radius). A rule is needed: the centre place counts as held when the loop lies inside its disc |
| **Wear and the run** | Unchanged: the wear list is in the place's frame, and a loop crossing the same place reads the same records. A place can be fought over from a great circle and from a loop |
| **A smaller planet instead** | The other way to a small arena is a smaller planet (a great circle of radius 6,111). It needs the same `W` change, plus smaller cities and a different planet for each arena size. A small circle on the one planet keeps one set of names, cities and landmarks for every arena size: **recommended** |

## 4. What I would do

1. **Orb's call first:** do small arenas appeal enough to build? They fit "the same places recur" and give the polar fights a natural ring.
2. **If yes:** Simulation's `arena` setup key (the static-variable change above) with its golden proof; World's picker with centres and the scaled minimum (planet window 1's module extended, no golden); then Rendering's far land for a loop. In that order, each a window.
3. **A good use:** size by stage. A Rampage or a Trial in a small arena (more crowded, faster, fewer places to run to), the boss on the great circle (the whole planet), the polar stages as rings. Game Design's.
