# The liftable wreck: World's query for Wreck Toss (2026-10-25)

For Game Design 23d section 8 item 20 (the Cyborg's Wreck Toss; Legal RL-183 and ln04). `sim/world/wrecks.gd` (`WorldWrecks`), data `data/biomes/wrecks.json`, proof `sim/world/tools/wrecks_check.gd` (16 checks, 0 failed). Parked in `docs/world/pending/wreck-toss/`. Nothing in the sim calls it until Encounter does, so no golden moves.

**What a wreck is.** A building that has fallen (not alive) and has not been hurled. Its mass is a share of its box in cubic body heights (`fill`, 0.012: the piece he can take hold of, not the heap); the city's houses run 0.5 to 6 (median 1.1) and its towers 2 to 57 (median 16). A fallen building holds no one: its people are gone as it falls, so a wreck is always empty.

**The query Encounter calls** (the pick-up press): `WorldWrecks.pick(S, x, y, z, maxMass)` returns `{}` or `{id, mass, x, y, z, w, h, d, dist}`: the nearest wreck within 2 body heights (to the wreck's box: along x, across depth, up from the ground it lies on), no heavier than `maxMass` (`WorldWrecks.lift(tier)` or Combat's own number), empty, with **no living civilian in a standing building within 3 body heights of its edge** (`nearBh`; fighters are not counted unless `fighters` is set true: reading "nothing living near it" as civilians, since the thrower himself stands within 2 bh. *Legal: confirm that reading.*). `WorldWrecks.within(S, x, y, z, maxMass)` lists every wreck in reach with why it is refused (small, heavy, occupied, living): the debug feed.

**The burst** (when the hurled wreck lands): `WorldWrecks.burst(S, id, x, y, tier, cause)` spends the wreck (`Building.marks` = -1000, hashed when not 0; a dead building is never struck again, so brunt's repeat marks never read it) and damages structures near the landing by the brunt ladder: speed 900 x the tier's `tierFactor` x `bruntMul` (the same ladder as a body's brunt), scaled by the square root of the wreck's mass over 1.5, over a radius of 1.6 body heights scaled the same (1 to 3.5). Measured on the toughest tower: 135, 303, 527 and 819 hp at tiers 1 to 4. A blast cannot finish a standing building (stages.json `leaveFrac`), so a scrap leaves a tower standing and the next blow takes it, as a blast does. The look is the existing debris, dust and shake (no new event, no Simulation line).

**Numbers that are not mine.** `tierLift` [0.8, 2.0, 5.0, 14.0] (the heaviest wreck each tier lifts) is a placeholder on the city's masses: tier 1 lifts the smallest houses, tier 4 a small tower. Game Design and Combat own the real ones; Encounter may pass its own `maxMass`.
