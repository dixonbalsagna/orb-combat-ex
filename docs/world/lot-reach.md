# A lot answers only near itself (World, 2026-11-02)

Simulation's find, through the EP: a big lot's flat has no distance off its street's plane, so it also answers in a tube through the planet; on planet 11 it raised the ground to its own height at points 28,000 to 49,000 units from its centre.

**Verified.** `WorldField.applyRecords` reads a lot by two tests, along the street and across it, against the footprint's half length and half width plus the blends (`ld < 1`). The chord vector from the lot's centre has a third part, off the street's plane, which neither test sees: any point whose projection on the plane falls in the footprint passes, however far through the planet it is. Points near the planet's far side (a chord of about 48,900 units, the diameter) project to the footprint's middle. In `lot_reach_check.gd` (`LOTREACH`), a lot with a half width and length of 12,000 units changes the ground at **20 of 64 points** from beside it to the far side, the farthest at **48,892 units**; with the fix, none.

**Why my planets did not show it.** Every real lot of planets 7 and 11 has a half diagonal under about 2,000 units, and the record index lists a record by its box, so a lot is only read near itself: a lot with a reach past 2,048 was in the index's "big" list, read at every point (that is the exposure Simulation's records had, with lots large enough to be listed there), and past `water-read` a lot up to 16,384 is in the coarse grid and local, so only a lot over that would answer in the tube. The formula should not rely on the index, so it is fixed at the record.

**The fix** (`sim/world/field.gd`, `applyRecords`, the lot branch): before the two tests, a distance test: a point farther from the lot's centre than `1.3 x (half diagonal + blend + across blend) + 400` units (a chord, in the same units) is not the lot's. A point that passes the two tests is within the half diagonal and the blends of the centre by its projection, and its chord is longer than that only by the sphere's curve (a few per cent for a lot of 12,000), so no legitimate point is dropped; the 1.3 and the 400 are slack. **Exact for the lots there are:** on planets 7 and 11 no lot acted beyond its reach before the fix (0 of 3,968 points), so every hash is the same; the golden is not moved by it.

**Not done.** `pad` and `platform` records already use the full chord distance and are not affected. The records Simulation is re-cutting (places-records) have the shape of lots with a bigger reach; this fix is at the reader, so it covers them.
