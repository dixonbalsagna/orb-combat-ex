# World: biomes W6, the launch candidates' flight hooks and a prop query the sim owns (cloud, 2026-10-28)

Brief: `docs/ep/cloud-briefs/world-w6-flight-hooks.md`. Base: origin/main cb73741. Branch: `cloud/world-environment/biomes-w6`. Paper: `docs/world/flight-hooks-w6.md`.

## SUMMARY

While a launched fighter flies, World's flight now reads his plan's biome fields, so each candidate plays as biomes.md section 3 says:
- **MARSH SWALLOW** always embeds, 1.8 times as deep;
- **TREE LINE:** each trunk on his path snaps and takes 12% off his speed;
- **PROP SMASH:** the prop breaks and he stops at it;
- **a soft landing** multiplies the journey's damage;
- **ICE SKID** hands control back at the rock stop, then he skates on the ice brake.

`WorldGround.matchProps` is the one prop query for the director and the renderer. It is keyed by the planet's seed and leaves out the props launches have broken, and the director now uses it.

All of it runs only with Combat's `biome.enabled`, which stays **off**. With it off, the hand-made world, gen 1 and gen 2 give the same state hashes as origin/main (990 hashes), and the golden is byte-identical. Every gate passes. Look evidence pending (local).

## DECISIONS

1. **The plan's fields ride in the fighter's director integers (`f.act.dirI`), written by `doLaunch`.**
   - Why: World can't add a field to the fighter or the state (core files outside the grant). `dirI` is already hashed and snapshotted, and is the director's own per-fighter state.
   - Where: the block follows the rally's and is sized only with the switch on, so the hashed count doesn't move with it off.
   - Lifetime: a record belongs to one launch (its launch number), and a plan with no field writes none.
   - World reads it through `DirLaunch.flightHook`, reached as `SimDir._d("launch.gd")`, so the world's load still compiles none of the director (load_split passes).
2. **The prop query is keyed by the planet's seed, not the director's FNV seed.** That is the seed and the arguments W5 gave Rendering, so the props the sim reads are the drawn ones. Broken props are the director's state (a ring of the last 4 a fighter broke), passed to the query as a list. A broken prop stays out for the match.
3. **Each trunk his path crosses snaps,** in the air or on the ground, while he is lower than its top. Not only the planner's count.
4. **PROP SMASH stops him a body's radius (30) short,** with the stop's light brunt (`bump`, as a heap: no dent). In the air he drops from there.
5. **`soft` multiplies the whole journey's wear and its cap,** so the journey hurts `soft` times as much.
6. **ICE SKID's "rock stop" is ruling 12's reference run.** Sea ice's surface is rock, so the reference braking is rock's 0.8. When the journey ends first (off the floe into a lead), nothing changes.
7. **The hooks are the runtime's only.** The planner's journey predictor does not see them.

## RISKS

- **ICE SKID rarely hands over:** 2 of 18 launches. Its end bonus prefers sends that end in a lead, and most floes end before the rock stop would come. The hook is right; QA may want to see the share.
- **The record's block is the tail of the director's `dirI` chain.** A future director block must be placed after it (`DirLaunch._hkBase() + HK_N`).
- **A fighter's broken-props ring holds 4.** A fifth prop he breaks lets the oldest come back.
- **Changed choices with the switch on:** the director's props changed seed, so VENT LIFT and PROP SMASH spots differ from W3's. launchbiome_probe still passes (VENT LIFT 6 of 16, PROP SMASH 3 of 60).
- **One director call a tick for each launched fighter,** in every match. It is a size check while the switch is off, and the state is unchanged (990 hashes match).
