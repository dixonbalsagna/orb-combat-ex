# World: biomes W5, props on gen 2 planets (cloud, 2026-10-28)

Brief: `docs/ep/cloud-briefs/world-w5-props.md`. Base: origin/main 31ae23e. Paper: `docs/world/props-w5.md`.

## SUMMARY

The provisional props are now placed on gen 2 rings, with `provisionalProps` still **off**.
- **Bergs:** one on each of the planet's own berg cells in the ring, at the cell's peak and sized by its height. The row's keyed draw adds more on the floes and in open water within one berg cell of the pack.
- **Lone trees** (savanna, beach) keep their gap from the ring's trees, from lakes and from each other.
- **`WorldGround.propsNear(S, x, reachBh)`** gives PROP SMASH its targets by shortest arc.

Everything is a pure function of the columns, keyed by the planet's seed. It is the same twice, under any split of the ring, across the seam, and on the columns a turn keeps. Nothing in the sim calls it. A gen 2 match is bit-identical with the switch on, and gen 1, the hand-made world and the golden are unchanged. Every gate passes. Look evidence pending (local).

## DECISIONS

1. **A berg cell is a run of sea ice columns at least `bergH0` (45) high.** No floe stands above 38, so the planet's own numbers tell the two apart. Each cell gets one berg at its peak (a tie goes to the west), sized by its height over 75, and marked `cell: true` because the terrain already stands there.
2. **"Within the pack's band" means within one berg cell (`bergCell`, 1,100 units) of the ring's sea ice.** The ring's columns don't carry the planet's pack concentration, and this needs no new number. Drawn bergs stay out of a cell's largest size (7 columns), and the W4 draw keys are kept.
3. **Lone-tree gap rule:**
   - a drawn tree is dropped when a ring tree is within its keyed gap, or a lake holds its column;
   - where two drawn trees are closer than the larger of their two gaps, the one with the higher keyed priority stands.
   This depends only on the columns, never on the range, so any split or window gives the same trees.
4. **I cut the gaps to about a third.** W4's gaps (960 to 3,200 units) were wider than the ring's own savanna and beach tree spacing (about 1,240 units), so nearly no lone tree could stand. They are still provisional, for Art.
5. **The seed is the planet's (`S.planetRing.seed`), not the slice hash.** The columns a turn keeps keep their props with no state carried.
6. **`propsNear` returns `[]` while `provisionalProps` is off**, as well as on gen 1 and the hand-made world. The targets wait on Art's sizes, and nothing in the sim calls it until PROP SMASH's switch.

## NEEDS FROM EP

1. **Combat / Encounter (PROP SMASH):** call `WorldGround.propsNear(S, x, 40)` behind the candidate's own switch. Each target comes as `{x, kind, size, col, d}`, nearest first. Combat should confirm the target kinds: `lone_tree`, `boulder` and `berg` today, plus `outcrop` once a row has one. Should `lava_rock` or `lichen_rock` count?
2. **Rendering:** draw the props from `propsIn(fine, stored, base, S.planetRing.seed, i0, i1, S.trees, S.planetRing.lakes)`. That is the same seed and arguments `propsNear` uses, so what is drawn is what PROP SMASH hits. A berg with `cell: true` sits on terrain that is already the berg's shape.
3. **Art:** set the berg and lone-tree sizes and gaps, then turn `provisionalProps` on.
4. **Look evidence pending (local):** the side-by-side with the props on, once Rendering draws them.

## RISKS

- **Lone trees are rare on wooded savanna.** On planet 8, touch 3 (157 ring trees), all of them were dropped. On planet 6, touch 2, four stand on 2,400 columns.
- **On a turn's blend band** (weight between 0 and 0.5), `base` is mixed, so a berg cell's edge or a dry column can change there. The kept core is exact.
- **A prop broken by PROP SMASH comes back** if the director re-asks, because the props are stateless. A broken-props list is Encounter's state to keep.
- **Each call costs about 70 column reads for a drawn candidate,** but candidates are rare (about 1 in 80 to 1 in 160 columns), and the berg pass only runs with the switch on.
