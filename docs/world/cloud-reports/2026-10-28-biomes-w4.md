## DECISIONS

1. **Keyed by look class** (`WorldContact.lookAt`), so the beam strips dig their own rows (burnt grass digs ash, a glaze ice, an ember strip crust). A gen 1 or hand-made world only has today's five classes, which `touchBuilt` gates, so it digs today's bowls even with the module on.
2. **Each factor multiplies today's bowl:** the depth and its relief cap come from today's radius, not the widened one (sand is 1.3 wide and 0.7 deep, not 0.91), and the rim from today's depth.
3. **steep** is the power of the bowl, `(1 - u^(2 steep))^2`: 1 is today's polynomial exactly, so the hash cannot move with it at 1.
4. **crater_shape without a hash.gd line:** the dig writes the material into the existing crater event's `surface` (not one of its hashed fields); `eventFor(e, e.surface)` gives the rest. No new state and no static.
5. **Break-through** when the bowl's floor goes below the sea on a sea-ice column that stood above it. The slot is as wide as the bowl's columns below the sea. He is not embedded (no bowl, the water rules take him), and there is no fill or refill.
6. **Refill:** the bowl rises by its share in its own shape; standing water keeps its level. `cracks`, `ejecta`, `rimKind`, `treesLean` and `glowTicks` are carried for the event only.
7. **Props:** my sizes. A berg is 0.6 to 2.9 fighter heights, which matches planet-ice.md's 45 to 215 units. Lone trees get a `gap` (clear ground to any other tree) for W5's placement; propsIn doesn't read it yet.

## RISKS

- **A mud bowl holds water above the sea** (to the wetland's water table), as after.md's flood kind already does. Away from wetland and the shore's 30 columns, craters still never flood inland.
- **With depth rows on,** the refill and the fill work the slam's own row only; the neighbouring rows keep the dug bowl (`_digNeighbours` does take the wall shape).
- **`deepen`** (a blow into the crater he lies in) lowers with today's bowl shape.
- **Slams are rare on gen 2:** 0 to 4 a match in 12 probe matches (2,400 ticks each), and 3 of those landed on rows (mud, snow, turf). QA's embed band (0.2 to 1 a minute) needs a longer gen 2 batch with the switch on.
- **A shaped bowl near a floe edge** cuts a slot as wide as its below-sea columns, up to the bowl's width (10 columns for a 2-energy slam on a floe 12 above the sea).
