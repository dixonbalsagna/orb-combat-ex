## SUMMARY

A Half ring on QA's 40 gen 2 rings stands **77.6 structures on average (38.5 before)**, a Close ring 47.8 (22.8 before), a Whole ring 214 (111.1 before). The places are the planet's own, unchanged: a gen 2 ring's city and town archetypes are laid denser (shorter gaps, narrower houses, the villages' back street built). No site is added, no kind changes, gen 1 and the hand-made world never read it.

QA's gen 2 arm (200 matches, same seeds and overlay as QA's baseline) with it on, against the same build with it off:
- structures levelled a minute at tier 2: **3.06%** (4.08% off; band at most 4%);
- journeys over 4,000 units: **11.9%** (11.7% off; band at most 20%);
- bounces per bounced journey: **1.94** (1.85 off; band 1.2 to 2.0).

The long-journey and bounce rows were already inside their bands on this build (see DECISIONS 3 and 4). The gen 1 worlds and the golden are byte-identical.

## DECISIONS

1. **Density, not sites.** Game Design's preference; the stored planet's cities and towns are shared with gen 1 and cannot move. The archetypes' gap, width and rows are the lever. Tried on the 40 rings (mean structures): 0.5/0.8 city and 0.4/0.75 town 68; 0.4/0.7 and 0.35/0.65 79; 0.3/0.6 both 89; 0.2/0.5 both 112. The shipped pair (city 0.4/0.8, town 0.3/0.6) reads 77.6 and keeps the towers 80% as wide. A city gains little from gaps alone (downtown gaps are already 0.4 to 1.4 fighter heights); the width does it.
2. **A separate data file, off `settlements.json`.** The replay header hashes `settlements.json`, so editing it would move every replay's data hash; `density.json` is read only on a gen 2 ring and is not in that hash.
3. **Long journeys: the buildings alone were more than enough, and the row was inside already.** QA's 20.8% (767ed0d) reads 11.7% on this build with density off and 11.9% with it on (main has moved since 767ed0d: W6's flight hooks and Combat's changes among them; I did not isolate which change brought the row down). Density did not move it either way (87 of 730 journeys against 81 of 692). Nothing else is proposed.
4. **Bounces: no absorb change shipped.**
   - The row reads 1.85 off and 1.94 on, inside 1.2 to 2.0; the bootstrap interval spans the edge in both (136 to 143 bounced journeys, plus or minus 0.2).
   - Absorb is a weak lever: a probe of 26,304 synthetic journeys from the 40 rings' ground (`bounce_probe.gd`, scratch) gives 1.534 bounces per bounced journey; scaling every gen 2 absorb by 0.85 gives 1.494 (-2.6%), by 0.7 gives 1.457 (-5%). The probe's bounced share did not move (25.2%), because absorb acts after the first bounce.
   - I tried the modest trim the brief names (savanna 0.95 to 0.90, tundra 1.05 to 0.95, highland 1.0 to 0.90, volcanic 1.05 to 0.95) in a full 200-match run: 1.99 (136 to 135 bounced journeys; noise). It did nothing the run can see, so I took it out.
   - If QA wants margin on this row, the lever is a larger cut (all gen 2 absorb x0.7 buys about 5%), not these four; or the interval, with more matches.
5. **Rows 1 to 3 for every district, row 0 untouched** (the foreground stays empty until Camera frames it).

## RISKS

- **Narrower buildings change the skyline.** A tower 80% as wide is capped lower by `aspect_max` (no tower thinner than a ratio of its height). The look is unreviewed; the switch `enabled` turns the whole change off.
- **Sparse rings stay sparse.** Density multiplies places; a ring with one town stands 20 to 30 structures and one with none, none. The campaign's picker asks 1 city and 2 towns for a Half ring, which the QA setup does not, so shipped rings should read higher than QA's. A floor of 40 for any ring would need farmsteads (a new kind of place), which Game Design's preference excludes; not built.
- **The bounce and long-journey rows are inside their bands by the interval's edge** (1.94 bounces; bootstrap 1.72 to 2.18). A re-read on more matches may show either side of 2.0.
- **A gen 2 replay's buildings follow `density.json`**, which the replay header does not hash.
