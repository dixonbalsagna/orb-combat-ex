# Window 13: B's volcanic score and the climate on B plates (parked; only when `land.json` `b.enabled` is on)

World and Environment, 2026-10-25. `gen2-w13.diff` (sha1 79a18c794f3e, 8 files) is a git-apply diff, applied on the EP's gate tree (main plus windows 9 to 12 and the host diff). It changes nothing with B off (`WorldLand.plateB()` false): gen 1 and the parked gen 2 are as they were.

- **(a) The volcanic fields.** `WorldPlateB.volcAt(P, x, y, z)` is the volcanic score of the last ground read at that point (a read is made if the last was elsewhere); `WorldClimate.plain()` with B on makes a land point whose score is over plates-b's `volcanic` (0.55), not colder than the first temperature edge and not a beach, the `volcanic` fine biome, also on ground that would be mountains, and does not read the climate's own noise. Research's approach B does the same. On planet 7, 92 of 120 land points over the threshold are volcanic and the other 28 are too cold or a beach (`b_check.gd`).
- **(b) The climate on B.** B's ground is taller than window 3's, so the stored mountain line (3,200) and the highland line (2,300) made 15% of the non-ice land rock where Research's own climate gives about 6%. `climate.json` gets a `b` block (mountains above 4,200, highland from 3,000) read only with B on; on seeds 7, 11 and 42 mountains plus highland are now 7%, 4.5% and 8% of the non-ice land. `sim/world/tools/b_heights.gd` prints the land-height percentiles the lines were chosen from. The dry share (desert and savanna) is 21%, 30% and 30% of the non-ice land, against Research's 18 to 33% for the same seeds (which adds its shrubland and steppe).
