# water-window-warm: a new flow window reads its span in chunks (World, 2026-11-01)

Performance's spike (`docs/perf/orb-first-play-spikes-2026-11-01.md`): `WorldWater.step` takes 27.1 and 18.9 ms at a beam's first water window (ticks 11548 and 11570 of Orb's replay), 309 and 421 ground reads in one tick. **Off by default** (`water.json` `flowWarm.cols` 0). Design and what it costs: `docs/world/water-window-warm.md`.

**Apply:** `water-window-warm.diff` (5 files: `sim/world/water.gd` (`warmCols`, a new window's negative age, `_warmChunk`), `data/biomes/water.json` (`flowWarm`), its schema (an optional key), `tools/world-checks.json` (a line for the check) and the new `sim/world/tools/water_warm_check.gd`). The first three apply on HEAD (`git apply --check --include=` those files); the whole diff applies on the tree with `crater-collapse` applied (the `world-checks.json` hunk sits next to its line: hand-merge if you land them in the other order, one line each).

**What it does, and what it does not prove.** With `cols` 48 a new flow window (WorldWater's, not water v2's fill windows) starts at minus the steps its span needs (351 columns: 8 steps); each step it reads 48 more of its span and no water moves in it until all are read, so the first flow comes 16 ticks (0.27 s) later. No tick reads more than 48 columns of a window. `WATERWARM` 0 failed on my tree: off starts at age 0; on starts at -8 and the first flow step comes exactly 16 ticks later; the same setup gives the same state twice; after 1,200 ticks the water is within 0.04% of the off run's. **I could not reproduce the spike**: on my tree a flow step takes 0.3 to 0.8 ms even with the memo cold and a record just pushed, because the ground at my test coast is already read; Orb's 19 to 27 ms is on ground the match had not read (cold nodes) or with many records in the grid. So **the number that matters is Performance's: run Orb's replay with `flowWarm.cols` 48 and read ticks 11548 and 11570.** If the worst tick there falls to under 5 ms, land it; if it does not move, the cost is the records' height in the memo-miss reads (not the nodes) and the fix is Simulation's field-place (per-place forgetting), not this.

**With it on, every hash with a window moves** (the window's age is hashed, and the water starts 0.27 s later), so it is not for a replay recorded without it.
