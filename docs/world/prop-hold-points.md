# Hold points on the throwable objects (World, 2026-10-18)

Animation's ask (`docs/animation/throwables-sizing.md`, T1): the clinch's grip layer puts a hand on a region of a fighter's body; a prop has no regions, so it needs **hold points** in World's prop data, read as a world point the way a body region is read. This is that data and the function that reads it. **Parked** in `docs/world/scratch-build/repeats-props/`; nothing is in the tree yet.

- **Data:** `data/biomes/props.json` (schema `biomes.props/1`, `tools/schemas/biomes-props.schema.json`). Four classes from Art's cars (`data/art/vehicles.json`): bike, sedan, van, bus, each with its box (length, height, depth in world units; a fighter is 75), mass class and first lifting tier, and six hold points.
- **The six slots** (Animation's names): `frame` (the middle of the body), `underside_l` and `underside_r` (under the chassis, 0.28 of the length either side of the middle, 0.9 of the wheel radius up), `top` (the roof), `end_a` (the rear) and `end_b` (the front), the ends at 0.45 of the height.
- **The prop's frame:** the origin on the ground under its centre, x along its length with the front at +x, y up, z across (positive toward the camera). A point is `[x, y, z]` in it.
- **The read:** `WorldProps.holdPoint(class, slot, x, y, z, turn, facing)` gives the world point for a prop whose ground centre is at (x, y, z), turned `turn` radians counter-clockwise about the middle of its box, `facing` +1 or -1 (-1 mirrors x). `holdPoints(...)` gives all six by name for the view's per-tick read. x is wrapped at the seam. Pure and deterministic (`SimDetMath` sin and cos).
- **What is not here:** the prop itself. World's option B (`docs/world/cars-plan.md`, `S.props`) is not built, so there is no prop position or turn in the sim to read each tick (T1's "view read") and no `hold` state or height for T2; the function takes them as arguments and the sim does not call it, so no golden moves. A ship (tier 3) is not among Art's four silhouettes.
- **First values.** The points come from the boxes. Animation measures them against the poses and edits the data; the check (`sim/world/tools/props_check.gd`) keeps every point inside its box and every class complete.
