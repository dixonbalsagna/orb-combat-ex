# A read for the zip: is this point inside ground or a building, with 1 bh to spare

Owner: World and Environment. Status: scratch function and note, 2026-10-04 (for `docs/design/melee-press-feel.md` section 2c, "Ground, buildings and the wrap"). Not in the tree.

**What exists.** `WorldTerrain.groundY(S, x, z)` (the wrapped heightfield: base relief, craters, heaps; `x` is wrapped inside, any x is fine). `WorldStructures.near(S, x, r)` (the buildings by bucket), `baseY(b)`, `curH(b)` (the standing height now), `b.w`, `b.d`, `b.z`, `dz(b)`, `Z_REACH` (the reach every blast uses). The shortest-arc math is `SimWrap.sdx`. Water is `WorldWater.surfaceAt`, which the zip ignores (it is not an obstacle).

**The read: `WorldStructures.blockedAt(S, x, y, z = 0.0, margin = 75.0) -> bool`.** True when the point is inside the ground or within `margin` of it (its height above the ground under 1 bh), or inside a standing building's box widened by `margin` (from its ground to its standing height now), when the building's nearest face is within `Z_REACH` in depth of `z` (the same filter as shots and blasts; the front street stands about 400 units behind the fight plane, so at depth 0 only buildings that reach forward count, as for every blast). The wrap is inside it (`sdx`), so a point across the seam is the same point. Pure: no draw, no state, no event. The function is in `docs/world/scratch-build/stage1/blocked_at.gd.txt` (about 25 lines in `structures.gd`) with `blockcheck.gd`; it reuses the helpers of the stage slice.

**Cost.** 1.6 microseconds a probe over open ground, 3.5 in the densest streets: a full slide of 24 probes is 84 microseconds, once per zip. Nothing to budget.

**Does a staged building block?** Yes, by what is standing: a damaged building or a shell (stage 3) blocks up to its **current** standing height (`curH`, 30 to 45% of its full height for a shell), so a point at the old roof height above a shell is clear and a point inside it is not. A skyscraper's cleared floors (a tunnel: `fmask` holes) are open at the floor's own height and clear of the walls by the margin; with a floor of 149 units and a margin of 75 that is a sliver, so in practice the zip does not end inside one. A building at stage 4 is gone (not alive) and its rubble heap is ground, so `groundY` already blocks by it. A building's windows or part-gone state (stages 1 and 2) change nothing here.

**What the zip does with it** (Encounter's): probe the exit point, then slide in 15 degree steps up to 24 probes to the first point where `blockedAt` is false, as section 2c says. The way out (round or over) and the way in are Encounter's; this read is only the point test. If the zip should also refuse an exit inside the fight-plane buildings of the rows behind (`dz` over `Z_REACH`), that is already the filter; pass `z` of the fighter's depth when depth lanes are on.
